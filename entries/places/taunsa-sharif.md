## Overview

In the town of Taunsa a manuscript of *Adab al-muridin* by Muhammad Chishti was copied around 1790; it is the source manuscript of the appendix to Ernst and Lawrence's study of Chishti Sufism (Ernst and Lawrence, *Sufi Martyrs of Love*, p. 159, folio 147).

## Where the Sources Disagree

- **The title and the date.** The book's bibliography gives the same town under another title, *Adab al-talibin*, for a manuscript "copied in Tawnsah, probably after" 1790, a year whose reading is uncertain in the printed text (Ernst and Lawrence, *Sufi Martyrs of Love*, p. 243, folio 233), against the appendix's *Adab al-muridin*, copied "ca. 1790" (Ernst and Lawrence, *Sufi Martyrs of Love*, p. 159, folio 147).

## Bibliography

- Carl W. Ernst and Bruce B. Lawrence, *Sufi Martyrs of Love: Chishti Sufism in South Asia and Beyond* (2002).
