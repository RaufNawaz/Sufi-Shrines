## Overview

The name of Mithankot stands in the title of a work that Robert Rozehnal cites in an endnote to the first chapter of his study of politics and piety in Pakistan (Rozehnal, *Islamic Sufism Unbound*, p. 241, folio 234). The title reads "Journal of Captain C.M. Wade's Voyage from Lodiana to Mithankot by the River Satlaj on His Mission to Lahore and Bahawalpur", and it ends with the years 1832 to 1833 (Rozehnal, *Islamic Sufism Unbound*, p. 241, folio 234).
