## Overview

Michel Boivin names Tando Muhammad Khan in placing Tando Saindad, which he describes as near it, and names Mohsin Shah as the *gaddi nashin* of Tando Saindad and a Sufi affiliated to the Qalandariyya (Boivin, *Hindu Sufis of South Asia*, p. 123, folio 110).

## Tando Saindad and Mohsin Shah

Boivin writes of "Mohsin Shah, the gaddi nashin of Tando Saindad near Tando Muhammad Khan" (Boivin, *Hindu Sufis of South Asia*, p. 123, folio 110). The site he describes is therefore Tando Saindad, which he places near Tando Muhammad Khan; of Tando Muhammad Khan itself the passage says nothing more (Boivin, *Hindu Sufis of South Asia*, p. 123, folio 110).

Of Mohsin Shah, Boivin adds that he was "a Sufi who was affiliated to the Qalandariyya" (Boivin, *Hindu Sufis of South Asia*, p. 123, folio 110). For the line on Mohsin Shah and Tando Saindad, Boivin draws on Kripalani, citing page 38 of Kripalani's work of 1986 (Boivin, *Hindu Sufis of South Asia*, p. 123, folio 110).
