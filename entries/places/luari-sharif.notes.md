## Framing

- **The spelling.** Said once, in the Overview's first sentence: "Luari Sharif, which his book spells Lowari Sharif", cited to p. 22, folio 9, where the book's spelling appears. Quotations keep "Lowari Sharif" as printed.
- **The garbled printing on p. 228.** The book prints "Lowari Shari [text-layer? Lowari Sharif]" there. I quoted around that run ("have several important centres in Sindh, especially that of" ... "located in Southern Sindh, near Badin"), put the name between the two quotations in the article's own words, and said in plain words that "On that page the name of the place is uncertainly printed". The marker itself is not reproduced.
- **Makhdum Muhammad Hashim.** The source gives "The main author was **Makhdum Muhammad Hashim (1692–1762)**, who was qazi in Thatta" with no page reference of its own, joined by "of whom" to the p. 22, folio 9 quotation. I cited it to p. 22, folio 9 and introduced it as "In the same passage", not as "of these scholars", so the article does not itself assert that he came from the village. The dates are printed with an en dash, which the house rule forbids in the file, so they are given outside the quotation as "whose years he gives as 1692 to 1762" (no reading of them as birth and death). "was qazi in Thatta" is quoted verbatim. The material does not say what he was the main author of, and the article does not guess. See question 2.
- **Khwaja Muhammad Zaman is not named**, as the book does not name him. Nor is any shrine, tomb, ʿurs or custodian, since the material names none.
- **Bold removed** inside the quotations ("a village close to Badin in Southern Sindh", "Makhdum Muhammad Hashim", "the official book devoted to the Naqshbandis of Lowari Sharif was written by a Hindu follower"), as the rewrite brief directs.
- **Bibliography** copied exactly from matiari.md and shikarpur.md, so the book is listed the same way on every place page.

## Left out, and why

- "Indexed "Lowari Sharif (Badin, Sindh) 9" (p. 245, folio 232)": an index locator. It adds only that the index places the village in Badin, which the two passages already say in their own words. Left out.
- "**No saint, no tomb, no ʿurs and no custodian is named there, and the row's principal figure, Khwaja Muhammad Zaman, is not named anywhere in the book: (not stated).**": the note taker's remark, apparatus. Used only as a guide not to claim any of these.
- "The spelling difference ... is predicted by the book's own stated refusal of academic transliteration (p. 12, folio not stated).": the note taker's inference, apparatus. The article says plainly that the book spells the name Lowari Sharif and does not explain why. See question 3.
- "the book's only substantive prose about the centre": the note taker's judgement, not a statement of the book; not repeated.
- "*Attached to 1 row(s): dargah-of-khwaja-muhammad-zaman-luari-sharif*" and the ruling (a) boilerplate: apparatus.

## Weak material and how I treated it

- The index line (above): left out.
- "near Badin" and "close to Badin": these place Luari Sharif itself, not another site near it, so they are kept as the book's description of where the village lies.
- The Hindu follower and the official book are named in neither the quotation nor the material, so the article names neither and does not say that the book leaves them unnamed (the full page might name them). See question 1.

## Questions for Rauf

1. **The Hindu follower.** Boivin says "the official book devoted to the Naqshbandis of Lowari Sharif was written by a Hindu follower" (p. 228, folio 215). Neither the man nor the book is named in the material. If someone can check the page, naming them would make the Communities section worth much more.
2. **"The main author" of what?** Makhdum Muhammad Hashim is "The main author" in the p. 22 passage, but the material does not say of which work or body of writing, nor that he himself came from Luari Sharif. The article leaves both open. Worth a look at p. 22 (folio 9).
3. **The spelling note.** The note taker ties "Lowari" against "Luari" to the book's stated refusal of academic transliteration (p. 12). I left that explanation out as the note taker's inference. Should the article give it, cited to p. 12, folio not stated?
4. **Khwaja Muhammad Zaman.** The archive's site at Luari Sharif is his dargah, but the book never names him, so the article is silent on him and on the dargah. Is that acceptable for the place page, which lists the site separately?
