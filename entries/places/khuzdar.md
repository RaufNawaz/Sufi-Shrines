## Overview

Jürgen Schaflechner's study of the temple of Hinglaj Devi names Lahoot as a stop on Shah Abdul Latif's wandering, and places it in Khuzdar, writing "Lahoot (Khuzdar)" (Schaflechner, *Hinglaj Devi*, p. 95, folio not stated). The name of Lahoot also stands in the titles of two works the study cites, one by Abro and one by Nazamani (Schaflechner, *Hinglaj Devi*, pp. 94, 241, folios not stated; Schaflechner, *Hinglaj Devi*, p. 108, folio not stated).

## A Stop on Shah Abdul Latif's Wandering

In Schaflechner's book Lahoot is one of the stops on the wandering of Shah Abdul Latif (Schaflechner, *Hinglaj Devi*, p. 95, folio not stated). The book gives the stop as "Lahoot (Khuzdar)", and so places Lahoot in Khuzdar (Schaflechner, *Hinglaj Devi*, p. 95, folio not stated).

## The Place in the Books

The name of the place is carried in the titles of two works that Schaflechner cites. One is Abro's *Hinglāj ai Lāhūt* (Schaflechner, *Hinglaj Devi*, pp. 94, 241, folios not stated). The other is Nazamani's *Lāhūṭ lā Makān* (Schaflechner, *Hinglaj Devi*, p. 108, folio not stated).
