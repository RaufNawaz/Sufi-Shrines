## Framing

- **The myth.** Abbas calls Hir's story "the myth" (p. 205), so the article frames the story through that word: the Overview says the story is "in her words, a myth 'several hundred years old'", and the body opens "Abbas, who calls the story of Hir a myth". The p. 121 to 122 lines themselves are told by the book as plain narrative; they are quoted, never restated in the narrator's own voice as history.
- **The tomb.** A fact only as "villagers in the district of Jhang do identify Hir's tomb to this day" (p. 205), quoted whole. The article does not say Hir is buried there, does not locate the tomb within the district, and names no building or rite; the second sentence of Hir's Tomb says so plainly, since the obvious question is where and what the tomb is.
- **Persons.** Hir and Cucak Siyal are identified only as the lines identify them (daughter; "the powerful Jat chief of Jhang Siyal"). Hir is not called a heroine or a figure of Punjabi romance. **Ranjha is not named**: no line of this material names him, and nothing is brought in from the gujrat article.
- **The Siyal.** The book uses three words for them: "the family of the powerful Siyal Jats of Jhang" (p. 121), "Clan name" (glossary, p. 188), "The Siyal tribe" (p. 121). Each is kept in its own quotation, unreconciled. The inheritance custom is given as the book gives it, of the Siyal tribe, and not turned into a statement about Jhang's women or about the district's law.
- **"The glossary of her book".** The source names the p. 188 line as the glossary's. Kept, because the line reads as a glossary headword ("Siyal Clan name of Hir's family ...") and the reader should know it is a definition, not narrative. The p. 121 inheritance line is not said to be from the glossary: the source does not make that clear.
- **Spelling.** Inside quotations the book's spellings stand (Siyal, Panjab, Cucak). In the article's own prose: Hir, Cucak Siyal, Siyal, Jat, Jhang, Jhang Siyal. "Punjab" and "Panjab" are never used outside a quotation, so there is only one spelling of each name.
- **Bibliography**: the item gujrat already uses for this book, copied exactly.
