## Persian put into English (paraphrase, not in quotation marks; the source glosses none of it)

- p. 511, "پایان قصبه جهلم که درین محل شاهگذر[OCR?] واقع است", "و جهلم نام می یابد": "passes below the town of Jhelum, at the place where a crossing lies, and there takes the name Jhelum". Basis: پایان, below (downstream of); قصبه, town; که درین محل ... واقع است, where at this place ... lies; جهلم نام می یابد, takes (finds) the name Jhelum. "The Bihat ... passes" is the source's own English. The uncertain word شاهگذر[OCR?] is rendered only as "a crossing", since گذر is a crossing or ford, and the whole word's reading is uncertain; the article says "The name of that crossing is uncertain in the printed text" and does not romanise it (it could be read as a royal crossing, but that would be my reading, not the book's).
- "Bihat" and "Jhelum" are the source's own romanisations; no other Persian word is romanised.
