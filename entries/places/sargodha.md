## Overview

In Pnina Werbner and Helene Basu's volume, Malik refers to "the tomb to which his ancestors were attached" in the district of Sargodha, but names neither the tomb nor the village (Werbner and Basu, *Embodying Charisma*, p. 203, folio 192).

## Bibliography
