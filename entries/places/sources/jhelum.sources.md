## From Haroon Khalid, In Search of Shiva

### Place-level material: Jhelum

*Attached to 2 row(s): gurdwara-choa-sahib, tilla-jogian*

*The book's material headed "Jhelum (place-level: 2 rows)", copied into every existing entry that heading lists (ruling (a); Rauf, 10 October 2026). It is about the place in general, not about this shrine alone.*

Place-level under ruling (a), ch. 3. **Judgement call:** this is a pseudo-Alexander legend, an informant's tree, and a home-town mention ("from the Jhelum", which may mean the river).

- The author tells Salman Rashid of "a book which allegedly contains the letters of Alexander the Great", which the author says were written after Alexander's death. In one, "an old Indian man visited him in Jhelum after his war with Porus, and told him of two talking trees, one male and the other female at a little distance from Jhelum"; Alexander "then visited the trees and indeed found them to be talking to each other" (p. 9, ch. 3, folio not stated).
- Salman Rashid (informant): "I have heard of a bodhi tree in Islamabad and one near Jhelum" (p. 9, ch. 3, folio not stated).
- The author's ancestor Ghasita Ram "moved to this area from the Jhelum sometime in the late nineteenth century" (p. 9, ch. 3, folio not stated).
