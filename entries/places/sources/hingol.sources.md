## From Michel Boivin, Hindu Sufis of South Asia

### Place-level material: Hinglaj

- **shaktipeeth-shri-hinglaj-mata-mandir** (the book's "**Hinglaj**") — **place-level / site-level.**
  The book names it three times and once gives it a category: "Parsram claimed that the universal wisdom
  is kept in Sindh, **in Hinglaj to be exact, which is a pilgrimage centre located in the Sindhi-speaking
  area of Baluchistan**." (p. 50, folio 37) — the claim is Parsram's, reported by the book, and it stands
  inside a Theosophical narrative of "a universal wisdom, a kind of primordial wisdom through which
  humanity could reach immortality… lost many centuries ago, except for in a remote area Blavatsly
  [text-layer? — possibly `Blavatsky`] usually located in Tibet" (p. 50, folio 37). "He probably
  travelled with them on a number of **Hindu pilgrimages, including Dwarka and Hinglaj**." (p. 92,
  folio 79) — of Shah Abd al-Latif with the Nathpanthi jogis, **the hedge "probably" being the book's
  own; this is the sentence that links `bhit-bhit-shah` to this row.** "There are also a number of sacred
  Hindu places, such as **Hinglaj**, Kasi, or Kashi (Banaras), Sri Lanka and the Ganga" (p. 106,
  folio 93) — in Sachal Sarmast's poetry. Indexed "Hinglaj 37, 79, 93" (p. 243, folio 230), beside
  "Junagarh 79", "Dwarka 79", "Diach, Rao 79", "kamach 79", "chang 79", "charan 79" and "Karbala 79, 80,
  196–7 n.14" (pp. 240, 242, 244, folios 227, 229, 231) — folio 79 is the book's page of pilgrimage
  places and instruments. **The book gives Hinglaj no deity, no temple description, no custodian and no
  ʿurs: (not stated).** **The Pass-1 notes refused this mapping four times over on the ground that the
  archive row "carries an empty id cell" and called the defect blocking. That is a misreading of
  `shrine_index.tsv`: the row's `id` is `shaktipeeth-shri-hinglaj-mata-mandir` and it is present; the
  empty cells on that line are `category` and `principal_figure`. The material is therefore mapped here.
  See the report to the Doubts worker.**
