- **shrine-of-makhdoom-nooh-hala** (the book's "Hala" — 1 row)
  - **The book locates a major Sindhi shrine "in Hala" and describes the place as an agricultural
    economy.** Kolhi and Bhil women go to Shah Abdul Latif's shrine "primarily because the shrine is
    located in Hala, the heart of a fertile agricultural belt, where they can make a living working on the
    large feudal farms" (p. 46, folio not stated). **Under ruling (a) this is Hala's material. The book
    elsewhere places the same shrine "in Bhit Shah, Sind" without mentioning Hala (p. 50, folio not
    stated); both statements are recorded as printed and neither has been tidied into the other — see
    Doubts.**
