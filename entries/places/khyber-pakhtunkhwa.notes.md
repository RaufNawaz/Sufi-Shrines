## Whose unit, and when

- Khalid, *A White Trail*: "the province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KPK)", in the book's present (c. 2011). He also uses the modern name of Ranjit Singh's conquests (pp. 161 to 162); the article says he does so ("Khalid uses the modern name of the province even of the Sikh period") rather than mapping a Sikh period unit onto the modern province.
- Werbner and Basu (1998): "NWFP (North West Frontier Province)" (Lindholm) and "North-West Frontier Province" (Frembgen). Both used under those names.
- Rozehnal (2007): "Northwest Frontier Province" for the 2002 elections; his informant's "the frontier", lower case, which the article gives as her word for her home region, not as the province.
- Khalid, *Walking with Nanak*: "Khyber Pakhtunkhwa" in the present day.
