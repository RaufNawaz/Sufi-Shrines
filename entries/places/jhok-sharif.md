## Overview

Michel Boivin's book on the Hindu Sufis of South Asia names Jhok Sharif twice. It is one of the places Bedil visited, named with Daraza (Boivin, *Hindu Sufis of South Asia*, p. 97, folio 84), and the book names Sufi Hazrat Abdul Sattar Shah 'of Jhok Sharif', who speaks of *jam wahdat jo*, 'the cup of unity' (Boivin, *Hindu Sufis of South Asia*, p. 101, folio 88).

## A Place Bedil Visited

Of Bedil, Boivin writes: "He also visited Jhok Sharif and Daraza" (Boivin, *Hindu Sufis of South Asia*, p. 97, folio 84). Daraza is named only as the other place of that sentence, and the sentence gives no account of what Bedil did or found at Jhok Sharif (Boivin, *Hindu Sufis of South Asia*, p. 97, folio 84).

## Abdul Sattar Shah and the Cup of Unity

Boivin names "Sufi Hazrat Abdul Sattar Shah of Jhok Sharif", who, he writes, "speaks of jam wahdat jo", 'the cup of unity' (Boivin, *Hindu Sufis of South Asia*, p. 101, folio 88). For this Boivin gives a reference to Nasir Faqir, 1972 (Boivin, *Hindu Sufis of South Asia*, p. 101, folio 88).
