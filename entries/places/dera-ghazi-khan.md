## Overview

Dera Ghazi Khan is named once in Carl W. Ernst and Bruce B. Lawrence's study, as the provenance of one manuscript among several, in the words "one other Dera Ghazi Khan" (Ernst and Lawrence, *Sufi Martyrs of Love*, p. 239, folio 229).

## Bibliography

- Carl W. Ernst and Bruce B. Lawrence, *Sufi Martyrs of Love: Chishti Sufism in South Asia and Beyond* (2002).
