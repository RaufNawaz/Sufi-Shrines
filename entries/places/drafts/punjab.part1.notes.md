# Punjab, part 1: notes for Rauf and the composer

Material: `entries/places/sources/punjab.sources.md`, "## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore" (lines 445 to 671), read in full. Written 30 October 2026.

## The material

- The section holds five copies of the same 36 province bullets (attached to 50 rows, then to gurdwara-guru-ram-das-ji, loh-temple-lava-temple, mausoleum-of-waris-shah and peer-makki). Compared line by line: the copies differ only in the spelling of a few words (مین against میں, کلان against کلاں, پنجابیون against پنجابیوں) in four bullets (Deb Chand p. 14; the Ghurids p. 24; Ranjit Singh pp. 9, 112, 217, 96; Kharak Singh and Sher Singh pp. 221, 210; Heer Ranjha p. 10). No difference of content, so each bullet was used once.
- Every one of the 36 bullets is used in the part. Nothing was dropped as a whole.

## Left out, and why

- The date of Ranjit Singh's death, given in the bullet's context line as "۱۸۹۶ [OCR?] مہینہ ہگرمی [OCR?]", era not stated: it carries no page reference of its own (the bullet's pages are 9, 112, 217 and 96), and the reading is doubly uncertain. Left out.
- The mosque date "سنہ ١٢٢٣[OCR?] ہجری" in the context line of the Moran coin bullet (p. 179): no page of its own, and it dates a single site (the mosque), not the province. Left out.
- "سنہ ۱۸۴۷ع" given for John Lawrence as acting Resident, in the context line of the annexation bullet (pp. 33, 68, 141): no page of its own. Left out. The prose gives "Colonel Lawrence" only from p. 399.
- The Garhi Shahu bullet's own comment, "a thin statement about the region's climate, kept only because it is about the region and not the city" (pp. 362–363): treated as weak; used in one clause (Abul Khair liked the Punjab's climate and settled in Lahore), nothing more.
- The eighth blow (p. 29): the sentence breaks off at the page seam; the part says so in plain words and does not complete it.

## Judgement calls the composer should know

- **1864 for the museum exhibition.** The bullet's context line gives "سنہ ۱۸۶۴ء" and "جنوری سنہ ۱۸۸۲[OCR?]ء"; the 1882 date is tied to p. 387 in the bullet, so 1864 was attached to the only other page, p. 402 (folio 409). If the composer or Rauf prefers strictness, drop "in 1864" from that sentence.
- **"Blows".** The bullets' context lines number the invasions as "the fourth blow" to "the eighth blow". The part says Kanhaiya Lal "counts the blows that fell on the province one after another". If the ordinal framing is the bullet writer's and not the book's, soften to "the fourth of these invasions".
- **"Mughal" for مغل.** Kept as the book's word for the Chingizid armies, with one sentence saying so (p. 165). The Lahore version used "Mongol" for Bhandari; the composer may want one usage across parts.
- **"When the Sikh period came" (p. 336).** The book's phrase is "جب سکہی عمارت گری کا وقت آیا"; "عمارت گری" reads literally as building, but may be a misreading of plunder (غارت گری). Given neutrally.
- **The infantry figure (p. 96).** "جسکی تعداد و ستر ہزار سے کم نہ تہی": given as "not less than seventy thousand in wording that is not clean"; the "و" before ستر is unexplained.
- **The sixth blow (pp. 27–28).** The besieged place "ملتان [OCR?] سری نگر [OCR?] کا [illegible]" is given only as "a place whose printed name cannot be read with certainty".
- **Lakhpat Rai's gift (p. 337).** "ایک ایک گنڈو[OCR?] ٹکا[OCR?] اٹہائی[OCR?] ٹہائی[OCR?] سیر وزن کہانڈ[OCR?] کا": given as "a gift whose printed description, which seems to include a weight of sugar measured in seers, cannot be read with certainty".
- **British names.** Egerton (اجرٹن), McLeod (مکلوڈ [OCR?]), Bowring (بورنگ[OCR?]), Macgregor (میکلیگر[OCR?]), Montgomery, Colonel Lawrence (کرنیل لارنس) are romanised as the Lahore version spells them where it has them; the uncertain ones are flagged as uncertainly printed. The School of Art principal "کپننگ[OCR?]" is NOT named in the prose (any romanisation would be a guess).
- **Bibliography title.** The Source line prints *Tarikh-e-Lahore*; the item uses *Tarikh-e Lahore*, as the finished Lahore version does, because the cited title must be a substring of the "## From" heading and appear in the item (checker B2).
- The Fakir sahibs' plea (p. 148) is used twice, briefly, under Communities and under Shrines and the State; the composer may keep one.

## Disagreements (in "## Where the Sources Disagree")

1. The fourth blow: set in the time of Jalal al-Din Firuz Shah Khalji, but the absent sultan is named "شمس الدین التمش [OCR?]" (p. 26, folio 29).
2. Zakariya Khan's office: the inscription's "زکریا خان صوبہ پنجاب" against the book's heading "نواب زکریا خان [margin] زکریا خان [OCR?] المعروف خان بہادر صوبہ لاہور" (pp. 262–263, folios 265–266); "صوبہ لاہور" again at p. 337, folio 344; "حکام پنجاب" at p. 90, folio 93.
- Not listed, one side only: Kanhaiya Lal sets the Deb Chand account (from the *Tuhfat al-Wasilin* of Shaikh Ahmad Zanjani) against "صاحب خلاصۃ التواریخ", but the material does not give the Khulasat's version (p. 14, folio 9). It is stated in the prose instead. The composer may pair it with the Bhandari part if another part carries the Khulasat's founding story.
