## Framing

- **The name.** Las Bela throughout, as Schaflechner prints it; "Bela" only inside his quotation about the HSM's founding. The archive's name, Lasbela District, is not used in the prose because the book never prints it.
- **The park lines.** The brief allowed them only if the article could say what they say without claiming the park lies in the district. They are given as Schaflechner's account of the shrine's setting ("The shrine itself he sets in a park"), and one sentence says plainly that "He does not say in these lines whether the park lies within the district of Las Bela", cited to pp. 79 and 295, the two lines it compares. This sentence states a silence rather than a fact; see question 3.
- **"the shrine" at p. 295.** Unlike Hingol ("the shrine being Hinglaj's"), the article does not name the shrine in its own voice. It places the sentence after "a district close to Hinglaj" so the reader can follow, but leaves Schaflechner's "the shrine" as printed.
- **"in his study of the Hinglaj temple"** (Overview): from the book's title (*Identity, Change, and Solidification at a Hindu Temple in Pakistan*) as the Quetta article frames the same book.
- **The HSM.** Not expanded, not described: "the body he calls the HSM", as in Hingol. The Overview says it "was created at Bela in 1986 and has consisted mainly of Lohanas from the district", a close paraphrase of the two p. 82 quotations.
- **The Tumbel and "the Goddess".** Kept as Schaflechner's words; who the Tumbel are and the Goddess's name are not in the material and are not said.
- **The Dewanis.** "one Lohana family of the district" in the lead in sentence rests on the quotation itself ("a Sindhi Lohana family from Las Bela").
- **Signpost sentences without a citation**, each introducing a cited sentence and adding no fact: "Schaflechner names Las Bela as a place of the Goddess's renown"; "Las Bela is also where the body he calls the HSM began" (restating p. 82, cited in the next sentence). Both copied from Hingol.

## Questions for Rauf

1. **The HSM.** Never expanded in the material, so the article says only "the body he calls the HSM". Should the full name be brought in from Schaflechner's site-specific material (the Hinglaj temple row) into the place block, so both Las Bela and Hingol can give it?
2. **The Tumbel.** Who they are is not in the material; the name stands bare. Same question as for Hingol.
3. **The silence sentence.** "He does not say in these lines whether the park lies within the district of Las Bela" guards against a reader assuming it. Keep it, or drop the two park lines altogether from the Las Bela article, since Hingol already carries them?
4. **"the shrine".** Hingol says "the shrine being Hinglaj's"; Las Bela leaves "the shrine" as printed. Should the two be aligned, and which way?
5. **Spelling.** Las Bela in the prose (as printed), Balochistan only inside Schaflechner's quotation. The site's place name is Lasbela District; the public version will need to choose one form for its own prose and keep it consistent with Hingol's.
