The kings of every era took care of this place. Now, in the present era, the Auqaf Department holds its responsibility. Even nowadays, construction work is ongoing here.

## 9. اگر یہ جگہ کسی تاریخی یا روحانی شخصیت سے منسلک ہے تو براہِ کرم ہمیں اس شخصیت کی زندگی کے بارے میں تفصیل سے بتائیں۔
*English gloss: If this place is linked to a historical or spiritual figure, please tell us in detail about that figure's life.*

**Answer:**
Hazrat Shah Abul Muali's real name was Syed Muhammad Khair ul Deen. His kunyat was Abul Muali, and he is known among people by this name. Hazrat Shah Abul Muali was born on 10 Zil Hajj 960 Hijri in Kirman, Iran. His father's name was Syed Rehmatullah Kirmani. His family lineage was from Kirman Sadaat. His father had three brothers. One of his father's brothers was named Sheikh Dawood Bandgi Kirmani, whose shrine is in Shergarh, and the other brother was Syed Jalal ud Deen, whose shrine is in Sindh. Along with his father, his uncles were also great scholars. His father migrated from Iran and settled in Depalpur, Okara on the land of Hindustan. Hazrat Shah Abul Muali received his early education from his father. His father himself was a fountain of knowledge and arts, and was also an Urdu and Persian literary poet. His father bestowed all his teachings upon him. After his father's passing, he became sorrowful. After his father, his uncle Hazrat Sheikh Dawood Bandgi took him under his patronage. His uncle Dawood Bandgi was also his father-in-law. He received education from him until the age of 30, and upon his command, he migrated to Lahore. He learned exoteric knowledge from his father and uncle, but in search of esoteric knowledge, he always sought the gatherings of pious people of Allah. He came to Lahore and set up his base near Mochi Darwaza, where he built a mosque and established his seminary there. He would offer congregational prayers and impart religious education to the people. On his way to Lahore, he had many wells, mosques, and madrasas built. He was always at the forefront of virtuous deeds. He wrote many books in Urdu and Persian languages during his lifetime. He was also an Urdu and Persian writer. Along with writing books, he also used to compose poetry, which is a point of attention in his books, however, poetry was only to a certain extent, and his entire focus was towards the preaching of Islam. As soon as he came to Lahore, he set out in search of spiritual elders of the religion so that he could find a pious saint of Allah, pledge allegiance at his hands, and gain esoteric knowledge from them. In 980, he went to the house of a companion, Sheikh Hamid Karishmi, in Delhi. On the way, he saw a majzub (spiritually absorbed person) sitting by the roadside, with whom he conversed. As soon as the majzub saw him, he said that the one you are wandering in search of is present in your own home. He asked with great surprise, who is that? He took the name of his uncle Dawood Bandgi Kirmani. He was very surprised as to how this person had this knowledge, nevertheless, without thinking much, he went back to his uncle and narrated the whole incident, upon which the uncle recited a verse which meant something like, "Wander and see the whole world, blister your feet, if you find anyone like us, then say." He requested his uncle to accept his pledge of allegiance, which was accepted. In this way, he achieved his allegiance. After staying with him for a few days, his uncle ordered him to go back to Lahore, saying, "Go and spend your life in the path of Allah." He returned to his seminary in Lahore and started serving the people. People would present themselves before him, and without seeing or asking, he would tell them the purpose of their visit. Millions of people pledged allegiance at his hands. After taking his allegiance, colors would fill their lives. He used to distribute his knowledge among his followers. His popularity continued to increase day by day. In the present eras, kings would present themselves in his service with requests. He did not want to reveal his spiritual eminence, but in some places, to help people or fulfill the desires of those who loved him, he would show miracles. His life was a speaking proof of spirituality. Because of him, millions of people entered the fold of Islam. He had immense love for Sheikh Abdul Qadir Jilani and Mian Mir. Naturally, his followers would also fall in love with his favorite personalities. People would eagerly come to keep his company. He established institutions imparting Islamic teachings so that Islam could be elevated among the people. He was a very kind-hearted and simple-natured person in whom the question of pride and arrogance did not even arise. He spent many seclusions (chillas) and nights in the remembrance of God during his life. He lived a life of 65 years and on 16 Rabi ul Awal 1024 Hijri, during the reign of Shah Jahan, he met his Lord. His shrine was built at his seminary itself. Which is very beautiful. Daily, thousands of people come to his shrine to offer salutations, recite Fateha, and bring their requests. He is counted among the pious people of Allah.

## 10. اس جگہ کے بارے میں ایسی کون سی کہانیاں یا افسانے ہیں جو آپ کے خیال میں آنے والی نسلوں کو یاد رکھنی چاہئیں؟
*English gloss: What stories or legends about this place do you think coming generations should remember?*

**Answer:**
Hazrat Shah Abul Muali was a pious servant of Allah. He was a personality to whom people would come themselves to receive blessings, and he never differentiated between the rich and the poor. Kings themselves would walk to come to him. He has many miracles which the people present in his era narrated about him, and some have mentioned them in the books they wrote about him. Hazrat Shah Abul Muali loved Hazrat Sheikh Abdul Qadir Jilani immensely; it is said that whoever pledged allegiance at his hands, Hazrat Sheikh Abdul Qadir Jilani would appear in their dream that very night. Which was due to his miracle. One incident is that the Mughal emperor Dara Shikoh writes that he once went with Hazrat Shah Abul Muali to the court of his teacher Sheikh Imam Niyamat Ali, where someone gave Shah Abul Muali a rosary as a gift. The Mughal emperor states that his heart was set on that rosary, and he started thinking in his heart, "I wish Shah Sahab would give this rosary to me." When Hazrat Abul Shah Muali and Dara Shikoh returned to their seminary, Hazrat Shah Abul Muali gave that very rosary to Dara Shikoh and said, "Keep reciting Darood on it daily so that the one who gave the rosary, you, and I receive the reward." Dara Shikoh was amazed to see this, wondering how he knew the thought in his heart. Another incident Dara Shikoh narrates is that he had immense love for Hazrat Sheikh Abdul Qadir Jilani, but had never told Shah Abul Muali. One night, Hazrat Sheikh Abdul Qadir Jilani came in his dream, gave him a white turban as a gift, and left. When he woke up in the morning and presented himself in the service of Shah Abul Muali, he saw that Shah Abul Muali gave him a white turban and said, "Hazrat Sheikh Abdul Qadir Jilani has asked to give this to you." Seeing this, he understood his spirituality, realizing that his status had become very high. Another famous incident is that once it was not raining in Lahore, due to which everyone was very worried. At that time, Hakim Afzal Agha was ruling; he gathered a very large number of people at the Eidgah outside the city so that everyone could offer congregational prayers and pray to Allah for rain. Hakim Afzal Agha came to his shrine and invited him to join the congregational prayer, upon which he refused him and said, "Consider me excused, as I cannot walk." The Hakim insisted a lot for him to walk, saying that if you do not go, I will also stay sitting here. After so much insistence, he told Hakim Afzal Agha, "Go, you leave, it will definitely rain after two days." The Hakim nevertheless listened and left sorrowfully because Shah Abul Muali did not come with them. Consequently, it did not rain for two days, but on the third day, it suddenly rained heavily, upon which Hakim Afzal Agha immediately came to him and said, "How is this possible that you had an idea it would happen like this?" He replied that if it had rained that day, perhaps the arrogant people present among the worshippers would have considered it their own wisdom, and later it would have become a cause of sin, which is why Allah delayed it a bit and sent rain today. In short, these famous incidents which are very well known among the people reveal the status of Hazrat Shah Abul Muali, showing that he was a special servant of Allah.
