# Bhai Sant Thawan Das Mandir
bhai-sant-thawan-das-mandir / researched 5 September 2026 / batch D · Sindh Hindu-Nanakpanthi-Sikh and Balochistan

## Verified findings

1. **Official register confirms location and functional status.** The Evacuee Trust Property
   Board's own published register of functional mandirs lists this site by the exact name and
   location the current entry uses.

   > "Bhai Sant Thawan Das Mandir" — location "Old Shahi Bazar, Mehar,Dadu, Sindh"

   Citation: Evacuee Trust Property Board (Pakistan), "List of Mandirs" [Functional register],
   https://etpb.gov.pk/shrines/mandirs/functional/, accessed 5 September 2026 — entry 10 of the
   list. Reliability: official body (statutory board of the Government of Pakistan) — citable.

Nothing else about this mandir — its founding date, architecture, or the identity/dates of its
namesake Thawan Das — was found in any source checked. Specifically checked and confirmed **no
mention** of "Thawan Das" in: Zulfiqar Ali Kalhoro's "Nanakpanthi Saints of Sindh" (*The Friday
Times*, 13 April 2018), "Historic Temples and Darbars of Chak" (*The Friday Times*, 2 February
2023), "Gurus in Shikarpur" (*The Friday Times*, 12 January 2018), and "The Sikhs of Sindh"
(sikhchic.com, 26 May 2013) — all fetched directly and searched. Also checked directly: Iqbal
Qaiser's *Historical Sikh Shrines in Pakistan* (1998, full text) and Khan Mohammad Waliullah
Khan's *Sikh Shrines in West Pakistan* (1962, full text) — both cover gurdwaras rather than
Hindu mandirs and, as expected, contain no mention of this site.

## Conflicts

None found. Unlike the neighbouring Gurdas Ram Mandir entry (Radhan Town, also in Mehar
Taluka), Wikipedia's "List of Hindu temples in Pakistan" lists "Bhai Sant Thawan Das Mandir"
with no "(non-functional)" qualifier, and its underlying citation (chased directly, not cited
here as Wikipedia itself) resolves to the same ETPB *functional* register used in finding 1 —
consistent, not contradictory.

Current entry vs. sources found here: no conflict, but also no independent corroboration beyond
the bare ETPB listing — the current entry's own honest framing ("almost nothing about his life,
era, or teachings has survived") is not contradicted by anything found in this pass.
