# Shrine of Syed Musa Pak
shrine-of-syed-musa-pak / researched 5 September 2026 / batch A · Multan

## Verified findings

1. Circumstances and date of martyrdom, lineage, burial location, and a named descendant
   (official government account).

   > "was martyred in 1592 AD (1001 AH) during a civil war of Langah tribe, when he received a
   > bullet in his chest by chance." "Syed Musa Pak (RA) buried near Pak Gate inside the walled
   > city of Multan." He "was son of Syed Hamid Bakhsh Gillani (RA)." "Syed Yousaf Raza Gillani
   > is a descendent of Musa Pak Shaheed (RA)." "The Urs of Syed Musa Pak Shaheed (RA) takes
   > place annually at his mausoleum."

   Citation: "Hazrat Syed Musa Pak Shaheed (RA), Multan," Auqaf & Religious Affairs Department,
   Government of Punjab, https://auqaf.punjab.gov.pk/shrine_musa_pak_shaheed — accessed
   5 September 2026. Reliability: official government body — citable.

2. An active conservation project and architectural description, from a second official body.

   > "The Walled city of Lahore authority undertake the conservation of shrine that is expected
   > to be finished within 24 months from its initiation date." The shrine's tomb is described
   > as octagonal in plan, with upward-sloping circular corner towers, glazed tile decoration,
   > and "nokhani" design at the base.

   Citation: "Multan's Conservation Projects: Restoring Historic Landmarks," Walled City of
   Lahore Authority (WCLA), Government of Punjab, https://walledcitylahore.gop.pk/multan-projects/
   — accessed 5 September 2026. Reliability: official government body — citable.

## Conflicts

**The current entry's `year_built: 1592 (circa)` is exactly the year the official Auqaf page
gives for the saint's martyrdom, not for any construction event** — the current entry's
`figure_died` field, by contrast, is empty. This is worth flagging directly: either the sheet's
`year_built` was populated from the death year (a natural but different fact from when the tomb
was actually built), or the tomb genuinely rose in the same year as the martyrdom and
`figure_died` should be filled in to match. I cannot tell which from what I verified — only that
the two fields as they currently stand (a populated `year_built` and an empty `figure_died`)
line up suspiciously with a single fact (1592 martyrdom) rather than two.

A significant, separately-sourced claim that is **not in the current entry at all**: an
architecture-history source (see Unverified leads, reliability caveated) describes a tomb of
"Musa Pak Shaheed" at **Uch Sharif**, not Multan, stating plainly that it is "in dilapidated
condition due to the fact that the Corp[se] of the saint was shifted to Multan" — implying the
saint may have first been interred at Uch Sharif (home ground of the wider Gilani/Gardezi sayyid
network) before his remains were moved to the Multan shrine this entry describes. I could not
verify this through a citable source, so it is not asserted as fact, but it is too specific and
on-topic to omit; a human researcher with access to Ahmad Nabi Khan or a similar architectural
survey of Multan-Uch tombs should check it before it either enters or is ruled out of the record.

An unverified web-search summary (never independently fetched — see below) separately claimed
the tomb was "built in 1572," twenty years before the martyrdom date given by the official Auqaf
page. Recorded as an unverified lead, not a conflict resting on two verified sources.

## Verdict

does not mention.
