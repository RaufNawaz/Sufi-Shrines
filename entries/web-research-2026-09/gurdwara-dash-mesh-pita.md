# Gurdwara Dash Mesh Pita
gurdwara-dash-mesh-pita / researched 5 September 2026 / batch D · Sindh Hindu-Nanakpanthi-Sikh and Balochistan

## Verified findings

1. **Official register confirms location, name, and plot size.** The Evacuee Trust Property
   Board's own published register of functional gurdwaras lists this site, matching the current
   entry's "some 300 square yards" claim exactly.

   > "Gurdwara Dash Mesh Pita | Sukkur | 300 sq yard"

   Citation: Evacuee Trust Property Board (Pakistan), "List of Gurdwaras" [Functional register],
   https://etpb.gov.pk/shrines/gurdwaras/functional/, accessed 5 September 2026 — row 20 of the
   list, coordinates given as 27.697778, 68.860833. Reliability: official body — citable.

2. **Khan Mohammad Waliullah Khan's 1962 register, checked directly, names only two gurdwaras
   for the whole of Sukkur District — neither this one, both at Rohri, not Sukkur city.**

   > "There are two gurdwaras in this district both situated at Rohri" — naming "GURDWARA BHAI
   > TERIA SINGH KANDHERA" and "GURDWARA JENRIAN SINGH KANDHERA."

   Citation: Khan Mohammad Waliullah Khan, *Sikh Shrines in West Pakistan* (Department of
   Archaeology, Government of Pakistan, 1962), full-text scan, archive.org identifier
   `furg-sikh-shrines-in-west-pakistan-by-khan-mohammad-wal`, accessed 5 September 2026.
   Reliability: published official-body book (full-text scan on archive.org) — citable. This is
   a negative finding, not a confirmation: it shows that as of 1962 no gurdwara existed in
   Sukkur city itself under any name in this authoritative register, consistent with (not proof
   of) the current entry's implicit suggestion that Dash Mesh Pita is a later foundation.

Iqbal Qaiser's *Historical Sikh Shrines in Pakistan* (1998, full text, checked directly) also
contains no mention of "Dashmesh"/"Dasmesh" anywhere in the book.

## Conflicts

None found regarding Dash Mesh Pita itself. The current entry's claim of a distinct, differently
named gurdwara — "Dashmesh Darbar Singh Sabha" at Jacobabad — could not be confirmed against any
source meeting this project's reliability bar, but its existence as *something* real and
separate is corroborated by multiple independent (if individually sub-bar) web presences: a
Facebook page ("Gurdwara dashmesh darbar singh sabha jcd sindh pakistan"), a Sahapedia map entry,
and a SikhiWiki page — all describing a Jacobabad site, a different city/district from Sukkur.
Neither ETPB register (functional or non-functional gurdwaras, both fetched directly and
searched) contains any Jacobabad entry at all, under this name or otherwise — meaning if this
Jacobabad gurdwara is real, it is not one ETPB itself administers, which is consistent with (not
proof of) it being a community/Sikh-Sabha-run institution rather than an evacuee-trust property
like Dash Mesh Pita.
