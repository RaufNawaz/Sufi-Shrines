# Shrine of Pir Sher Muhammad
shrine-of-pir-sher-muhammad / researched 5 September 2026 / batch B · Uch Sharif and west-Punjab khanqahs

## Verified findings

1. The shrine is officially administered by the Punjab Auqaf & Religious Affairs Department,
   listed by its official name.

   > "Darbar & Masjid Hazrat Mian Sher Muhammad Sharaq Pur Sharif"

   — listed under the department's "Lahore Zone," without an individual page or further
   descriptive text.

   Citation: Government of Punjab, Auqaf & Religious Affairs Department, "Shrines,"
   https://auqaf.punjab.gov.pk/shrines — accessed 5 September 2026. Reliability: official body —
   citable, though the listing itself carries no biographical or historical content beyond the
   shrine's official name and administrative zone.

## Conflicts

- **Silsila: this shrine's own claimed order is disputed, and neither version could be verified
  from a citable source.** The current entry states the saint united "the Qadiri and Chishti
  lineages" (silsila field: Qadiri). A devotional website (fetched directly, but a blog and
  therefore excluded by the reliability bar) states instead that he belonged to the
  "Naqshbandiyyah" order, as a disciple of a named teacher, "Hazrat Baaba Jee Ameer Ala-ud-deen
  Naqshbandi." Neither claim — Qadiri/Chishti (the sheet's) or Naqshbandi (the blog's) — could be
  corroborated by any source this pass could verify as citable. Reported as a live, unresolved
  conflict on a core taxonomy field (silsila), not a confirmed correction: the sheet's claim may
  well be right, but this pass found nothing citable that confirms it, and found a specific,
  dated, named alternative that this pass also cannot confirm.
- No conflict found on the saint's birth/death dates (1865-1928): the same non-citable source
  gives "Born: 1282 AH (1865 AD)... Died: 3 Rabi-ul-Awwal, 1347 AH (August 20, 1928)," consistent
  with the sheet, though again not independently verifiable from a citable source this pass could
  locate.
