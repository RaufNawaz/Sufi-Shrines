## Conflicts

The current entry's `year_built: 1152 (circa)` note reads "Blue-and-white kashi tilework" — which
could be read as dating the signature tilework to the saint's own era (early-mid 12th century).
The one source verified this session instead dates **the visible blue tiles specifically to the
reign of the Mughal emperor Humayun** (16th century) — several hundred years after the
1150s tomb tradition the current entry and most secondary sources agree on. This does not
contradict a 12th-century tomb existing, but it does suggest the *tilework itself*, which is
what the current entry singles out in its note, may not be original fabric. Worth a human check
before treating "1152 (circa)" as covering the tiles as well as the structure.

No conflict found on the saint's arrival/death dates as such — the current entry already hedges
these ("tradition places his arrival in the years around 1088 and his death in the first half of
the 1100s"), and nothing verified this session sharpens or contradicts that hedge; the several
web sources giving crisp figures (arrival 1088, death 1136 or 1150, tomb built 1152 "fourteen
years after death") were only ever seen as search-result summaries, never as a page independently
fetched, so they are listed below as unverified leads rather than used to tighten the entry.
