# Shrine of Hafiz Muhammad Jamal Multani
shrine-of-hafiz-muhammad-jamal-multani / researched 5 September 2026 / batch A · Multan

## Verified findings

1. Detailed biography — parentage, exact death date and age, and the origin of an honorific not
   in the current entry — from an established-press feature with a named byline.

   > Born 1747 AD; father "Hafiz Muhammad Yusuf," grandfather "Hafiz Abdul Rasheed." Died "5
   > Jamadi ul Sani 1226" (7 May 1811), "age 66." Given the honorific "Jamal Allah" by his
   > spiritual master, "Khawaja Noor Muhammad Chisti Nizami Maharvi (1746-93)." "Hafiz Jamal is
   > usually compared to Abu Hanifa (R.A), Ganj Shakar (R.A)" for his command of the Quran. He
   > wore a ring engraved "Allahu jamilun wa Yohibbul Jamal."

   Citation: Summaiyya Qureshi, "Hafiz Jamal Multani: Another pearl of city of Saints," *Minute
   Mirror*, 17 March 2023, https://minutemirror.com.pk/hafiz-jamal-multani-another-pearl-of-city-of-saints-96982/
   — accessed 5 September 2026. Reliability: established Pakistani English-language newspaper,
   staff byline — citable. This corroborates the current entry's birth year (~1747), death year
   (1811), and master (Khwaja Noor Muhammad Maharvi), and adds specific detail — parentage, the
   exact death date, and the "Jamal Allah" honorific — that the current entry does not carry.

2. Architectural description of the tomb, same source.

   > Located near Daulat Gate. "The area surrounding the tomb is coated with marble and black
   > slate." "The tomb is situated on a marble pedestal," with a "tunnel-like, arched corridor on
   > the north and west sides" and a "congregational hall with a decoratively ornamented timber
   > roof." The mosque's architect was "inspired by the Mughals." Current hereditary
   > administrator (as of 2023): "Makhdoom Muhammad Javed Ashraf Jamali." Annual Urs held 3–5
   > Jamadi ul Awal.

   Citation: same as #1.

## Conflicts

None found against the current entry's core claims — this source corroborates the birth year,
death year, and master, and adds detail rather than contradicting anything.

**What the current entry asserts that I could not verify, restated from this pass:** the
current entry's own `qa_note` already flags that the birth year, *Siharfi* poem authorship, and
wall-painting-preservation claims "predate this session and have no traceable citation of their
own." This pass does not close that gap — the Minute Mirror feature verified above says nothing
about the *Siharfi*, the wall paintings, or his swordsmanship/archery skill (a detail that
appears in some search summaries of this same article but which I could not confirm was in the
fetched text itself, so I have not included it above). Those specific claims remain unverified
after this pass, as they were before it.
