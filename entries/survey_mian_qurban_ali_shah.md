## 8. اس جگہ کی تاریخ کیا ہے؟ براہِ کرم ہمیں تفصیل سے بتائیں کہ یہ جگہ کیسے اور کیوں قائم ہوئی اور وقت کے ساتھ اس میں کیا تبدیلیاں آئیں۔
*English gloss: What is the history of this place? Please tell us in detail how and why it was established and what changes came to it over time.*

**Answer:**
Hazrat Qurban Ali Shah took upon himself the responsibility of preaching Islam, and while preaching the Deen (religion), he established his final camp at this location of Mint Stop, Lahore, where he spent the remaining part of his life. His demise occurred at this location, and his camp itself was made into his shrine. His disciples built a beautiful shrine, and alongside the shrine, a mosque was also constructed in his name. In 2005, the Auqaf Department took responsibility for this shrine and mosque, but even now, most of the maintenance and decoration is taken care of by his devotees themselves. Recently, in 2022, the reconstruction of this shrine was carried out once again.

## 9. اگر یہ جگہ کسی تاریخی یا روحانی شخصیت سے منسلک ہے تو براہِ کرم ہمیں اس شخصیت کی زندگی کے بارے میں تفصیل سے بتائیں۔
*English gloss: If this place is linked to a historical or spiritual figure, please tell us in detail about that figure's life.*

**Answer:**
Hazrat Qurban Ali Shah was born in 1341 Hijri in Malerkotla, India. His original name was Qurban Ali, but later, on account of his knowledge, people began calling him by the name of Mian Qurban Ali Shah. His father's name was Hazrat Mian Fazal Deen, who himself was a very great scholar of Deen (religion). Qurban Ali Shah received all of his life's education from his father; his father was the possessor of renowned fame. His father was part of the tradition of spiritual mentorship (peeri-muridi), which is why a rush of people was present with him at all times. His father had thousands of disciples. His father bestowed his knowledge and spiritual skill upon him and told him to spend his life preaching the religion of Islam. It is stated in history that his father and all family members kept the propagation of the call of Islam alive throughout their lives. His family members were also a center of the remembrance of Allah. Virtuous people and Sufis used to be born in his family. When Hazrat Qurban Ali Shah came of age, by the command of his father, he left his ancestral home and set out for preaching, and he would adorn every non-Muslim he met along the way with the light of Deen and invite them to enter Islam. It is also stated in history that he and his family members considered the service of humanity as their duty and never forced or imposed their religion on anyone; therefore, people would enter Islam after being deeply impressed by his knowledge and character. Preaching Islam along the way, he arrived at this area of Mint Stop in Lahore and stayed for a short while, but made this very place his final abode. After his arrival here, the inclination of the people was automatically directed toward him. Anyone who left impressed would further spread his message. At his camp, there was a dwelling of people of all kinds—young, old, children, and the elderly. He would also arrange for their food, drink, and lodging. He brought a very large number of Marzaiyas (Ahmadis/Qadianis), whose perspective on life was entirely different, into the fold of Muslims. He brought many people to the right path. He used to recite the Quran to the people. He possessed a dervish-like, Sufi, and simple character, but he did not let his true spiritual self be revealed to the people. A vast number of people used to come to his camp to have prayers said for them, and their prayers would be accepted. Thousands of people took the oath of allegiance (ba'ait) at his hand, and entire families of theirs also entered into his allegiance. During the day, he would serve humanity, and at night, he would remain busy in the remembrance of Allah. In his youth, he left his home by the command of his father to preach the Deen and never returned back. In the final part of his life, he passed on his spiritual seat (gaddi) to his special disciples. He met his True Creator on 25 Rabi-ul-Sani. He was buried at his camp itself. The wealthy people of that era took responsibility for his shrine and constructed a beautiful shrine along with a mosque.

**Answer:**
Hazrat Qurban Ali Shah was the person who illuminated the light of Islam in an era of ignorance. He spread Islam and the command of Allah among the people. At his doorstep, thousands of people benefited. Those seeking a cure for illness, the unemployed seeking employment, and people trapped in difficulties and worries would present themselves to him, and he would pray to Allah on their behalf, and in no time, all those prayers would be accepted. Helpless and compelled people would come to his camp, and their life's purpose and lifestyle would change. He fulfilled the duty of preaching Islam in an era when people were inclined toward ever-new inventions. All these deeds are still remembered today for his life's renown. His disciples and devotees still visit his shrine today to pray to Allah through his intercession (waseela), and they say that they do not return empty-handed.
