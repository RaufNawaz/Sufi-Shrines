## 9. اگر یہ جگہ کسی تاریخی یا روحانی شخصیت سے منسلک ہے تو براہِ کرم ہمیں اس شخصیت کی زندگی کے بارے میں تفصیل سے بتائیں۔
*English gloss: If this place is linked to a historical or spiritual figure, please tell us in detail about that figure's life.*

**Answer:**
Malik Ayaz Ahmad was educated in Georgia in 993 AD. When the infidels attacked your country, you were made a slave and taken to Baghdad market for sale. At that time, selling and buying slaves was the norm. You were very weak. People did not like to buy you. Suddenly one day a king Mahmud Ghaznavi came to that market and changed your life. He suddenly passed by you and asked for money to buy you. Your minister said, yes, what is the use of us that you are buying him, on which Badshah Mehmood Ghaznavi said, what I am seeing in this boy, you are not able to see him. Hearing this, the people of the market and the minister were very surprised. Badshah Mehmood Ghaznavi said, when your master asked for your amount, he said it was 10 thousand dinars, because after your words, greed had also arisen in his heart, he thought for himself that he would buy you at that price. Lahaza bought you and took you with him and included you in the army of his soldiers. It is said that in this way the time of his life passed so much that the relationship of master and slave turned into love. Malik Ahmad Ayaz spent his entire life loving Mehmood Ghaznavi. After his death, he considered everything of his to be his king Mehmood Ghaznavi. Malik Ayaz played the role of a true soldier. Mehmood Ghaznavi loved him so much that he used to get things and food cooked in his hands. One day Badshah Mehmood Ghaznavi, who was known as the most powerful king of Asia, was cutting a melon with his hands and feeding it to Malik Ayaz. Suddenly he ate the same melon himself and felt that it was tasteless and bad. Ayaz, you are making me eat this and why didn't you tell me anything, on which Malik Ahmad Ayaz said, Sardar, all my life I have got to eat sweets from your hands, if I ever get to eat it, then why should I complain about it. Mehmood Ghaznavi fought almost 17 battles with Hindus, in which Malik Ayaz played the role of the mastermind and his performance in that battle was remarkable. It is said that Malik Ayaz's faith was so strong that he did not fall for the infidels. There is a famous story that in a war when King Mahmud Ghaznavi attacked Hindus, he went to the Onkay temple where he saw that his idol was hanging in the air. Seeing this, Mgr Malik Ayaz became very surprised and gave proof of his intelligence there and said Sardar, he is a magnet in the temple, that is why he is supporting this clay idol, if we demolish his walls, he will fall down on his own. Unfortunately, the King did the same, as he demolished the walls, the idol itself is falling down due to which the King was very happy with your intelligence. Badshah Mehmood Ghaznavi's family, family members and his ministers used to tease Malik Ayaz and Mehmood Ghaznavi's love. Whenever the Prime Minister tested Malik Ayaz and his loved ones, the first number was of Malik Ahmad Ayaz. One day, at midnight, he suddenly asked everyone to eat, then Malik Ahmad Ayaz appeared with his full preparation and after this, one by one, many of your family members and ministers came, among whom many people were in a state of intoxication and were sleeping. I said that you people did not consider Malik Ahmad Ayaz good, Mr. When I tried him in a difficult situation today, everyone simply said that he was my loyal companion. Seeing him, everyone was very surprised. I demonstrated the cruelty of the king and the slave by staring at the love, slavery and rule of the ruler and set a prime example that his secrets are cruel. Malik Ayaz is in the reign of Mehmood Ghaznavi. Every night, after taking off his valuable clothes, he would throw away his old clothes in which he had brought the money for sale and would remember his past. He would be alone and please his God. King Mehmood Ghaznavi appointed you as his minister. Always considering yourself as his slave, you protected and supported the king. Malik Ayaz spent a lot of time in the remembrance of Allah in the last 10 years of his life. In his last days, Mahmood Ghaznavi wanted to give his place to Malik Ayaz. He refused to take it and asked to give away one of his two sons, Saad, upon which Badshah Mahmood Ghaznavi gave up his power. When Malik Ayaz ruled Lahore, the condition of Lahore had become bad and devastated due to wars. Every thing and system of life, decisions regarding food and drink, business system and wages, everything was finished. With his intelligence, he gave this city its shape again and stood on its own feet. Seeing his bad behavior in his last days, the people of your IRD guard told Ghaznavi to accept him because he loved Lahore and did not want to leave it. One day suddenly he became ill and he died, on which it is said that Malik Ayaz was poisoned because he loved him and he had this rebellion and hatred and in some places it is said that he died a natural death. He died on 8 August 1041. Your mausoleum was built in Shah Alam market near the Darbar Ali Hajveri Gunj Bakhsh.
