## Disagreements listed (16)

1. **Birth year.** Current: 1630. Sharib: 1039 AH (line 218). Rafat: 1631 (516). Alam: 1629 (633).
   Abbas: "the Panjab" only (845).
2. **Death.** Current, Rafat, Schimmel MD and the ATAV index: 1691 (516, 1011–1012, 951). Alam: 1690
   (633). P&G: 1691 and 1694 (429–432). Sharib: 1 Jumada al-Thani 1102 AH (278). Lahori's author: no
   year, filed among the undated (55). Lahori's editor: 1102 AH, third watch of Friday night, no day
   (61). No CE conversion is given.
3. **Where he lies.** Current: Garh Maharaja. Sharib: Shorkot, never Garh Maharaja (283–286). Lahori:
   Jhang region, 15 kos from the Ravi–Chenab confluence (66–67).
4. **Moving the tomb.** Current: twice / more than once. Sharib: one disinterment after a flood, 70
   years on, told as a miracle (287–293).
5. **Order name.** Current: Sarwari Qadiri. Sharib: "قادریہ مسروریہ" (265–268, 381–384). Lahori: the
   Qadiri silsila (59). Schimmel ATAV: Qadiriyya (937–940, 970).
6. **Father.** Current: an unnamed Mughal official. Sharib: Bayazid Muhammad, mansabdar (206–215).
   Lahori's editor: "بازید عرف اعوان" (62).
7. **Origin of the name.** Current: tradition credits his mother. Sharib: his mother named him; the
   word in "ہوا"[OCR?] is uncertain (220–224). Schimmel MD: the surname comes from the *hu* that ends
   each line (1013–1015).
8. **Master and the path to him.** Current: Sayyid Abdul Rahman of Delhi. Sharib: the Prophet's court,
   then Shah Habibullah, then the master, printed as Shaykh and as Sayyid (227–249). Lahori's editor:
   the chain begins "سید عبد الرحمان" (63).
9. **Learning and the language of his books.** Current: works in Persian. Lahori: Arabic and Persian,
   "بڑے عالم متبحر" (58). Sharib: unlettered, 24 titles (269–273). Abbas: Arabic and Persian except
   the abiat and siharfi (845–846).
10. **Verse form.** Current: si-harfi, and most famously the abyat as quatrains. P&G: the most famous
    are in siharfi (429–431). Abbas: the bait (868–869). Rafat: the bait, with the siharfi as his
    favourite device (516–517, 523–526).
11. **Worth of the verse.** Current: "gloriously musical". Schimmel MD: "do nothing but develop
    ideas…" (1023–1025). Rafat: "tedious mannerism" (523–526).
12. **The preceptor.** Current: the murshid's role, and the master who planted the jasmine. Schimmel's
    stanza names the preceptor. Rafat: "no personal reference … to an actual preceptor-beloved"
    (520–522).
13. **Music.** Current: qawwali and the singing of the abyat at the shrine. Abbas: his texts sung
    (853–867). Sharib: his teaching under the heading سماع (313–316). This is a tension rather than a
    contradiction of fact. **Rauf may prefer to drop it from the list.**
14. **The first line in English.** Current, ATAV and MD differ (941–942, 1027).
15. **Number of sons.** Sharib: eight counted, nine named (256–260). This is internal to Sharib.
16. **The Sherani shelfmark.** ۲۴۷ against ۲۹۷ (70–71). This is internal to Lahori's editor.
