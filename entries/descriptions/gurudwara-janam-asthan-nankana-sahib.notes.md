## The shape of this row

Three of Khalid's books name the site itself. *A White Trail* is the main source: reportage of the 2011
festivals, the 1921 account and the site's people (lines 582–612). *Walking with Nanak* has the description of
the shrine, the market and the plate (lines 411–419). *In Search of Shiva* has one anecdote (lines 715–720).
Rizvi has one row-specific sentence: birth at Talwandi, "later known as Nankana Sahib" (line 42). All the other
material is place-level (Nankana/Talwandi: lines 146, 462–484, 552–563, 647–684, 742–748, 821–827),
person-level (Guru Nanak), or province context. The article frames each of these as such. "Guru Nanak in the
Wider Record", "Legends and Miracles" and "In Verse" each open by saying that they concern the Guru, not the site.
