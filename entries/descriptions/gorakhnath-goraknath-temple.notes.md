## Disagreements listed (8)

   Gorkhattri (Rizvi) / کورکهتری Kor Khatri (Bhandari).
2. Whether the place exists: Trumpp vs McLeod, as Rizvi reports them.
3. Before the yogis: current text's Gandhara-to-Mughal sanctity vs Rizvi/Dani's Buddhist relic site.
4. Under the Mughals: current text (Nath resort; Babur's curiosity; Jahan Ara's sarai, 1641) / Rizvi
   (Babur "mentions his visit"; yogi pilgrimage centre under Akbar and Jahangir) / Bhandari (jogis'
   temple demolished under Shah Jahan).
5. Gorakhnath's date: current 9th–12th c. / Dhillon 11th c. and the Puratan Janamsakhis' conversation
   with Nanak / Rizvi no date, Naths' spread "From the eleventh century".
6. Gorakhnath's standing: mahasiddha, founder, disciple of Matsyendranath (current) / born from Shiva's
   sweat, Kanphata founder (Rizvi) / believed avatar of Shiva (Boivin) / head of the Kanphat yogis
   (Shackle).
7. Peshawar's Hindus after 1947: "departure of much of Peshawar's Hindu population" (current) / many
   Hindus and Sikhs stayed (Khalid, of the province).
8. Why the province was spared: Congress (Khalid p. 49) / Abdul Ghaffar Khan (Khalid p. 162).
