## Decisions for Rauf

1. **Her relation is three-way.** Current text (Makhdoom Jahaniyan's great-granddaughter), Khalid (Jalaludin Bokhari's great-granddaughter — which Jalal is not said) and Rozehnal (wife of a Jalal ad-Din Bukhari with dates 1303–1383, which match neither Rizvi's 1308 birth for Makhdum-i Jahaniyan nor Kasmani's 1291 for Surkh-Posh). Kept side by side with the neighbouring dates; no verdict. Consider whether the Schimmel/Rizvi/Kasmani dates belong in that bullet or should come out.
2. **Founded/Opened cell says 1291**, year_built 1493 (circa); Khalid's board says 1494. The Description now records 1493 (tradition) and 1494 (board). The 1291 cell is untouched (outside scope) but probably wants review.
3. **Events cell "Annual urs"**: no book records an *ʿurs*; Rozehnal's entry explicitly says no ʿurs is stated. The draft says neither book describes devotion at the tomb. The cell may need review.
4. **The current text's unsourced specifics** (Dilshad as builder, octagonal tiers, corner turrets, UNESCO Tentative List, Baha al-Halim, nineteenth-century flood) are kept verbatim as required; no book in the entry confirms any of them, and Khalid contradicts the flood century.
