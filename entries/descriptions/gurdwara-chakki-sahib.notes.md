## Disagreements listed (9)

1. Date of the sack: site c. 1520-21; Rizvi 926/1520; *Babur Bani* gloss 1521; Dhillon no year
   (fourth journey ends 1521 in both schemes).
2. What the site was: millstone kept (site) vs prison (Qaiser).
3. How the millstone turned: self-turning (site; Janamsakhis per Khalid "using his miraculous
   powers") vs forced labour, no miracle (Qaiser).
4. Babur and the Guru: Babur came, sought blessing, freed prisoners (site) vs Babur sent for him,
   asked a blessing to conquer India, was rebuked (Janamsakhis per Khalid) vs may never have met
   ("later constructions", Qaiser).
5. Town names: Eminabad/Saidpur; plaque "Eimanabad"; Rizvi "Saiyidpur (Amanabad)", "Amnabad";
   Bhandari "امن آباد"; two renaming stories in Khalid (Babur; Eni).
6. Creation of the pargana: Bhandari's two sentences on p. 176 (entry line 609 says "See Doubts on
   how this squares"; no Doubts block is in this entry).
7. The Guru's dates: Khalid/Dhillon 1469–1539 vs Bhandari Samvat 1526 / 880 H and death under
   Salim Shah aged 70–80.
8. Number of journeys: five vs four; 24 vs 23 years.
9. Miracles: site, Janamsakhis, Bhandari vs Dhillon and Khalid on the Guru's disavowal.

Not listed as a disagreement: the building's description (site "simple flat-roofed building within
a brick-paved compound", Nishan Sahib in the courtyard; Khalid "a small house … ordinary structure",
pole with Khalsa flag behind its walls). The two differ in wording but do not contradict.
