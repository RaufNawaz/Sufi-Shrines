## Decisions needing Rauf

1. **Location of the tomb (Doubt B6, lines 250–261; also 99–101, 239–240).** No book names Ranmal Sharif or Phalia. Lahori's editor puts the mazar "at present" at Sahnpal Sharif, district Gujrat (p. 71, folio 59, حاشیہ); Chishti's damaged passage names Shahpur and Sahn Pal [OCR?], Gujrat (pp. 483–484). B6 says this "must be resolved before the row cites this book on location". I kept the current Description's claim (buried at Ranmal Sharif) verbatim, said early in the Overview that no book names the site, and set Sahnpal beside it in Where the Sources Disagree, no verdict. If the row is in fact misplaced, the row's Location needs your ruling, not this article.
2. **"Syed" (B6, lines 257–260).** Lahori heads the entry "شیخ حاجی محمد قادری"; the index gives "حاجی محمد". I listed the name difference as a disagreement. I did not carry B6's own inference that "Syed" is unsupported, or the editor's claim that Shah Sulaiman's family was Qureshi (p. 67).
3. **Kanhaiya Lal (lines 348–362).** The book's Doubt 13 asks a human to confirm or drop the match, which rests only on "نو شاہ گنج بخش" heading a Naushahi shajra. I kept it, framed in Legacy as about a Lahore tomb, saying it does not speak of this site. Drop if you rule against it (the article loses one paragraph and one bibliography item).
4. **The Alamgiri-court placement (C39, lines 269–273).** I quoted the book's description of Shah Abdullah, "منصبدار ہفت [OCR?] ہزاری دربار عالمگیری", next to the deathbed bai'at, but did **not** argue from it about the 1654 date, as C39 suggests checking. Your call whether to add or drop it.
5. **The four disciples' dates (C39).** C39 says a human must decide which tradition the row follows before the disciples' dates enter it. I gave every date only in Where the Sources Disagree, author and editor side by side, adopting neither.
