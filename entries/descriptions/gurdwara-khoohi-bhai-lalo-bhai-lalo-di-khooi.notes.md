## The shape of this row

Two principal figures: Guru Nanak and Bhai Lalo. Only one book names and describes the gurdwara:
Khalid's *Walking with Nanak* (row section, lines 293–307: Lalo, the locked gurdwara, Qaiser's history of
the migrant family and the 1989 purchase, pilgrims, the milk-and-blood story). Dhillon's *Janamsakhis*
(lines 398–408) is Lalo person-level material set at "Saidpur", which Dhillon does not identify with
Eminabad (Doubt, line 412); it is used throughout, always framed as Dhillon's retelling at Saidpur, and the
Overview says so early. Everything else is Guru Nanak person-level, Eminabad/Saidpur and Gujranwala
place-level, or province context, framed as being about the figure or the place.

Because Khalid does name the site, the Overview does not say "the books do not mention the site"; it says
instead that only one book describes the gurdwara and that Dhillon's Saidpur is not identified with
Eminabad.

## Disagreements listed (12)

1. Whether Saidpur is Eminabad: current text / Khalid p. 115 vs Dhillon p. 54 ("in Pakistan"); Bhandari
   spelling only.
2. How the town got its name: Babur renaming (Khalid p. 115) vs Eni legend (Khalid p. 116).
3. When the Guru came: current "early sixteenth century" vs Dhillon's first journey (1499–1506 Puratan /
   1499–1505 modern; Haridwar 1504) and return from the fourth; Khalid reconstruction gives no year.
4. Lalo's house: *dharamsala* (current) vs poorest house in the city (Khalid) vs thatched shanty on the
   outskirts (Dhillon).
5. Lalo's caste: *Getaura* (current, Qaiser 1998) vs carpenter caste (Qaiser in Khalid p. 117) vs low-caste
   carpenter (Dhillon p. 57).
6. The milk and the blood: miracle as remembered (current) / as Janamsakhi story and doubted (Khalid
   p. 112) / spoken (Khalid reconstruction pp. 111–112); and "sweat" on Lalo's side (current) vs on Bhago's
   side (Dhillon p. 71).
7. The building after Partition: "maintained since Partition" vs refugee/Muslim migrant family until 1989
   (current's own Qaiser paragraph; Khalid p. 116). **This is a tension inside the current text.**
8. The well: "still survives" / "still preserved" vs "covered over with a platform" — **both inside the
   current text**; listed because a reader sees both.
9. Names: Bhai Lalu Di Khoi (current) vs Bhai Lalo di Khoi (Khalid); Qaiser's title "in Pakistan" (current
   bibliography) vs "of Pais [OCR?] [Pakistan]" (Khalid p. 116).
10. When Babur came: 926/1520 (Rizvi p. 409) vs 1521 (Babur Bani gloss, Khalid pp. 113–114); Dhillon no
    year.
11. The Guru's dates: 1469–1539 (Khalid, Dhillon) vs Samvat 1526 / 880 H, death under Salim Shah aged
    70–80 (Bhandari p. 520).
12. How many journeys: five vs four (Dhillon); twenty-four years (Khalid) vs twenty-three (Dhillon).
