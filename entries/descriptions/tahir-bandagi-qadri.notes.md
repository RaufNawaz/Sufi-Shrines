## Decisions needing Rauf

1. **The survey's "Chishti Nizamia Qadria" answer.** The row's `qa_note` says the survey's opening sect answer named the "Chishti Nizamia Qadria order", and the `silsila` cell says "see … Description for a discrepancy". The current Description never states that discrepancy. Because facts may come only from the entry or the current Description, the draft leaves it out. Should the article carry it as the survey's answer?
2. **Chishti's qita by "مفتی غلام سرور".** This is the same name as the author of *Hadiqat al-Awliya*, whose entry also closes with a chronogram quatrain. I did not identify the two. Confirm that they should stay unlinked.
3. **The custodians named Shahsawar.** Lahori's "سید شہسوار" and Chishti's "سید شاہ سوارا[OCR?]" (of Abd al-Qadir Thani's descendants) and "شہسوار" look like one man, but the draft does not say so. Should it?
4. **Identification.**
   - Chishti: the entry's mapping note calls one passage a name-match only (l. 237). The chapter heading on p. 186 names "مقبرہ حضرت شیخ طاہر بندگی" at Miani, so I used Chishti throughout.
   - Kanhaiya Lal: Doubt 19 (l. 439–448) asks a human to confirm the reading ظاہر / طاہر / بندگی. The draft quotes "شیخ ظاہر بندگی [OCR?]" as printed.
5. **Chishti's couplet.** It is quoted untranslated, with the two words glossed. Keep it, or drop it until a translation is sourced?
6. **Wives.** The survey's "Ismatullah, daughter of an Afghan king" and Chishti's "Ismat al-Nisa, daughter of Sayyid Shah[…]" are listed as a disagreement, not as a variant spelling of one name. Is that framing right?
