## Decisions for Rauf

1. **Identity.** Both books that name the village make Makhdum Abdul Karim its master; the draft
   keeps the current text's Burhan-ud-din attribution and sets the books beside it. Is the row's
   identification to stand?
2. **Chishti's Mian Wadda and Kanhaiya Lal's Shaikh Muhammad Ismail** are both pupils of Abdul Karim
   of Langar Makhdum. The entry does not say they are one man and the draft says the books do not say;
   if a source in the corpus identifies them, the two sections could be joined.
3. **Current text's dates are internally inconsistent**: 1245 CE is given as "666 of the Hijri
   calendar" (666 AH falls in the 1260s CE). Kept verbatim per the brief; not raised in the article,
   since no book gives a date. The sheet's Founded/Opened and figure_died also read 1245.
4. **Province-level Setting section** ("The Sufis and the Rural Punjab") is included because the
   current text makes province-level claims (Farid's age, Sufis converting the countryside) that
   Eaton's material directly addresses. Cut it if it reads as off-site.
5. The current text places the shrine at Langar Makhdoom but the grave "near the village of
   Changranwala"; the draft keeps both as given.
