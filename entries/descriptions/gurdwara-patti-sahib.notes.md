## Decisions for Rauf

1. **The poem itself.** *Walking with Nanak* quotes the lines of the poem Nanak wrote here (p. 90, folio 72), but the entry does not reproduce them, so the draft only says that Khalid quotes them. If you want the Patti verse in the article, the lines would have to be added to the entry from the book first.
2. **The current text against Khalid.** The site's account says the shrine is pilgrim-only, well kept and maintained by the authorities, and names the *maulvi* Qutbuddin. Khalid describes resident refugee families, "no security", and a teacher named Pandit Gopal. Everything is kept and set side by side. You may want to revise the current claims, which none of the books supports.
3. **Schimmel's "Golden Alphabets"** (*As Through a Veil*, p. 222) is used as province-level context for the alphabet poem. The link to the *Patti Likhi* is mine, and Schimmel's alphabets are Muslim poems to the Prophet. The draft says she does not speak of Nanak. Cut it if the juxtaposition is too much.
4. **year_built (1935, "Present building 1930s-1940s").** No book dates the building. The draft says "None of the books consulted dates the present building or names its builder."
5. **Kartar's Giani Pratap story** (*A White Trail*, p. 184) may concern Patti Sahib, but this is not stated (see Hedged pointers).
