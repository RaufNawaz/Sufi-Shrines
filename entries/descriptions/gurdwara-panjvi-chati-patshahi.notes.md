## Disagreements listed (16)

1. Name (Panjvi Chati / Panjvin and Chhevin / Panjvin and Chevin).
2. Who came and when (current: Hargobind, Har Samvat 1670 / 1613, from Kashmir; Arjan "in time" vs
   Khalid: Arjan and Hargobind together, undated).
3. What happened at the spot (rest and Gur Mela vs horses tied to the Waan tree, 700 m walk).
4. The buildings (two shrines, domeless and domed, common wall vs one small double-storey mango-yellow
   gurdwara with Nishan Sahib, rooms, upper room).
5. Location (near Tambu Sahib on the road to Janam Asthan, eastern cluster vs 200 m from Tambu Sahib
   towards the railway station).
6. "Most of the town's shrines" vs "the only one … not in memory of Guru Nanak".
7. The Sikhs of Nankana (no congregation since 1947 vs 200-odd households; A White Trail's three sets
   of household figures).
8. Festival attendance (official 10–15k vs media 3,000 in 2011).
9. Why Guru Arjan died (Chandu Shah / Chandu Lal vs Jahangir's Khusro; A White Trail's tilak), with the
   Diwan's name Chandu Shah vs Chandu Lal.
10. How his life ended (thrown into the river; samadhi where he lost his life; Ravi miracle vs remains
    interred in Lahore).
11. Fifth or fourth Guru in A White Trail (pp. 162, 188, 198 vs p. 168).
12. Why Hargobind was imprisoned (retained money vs Emperor felt threatened; "some claims" of service).
13. Size of his force (500 vs Mohsin Fani's 700 cavalry and 60 artillery).
14. When militarisation began (Hargobind "began" vs Grewal: under Arjan).
15. Guru Nanak's birth (October 20 vs 14 April 1469; Meherban's Dera Chahal).
16. Number of gurdwaras in the town (six vs seven).
