## How the row was framed

Only Kanhaiya Lal (lines 309–319), Chishti (lines 133–143) and *A White Trail* (lines 1275–1294)
describe the monument. Everything else — Lahori's majzubs and deathbed, Chishti's genealogy, reign,
court, grants and spoliations, Kanhaiya Lal's city history, Khalid's gurdwara-building, Eaton's
Pakpattan seizure — is person- or place-level and is written as being about Ranjit Singh or Lahore,
never about the samadhi. The Overview says so in so many words.
