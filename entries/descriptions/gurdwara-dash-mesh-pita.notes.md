2. **His name.** Rafat's 'Guru Govind Singh' (215). Bhandari's Gobind Rai, which is hedged (709–714).
3. **His death.**
   - Khalid: "Pathan horse traders" (599) against "a Pathan horse trader" (598).
   - Chishti: "آخر کار مارا گیا" (32) and "دیوانہ ہوکر مرگیا" (33).
   - Chishti's table: year سمت ۱۷۶۵[OCR?] (30). Boivin and *A White Trail*: 1708 (338, 650).
4. **His sons.**
   - *Walking with Nanak*: all of his sons, by the Mughal authorities, in 1705 (598).
   - *A White Trail*: four sons, martyred fighting, against assassinated (652). The entry already sets
     these two side by side.
   - Chishti: two sons killed (33).
5. **When the line of Gurus was closed.** *A White Trail* says 1699 (652). *Walking with Nanak* says 1708,

6. **Where he lived.** Khalid: Anandpur (601, 602). Chishti: موضع امدپور [OCR?] علاقہ کوہستان (34).
   Bhandari: Makhowal on the Sutlej, for "Gobind Rai" (710).
7. **His birthday.** 10 January 2011 against 11 January 2011, and the old calendar against the new one
   (657). The entry already sets these side by side.
8. **Whether Sindh's followers of Nanak are Sikhs.**
   - Boivin: not included in the Sikh community (403).

## My own near-literal renderings (no English gloss in the entry)

1. "دسویں گورو گوبند سنگہ صاحب": the tenth Guru Gobind Singh Sahib (29).
2. "دسواں گورو گوبند سنگھ خلف گورو تیج بہا[OCR?] اور آخری گورو": the tenth Guru Gobind Singh, son of Guru
   Tegh Bahadur, and the last Guru (32). The Urdu spells تیج; "Tegh" follows the entry's own gloss at
   line 33.
3. "دسوین بادشاہ کا": of the tenth Padshah (36).
4. "مقام پٹنہ": at Patna (30). The entry glosses "at"; "Patna" is my transliteration.
5. "تمام عمر اوسکی تون [OCR?] کے عوض لینے مین صرف کی": his whole life was spent in taking requital (33).
6. "وہ اسی غم مین دیوانہ ہوکر مرگیا": he went mad from that same grief and died (33).
7. "آخر کار مارا گیا": was killed in the end (32). عالمگیر is transliterated as Alamgir.
8. "قرین قیاس": plausible (36).
9. "نظم [OCR?]": verse (34).
10. "الحال که این نسخه بتحریر می درآید": now, as this copy is being written (709).

13. "از بهکر گذشته بست و پنجم محرم در سکهر نزول نمود": having passed Bhakkar he had halted at Sukkur on
    the twenty-fifth of Muharram (722).

    river … on the other" stands for باین رود سے[OCR?] آب / ازانطرف دریا. "Kos" for کروه is not glossed in
