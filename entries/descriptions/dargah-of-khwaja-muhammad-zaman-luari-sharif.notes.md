## Disagreements listed (7)

1. **Name and titles**: the site's text has Khwaja Muhammad Zaman, Sultan al-Aulia. Alam Faqri, as the
   site's text reports him, has Makhdoom Muhammad Zaman Naqshbandi, Khwaja Kalan. Schimmel has Makhdum
   Muhammad Zaman / Muhammad Zaman Lanwari and "the famous saint" (lines 17–26).
2. **Place-name**: the site's text has Luari. Boivin has Lowari Sharif and "Lowari Shari" (lines
   175–182). Schimmel has "Lanwari".
3. **Teachers**: the site's text has his father Shaikh Abdul Latif and Khwaja Abul Masakin. Schimmel has
   Mian Abu'l-qasim Naqshbandi and Mian Muhammad Thattawi (lines 24–25). *Abul Masakin and Abu'l-qasim
   may or may not be one person; no verdict is given.*
4. **Writings**: the site's text has Sindhi verse, c. 85 poems. Schimmel has "Sindhi apophtegmata"
   (line 31).
5. **Dates of the Naqshbandi centre**: the site's text has the khanqah in 1737, its founder born 1713.
   Boivin has Lowari Sharif Naqshbandi scholars writing in the early eighteenth century, with Muhammad
   Hashim (1692–1762) as "The main author" (lines 177–178, 195).
6. **Muhammad Hashim's place**: Boivin counts him among the Lowari Sharif scholars and says he was qazi
   in Thatta. Schimmel, *Mystical Dimensions*, has "of Thatta" (line 389). *This is mild, since Boivin
   too places him in office at Thatta. Rauf may drop it.*
7. **Gidumal's gift**: *Pain and Grace* places it in Ahmad Shah Abdali's invasion (line 56).
   *Mystical Dimensions* places it with Nadir Shah (1739) (line 387). The gift is province context,
   not this site.

Dropped from the list as not a true conflict: Boivin's "from the sixteenth century onwards" for the
Naqshbandiyya's arrival (line 235) against Schimmel's "strong Naqshbandiyya influence in the beginning
of the eighteenth century" (line 389). Both are quoted in the prose.
