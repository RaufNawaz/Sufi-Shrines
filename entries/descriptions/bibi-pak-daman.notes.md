## English I wrote for unglossed non-English prose (please check)

The entry glosses most Kanhaiya Lal passages and some Lahori ones. These senses are mine:

- Overview: "احوال مقبرہ عالیہ حضرت بی بی پاک دامناں…" → "the account of the exalted tomb of the Bibi Pak Daman" (Chishti p. 352).
- "آپ تدریس پردہ میں بیٹھکر [OCR?] فرما کرتی تہیں" → "she used to give instruction seated behind the veil" (Chishti p. 364).
- "ہم کو نامحرموں کی نظر سے بچا لے اور پیوند زمین کر لے" → "to be kept from the sight of men outside their kin and joined to the earth"; "سب بیبیاں معہ خدام کے پیوند زمین ہو گئیں" → "all of them, with their servants, became one with the earth" (Lahori pp. 247–248).
- "وہاں ہی مشرف باسلام ہو کر رہ جاتا" → "stayed there as a Muslim"; "اس نے بھی اسلام کا خلعت پہن لیا" → "he too put on the robe of Islam"; "بہت سے لوگ ان کے قدم کی برکت سے…" → "many came to Islam through the blessing of their footsteps" (Lahori p. 247).
- Chishti p. 357: the lament "اول جدائی برادران…" → "first the parting from their brothers and the calamity of Karbala, then a strange land where no one understands even their speech"; "ہم غریب مسافر…" → "poor travellers, wronged and homeless, beyond all help"; "یا الٰہی! زمین کو حکم دے کہ ہمکو امان دیوے" → "asking God to command the earth to give them refuge"; "تمام بی بیان و کنیزیں سما گئیں اور پوشیدہ ہو ئیں" → "all the Bibis and their maidservants were taken in and hidden"; "عرب ترک[OCR?] اولاد رسول اللہ کی آئی ہیں" → "that descendants of the Prophet had come" (I left the flagged ترک unglossed).
- Chishti p. 357, "سات سو چار[OCR?] آدمی": in the disagreement list I call it "a company printed as seven hundred and four". That reading is mine, and the last word is flagged. Abbas's "seven hundred male scholars" may be the same tradition.
- Chishti p. 358: "ماہ چہار دہم" → "the moon of the fourteenth night"; "چھ سات ہزار[OCR?]" → "six or seven thousand".
- Chishti p. 359: "بعہد بادشاہ اکبر بہت عمارات تیار ہوئیں اور قبرستان کے مقرر ہوا" → "many buildings were raised and the graveyard was laid out". The grant "چار جاہ[OCR?] مزروعہ…" is quoted and left untranslated.
- Chishti p. 295: Mian Mir "در بسا اوقات … متوجہ ہوتی تھی" → "often went there in devotion" (the entry's own words are only "went there").
- Chishti p. 70 (province-level): "ماتمِ حسین میں شورِ کربلا پنجاب میں برپا ہو گیا تھا" → "the lament of Karbala rose in the Punjab too".
- Lahori p. 199: "بی بی حاج و تاج دو لڑکیاں اپنے ہمراہ رکھتے تھے" → "kept two of his daughters with him"; "یہ بزرگ سید حسینی تھے" → "a Husaini sayyid"; "توسل خاندان جنیدیہ" → "affiliation with the Junaidi family".
- Lahori p. 200: "…طویلہ حضرت غلام محی الدین کے زیارت گاہ خلق ہے" → "in the stable of Hazrat Ghulam Muhi al-Din, where people visit it".
- Lahori p. 248: "سید احمد تختہ ترمذی جو … قطب یگانہ و غوث زمانہ تھے" → "the unique Qutb and the Ghaus of his age"; "شہر کے باہر اپنے صومعہ…" → "outside the city, in the cell where their father had lived"; "الٰہی ہم کو پیوند زمین کر لے اور نامحرم مردوں کی صورت نہ دکھلا" → "to be joined to the earth and not shown the faces of men outside their kin"; the Mongol passage's English sense ("laid the whole country waste", "every inhabitant be killed, and not even an animal spared", "thousands of people and beasts were slain"); the author's hedge "یہ تقریر جو مشہور نہیں ہے…" → "not well known… perhaps no one will believe it, yet it would be no wonder if it had happened so"; the author's rejection of the Karbala origin (my fuller rendering; it is close to the current text's own paraphrase).
- Lahori p. 249: "خود مفتی صاحب کو ۱۲۹۲ ھ میں…" → "the Mufti himself, when the *Tazkira-e Hamidiya* came into his hands in 1292 AH, began to look on those tales with doubt"; "ہمارے نزدیک بھی…" → "to him too this account seems close to reason". "لا یعنی روایات" → "idle tales".
- Lahori p. 183: "…لوگ اس کو مزار استاد بی بی پاک دامناں کہتے ہیں" → "people call it the tomb of the Bibis' teacher" (entry: "the mazar of the ustad of the Bibis").
