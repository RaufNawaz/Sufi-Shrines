2. The name of the seat: the current text writes *tikanas*/*tikkano* and Jagiasi/Jagiasu, Schaflechner writes *tikānā*, Boivin writes *darbar*. **Rauf: the current text spells the lineage two ways (Jagiasi, Jagiasu) and the seat two ways (tikanas, tikkano). I kept both as written. Should one form be adopted?**
3. Tando Jahaniyya: Boivin's Qutub Ali Shah dargah "in Jahaniyya" against Abbas's Qutb Din Darya shrine "Tando Jahana" in the Civil Lines. No identity is asserted.
4. Pakistan's Hindu population: Schaflechner, Boivin and Khalid.
5. Hindus who left Sindh at Partition: Boivin's own range.
6. Caste in Sindh: Boivin against Schaflechner/Thakur.
7. Whether Sindh was free of communal strife: Schaflechner against Boivin.

Items 4–7 are province-level disputes, included as context in the same way as the Karachi Hanuman draft.
