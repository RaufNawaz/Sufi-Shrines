## The basic situation

No book in the entry names the shrine or Pir Sher Muhammad. Row-specific material is only:
- Hadiqat al-Awliya (entry lines 40–61): Sharaqpur in the place index, the different saint Muhammad Saeed Chishti Sabri Sharaqpuri (Doubt I9: "Do not map"), and two bibliography items with a Sharaqpur connection.
- Tahqiqat-e Chishti (lines 100–102): a jagir "علاقہ شرقپور مین" given by Rani Jindan.
Everything else is province-level `punjab` material (lines 63–94, 104–125, 131–172, 178–726). Following §9.336 ("keep the context"), the article says early (Overview, para 2) that the books do not mention the site or saint, and every province-level passage is framed as being about the Punjab, under headings "Setting: …" and "The Punjab in Verse".

All current-Description claims are kept (Overview, Life, Shrine, Legacy text verbatim except `_naat_`→`*naat*`, "urs"→"*ʿurs*", and two paragraph splits); a sentence was added to Life and to Shrine saying the books record none of it.

## Hedged / framed material

- **Muhammad Saeed Chishti Sabri Sharaqpuri** (lines 43–44, 55–58): given explicitly as *not* this shrine's saint ("He is not the saint of this shrine…"), with his 1214 AH death and the site's 1928 side by side. - **Rani Jindan's jagir** (line 102): stated, with "the grant has nothing to do with the shrine".

## Decisions for Rauf

1. **The current text itself is unsourced.** The entry's own header (lines 3, 34) says it is "compendium/knowledge-based" and asks that the dates 1865–1928, the Qadiri-Chishti lineage and the Abul Faiz Qalandar Ali Suhrawardi link be verified against a Sharaqpur source. No book here confirms any of it. Kept verbatim per the brief; the article says the books record none of it. The sheet's `silsila` cell is "Qadiri" while the text says "Qadiri-Chishti".
2. **Is province-level context this heavy acceptable** for a row with no site-level material? ~75% of the article is "Setting" and "The Punjab in Verse". It can be cut back to the Sufi-orders section alone.
3. **Hadiqat bibliography line** (house standard, "Persian, composed 1292 AH") sits awkwardly with the book's own preface and closing (lines 67, 92), which call it a short *Urdu* collection written because the Persian *Khazinat al-Asfiya* was too long. Not changed; flag for the bibliography template.
4. This row has only Sharaqpur place-level hits, not row-specific ones — worth checking whether it belongs in the 2+-books list at all.
