## Needs Rauf's decision

1. **Length and weighting.** At 129k characters the article is almost nine times the current cell.
   - About 20k of it (His Teaching, Hujwiri and Samaʿ, much of Kashf al-Mahjub) is person-level
     material taken under the 18 September ruling. Several books' own Doubts say to weigh it apart from
     shrine material: Ernst and Lawrence (lines 962–965), Qureshi (lines 1655–1663), *As Through a
     Veil* (lines 1964–1968, 1978–1981) and Werbner and Basu (lines 1444–1451).
   - It is all cited and framed as the books' account of the man and his book, not of the site.
   - Should those sections stay at this length, or be cut back toward shrine-relevant material?
2. **Unglossed quotations.** Where the entry gives no English, the quotation stands with only a
   statement of what it is about. I did not translate.
   - Lahori: "تمام عمر حضرت لاہور میں رہے" (line 110), "اس وقت لاہور مرجع علما و فضلا تھا"
     (line 110), "انھوں نے لاہور میں آ کر …" (line 109), the Dara Shikoh forty-Thursdays line (line 130),
     and the editor's Kashf al-Asrar line (line 116).
   - Bhandari: "که فضیلت را با ولایت هم آغوش داشت" (line 2658), "شهریست بزرگ و مصریست سترگ", the
     mosques and gardens lines (lines 2691–2692), and the tomb line (line 2661, "the tomb is in Lahore").
   - Chishti: "حصول مرادات" (line 235).
   - Disagreement 4 depends on the unglossed Lahori line. Rauf may want an Urdu reader to supply glosses.
3. **"A writer she calls Lahori."** Abbas's source for the *hazri* musicians (lines 1519–1525) is
   probably the Tahir Lahori of her bibliography, "Aulīyā ke Urs" (line 1560, place-level). The draft

4. **Mufti Ghulam Sarwar's chronograms in Chishti** (line 222): the draft does not say he is the author
