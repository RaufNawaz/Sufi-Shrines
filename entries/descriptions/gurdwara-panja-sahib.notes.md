## The shape of this row

Khalid's *Walking with Nanak* (legend, travelogue and the novelised counter-narrative, lines 676–694).
Dhillon (762–767), *In Search of Shiva* (920–927) and Bhandari (989–999) only touch the town. Everything

section "Guru Nanak in the Wider Record" opens by saying it is about the Guru, not the site, and "In Verse"
says that none of the quoted lines is tied to Hasan Abdal.

## Disagreements listed (13)

1. When the Guru came: the current text says "around 1521"; *A White Trail* says the 1st of Vaisakh, about
   500 years ago (line 827); the *Walking with Nanak* reconstruction says the 1st of Vaisakh, citing Bala
   (line 680); Dhillon says the return from the fourth udasi, dated 1518–1521 in the modern scheme
   (lines 779, 792). **The draft puts these two Dhillon facts next to each other. Dhillon himself does not
   date the Hasan Abdal passage; please check that the juxtaposition is acceptable.**
2. Companions: Mardana alone (current text) against Mardana and Bala (*A White Trail*, line 827).
3. Where he sat: the foot of the hill against a peepal tree.
4. How the spring rose: "touched a stone or scooped" against "removed a stone".
5. How old the pool is: made by the miracle (*A White Trail*) against pre-Buddhist and seen by Hiuen Tsang
   (*Walking with Nanak*, line 683).
6. The boulder: hurled or rolled; Iqbal Qaiser doubts the distance (line 692).
7. The handprint: the tradition against Khalid's Kamma/Naju counter-narrative (lines 693–694).
8. Who built the gurdwara: Hari Singh Nalwa (current text) against Ranjit Singh's tenure (lines 683, 830)
   against Hari Singh's order in the reconstruction (line 693). Neither book supports the current text's

9. Kandhari against Qandhari.
10. Who may enter: Muslims barred "Even till recently" in 2011 (line 841), while in *Walking with Nanak*
    the author, a Muslim Pakistani, put his hand on the rock. **This is a weak pairing because the books

11. Vishal Chand against Vishal Nasir Chand (line 849).
12. Guru Nanak's dates: Dhillon, Khalid, Chishti (two versions) and Bhandari (lines 110–111, 775, 782,

## Left out, and why

- **Lahori, *Hadiqat al-Awliya*** (lines 39–41): there is only an index locator, and the subject of the
  page is not determinable. Nothing about the gurdwara.
- **The donors' inscriptions** on the pool stairs (line 832): they name private people, one of them a
  teenager who died. The draft says only that the stairs carry the names of donors from Canada and the
  United States. **Rauf may want the inscriptions quoted.**
- **The informant-pseudonym caveat**: the Source line for *A White Trail* in `out/descriptions/book_sources.md`
  says the author changed informant names. This is not in the entry, so it is not in the article. The
  named informants (Surjeet Singh, Avtar Kaur, Harvinder Singh, Jasdeep Singh, Virinder Kaur, Maninder
  Singh, Vishal Chand, Nadeem, Amir) may be pseudonyms. **Rauf may want a sentence saying so.**
- **Doubts on *A White Trail* pp. 196 and 201** (line 862: Hassan Abdal "was not mapped"): the entry
  carries no text for these pages.
- **Most person-level Guru Nanak material**: Sultanpur, Sajjan, Kartarpur, the succession, Mecca, Baghdad,
  Chishti's family material, Bhandari's succession, and Tahqiqat's samadh material. It is long and is
  about other sites, so only items relevant here were used: dates, udasis, companions, miracles, verse,
  langar and Muslim devotion. **If Rauf wants the fuller biography, as in `gurdwara-dash-mesh-pita`, it
  can be added.**

  Shackle, Sorley, Schimmel ×3, Rafat, Waris Shah, Madho Lal Hussein, Eaton, Ernst and Lawrence, Werbner
