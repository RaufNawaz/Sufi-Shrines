## Does a book name the site?

Yes. Lahori names mauza Nigaha as the place of both the martyrdom and the *roza* (l. 87–92), and Khalid names "the shrine of Sakhi Sarwar in Dera Ghazi Khan" (l. 676, 711). So the article does not open with a "no book names the site" statement. Bhandari's tomb (forty kos west of Multan, at the foot of the Baloch hills) is mapped to this row through the saint's name and the location only. The book never writes سخی, Nigaha or Dera Ghazi Khan (l. 781). The prose says so in the Overview. Chishti's chapter is about the Lahore *chauki*, and his Mapping Doubt says "Nothing in the book is about the Dera Ghazi Khan shrine" (l. 167). His life of the saint is still the saint's own story, so it is given as Chishti's account of the saint. His martyrdom place "مقام نگاہی[OCR?]" (l. 178) is quoted as printed and not identified with Nigaha in the prose. The Lahore *chauki* has a section of its own, framed as not the tomb.
