## Disagreements listed (Where the Sources Disagree), no verdicts

1. **Name**:
   - The survey: Malik Ahmad Ayaz / Malik Ayaz Ahmad / Malik Ayaz.
   - Lahori: شیخ ایاز لاہوری, with no Malik or Ahmad (lines 52–56).
   - Kanhaiya Lal: ملک ایاز, never احمد (line 140).
   - Bhandari: ملک ایاز (line 370).
   - Schimmel: Ayaz only (line 256).
2. **Origin**:
   - The survey: Georgia, 993 AD.
   - Bhandari: son of the ruler of Kashmir, kidnapped (line 366).
   - Schimmel: "Turkish officer" (line 202).
   - Nizami: "the Turk" (line 396).
3. **Purchase**:
   - The survey: Baghdad, too weak to sell, ten thousand dinars.
   - Bhandari: Ghazni, drew crowds for his beauty, "what the merchant asked and more" (lines 367–369).
4. **Looks**:
   - The survey: too weak.
   - Bhandari: as beautiful as Yusuf (line 368), but also his "some poets" view (line 371).
   - Nizami: "not remarkably handsome" (line 397).
5. **Office**:
   - The survey: soldier, minister, governor of Lahore.
   - Lahori: *atalik* to Majdud (line 64).
   - Kanhaiya Lal: *hakim* of the Punjab (line 140).
   - Bhandari: intimate, foremost slave (lines 369–370).
   - Schimmel: "Turkish officer" only (lines 170–172).
   - Lahori's editor: commander of the cantonment (line 113).
6. **Order of deaths**:
   - The survey: Mahmud is still reigning in Ayaz's last days, yet Ayaz "left his residence" after
     Mahmud's death; Ayaz dies 8 August 1041.
   - Lahori: Ayaz outlives Mahmud, Masud and Majdud (lines 62–74).
   - Schimmel: Mahmud d. 1030 (line 264).
   - I quoted the survey's "one of his two sons" and did not say whose sons they are, because the
     current text is ambiguous.
7. **Year of death**:
   - The survey: 8 August 1041, no era.
   - Lahori: 450 AH.
   - The editor: 449 AH / 1057 CE (Ibn Athir), and Firishta as he reports him (lines 75–79, 106–108).
   - The sentence "The survey's date agrees with neither year" follows the entry's own words (lines 79,
     106). Rauf may prefer the bare juxtaposition.
8. **Burial**:
   - The survey: Shah Alam Market near Darbar Ali Hajveri Ganj Bakhsh.
   - Lahori: inside the city, no quarter named (lines 75–80).
   - Kanhaiya Lal: listed among the inside-the-city buildings (line 141).
   - Lahori's appendix: چوک رنگ محل, given as untied (lines 116–119).
9. **Refounding of Lahore**:
   - The survey: "new map of Lahore".
   - Lahori: counted the founder (lines 83–90).
   - Kanhaiya Lal: resettled under a *farman* (line 140).
   - Bhandari: restored it, with a *pakka* fort (line 373).
   - Lahori's editor: rejects it as "later writers' conjecture" (lines 90–92, 111–112).
10. **The romance**:
    - The survey: "love and romance".
    - Schimmel: the topos.
    - Lahori's editor: post-Seljuq literary growth (lines 112–113).
11. **Old clothes**:
    - The survey: each night.
    - Rūmī, in Schimmel: "every morning" (lines 285–289).
12. **Temple counsel**:
    - The survey: the "Onkay temple" and the magnet.
    - Chishti: Somnath, break the idol rather than take the crores (line 131).
    - The prose says the books do not make these one episode.
