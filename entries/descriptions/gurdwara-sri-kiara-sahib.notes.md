## What the article rests on

Only two book lines are about this gurdwara, both Haroon Khalid's:

- *Walking with Nanak*, entry l. 308–313: "Kiari Sahib", with Malji Sahib "the furthest from Gurdwara Janamsthan", and "said to mark the spot where Guru Nanak's buffaloes spoilt the fields of a local farmer who is reported to have complained to Nanak's father" (p. 91, folio 73).
- *A White Trail*, entry l. 472–475: "Gurdwara Kiara Sahib", Nankana Sahib, "where his cattle destroyed the fields of a farmer while he slept" (p. 222, folio not stated). The identification rests on the spelling join in the Doubt at l. 479 ("Kiara Sahib" = this row).

Everything else is person-level (Guru Nanak), place-level (Nankana Sahib / Talwandi) or province-level material, and the article frames it that way. The Overview says early that only these two books name the gurdwara, and that the rest is about the Guru and the town. All four paragraphs of the current Description, and all four of its bibliography items, are kept verbatim (Overview ¶1, Miracle ¶1–2, Gurdwara ¶1 and ¶3, Legacy ¶1).

## English supplied for unglossed non-English prose (my wording)

These are prose glosses only; there is no verse translation of my own.

- Bhandari "درخورد سالگی(۱) علامات کشف ..." → "in his childhood signs of spiritual unveiling and of wonders appeared in him". The variant "ده سالگی" → "at the age of ten". The entry gives no gloss for this line. The Sacha Sauda draft has its own paraphrase.
- "گویند" → "it is said".
- "قافله سالار مسالک حقیقت و مشعله دار طریق طریقت" → "a caravan-leader on the paths of truth and torch-bearer of the mystic way".
- "دوازده کروهے بتاله ..." → "twelve kos from Batala on the bank of the Ravi is the place of Baba Nanak, where his descendants still live".
- "عالم عالم خلایق ..." → "people came from every land to become his disciples".
- The birth-date clause → "in Samvat 1526, corresponding to 880 Hijri, in the reign of Sultan Bahlul Lodi".
- The death clause → "in the reign of Salim Shah Afghan, between the ages of seventy and eighty".
- "بعبارات واضحه و استعارات لایحه" → "in clear words and shining metaphors".
- "در دام عقیدت" → "into the snare of devotion".
- "عبادت عظمی" → "the highest worship".
- "اشعار هندی" is left untranslated.

All of these follow the wording already used in the approved-format sister drafts (Janam Asthan, Sacha Sauda), for consistency.
