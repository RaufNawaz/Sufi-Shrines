## Disagreements listed (in `## Where the Sources Disagree`)

1. Birth year: site 1859 vs Rozehnal 1856 (p. 103, folio 94).
2. Location: site "edge of Islamabad"; Ernst and Lawrence "outskirts of present-day Islamabad"; Werbner "Golra/Islamabad"; Eaton "Rawalpindi District".
3. Naming of his order: site Chishti / Sialvi line; Ernst and Lawrence direct pir Shams ad-Din Siyal; Rozehnal "Chishti Nizami". (Complementary rather than contradictory; listed for completeness.)
4. Successor: site names Pir Ghulam Mohyuddin (Babuji) and descendants as successors at the shrine; Ernst and Lawrence call Khwaja Hasan Nizami "his successor (khalifa)". Possibly different senses of "successor" (sajjada-nashin vs khalifa); listed side by side, no verdict. **Rauf may prefer to drop this bullet** if he judges it not a real disagreement.

Also noted in prose, not as a disagreement: Werbner and Basu print the Einzmann title once as "Golra, Sharif" (with a comma, p. 35) and once without (p. 169).
