## The site is not named by Lahori

Lahori never names the place of Shah Latif's *rauza*; the identification with Bari Imam rests on the annotator's citation of *Hayat-e Bari Imam* (Rawalpindi) and the Pothohar meeting (entry lines 43-44, 82-83, 108-113, Doubt I20). The Overview says so in its second paragraph, and the Hadiqat material is framed throughout as about "Lahori's Shah Latif". Frembgen does name Nurpur Shahan (line 171). **Rauf: confirm that the Hadiqat's Shah Latif Bari Qadiri should be presented as this saint at all.** I treated it as the entry's (and the integration's) assignment, with the doubt stated in the Overview.

## Disagreements listed (5)

1. Death year: current 1705; Frembgen 1705/1706; Lahori's author ۹۹۴ھ [OCR?]; annotator 1117 AH / 1706 CE.
2. Name: current Sayyid Abdul Latif Kazmi / Bari Imam; Lahori Shah Latif Bari Qadiri and (in the Persian) Sayyid Shah Latif Majzub; Frembgen "Barri Imam" only.
3. Masters: current names none; Lahori Hayat al-Mir Zinda Pir and Shaikh Nasir al-Din Qurashi Multani; annotator says the sobriquet Hayat al-Mir has no historical proof.
4. Tomb's place: current and Frembgen Nurpur Shahan; Lahori names none.
5. Five lamps at Mama Ji's langar: in honour of Barri Imam vs symbolising the Holy Family, same page (line 190-194).

Not listed as disagreements because the sources agree: the Qadiri order (current, Lahori's chapter, Frembgen); spring urs (current) and "first week of May" 1994 (Frembgen); "several days" (current) and five days (Frembgen).
