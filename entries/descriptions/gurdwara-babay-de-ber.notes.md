## Basis

- Only two books carry row-level material: Khalid, *Walking with Nanak* (l. 332–356) and Dhillon, *Janamsakhis* (l. 438–444). Both are **judgement identifications**: Khalid prints only "Baba Bair", never "Babay de Ber" (l. 332, 356); Dhillon names no gurdwara, only Sialkot plus "an old beri (jujube) tree" (l. 438, 444). The Overview says this in its second paragraph, in plain words ("taken to be this gurdwara on the strength of the name and the city").
- Every other book is person-level (Guru Nanak) or place-level (Sialkot) and is framed as context: Rizvi, Kasmani, Boivin, Schaflechner, Werbner & Basu, Abbas, Rozehnal, Schimmel *Mystical Dimensions*, Khalid *A White Trail*, Khalid *In Search of Shiva*, Bhandari *Khulasat*.
- The current Description's paragraphs are kept verbatim, first in their sections (Overview, Guru Nanak and Hamza Ghaus, The Gurdwara, Legacy); all four bibliography items kept verbatim and first.
