## No book names the site

None of the books names the shrine, Girhor Sharif, a tomb, an *ʿurs* or a custodian. *Pain and Grace* does not give a burial place even in its account of his death (entry lines 17-19). The Overview says this in its second paragraph, and every book passage is framed as being about the man or the area. All site facts (location, khanqah, Muslim and Hindu pilgrims, *urs*, custodian) come from the current Description only.

## Current Description

Every claim is kept, mostly word for word, and spread across Overview, Life, His Writings, His Death, The Shrine and Legacy. The Mangrio-tribe and khanqah sentence appears once, in the Overview. The two bibliography items are kept verbatim and first.

## Disagreements listed (4)

1. Order: Naqshbandi (current; *Pain and Grace* p. 27 f.23; *Veil* p. 224 f.206) against "Suhrawardi" (*Mystical Dimensions* p. 418 f.387). Entry lines 514-516 and 547-551.
2. Death: Kalhoro (current) says a rival Hindu ascetic and his *marhi*, and that he died of his wounds. *Pain and Grace* says 72 companions and the Shiva idol in Hathungo, District Khairpur. *Veil* says "a Shiva temple", with no place. *Mystical Dimensions* says only "missionary zeal".
3. Master's name: current "Khwaja Muhammad Zaman of Luari Sharif"; *Pain and Grace* "Makhdum Muhammad Zaman" (p. 27) and "Muhammad Zaman Lanwari" (pp. 260-261). "Lanwari" may be a misprint or misreading of Luari/Lowari. The entry carries no [OCR?] on it, so it is listed as printed. **Rauf: keep this as a disagreement, or drop it as a spelling variant?**
4. Place of the Daudpota edition of *Kalam-i Girhori*, 1956: Karachi (*Pain and Grace* p. 295), Karachi: Matbaʿ al-ʿArab (*Veil* p. 318 f.300), Hyderabad, Sind (*Mystical Dimensions* p. 475 f.444; also p. 147 f.116).

There is no disagreement on dates. 1739-1778 (*Pain and Grace*) and d. 1778 (*Veil* text and index, *Mystical Dimensions* index) all agree with the current text.

## English I supplied for non-English prose (Bhandari, *Khulasat al-Tawarikh*, p. 284)
