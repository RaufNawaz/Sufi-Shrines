## Disagreements listed (5)

1. Hanuman's standing beside Rama: the current text's "devoted servant" against Schaflechner's
   "close ally", Khalid's "mighty warrior … Hercules", Rafat's "two poles" and Schimmel's "one and the
   same".
2. The god's name: Hanuman / monkey-god against Khalid's "monkey deity" and Shackle's Punjabi form
   Hanūvant.
3. Hindu numbers in Pakistan and Sindh: Schaflechner's 6–10 million, over 90% scheduled castes;
   Boivin's "several millions", a majority scheduled castes; Khalid's 1998 census "largest minority".
4. Hindu emigration at Partition: Boivin's own 0.8 million against the consensus of 1 million (p. 61).
5. Whether Sindh was free of communal strife: Sorley against Schaflechner against Boivin.

Not listed as a disagreement: the old man's "one hundred and fifty years" against the officer's "I
don't know exactly how old" for the Ram Bagh temple (945). Both are in `## Setting`. It is a
different temple, and putting it among the disagreements risked reading as a statement about this
temple's age. **Say if you want it listed.**
