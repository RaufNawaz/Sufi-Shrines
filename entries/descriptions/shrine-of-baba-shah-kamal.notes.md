   Kanhaiya Lal (line 239) are both "attached as this book's claim". The draft frames Lahori's brother of
   Shah Jamal as the book's claim about Shah Jamal's brother and says outright that nothing in the book ties

   attribution should move out of the Life into a hedged paragraph of their own.
2. **"Elder" vs "second brother".** The current text says Shah Kamal was the *elder* brother. Lahori's

   whether "second brother" / "دوسرے" means birth order is not settled by the entry. Drop the bullet if you
   read it as "other brother".
3. **No verse.** The entry carries no verse, chronogram or inscription for this row, so the article has no
   verse excerpt. The nearest thing is the tasbih the date palms of Ichhra recited (Setting), which is prose.
4. **New section "Other Saints of the Name".** Built from the namesakes the entry lists (Kaithal Kamal,
   Kamal Bukhari/Pir Jahaniyan, Sayyid Kamal, Sayyid Kamil Shah Lahori, Sayyid Kamal Shah Chishti, the
   contents-page "مزار سید کمال[OCR?]"), each stated as *not* this saint. Keep, or cut to one sentence?
5. **"A Legend of Lahore's Founding".** The Raja Ram Chand legend (line 140) sits in the entry's Lahore
