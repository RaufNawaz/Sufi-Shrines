## Disagreements listed (Where the Sources Disagree)

1. **Name.** Site's account: Sayyid Yusuf, called Shah Yusuf Gardez. *Khulasat* index: یوسف گردیزی,
   no Shah, no Multan (lines 950, 954). *Hadiqat* notice (hedged): only "حضرت شاہ گردیز ملتانی", no
   personal name (lines 43, 60–61); its index گردیز شاہی ملتانی (lines 56, 63).
2. **Dates.** Site's account: arrival around 1088, death first half of the 1100s, both "by
   tradition". *Hadiqat* notice: no death year (lines 43, 62). *Khulasat*: nothing beyond the list
   (line 948). Strictly this is silence against a date, not a contradiction; listed so the reader
   sees that no book supports the traditional dates.
