## Disagreements listed (14)

Not listed as disagreements (no conflict, or only within one account): the relative position of the samadh (Kanhaiya Lal: fort east, Ranjit Singh samadh west; Chishti p. 498: between Roshnai gate and the fort, to the west; Khalid: facing the fort, beside the mosque) — the readings may conflict, decide if you want a bullet; Khalid's "teeming with visitors" (p. 195) against "Today, this place is deserted" (p. 196), both narrated as he gives them.
