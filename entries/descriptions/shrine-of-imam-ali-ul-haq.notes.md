## Hedged / framed placements

- Bhandari's account of the coming and martyrdom is framed as hearsay ("گویند", as the Doubt at l. 736 notes); the plural verb "یافتند" is stated (the martyrdom is the band's).
- "خلف امام زین العابدین" given as "son of Imam Zain al-Abidin", the entry's gloss, "as the book prints it" (l. 731, 736).
- Lahori: index entry "علی الحق سیالکوٹی | ۱۸۷" given as an index entry, the number 187 quoted as the index prints it, not turned into a page (l. 42). The other names sharing 187 (l. 45) are stated as a fact of the index, with no inference drawn. The separate "علی الحق شاہ | ۲۲۳" (l. 47) is mentioned only to say it is not cross-referenced.
- Lahori's editor citing the same *Waqa'i Sialkot* for Husain Zanjani's 600 AH (l. 56–58, p. 199): stated as a fact about the editor's use, with "The book does not say what the *Waqa'i* itself reports of the Imam's dates." I did not connect this with Chishti's "companion of the Zanjanis". **Check you are happy with that juxtaposition being left for the reader.**
- Sabuktigin context (Kanhaiya Lal p. 14, folio 9, l. 157; Eaton p. 110, folio 98, l. 379): framed as about the province, with "The books consulted do not mention the saint in that connection".
- The name Salbahan: I note that the title the current text gives the raja (Salbahan) recurs in Bhandari's founder "راجه سالباهن" and Khalid's "Raja Salivahan", and say none of the books connects that king with the Imam. This is my observation of a name match, not a source's. **Decision for Rauf: keep or drop that sentence.**
