## Decisions needing Rauf

1. **The identification is held.** All three Urdu books' material is "attached as this book's claim" (lines 22, 104, 148). Kanhaiya Lal's Doubt 2 (lines 167–187) says "A human must decide which figure the row is before anything from pp. 79–80, 281, 286, 296 or 299 is cited under it". I followed the later attach-as-claim ruling and the brief's rule for claim-noted material, so the draft cites those pages:
   - **p. 281–282**: given as Kanhaiya Lal's account of this tomb. It matches the row closely: Abd al-Razzaq bin Abd al-Wahhab Gilani titled Shah Chiragh, Aurangzeb's order, eight graves, Zakariya Khan's mosque.
   - **pp. 79–80**: given as his other account, with the one-man/two-man split stated in the Overview and under Disagreements.
   - **pp. 286, 296, 299**: given only as pointers under "Other Men of the Name".

   **Please confirm**, or rule that pp. 79–80 and 299 should be cut.
2. **Shah Dargahi's pir** (Lahori p. 267 entry ۲۲۰; Kanhaiya Lal p. 299) is given in the Life section as a hedged pointer: "Neither book settles that this pir is the Shah Chiragh of the Mall". Lahori's what-the-row-gains paragraph treats the link as established (line 38), but Doubt I3 counts him as a separate Chiragh figure (line 53). Please decide whether this pointer stays in the Life section or moves to "Other Men".
3. **Chishti's "عالمگیر ثانی [OCR?]"** (p. 109) is listed as a disagreement with the Aurangzeb attribution. It may be a misreading. A check of the lithograph would settle whether it belongs in the article.
4. **The current Description's "Corroborating Sources" paragraph** has been folded into `## Legacy`. Its parenthesis said the citation details were "not recoverable from the OCR text at hand", and that is a process word, so I reworded it to "have not been recovered, and they should be verified against the original". The claim is kept. "consulted separately from the tazkira below" became "from Alam Faqri's tazkira", because there is no longer a tazkira "below".
5. **Little verse.** The books carry almost none for this saint. See the Verse section below.
