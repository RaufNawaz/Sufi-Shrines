## Disagreements listed (Where the Sources Disagree)

1. **Who Rama was.** The current text calls him "Lord Rama". Shackle: "Hindu name of God, the divine
   hero …" (line 33). Schaflechner: "seventh incarnation of the God Vishnu" (line 237). Rizvi:
   "incarnation of Vishnu", with Kabir's Nirguna Rama set against Ram Chandar (line 164). Khalid: "a
   popular deity" (line 412). Chishti: Raja Ramchandar, an اوتار by Hindu belief (line 20), with the
   English historians' view that his divinity is poetic (line 22).
2. **When Rama lived.** Chishti gives twelve hundred years before the Christian era against the
   horoscope figure "[OCR?] نو سو اکسٹہہ [OCR?]" (line 21, "the two figures stand as printed"). He also
   reports Ichhwak at two thousand or two thousand two hundred years before that era (line 21).
3. **Rama's sons.** Chishti says three at p. 661 (line 19, reading uncertain) and two, Kushu and Lohu,
   at p. 795 (line 19). Khalid has twin sons, Luv and Kush (line 391). Bhandari has لو, son of a راجه
   رام چند whom the book does not identify further (lines 445, 450).
4. **Ravana's kingdom.** Shackle: Lanka (lines 39–40). Usborne's annotator: Ceylon (line 89). Sleeman,
   via Khalid, has Ram set out for Ceylon (line 410). Chishti: راجہ سنگلدیپ (line 22).
5. **Who killed Ravana.** Waris Shah's verse: "Did not a woman kill Ravana?" (line 93). Its annotator:
   Rama, in battle (lines 91–92). Shackle: Ram "who killed Ravan" (line 33). Amog Lila, via Khalid: Ram,
   after the goddess heard Sita's plea (line 390).

Considered and not listed as disagreements, because they need not conflict: who was exiled (the current
text's exile of Rama with Sita and Lakshmana, against Sita's banishment in Chishti, line 20, and Khalid,
line 391); what Dussehra marks (the annotator, line 92; Amog Lila, line 390; Sleeman's Dashara
procession to the cheonkul tree, line 410). Both are set side by side in the prose instead.
