## Disagreements listed (5)

1. **Visitors after 1947.** The current text says the gurdwara "has continued to receive Sikh
   visitors". Qaiser, as reported by Khalid on p. 33, says it "remained shut" until pilgrims were
   "Only recently" allowed in (line 51). Khalid also records a saffron flag and a resident priest
   (lines 48, 54).
2. **Her age.** The current text gives her birth as c. 1464 and says she was five years older than
   Nanak. Dhillon gives her death in 1518 "at the age of 6:fcy-four", read as sixty-four (line 119).
   I did not do the subtraction (which would give a birth around 1454) in the prose. It is left side
   by side, with no verdict.
3. **Her place among the first followers.** The current text calls her "the very first". Dhillon has
   "the most ardent ... his sister Nanki and her husband Jai Ram", and has Daulat Khan share her belief
   that Nanak lives (lines 117).
4. **Jai Ram's post.** The current text says "an official in the service of" Daulat Khan Lodi.
   Dhillon says "a revenue official" (line 116). Khalid says "in the court of Nawab Daulat Khan"
   (line 43). This is a mild difference, listed because the brief asks for any difference.
5. **Names.** The current text has Bebe or Bibi Nanaki, and the row name is Babay Nanki. Khalid has
   Bebe Nanaki and Bebe Nanki, Gurdwara Janamasthan Bebe Nanaki and Gurdwara Bebe Nanaki, and perhaps
   Gurdwara Dera Chahal. Dhillon has Nanki (lines 38, 114).
