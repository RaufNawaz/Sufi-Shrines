## Disagreements listed (15)

   (256–259) and grandfather role (202); Bhandari's bare name (1042, 1046); Lahori's later notices (54).
2. Which Jalal, a fourteenth-century Jalal ad-Din Bukhari: Ernst and Lawrence (486–495) and Rozehnal
   (774), against the site's 1192–1291.
3. Which Jalal, the five Pirs of the romance: Waris Shah (364–368).
4. Which Jalal met Guru Nanak: the Sikh tradition, against Khalid and Qaiser (875–876).
5. Where his migration ended and where he lies:
   - Uch: Lahori (51), Kanhaiya Lal (153), Chishti (108), Rizvi (544–545), Schimmel (817).
   - Multan: the note at Lahori p. 349 (52) and Sharib (209–214).
   - The annotator's Uch origin and Multan burial (369–371).
6. The road from Bukhara:
   - Bhakkar: the site and Rizvi (544).
   - Multan: Sharib, and the note at Lahori p. 349 (52), with its 635 AH / 1227 CE as printed.
7. His dates:
   - 1291: the site, Lahori's 690 AH (51) and Kasmani (600).
   - 1292: Schimmel (813–815).
   - No date: Rizvi (546–548) and Sharib (219–222).
   - Thirteenth century: Khalid (876).
8. His sons:
   - Five: Lahori (50).
   - Three: Sharib (215–218) and Rizvi (549–551).
   - Bhandari's Sayyid Mahmud (1046).
9. His order: the site says Suhrawardi.
   - The books say khalifa, murid or disciple of Zakariya (48, 209–212, 542–543, 816), and name no
     order: Rizvi (553–555), Sharib (219–221).
10. His epithet: Schimmel (814), Rizvi (546), Sharib (231), the note at Lahori p. 349 (52), Lahori's
    heading (47), Chishti (106, 108).
11. Bibi Jawindi: Khalid's "great granddaughter" (877) against Rozehnal's "wife" (774).
12. The relics in the romance: the cudgel and dagger inversion (365–366, 377–379).
13. Bahauddin Zakariya's seat:
    - Multan: the site, Sharib (210) and Eaton's "Baha' al-Haqq Zakariya … whose tomb is in Multan
      city" (473).
    - Uch: Rozehnal (748–749).
14. Where Uch lies: Rozehnal "seventy miles southwest of Multan" (746) against Schimmel "northeast of
    Multan" (834). Both are place-level.
15. The Qadiriyya at Uch:

    - The Hadiqat editor gives 887 AH (75).
    - All are place- or province-level, given as context.
