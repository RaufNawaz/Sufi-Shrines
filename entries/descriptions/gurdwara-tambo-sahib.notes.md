## The shape of this row

refuge legend, the gurdwara, Jatha Singh, cloth-tying, the name) and *A White Trail* (lines 478–498: the
legend, the tree and building, grounds, Kar Seva buildings, Lohri reportage, Guru Nanak's birthday, the

the bargain story only at place level (Talwandi, lines 452–459) and names no gurdwara. Everything else is
person-level Guru Nanak material or Nankana Sahib / Punjab context, each passage framed ("What follows is
said of the town, not of this gurdwara"; "said of the Guru, not of this site"; "Writing of the province
and not of this gurdwara"; "Writing of the town's Lohri and not of this gurdwara alone").
