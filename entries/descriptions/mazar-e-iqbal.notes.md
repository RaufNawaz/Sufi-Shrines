## Disagreements listed (10)

1. **Birthplace.** The current text has Sialkot. Boivin's note says "born in Lahore" (lines 887–890,
   902), though Boivin elsewhere prints the years 1877–1938 (line 883).
2. **Day of death.** The current text gives 21 April 1938, with the tomb records' 21 September. The
   books give the year only: Boivin 883, Rozehnal 1131, *As Through a Veil* 1222, *Mystical
   Dimensions* 1518.
3. **Span of the poetry.** The current text has *Zarb-e-Kalim* in 1936. *As Through a Veil* lists *Pas
   che bāyad kard* in 1936 and *Armaghān-i Ḥijāz* in 1938 (lines 1351, 1356). *Mystical Dimensions*
   says the works "appeared from 1915 until 1935" (line 1633).
4. **The dissertation.** The current text has Munich and "Persia". Rizvi has "Persia, London, 1908"
   (line 784). *Mystical Dimensions* has "Iran. Cambridge, 1908" (line 1668).
5. **The lectures' title and date.** *Six Lectures*, 1930 (lines 1348, 1672). Rozehnal 1934/1982
   (lines 1135–1138). Rizvi's reprint of 1944 (line 784).
6. **Iqbal and Sufism.** The current text's "decayed mysticism". Schimmel's counter-reading (lines
   1546–1550), which the entry says "must travel with the attack". Ernst and Lawrence's "reservations"
   together with the ghazal (lines 727–731).
7. **The Perfect Man and Nietzsche.** The current text sets the *mard-e-momin* beside the
   *Übermensch*. Schimmel says "rather than from the Nietzschean superman" (lines 1626–1628).
8. ***jabr mahmud*.** The same book gives two glosses, "higher predestination" and "agreeable
   constraint". The entry marks them "not reconciled" (lines 1579–1583).
9. **Bausani's article on the *Gulshan-i raz-i jadid*.** 1958 in *As Through a Veil* (lines
   1327–1329), 1959 in *Mystical Dimensions* (lines 1676–1677).
10. **Titles of Schimmel's own studies.** "Beginning" in Werbner and Basu (line 983) against
    "Beginnings" in *As Through a Veil* (line 1334). "Prophet of Islam" in *As Through a Veil* (line
    1335) against "Prophet" in *Mystical Dimensions* (line 1682).
