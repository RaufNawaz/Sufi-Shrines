## Disagreements listed (12)

3. Order: Suhrawardi (site) / chain to Baha al-Din Multani via Rajan Qattal + Chishti faiz (Lahori; the entry does not call the chain Suhrawardi, so neither do I) / Suhrawardi and also Qadiri (Malik).
4. Early life: travel to Sialkot / slave of a Sialkot man / treasury supervisor, mendicant, labourer.
5. Why he left Sialkot: entrusted with khanqah / master's dying glance / disputes with Sialkot people.
6. Descent (internal to Malik): Afghan vs Sayyid.
7. The bridge: Nala Dek (site) / river Deg near Eminabad and the Chenab (Bhandari). Not asserted to be the same bridge.
8. Tomb in vs near the town.
9. When the chuha tradition began: 1839 print (site) / late 19th c. after 1898 (Malik).
10. Cause of the condition: natural microcephaly (site) / colonial suspicion of artificial production; Kunjahi's "chemical reactions" (both reported by Malik).
11. Effect of Auqaf takeover: mid-20th c. and reform (site) / 1960s, and could not change mujawir's authority (Malik).
12. Spelling of the name: دولا / دولہ / شاه دولا / Daulah, and Malik's uncertain second-title form.
