## Consensus choices

- **Year of death:** "Accounts of the year of his death differ, and there is no settled view; it is placed between 998 and 1001 AH." Basis (d). (a) does not settle it: the shrine's account gives no year, only the sixteenth century. (b) does not: the one book gives 1001 AH in its text and 998 AH in its editor's note, one voice each. (c) does not cleanly: the author's entry is the earliest account devoted to the saint, but the 998 AH rests on the disciple's own chronogram on the death, which the editor uses to call 1001 AH wrong. So the range is given without choosing. The chronogram is kept (its verse paragraph byte for byte) with the year 998 AH that Abdul Haq derived from it, because the verse is part of the story. The third reading (death before 996 AH) is the team version's own inference, which it labels as one the book does not draw: dropped. No AH to CE conversion is made (the team version makes none for the death).
- **Succession to his father:** "Accounts of how he came to his father's seat differ, but he is remembered as having succeeded to the *masnad* ... after his father's death." Basis (c): the author's text is the earliest account devoted to the saint; the editor's note of a dispute lasting "long years" is the variant, signalled by the one clause and not listed.
- **His name and title:** Syed Musa Pak Shaheed (basis (a), the shrine's account; Lahori's heading agrees), with the full name Sayyid Jamal al-Din Abu al-Hasan Musa Pak Shaheed Gilani Multani (Lahori's later entry; no source contradicts it). Minor variants dropped silently: "Abul Hasan" against "Abu al-Hasan" (one spelling kept), Bhandari's "Shaikh Musa Gilani" (Shaikh not Sayyid; left out with the list, see below), Khalid's spelling "Moosa", kept only for the name on the Uch board.
- **Where he was first buried:** "He was buried in Multan, where his shrine arose." Basis (a) and (b): the shrine's account and Lahori. The Uch board (grave once at Uch, body later shifted to Multan) does not contradict burial in Multan; it is given in The Shrine as the team version frames it ("may bear on this saint", the link resting on the name and the board, a separate building), not as a rival account.
- **Abd al-Qadir Jilani / Gilani:** "Jilani" for the saint, as the team version's site narrative spells it; "Gilani" kept where the team version uses it of the hymns and of the family line.

## Left out

- **The inference "before 996 AH"** and, with it, Abdul Haq's dates in the Hijaz (arrived 996 AH, stayed until 999 AH, almost all the time with Abd al-Wahhab). Those dates are the disciple's later life, and read beside "after the saint's death" they would carry the inference the team version says the book does not draw. Abdul Haq's going on to Abd al-Wahhab after the saint's death, and the *faiz* and *khirqa* he received, are kept.

- **Bhandari's governor "Shaikh Musa Gilani" under Dara Shikoh:** the reading of the name is marked [OCR?] / uncertain in the printed text, and no link to the saint is made (rule 5).

- **Khalid's "It's unclear who he was":** a quoted characterisation; its hedge is carried by "may bear on this saint" and the sentence on what the link rests on.
- **The index entries and the phrase "the worthy son"** (the second epithet for Musa; "beloved son" is kept).
