## Consensus choices (rule 4)

- Death year: "Accounts of the year of his death differ; the year the shrine keeps is 1671." Basis (a), the shrine. Variants dropped: 1649 (also in the shrine's account), 1050 AH (Lahori), Chishti's illegible year. The phrase "later seventeenth century" was dropped because two of the three dated variants fall in the 1640s.
- Death day: commemorated on the 4th of Rabi-ul-Sani (basis a); Thursday kept (Chishti; only his year is illegible).
- Birth: 1587, framed as tradition (the shrine's account; no source contradicts it).
- Order: "no settled view; most often counted a Suhrawardi, though also remembered as a Qadiri". Basis (b): Lahori, Kanhaiya Lal, Alam Faqri and the shrine ("of the Suhrawardi line"). The shrine's joint Qadiri, Suhrawardi and Chishti claim, Chishti's double heading and Kugle's Qadiri grouping are not listed side by side; the inscription's "Qadiri" stays in The Shrine because it is part of the building.
- His master and chain: "Accounts differ over who his own master was and how his spiritual chain ran; the shrine remembers him as a special disciple of Sheikh Khokar Baig Suhrawardi, and his Suhrawardi line is most often traced back to Bahauddin Zakariya of Multan." Basis (a) for the master, (b) for Zakariya (Lahori, Alam Faqri). Lahori's eight step chain is left out: it starts from Shaikh Gada Beg, not the shrine's master, and joining it to Khokar Baig would merge chains no source joins (V1 rounds 1 and 2). This is a rule 4 over rule 7 judgement, recorded here for Rauf.
- Descent: Hussaini Syed (all sources), traced by the shrine to Jalaluddin Surkh-Posh Bukhari; the descendants at Sialkot (Lahori, 1870s) kept as a further fact, the present sajjada nashin as a descendant kept.
- Storeys: "until only two remained" (the shrine's telling); Lahori's four and Chishti's five dropped as minor variants.
- ʿUrs: 3rd to 5th of Rabi-ul-Sani (basis a); Chishti's 4th not listed.
- Chillas: the shrine's four places; only Sheikhupura kept from the nineteenth century list (the other two names are uncertain readings).
- Time anchors: Lahori 1875 and Kanhaiya Lal 1884 are firm; Chishti (1867, "the date unconfirmed") is anchored only to "the nineteenth century".
