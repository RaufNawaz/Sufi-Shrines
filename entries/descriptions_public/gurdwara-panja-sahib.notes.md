## Consensus choices (rule 4)

- **How old the pool is:** "said to have been created after the miracle of Nanak ...; accounts of its age differ". Basis (b) with (a): the festival account, consistent with the site's spring of the legend, against one speaker in *Walking with Nanak*. Left out: thousands of years, the Buddhist era, Hiuen Tsang's sacred lake (the speaker's name is also partly lost).

- **Guru Nanak's dates:** born in 1469 in what is today Nankana Sahib, died at Kartarpur in 1539. Basis (b) (the travelogues and Dhillon give 1469 to 1539; the birthplace and Kartarpur are the travelogue's, the clean statements). Left out: Dhillon's 14 April and 22 September, Chishti's 14 to 15 April at Talwandi and Katak of Samvat 1526 (uncertain in the team version), Bhandari's Samvat 1526, 880 Hijri, Bahlul Lodi, Salim Shah and the age of seventy to eighty, Chishti's Kartarpur or Kiratpur (uncertain).
