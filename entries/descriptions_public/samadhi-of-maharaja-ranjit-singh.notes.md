## Consensus choices (rule 4)

- **Date of death:** 27 June 1839 (lead and His Death). Basis (a), the site's date, with Khalid's years 1780 to 1839. No "accounts differ" clause: the only other year, Kanhaiya Lal's 1896, is in the Bikrami era (once with numeral and month uncertain), which the team version does not convert, so no source gives a rival year in the same calendar. Left out: the Bikrami year, and the year of the severe illness (uncertain). See Points for V1, 4.
- **Day of the anniversary:** minor (a day's difference, rule 4): the 2011 commemoration is told as "In June 2011"; Khalid's 29 June dropped silently. His "twelve days after the commemoration of Guru Arjan's death anniversary" kept, as it gives no date.
- **Taking of Lahore:** "won Lahore at the end of the eighteenth century; accounts of how and in which year the city came to him differ". Basis (d): Chishti's 1798 by Shah Zaman's leave and Khalid's 1799 march have one source each, and Chishti's own second telling (the elders' invitation) is a third; the envelope is the team version's own phrase (*Walking with Nanak*), as with the sixteenth century for Todarmal at Hinglaj. Left out: Shah Zaman's attack of 1797 and leave of 1798; the elders' invitation when Lahna Singh, the *hakim*, was destroying the monuments, on condition of restoring Shalamar (the restoration itself kept); the triumphant march of 1799 and the welcome by a population sick of lawlessness. Kept: the small fief around Gujranwala, Qazi Nizam-ud-Din at the conquest, the coronation of 1801 (site and Khalid agree).
- **Who died on the pyre:** the site's four queens and seven attendants, basis (a) (not every other source is against it: Khalid's "wives and concubines" is close), with "accounts of who they were differ". Left out: Kanhaiya Lal's eleven *ranis*, Khalid's eleven women, and both counts of memorials (eleven and two; thirteen). The two pigeons kept, framed as told; no source disputes them.
- **What marks his remains:** the site's marble urn shaped like a lotus beneath the marble pavilion, basis (a), with "accounts of how his remains are marked differ". Left out: Kanhaiya Lal's stone samadh at the centre, Chishti's square memorial under a covering, Khalid's spot where the last remains "were buried".
- **Where he was cremated:** on the spot where the samadhi stands, basis (a) with (b) (site and Kanhaiya Lal). Treated as minor, no clause: Khalid's "in the ground accompanying the building" dropped silently. See Points for V1, 5.
- **Images at the door:** the site's Ganesha, Devi and Brahma on the red sandstone doorways, basis (a), with "though accounts of which deities they show differ". Left out: Kanhaiya Lal's three stone images above the door (Shiv ji, Ganesh ji and a third uncertain) and Chishti's marble image on the east, perhaps Ganesh (uncertain). Khalid's balcony paintings (Krishna, Ganesha, Ram and Sita) are other works and kept.
- **The pavilion and its dome:** no contradiction. The site's marble pavilion inlaid with *pietra dura*; the nineteenth century marble pavilion (Kanhaiya Lal's "bungalow") with marble columns and a brick dome; its white marble pillars in 2011 (Khalid's "palanquin"). Told as one object, as the team version's own disagreement item compares them; "bungalow" and "palanquin" rendered "pavilion".
- **The great dome:** the site's gilded, fluted domes; nineteenth century mirrors and gilding; 2011 coloured glass in geometric patterns. Descriptions at different times, kept with their anchors.
- **How many storeys:** minor (the brief's own example), dropped silently: Chishti's two storeyed brick building, Khalid's four floors. The upper levels are given from Kanhaiya Lal's survey alone.
- **Where it stands beside Dera Sahib:** Kanhaiya Lal's "its wall lies to the west of the samadhi of Guru Arjan", basis (c), the earlier of the two accounts devoted to the site that place it; treated as minor, no clause. Left out: Khalid's "facing Gurdwara Dera Sahib" across a lawn and "Just behind the samadhi of Guru Arjan", with the minaret of the Badshahi Masjid behind.
- **The Hazuri Bagh pavilion:** "is said to have been built from part of an older tomb, though accounts differ over whose". Basis (d). Left out: Zeb-un-Nisa's tomb (Chishti; the link to the Hazuri Bagh is in a partly illegible line, and the clean p. 637 line "Zeb-un-Nisa's marble tomb was taken down for a baradari" is the same object, so it is left out of the despoiling paragraph too) and Jahangir's tomb (Khalid, twice, as hearsay).
- **His temper toward other faiths:** verdicts, left out: Khalid's "secular ruler" like Akbar, Chishti's "religious prejudice" and "hard-heartedness", Kanhaiya Lal's blame of the Sikhs for the city's earlier ruin (also a line with an illegible word). The facts under them (his gifts, his stripping of monuments, the revival of the city) are kept.
- **The succession after him:** compatible facts from two books, told without an added order word: Kharak Singh on the throne (Kanhaiya Lal, Chishti); Chand Kaur after the deaths of Kharak Singh and Nau Nihal Singh, set on the throne by the Sandhanwalias, Dhian Singh to Jammu (Kanhaiya Lal); the Sandhanwalias close kin of Chand Kaur and the Maharaja, Dhian Singh's word to Sher Singh, Sher Singh's accession and his killers' end (Chishti). Left out: Chishti's year for Kharak's death and Sher's accession (uncertain).
- **Spellings (minor):** Sada Kaur (not Sada Kunwar); Duleep Singh throughout (site, Lahori, Khalid's "Duleep"; Chishti's and Khalid's "Dalip" dropped); samadhi throughout for the books' "samadh"; Faqir Aziz al-Din (beside Faqir Nur al-Din) for Khalid's "Fakir Azizudin"; Ganesha (site) also in Khalid's list for his "Ganesh"; Roshnai Gate (Kanhaiya Lal, the team heading) for Khalid's "Roshani"; Badshahi Mosque for Khalid's "Royal Mosque" in the gate's placing; Khyber Pakhtunkhwa for the printed "Khyber-Pakhthunkwa". Shahid Ganj (the samadhi) and Shaheed Ganj (the mosque) are kept as the team version spells each, and not identified.
