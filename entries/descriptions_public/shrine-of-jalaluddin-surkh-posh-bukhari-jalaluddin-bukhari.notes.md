## Time anchors

- The site's account (current): present tense for the shrine, its devotions, the Bukhari line's standing and the town as a heritage site.
- Lahori, 1875: no anchor needed; what is used from him concerns the saint's own life (descent, deference in Hindustan, death date).
- Chishti, 1867 (the date unconfirmed): only "In the nineteenth century" (Abd al-Qadir Sani's descendants holding the *sajjada*, the four sons' graves, the sayyids of Uch and questions of descent). Uch Miran, the Gilani forebear and the Faqir family carry no anchor and are in the past.
