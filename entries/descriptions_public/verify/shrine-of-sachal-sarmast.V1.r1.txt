The team version says only that Sehwan was "hardly" mentioned until then, while Sachal and the others "were regularly featured during this time". NEW: In India Sachal's *versi* is celebrated on the 14th of Ramazan and Hari Ram's at the beginning of November, while at the Sachal Sain *darbar* the two *versis* celebrated are Sachal's and Pratap's.
