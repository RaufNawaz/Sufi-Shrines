## Not applied

[V3 46] خواجہ بہاری (whose grave adjoins the tomb) and خواجہ بہار (whose khanqah and tomb the Muhammad Sharif burial ground adjoins) are kept as two names, because the English keeps them as two (Khwaja Bihari and Khwaja Bahar) and does not say they are one man; unifying them would add an identification.
