1. (precision) A Painting of Ramdeo Pir, first paragraph. The team version limits the integration to Sindh ("In Sindh, he is today integrated into the temples dedicated to the pan-Indian divinities"). The draft keeps "in Sindh" only for the name, so "today he is integrated into the temples ..." can be read as a statement about such temples everywhere. Restore the source's scope.
OLD: Ramdeo Pir is still known in Sindh as Rama Pir, and today he is integrated into the temples dedicated to the divinities worshipped throughout India; the painting in Karachi is an instance of that.
NEW: Ramdeo Pir is still known in Sindh as Rama Pir, and in Sindh he is today integrated into the temples dedicated to the divinities worshipped throughout India; the painting in Karachi is an instance of that.

2. (precision) Under Threat, judgement call 12. In the team version "many" counts the temples the author visited ("I visited many temples that were renovated within the past decade, coinciding with changes in the political landscape"), and the coinciding belongs to the decade's renovations as a whole. "many of them coinciding" turns that into a share of the restorations and implies that the others did not coincide, which the team version does not say. The anchor "In the early twenty first century" is right for a book of 2018 speaking of the past decade, and "brought" fairly carries "is seen in"; only "many of them" goes.
OLD: In the early twenty first century, government funding for the places of minority communities brought a wave of temple restorations across Sindh, many of them coinciding with changes in the political landscape.
NEW: In the early twenty first century, government funding for the places of minority communities brought a wave of temple restorations across Sindh, coinciding with changes in the political landscape.

3. (precision) Sindh and Its Hindus, fourth paragraph. The team version gives two separate statements: by the census of 1998 the Hindu minority is the largest in the country, and, on another page and with no date, the majority of the Hindu community is based in Sindh and Southern Punjab. Joined as an absolute phrase, the distribution falls under "By the census of 1998", an anchor the team version does not give it. Split the sentence.
OLD: By the census of 1998 the Hindus were the largest minority in the country, the majority of them based in Sindh and southern Punjab.
NEW: By the census of 1998 the Hindus were the largest minority in the country. The majority of them are based in Sindh and southern Punjab.
