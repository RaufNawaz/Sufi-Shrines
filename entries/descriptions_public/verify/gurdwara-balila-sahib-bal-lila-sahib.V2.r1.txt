NOTES:
Rulings on the writer's doubtful choices:
1. Udam as اُدم: correct for this file. The English here spells "Udam", and V2 keeps apart what the English keeps apart; the Urdu adds no identification with the Janam Asthan article's "Udham" (ادھم). For the coordinator, not a fault in this Urdu: the two English articles spell what is probably the same man (Surjeet's companion) differently, "Udam" here and "Udham" in gurudwara-janam-asthan-nankana-sahib.md. If the English is ever made consistent, the Urdu should follow it; until then اُدم is right.
2. "بیمار اور قریب المرگ": slightly stronger than the English. "Failing" says his health was giving way; قریب المرگ says he was at death's door. Not a FAIL, since "sick and failing" beside a deathbed visit is close in sense, but the exact equivalent is better: "اور نانک رائے بلار کے سرہانے جا بیٹھے، جو بیمار تھے اور جن کی صحت جواب دے رہی تھی۔" (or, shorter, "بیمار اور نڈھال رائے بلار کے سرہانے جا بیٹھے").
3. The janeu line: correct. In the English "that the stories say he refused as a boy" governs only the refusal; that the poem is his is stated plainly. The Urdu has the same scope.
4. Lakhmi Das: correct. "it was said" in the English sits inside "His son Lakhmi Das ... was worthy"; the passing over is stated plainly (and the preceding sentence states it plainly too). The Urdu matches.
5. Nadeem split: acceptable. "a Muslim painter" becomes two plain facts about him, nothing added; ان کی نظر میں keeps the view his. The present ہیں against the past تھے is natural Urdu for a description of a living person and his reported view.
6. The 1985 officials: correct. Both names, both titles and "two officials" are there; نامی is fine here because the two men are new to the reader. قونصلر follows the ruling already made on gurdwara-malji-sahib (V3 r1 there: کونسلر reads as a municipal councillor), so it is kept.
7. "خالص مذہبی تقریبات بن گئے": acceptable. The English tautology ("went back to being religious festivals") means the politics left them; پھر سے carries "went back" and خالص carries the implied "only". No new fact.
8. "میل کچیل دھونے کے بارے میں": acceptable; the verse itself is about hands and feet smeared with slime, so the phrase adds no fact, and a bare دھونے کے بارے میں reads oddly. "سفید پنکھوں": keep the sense but prefer پروں. In Urdu پنکھ is chiefly a wing, and پنکھوں is also the plural of پنکھا (fan); پر is the ordinary Urdu word for feather: "*سفید پروں والے بگلے تیرتھ کے مقدس مقامات پر بسیرا کرتے ہیں۔*"
9. The Rai Bhoe sentence: correct. The English frames both: "It is told that ... and that its last rebirth came under Rai Bhoe, a Bhatti Rajput who had converted to Islam" (the conversion sits inside the "that" clause), and "In the same telling" frames the whole next sentence (Rai Bular as son, Shiv Ram as accountant, the succession of Kalyan Chand Das Bedi). روایت ہے کہ ... and اسی بیان کے مطابق ... cover exactly these, and the Ram Deo Bhatti sentence after them is plain in both languages.
10. PSGPC: correct. ملک right after پاکستانی کمیٹی can only be Pakistan, and the acronym is left unexpanded as in the English. The present نہیں کرتی is ordinary Urdu reported speech after کا خیال تھا.
