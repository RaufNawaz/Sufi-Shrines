But urdu-i18n/urdu-dictionary.json (shrineNames, line 411) gives the page title as گوردوارہ ٹمبو صاحب, with retroflex ٹ. If the title is left as it is, the Urdu page will be headed ٹمبو above an article that derives the name from تمبو. Correcting the dictionary entry to گوردوارہ تمبو صاحب would make title and article agree. The English "also written Tambo Sahib" is not carried over, and that is right: Tambu and Tambo are two romanisations of one name and have a single Urdu spelling, so nothing is lost.
