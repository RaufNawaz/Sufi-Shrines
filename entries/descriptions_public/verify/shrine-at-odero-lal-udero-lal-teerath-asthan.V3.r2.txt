1. Each of the 19 changed sentences was read in its paragraph. Every one is natural, correct, educated Urdu. Agreement, postpositions and izafat are right, there is no calque and no new ambiguity, and each fits the sentences around it:
   1 جس میں دونوں شریک ہیں follows جس کا ... احترام کرتے ہیں in a clean parallel and drops the flat "use" wording.
   2 The two short sentences read well. انہیں clearly means the saint, and یہ نام ... محترم ہے leaves nothing dangling.
   3 The restored head noun removes the ellipsis. The correlative اس احترام ... جو, together with the masculine پایا جاتا, ties the relative clause to احترام and not to علامت. The verb is left implicit after "، اور ... بھی", which is normal Urdu.
   4 Now a single sentence that does not state the birthday twice. ان is Jhulelal and اس is Cheti Chand.
   5 Apposition with یعنی and the postposition کو after the name is standard Urdu and does not cut the name off. The اور/دوسرے redundancy is gone.
   6 خضر کو ... اڈیرو لال ہی سے ملا کر ایک ہستی سمجھا جاتا ہے is idiomatic (ملا کر ایک سمجھنا), and the calque یکجا is gone.
   7 نظریۂ is written with U+06C2, the same as شجرۂ elsewhere in the file.
   8 The repeated اونٹ is deliberate. It is what removes the misreading "ran after God".
   9 The date phrase now qualifies زمانہ, so the time is no longer given twice.
   10 زیادہ سے زیادہ is gone, and رفتہ رفتہ ... ہوتی گئی carries "more and more".
   11 X کی تعظیم is the right construction, and شریک تھے reads well.
   12 ... کا دیوتا ہی سمجھا جانے لگا replaces the calque کے ساتھ ایک سمجھا.
   13 The apposition comes before the postposition, so the relative clause no longer floats.
   14 Fronting the place phrase removes the false pair ہندو مسلمان.
   15 قرآنی روایت کے ... اور ہندوستانی روایت کا is a balanced parallel. The repeated روایت is the parallel itself, not the careless repetition the brief warns about.
   16 مرد is dropped, and ایک دیوتا is the right indefinite for a figure that is identified in the next sentence.
   17 یہ مشہور ہندو دیوتا جھولے لال ہیں، جن کی ... is idiomatic and leaves no fragment.
   18 سانجھی عقیدت removes the doubled مشترک and echoes سب کے سانجھے ہیں earlier in the article. It is in the register of سانجھی تہذیب and not too colloquial.
   19 سب سے بڑے دیوتا is plain and idiomatic, and it removes the second سب سے بڑھ کر, which round 1 noted in two consecutive paragraphs.
