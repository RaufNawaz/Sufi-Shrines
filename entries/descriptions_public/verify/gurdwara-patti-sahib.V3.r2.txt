Not failed: تقسیم اور ہجرت کے تجربات is a stock Urdu collocation and the restatement is resumptive after انہی, so it reads as emphasis rather than a slip. A lighter wording would be ترکِ وطن کے انہی تجربات, which keeps an explicit antecedent without doubling either word.
