NOTES:
1. Round 1 V3 issues: all ten are resolved. Applied verbatim: issue 1 by change 10, 4 by change 3, 5 by change 4, 6 by change 8, 7 by change 7, 8 by change 11, 9 by change 2, 10 by change 1. Issue 2 is fixed by adaptation (change 9) and issue 3 was rejected without a change; rulings in notes 4 and 5. Round 1 notes 4, 8 and 11 are applied by changes 13, 6 and 12.
2. Change 1: دس پاتشاہیوں کے شمار میں چھٹی پاتشاہی گرو ہرگوبند کی ہے now names its noun, agreement is right (چھٹی پاتشاہی ... کی ہے), and اسی points clearly at the sixth patshahi. پاتشاہی twice in the sentence is deliberate (the ellipsis was the round 1 fault) and reads naturally.
3. Change 2: کنواں اور سبیل تھے, masculine plural for a mixed masculine and feminine pair, is standard. The sentence now reads lighter.
4. Change 9 (adapted from issue 2): پیر و مرشد is the right ruling. دادا would have changed what the sentence says, and پیر و مرشد puts the spiritual sense beyond doubt beside والد, which was the substance of the round 1 complaint. پیر و مرشد بھی تھے اور نویں کے والد بھی reads as a natural balanced contrast.
5. Issue 3 (rejected, مصرع kept): the rejection is right. The English itself says "a line ... Its opening runs", and in Urdu گرو ارجن کا ایک مصرع پڑھا ... اس کا آغاز یوں ہوتا ہے followed by words and an ellipsis reads consistently as the opening words of that مصرع, with the ellipsis standing for the rest of it. The round 1 premise, that the quotation is a full line and the ellipsis marks further lines, is only one reading. بند would be a different claim (a stanza) that the English does not make. مصرع is ordinary, correct Urdu for a line of verse.
6. Change 3: اس پر پھٹکار بھیجے has its object and the verb Urdu uses with a curse, on the model of لعنت بھیجنا; the two اس both point at Chandu Lal, and the implied subject کوئی سکھ carries over naturally. Taste: لعنت بھیجے is the commoner idiom, but پھٹکار keeps the English's word and is understood at once.
7. Change 4: اپنے والد کی سمادھ کے مقام پر is now consistent with the rest of the article, and سمادھ takes the feminine کی correctly. It also avoids سمادھی, which in Urdu can mean a meditative trance.
8. Change 5: گرو کے دربار میں ... یہ رسم ہرگوبند ہی نے شروع کی reads cleanly; dropping گرو before ہرگوبند avoids a doubled گرو, and the article uses ہرگوبند alone elsewhere. یہ رسم still links to the preceding sentence on offering arms and horses.
9. Change 6: کچھ راجا بھی قید تھے with ان راجاؤں in the next sentence is the consistent paradigm (راجا invariant in the direct plural, راجاؤں oblique).
10. Change 7: گرو نے دو ایک فوجی مہموں میں شہنشاہ کی مدد کی removes the redundant ان and reads idiomatically; the following sentence's ان (Jahangir) still resolves.
11. Change 8: اپنا جانشین مقرر کیا completes مقرر کرنا, and the coordinated clause is smoother than the old tacked-on جو یوں. Taste only: ہر رائے now stands twice in the sentence and again at the start of the next one (دادا کی روایت پر چلتے ہوئے ہر رائے نے بھی), and دادا closes one sentence and opens the next. The repetition of the name does referential work (after گرو as subject, a pronoun would be ambiguous), so it is not a fault; گدی پر بیٹھے instead of جگہ بیٹھے would match the article's own گدی and sound more idiomatic for a Guru's succession.
12. Change 10: جو ... چلے اور ... تلوار سنبھالے رہے: both verbs now take the direct case جو, and سنبھالے رہنا (kept holding) is idiomatic. Fixed.
13. Change 11: یہ پہلے لاہور کے باہر واقع ایک بستی تھی removes the clash of سابقہ with تھی and reads naturally after the موضع sentence; the second clause (شہر کے محلوں میں بھی اس کا شمار ہوتا تھا) still fits.
14. Change 12: ان کے جانے پر ماتم کیا گیا is natural, drops the bridal association of رخصتی, and no longer echoes رخصت ہوئے in the same sentence with a cognate noun.
15. Change 13: سکھوں کی سیاسی قوت کا مرکز removes the ambiguity of ان. سکھ now appears three times in the quoted sentence (کسی سکھ، ایک سکھ، سکھوں), but each carries a different sense (any Sikh, the one ruler, the community) as in the English, so the repetition is meaningful, not a slip.
16. The NEW sentences contain no Latin letters, no en or em dashes, and no calques; the sentences around them were not disturbed (replay identical, see the V2 r2 report).
