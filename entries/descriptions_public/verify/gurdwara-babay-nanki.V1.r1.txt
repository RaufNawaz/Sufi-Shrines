2. [implicature] "The Defence Housing Authority, the neatest and most expensive residential area of Lahore, sprawled across a vast tract of land, eating into the small historic villages of the area; congested and stricken by poverty, such villages were trapped within a wall after selling their land to it." -> Placed straight after Dera Chahal's own description ("the new houses of former farmers who had sold their land"), it invites the reader to take Dera Chahal as one of the walled, poverty-stricken villages. Suggested fix: open it with "More generally, the Defence Housing Authority, ..." (rest unchanged).
3. [inference] "on one occasion a sermon from its loudspeaker drowned out the talk inside the gurdwara." -> The team version says only "As they talked, a sermon from the mosque's loudspeaker drowned the conversation".
