NOTES: All eleven round 2 issues have been resolved, and the article now reads smoothly and idiomatically from start to finish. The writer's retained wordings read naturally on fluency grounds: "وقار کا احساس بھی" pairs well with "تسلی بھی ملتی ہے", "ان میں سے ہر ایک کے دور میں" is clear and unforced, and "بی بیوں کے مرقد کی دیوار" is fine. What follows is taste. (1) "عقیدت مندوں کے ٹھٹھ کے ٹھٹھ" in the opening: the commoner spelling of the idiom is "ٹھٹ کے ٹھٹ"; "ٹھٹھ" sits close to "ٹھٹھا" (jest), though the context carries it. (2) "وہ مرد صوفی علماء کے ساتھ روحانی مجلسیں کرتی تھیں" is a little colloquial for this register; "مجلسیں منعقد کرتی تھیں" would be more literary. (3) "کھچڑی کے نیچے چھپی دعوت": the food context makes "feast" clear, but دعوت comes one paragraph after تبلیغ; "چھپے پکوان" would remove even a passing doubt. (4) "اس پر بھروسا اسی لیے کیا گیا کہ" stresses a causal link more than the sentence needs; "اس لیے" is lighter. (5) In the custodians section, "روایت ہے"، "کہا جاتا ہے"، "یہ بھی کہا جاتا ہے" and "بیان کیا جاتا ہے" open four consecutive sentences. (6) "1974ء میں اس کا شمار محمد نگر کی بستی بیبیاں پاک دامناں میں ہوتا تھا": شمار sits most comfortably with a category (as in "شہر کے باہر واقع مسلم مقامات میں"); with a single locality "میں شامل تھا" or "کی حدود میں آتا تھا" would be plainer. (7) "ایک حویلی کی خاطر صاف کر دیا گیا" is acceptable; "ایک حویلی کے لیے" is plainer. (8) "تاریخ لکھنے والے" and "سے تاریخ نکالی گئی" stand in adjacent sentences in different senses; the chronogram idiom is clear to an educated reader. (9) "اسی نومسلم شہزادے کی اولاد ہیں جو ... کہلاتے ہیں": the plural verb could for a moment attach to the descendants, though the personal names resolve it at once. (10) "ختلی" still appears with no identification; diacritics (خُتّلی) would at least fix the reading, and identifying him is for the writer only if the source supports it. (11) Repetition across sections: "مزار کی معنویت" in the second section and again in the legacy section; "گہری عقیدت کا مرکز" for Shia devotion in the second section and again in the custodians section; "امڈ آنا" in the opening and the Muharram paragraph; "پناہ" four times across the article; "ثبوت" in two consecutive paragraphs on Data Sahib; "حصہ" twice in the Prem Gupta sentence; "اہلِ بیت کے شہیدوں کے عاشقوں کا ... انہی شہداء" is a heavy genitive chain that repeats the martyrs. Mechanical checks: no Latin letters, no dashes, Western digits only, ء and ھ on every year, six asterisks balanced, verse lines and italic translations untouched, بی بیوں spelt uniformly (بیبیاں only in place names).
