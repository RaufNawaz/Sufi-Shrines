Rulings on the flagged points.
(1) "کیونکہ ایمان دور مدینے میں دفن ہے، ھو" for "For i-t is buried far in Medina hu": accepted. It does not introduce a subject the English leaves open. In the English line pair, "it" has only one grammatical antecedent, "faith" in "Without Ali there is no faith", because Ali would take "he"; the hyphen in "i-t" only marks the singer's drawn vowel. کیونکہ also renders "For" better than the old کہ. Round 1 read the pronoun as open, so if the coordinator prefers to keep a pronoun, the more literal form that still points back to ایمان is "کیونکہ یہ دور مدینے میں دفن ہے، ھو". Either version is faithful.
(2) ارادت for "fiercely devotional": accepted. "والہانہ ارادت سے بھرپور" renders ardent devotion. ارادت leans towards a disciple's devotion, which suits Bahu's murshid centred verse, and it avoids repeating the عقیدت at the end of the sentence. دلکش نغمگی for "gloriously musical" is slightly softer than شاندار but keeps the claim.
(3) "دو پیشکشوں پر ان کا نام درج تھا": accepted. It renders "two items on the programme bore his name", and the two items keep their languages and co names exactly: Punjabi and Siraiki under his name alone, then Siraiki, Sindhi, Punjabi and Urdu with Shah Latif and Bulleh Shah.
(4) The repeated سے in "سلطان باہو کی شاعری سے، عبدالقادر جیلانی سے اور گرو گرنتھ صاحب سے": accepted and better. It follows "with ..., with ... and with" in the English and removes the reading in which the three are compared with each other. The writer was right not to take the V3 suggestion ان دعاؤں کی, which says what is being compared when the English does not.
(5) Jhang Siyal and Jhang Siyalan are kept apart: جھنگ سیالاں is the town where the Chenab meets the Bihat and Hir's home, and جھنگ سیال is the territory of Cucak Siyal. Neither sentence was touched in this round.
Other changed sentences confirmed. Splitting the Hir and Ranjha sentence now leaves the shrine clause outside "said to", as in the English. The singer is a woman (گلوکارہ), as "drew her words" shows. Dropping سنہری الف بے follows round 1 note (c), and the later Golden Alphabet is already اپنی سی حرفی. Other confirmed points: تیسری قسم کا مرشد, سائل for "a man who had come to ask something of him", وہاں کے بیابان for "that wild country", "مرنے سے پہلے مر جانا" for the title, and the Ramadan, Mughal official and mansabdar facts.
Optional precision notes, none of them a fidelity fault.
(a) "اس کے بول کھینچ کھینچ کر ادا کیے" gives "drawing out its words" where the English says vowels. This is the same looseness ruled acceptable in round 1. Closer: "اور گاتے ہوئے اس کے حروفِ علت کو کھینچ کھینچ کر ادا کیا؛"
(b) "پنجاب میں ان کی شہرت عام تھی" renders "his fame in the Punjab was complete" as widespread. The next clause, that no one failed to acknowledge his sanctity, carries the completeness, so this is acceptable.
