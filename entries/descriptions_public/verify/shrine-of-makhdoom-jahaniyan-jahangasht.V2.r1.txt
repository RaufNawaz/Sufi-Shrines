NOTES:
Rulings on the flagged points:
1. اپنے چچا: accepted. Urdu must choose a side. The English lists a Sayyid Muhammad among Surkh-Posh's sons, and the uncle is "Sadr al-Din, known as Muhammad Ghaus", so a paternal uncle fits the English. He is correctly kept apart from the brother Sadr al-Din (المعروف بہ راجو قتال) and from Shaikh Sadr al-Din Arif of Multan.
2. قطبِ عالم ان کے پوتے تھے: accepted. پوتا is the unmarked choice, and it agrees with the English's son Sayyid Mahmud Nasir al-Din. (The following sentence needs the fix in issue 4.)
3. سمیرہ: accepted. It keeps the English's distinction from Sumra (سومرا), the governor of Uch, in the Legends, and adds no identification. دودائی: accepted as a plain transliteration.
4. قِوام / قَوام: accepted and applied correctly. In the khirqa list, the English's "Qawam" is قَوام. In Disciples, Qiwam is قِوام and "a Shaikh Qawam al-Din" is قَوام الدین نام کے ایک شیخ. That last rendering also keeps the English's indefinite "a".
5. خزینۃ الفوائد الجلالیہ: accepted. It is the normal Urdu-script form of the title. Copying a Latin spelling slip into Urdu would serve no one, and no fact changes.
6. "ہمدان یعنی ہمہ دان تو علام الغیوب اللہ تعالیٰ کے سوا کوئی نہیں": accepted. It shows the pun that the English glosses as "the All Knowing". علام الغیوب is exactly "the Great Knower of the Invisible World", and "was none but" is kept as کے سوا کوئی نہیں.
7. سزائے موت سے بچا لیا: accepted. "From the axe" is figurative for execution, and the Urdu gives that meaning without adding a method. The hedge in that sentence still needs the fix in issue 3.
8. Titles of praise: all seven match the English's meaning and order. چراغِ خاندان is the lamp of the family. مظہرِ انوارِ ربانی is the manifestation of the lights of the Lord. مطلعِ تجلیاتِ سبحانی is the dawning place of the epiphanies of the All Glorious. کاشفِ اسرارِ طریقت is the unveiler of the mysteries of the Path. رہنمائے راہِ حقیقت is the guide on the ways of Reality. صاحبِ ارشاد is the master of guidance. سردارِ اوتاد is the leader of the awtad, and the gloss on the "pegs" is kept.
9. کلام: accepted. کافیاں would add a claim about genre that the English does not make.
10. بی بی جاوندی: confirmed in urdu-i18n/urdu-dictionary.json ("Bibi Jawindi").
11. Ratio 0.86: nothing of substance is dropped. All nine headings correspond. All 31 numerals in the English prose are present, and none are extra. Every number written as a word is present: nineteen, twentieth, six, fourteen, seven, four, three, five hundred, a hundred, twenty eight, seventh, ninth, thirty six, seventy and the rest. Every paragraph and every legend has its counterpart, and the hedges "it is told", "accounts differ", "most", "about", "seems" and "may be ascribed" are all present. The shortfall comes from dropped glosses of familiar terms, as the brief allows: Hijri, bin, kunya, qazi, bay'a, khirqa, khilafat, khalifa, khanqah, Hajj, Eid al-Adha, chasht, tahajjud, ulama, wazir, muharrir, murid, nazrana, pir, Shab-i Barat, Sharia, makruh, fatwa, sabr, taqwa, suhbat, tabarruk, wird, Ya Hayy Ya Qayyum, rak'at, qira'at, dhikr, sama', sajjada, Qattal "the Slayer", Malang, darbar, roza, qawwali, langar, urs, idi, i'tikaf, Jami Masjid, Isma'ili, qawwal, qasba, haveli, Siraiki, gurdwara and Khizr. The translator's credit is also dropped. One dropped gloss carries a name. "Sufis of the Qadiri order, which traces its descent to Shaikh Abd al-Qadir Jilani of Baghdad" is a gloss of a household name, so it is not counted as an omission. However, the English uses it to set up the second Abd al-Qadir, عبدالقادر جیلانی ثانی. Restoring it is optional but recommended: "اوچ گیلانی قادری صوفیوں کا بھی مرکز تھا، جن کا سلسلہ بغداد کے شیخ عبدالقادر جیلانی سے جا ملتا ہے۔"
Names kept apart correctly: the three Baha al-Dins (شیخ بہاؤالدین زکریا، سید بہاؤالدین، قاضی بہاؤالدین); Jamal Khandan Ru and Jamal al-Din Khandan; Shihab al-Din Suhrawardi (شیخ) and the conqueror Shihab al-Din (فاتح); the uncle Muhammad Ghaus and the later Hazrat Muhammad Ghaus and Shah Muhammad Ghaus of the Qadiris; and Shaikh Ahmad and Ahmad Khattu, both as in the English.
Style (does not block):
a. "سلطان محمد کے جانشین فیروز شاہ تغلق ان کے عقیدت مند": here ان sits next to سلطان محمد. Better: "مخدوم جہانیاں کے عقیدت مند".
b. Thatta, "نے ان کے ذریعے صلح کی شرائط طے کرانی چاہیں": the nearest antecedent of ان is سلطان فیروز. The English has the same ambiguity, but naming the saint is safer: "نے مخدوم جہانیاں کے ذریعے صلح کی شرائط طے کرانی چاہیں".
c. "قاضی عبدالملک المعروف بہ شاہ اجل اور ان کے چھوٹے بھائی راجو قتال" reads as Abd al-Malik's brother. Better: "اور خود مخدوم جہانیاں کے چھوٹے بھائی راجو قتال".
d. "اور بولے کہ ان میں بھی برکت ہے": ان could mean the dates. Better: "اور بولے کہ گٹھلیوں میں بھی برکت ہے".
e. "اور اس کے لیے یہ شرط نہیں": better "اور اس کے لیے صحبت شرط نہیں".
f. "شیخ احمد سے کہا گیا کہ" is weaker than "urged". Better: "شیخ احمد پر زور دیا گیا کہ".
g. "شریعت کی اس قدر بے حرمتی" is stronger than "disregard of the holy law". "شریعت سے ایسی بے اعتنائی" is closer.
