2. "Elsewhere, at a shrine of Durga": کہیں اور، درگا کے ایک مندر میں, indefinite. Every "a shrine of Durga" is درگا کے ایک مندر (کسی مندر in the comparisons), never named and never this temple; "the shrine of Durga Mata" in the pilgrims' belief is درگا ماتا کے مندر, as in the English. "Shrine" is مندر for Durga's, استھان for Ram Thamman's (not called a temple or samadhi, which the English does not say), مزار for Behlol's, and درگاہیں (the generic word, not مزار) for the shrines "where people still practise Hinduism".
3. "temples in Kashmir were ascribed to Durga": کشمیر میں ایسے مندر تھے جو درگا سے منسوب کیے جاتے تھے, no "some" or count added.
4. "Kali Mata is an incarnation of Durga, and the temple at Hinglaj honours an incarnation of her": درگا is named in both clauses so that "her" cannot be read as Kali; not ایک اور اوتار, which would assert an incarnation other than Kali.
5. "idol temple": بت خانہ. "seeking news of himself and of an enemy": اپنا اور اپنے کسی دشمن کا حال جاننا چاہتا (not انجام, which would add "fate").
6. "it was said that the animal of the party in the right lived, and the other died": کہا جاتا تھا کہ جو فریق حق پر ہوتا، اس کا جانور زندہ بچ جاتا اور دوسرے کا مر جاتا.
7. Lehna: "before he became a devotee of Guru Nanak" as گرو نانک کا بھگت بننے سے پہلے; "had been a worshipper" as درگا دیوی کی پوجا کیا کرتے تھے (not پجاری, which also means a temple priest); both clauses under one روایت ہے; "now in Himachal Pradesh" as آج کے ہماچل پردیش میں واقع.
8. "Mother Mary takes the place of Mata Durga, and the shawl replaces the chunri": وہاں ماتا درگا کی جگہ ماں مریم ہیں اور چنری کی جگہ شال (ماں مریم keeps "Mother"; حضرت مریم would drop it).
9. "a practice that has been called a Muslim version of Navratri, in which the goddess is replaced by a saint": one sentence under کہا گیا ہے, so the "in which" clause stays inside the claim.
10. "It has been argued that X, and that Y": X under یہ رائے ظاہر کی گئی ہے, Y under اسی رائے کے مطابق, so neither stands in the narrator's voice.
11. "most are Hindus living in the regions of southern Punjab, inner Sindh and Balochistan": بیشتر وہ ہندو ہیں جو ... رہتے ہیں, restrictive, so it does not say all Hindus live there; "inner Sindh" as اندرونِ سندھ.
12. "both the lower and the upper castes are represented in proportion": اس برادری میں ... دونوں ذاتیں اپنے تناسب سے موجود ہیں ("in this community" inside the sentence so that نمائندگی is not read as political representation; no fact added).
13. "Followers of Guru Nanak who refused the Khalsa": a sentence of their own, not stated to be the Nanak Panthis, as in the English. "baptised Sikhs" as امرت دھاری سکھ, the usual Pakistani Urdu term.
14. "large armies rarely went by land, because of the jungle and the lack of water": خشکی کے راستے میں جنگل تھے اور پانی کی قلت تھی، اس لیے بڑے لشکر شاذ ہی اس راہ سے سفر کرتے تھے (جنگل اور پانی کی قلت would read as "a scarcity of jungle and water").
15. "after it came Dewal": برہمن آباد کے بعد دیول کا دور آیا. "a seat of rule" پایۂ تخت, "the seat of government" صدر مقام, "whose chief's seat" سردار کی گدی.
16. No causal or contrastive word the English lacks (no کیونکہ، چنانچہ، تاہم، ممکن ہے). The English's two "because" (Sheranwali; the armies) are اس لیے; "The rite makes ..." is اس رسم کی بدولت.
17. Every hedge kept, each opening the clause it governs: "by some estimates" بعض اندازوں کے مطابق; "by many accounts" بہت سوں کے بقول; "are said to" کہا جاتا ہے; "as one man put it" ایک شخص کے بقول; "tradition holds" روایت ہے; "has been called" کہا گیا ہے; "it was said" کہا جاتا تھا; "were told" بیان کی جاتی تھیں; "was held to be" مانا جاتا تھا; "held to be sacred" مقدس سمجھا جاتا ہے.
18. "a powerful emblem": توانا علامت, not the calque طاقتور علامت. The evaluations of the Overview and Legacy ("one of the most important", "great beauty and antiquity", "one of the richest", "a hopeful counterpoint") kept at the English's strength, with no intensifier added.
