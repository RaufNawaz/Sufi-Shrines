NOTES: Rulings on the writer's flagged choices. (a) Quoted English prose lines as Urdu prose in quotation marks: acceptable for the Asrar line, both Javid Nama lines and the Mustafa line, which are Persian in the original, and for the Phukan lines, which the Urdu frames as a rendering of his English. The one the writer calls the Shikwa line is in fact the Jawab-e-Shikwa line, and that poem is Urdu: a prose sentence in quotation marks after "وہیں انہوں نے کہا" will read to an Urdu reader as Iqbal's own wording, which it is not. Meaning is kept, so this is not an issue, but I recommend reported speech without quotation marks: "اور وہیں یہ دعا کی کہ مدتوں سے اندھیرے میں ڈوبی دنیا محمدؐ کے روشن نام سے منور ہو جائے۔" (b) فکری باپ for "ideological father" and نظریاتی بانی for "ideological founder": both faithful; the English itself varies father and founder, so the different nouns are right. (c) Four English book titles transliterated, others translated: acceptable. Transliteration suits titles of English publications, and for Gabriel's Wing it usefully avoids confusion with بالِ جبریل. (d) موری: not acceptable, see issue 1. (e) زمانِ مسلسل، یعنی "زنار": acceptable; زنار is precisely the infidel's girdle. (f) The "I died as mineral" paraphrase (میں جماد کی صورت میں مرا): acceptable and faithful. (g) روحانی طور پر از سرِ نو زندہ ہونے کی دعوت: acceptable; روحانی حیاتِ نو کی دعوت would be tighter. Other style points. The dream paragraph closes the روایت sentence after the first clause; the waking conviction still reads as part of the same story, but folding "اور جب آنکھ کھلی" into the روایت sentence would make the scope explicit. "household servant" is rendered only as جاں نثار خادم; گھریلو خادم keeps the shade. ہم نوا for "companion" (Jawab-e-Shikwa, Rumuz-e-Bekhudi) means concurring; ساتھی نظم or تکملہ fits better. "Persian mystic" as فارسی صوفی reads more naturally as فارسی گو صوفی. Heading "Homage and Remembrance" is only خراجِ عقیدت; خراجِ عقیدت اور یاد would match fully.
