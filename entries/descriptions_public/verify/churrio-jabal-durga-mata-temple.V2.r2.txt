Style only, none of it changes the meaning:
1. In "پھر لڑکے پکارتے ہیں" the Urdu names the boys as speakers of the second call. The English leaves the speaker implied, but the boys are the only reading, so this is clearer rather than added.
2. "برہمن آباد کے بعد دیول کا دور آیا" ("then came Dewal's era") is a fair Urdu reading of "after it came Dewal", in the sense of the next seat of rule. A plainer option is "اس کے بعد دیول (کی باری آئی)".
3. "حقارت کی نظر سے دیکھتے ہیں" is the standard Urdu idiom for "look down upon". "کمتر سمجھتے ہیں" would be slightly milder, if wanted.
4. In the Overview, the "still" of "still inhabits Tharparkar" comes in the next sentence (آج بھی) rather than with کثیر ہندو آبادی. The meaning is kept.
