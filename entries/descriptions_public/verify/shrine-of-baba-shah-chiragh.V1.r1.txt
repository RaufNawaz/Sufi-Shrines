1. (precision) The death date, ruling on judgement call 2. "The year that corresponds to the 1657 of the account above" is the team writer's own conversion, not a book's. Set beside a named day it says more than it can. 1068 AH runs from the autumn of 1657 into 1658, and the twenty second of Dhu al-Qa'da 1068 falls in 1658, as the writer's note says. Dropping the clause loses nothing: 1657 stays in the Overview as the established year, and the clause "Accounts of the date of his death differ" covers the gap. "Most often" still holds, since the day is given by both legible books and the year by Lahori, and the site's 1657 does not stand against it.
OLD: Accounts of the date of his death differ, but he is most often said to have died on the twenty second of Dhu al-Qa'da, the eleventh month of the Islamic year, in 1068 AH, the year that corresponds to 1657.
NEW: Accounts of the date of his death differ, but he is most often said to have died on the twenty second of Dhu al-Qa'da, the eleventh month of the Islamic year, in 1068 AH.

8. Omission sweep: after reading the team version through, nothing important is missing. The family is named: the father, the line to Muhammad Ghaus Uchi, the son Sayyid Mustafa, the sahibzada Zain al-Abidin, Langar Khan Baloch and Zakariya Khan's mother. The building, the graves, the mosque and the bequest are kept, along with the colonial history, the keepers, the land, the fair, the Thursday music, the namesakes, the setting and the legacy.
