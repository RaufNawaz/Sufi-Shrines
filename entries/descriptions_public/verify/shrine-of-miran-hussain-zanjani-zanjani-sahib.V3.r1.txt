- Overall the prose is clear, and the Legends and nineteenth century description paragraphs read well. It fails on one sentence whose postposition reverses its meaning (1), two glosses (2, 3), a misspelt name (4), and a run of calques and redundancies. None of the problems is a grammar or agreement error.
- Taste: "زنجانی ان دونوں میں بڑے مانے جاتے ہیں" is read first as "the greater of the two", and the next sentence (داتا صاحب کی شہرت کہیں زیادہ ہے) then seems to contradict it. If the source means the elder or senior, as "میرے بڑے پیر بھائی" later suggests, "عمر میں بڑے" or "پیش رو" would say so; for the coordinator to check against the source.
- Taste: "صاحبِ مزار" occurs eight times and "بزرگ" thirteen. In "داتا گنج بخش شہر پہنچے تو زنجانی کا جنازہ اٹھ رہا تھا؛ بزرگ رات ہی کو..." the bare بزرگ could for a moment be Data Sahib. Some instances could be زنجانی or وہ.
- Taste: the article repeats several facts across sections: the plain raised platform with no dome (Introduction and Shrine), the death in the night before Data Sahib arrived (Introduction and Legends, almost word for word), Sadr Diwan coming with Zanjani (Saint and Shrine), and the neighbourhood named after him (three times).
