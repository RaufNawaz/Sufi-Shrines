NOTES:
- Structural repetition, for an editor to decide rather than a mechanical fix: "پہلے گرو تھے جنہوں نے دو تلواریں باندھیں" appears almost word for word in the second paragraph and at the end of the درشن paragraph. "سکھ برادری کو عسکری رنگ دینے کا آغاز ... انہی نے کیا" appears in the second paragraph and again in the paragraph on the سپاہی سنت. The protected-heritage sentence appears in the introduction and again under ورثہ, and the closing paragraph repeats the nineteenth-century description. This is acceptable as intro and summary, but the two verbatim echoes inside the body stand out.
- چبوترے کے اوپر جنگلے والا ایک محرابی در: a در (doorway) that has walls with pictures and a vaulted ceiling is hard to picture. Check the source term (perhaps a دالان or a chamber) before publication. Not changed here because the right word cannot be settled from the Urdu alone.
- وہاں کے تھانے دار: for the keeper of a fort, قلعہ دار would be the expected word, unless the source means a thanedar. Check against the source.
- In the quotation in the ورثہ section, جو ان کی سیاسی قوت کا مرکز تھا: after ایک سکھ, the pronoun ان can be read as an honorific for that one ruler or as "the Sikhs'". Writing سکھوں کی would remove the ambiguity, if that is the intended sense.
- In the critics sentence, "ناقدین نے ... انحراف قرار دیا، اور وہ پہلے گرو تھے جنہوں نے ایک بھی بھجن نہیں لکھا" joins a judgement and a separate fact with اور. Two sentences would read more cleanly.
- گرو ارجن کی طرف سے نامزدگی کے بعد is journalistic. گرو ارجن کے نامزد کرنے کے بعد would be lighter.
- اس مقام پر گرو ہرگوبند کی یاد کے احترام میں: کی یاد میں alone is more natural.
- راجے and then راجاؤں in consecutive sentences: both forms are current, but راجے with راجوں, or راجا with راجاؤں, would be more consistent.
- پناہ گزینوں and مہاجرین are used for the same group in consecutive sentences, which is acceptable variation. In the second sentence the order "کئی گوردوارے اور مندر مہاجرین نے" (object first) would read more smoothly as "مہاجرین نے کئی گوردوارے اور مندر".
- چومالا/بھاٹی دروازہ: a slash is foreign to Urdu prose. Parentheses or یعنی would read better once it is clear whether these are two names for one site.
- ان کی رخصتی پر ماتم: رخصتی strongly suggests a bride's departure. ان کے جانے پر would avoid that association.
- گولڈن ٹیمپل is current in Pakistani Urdu, but دربار صاحب or ہرمندر صاحب would be the more native-register choice if house style permits.
