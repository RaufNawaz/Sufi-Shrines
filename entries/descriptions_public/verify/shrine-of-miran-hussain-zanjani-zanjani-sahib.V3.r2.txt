ISSUES:
1. [calque listed in the brief, introduced by change 11] "ان کے بھائی کا اپنا مزار بھی" renders "whose own shrine" as X کا اپنا. The brief, section "The Urdu version" (English possessives and parentheses), names exactly this as the calque to avoid ("X's own" is not X کا اپنا). The coordinator is right that the brief's own form خود X کا does not work here either: sentence-initial خود ان کے بھائی reads as "his own brother" and puts the stress on the kinship, not on the shrine. The plain Urdu answer is neither form: بھی already says that the brother has a shrine of his own nearby, distinct from the grave beside which Zanjani lies, so اپنا adds only the English shape. Meaning unchanged.
