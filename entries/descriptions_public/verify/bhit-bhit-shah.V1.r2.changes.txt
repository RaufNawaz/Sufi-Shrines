Like his Punjabi contemporaries and his successors in Sindh, he accepted the Hindus who were striving on the way to perfection as true pilgrims in the eternal Light.

## The Shah Jo Risalo

His poetry, gathered after his death as the *Shah Jo Risalo*, is organised into *surs*, chapters named for the musical modes, the *raags*, to which they are sung, and each name also points to the chapter's contents.
