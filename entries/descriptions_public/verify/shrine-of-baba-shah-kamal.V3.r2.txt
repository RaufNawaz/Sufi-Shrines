NOTES:
- Italic asterisks on everyday Urdu words (عرس، جذب، خانقاہ، کیکر، پختہ، مریدوں، ڈھول، قوالی، دھمال، کرامات) do nothing for an Urdu reader and are applied unevenly: ڈھول and قوالی are plain in the opening paragraph but italic under "عقیدت اور عرس"; *پختہ* is italic while the synonym پکی in the next sentence is not; *مریدوں* is italic only once. If the house style keeps them, apply them consistently. Asterisks are currently balanced (24).
- Heavy factual repetition across sections: the murid builders (three times), the fallen wall (four), the open south door (three), the three miles distance (three), the housing scheme name (three) and continuing burials (twice). The two nineteenth century architecture paragraphs largely duplicate each other. This cannot be cut under the no removal rule, but a reader will notice it.
- The "قائم کردہ" glosses for the Suhrawardi and Qadiri orders are textbookish for Urdu readers; left in place because they carry identifications.
- "شاہ کمال کو ان دوسرے بزرگوں سے خلط ملط نہیں کرنا چاہیے جو ان کے ہم نام تھے۔" would read more tightly as "شاہ کمال کو ان کے ہم نام دوسرے بزرگوں سے خلط ملط نہیں کرنا چاہیے۔"

- Script hygiene is clean: Urdu comma, full stop and semicolon throughout, Farsi yeh and heh goal used correctly, ۂ precomposed, no Latin letters, no dashes, Western digits only.
