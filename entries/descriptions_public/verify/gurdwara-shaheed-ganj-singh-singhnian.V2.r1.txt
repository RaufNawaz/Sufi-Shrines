NOTES:
- Writer's flag, "laid siege to the city" as "لاہور کا محاصرہ": not an issue. In the English, Mir Mannu meets Ahmad Shah near Gujrat "before Ahmad Shah could reach Lahore"; on the 1751 raid "the Afghan bypassed the governor and laid siege to the city", the city the governor had kept him from before, whose citizens were trapped "within the walls of their city". The English means Lahore. In Urdu a bare "شہر" would attach to "گجرات شہر" in the previous sentence and point to the wrong city, so naming Lahore renders the English and adds nothing.
- Writer's flag, "asked Qaiser to supervise": the Martyrs paragraph is correct (مؤرخ اقبال قیصر سے گوردوارے کی تعمیرِ نو کی نگرانی کرنے کو کہا گیا تھا). The Dispute paragraph is issue 1.
- Writer's flag, پنگھیڑا for Panghedha: acceptable. It is the same person, and romanised "dh" often stands for ڑ (compare pedha / پیڑا). The native spelling is not attested anywhere in the repository. If Rauf has the Gurmukhi form and it is ڈھ or دھ, use پنگھیڈھا or پنگھیدھا instead. Either way, keep it the same in any later mention.
