Rulings asked for:
"ان کے ایک مسلمان دوست": fails. See issue 1.
"پچاس برس کے عرصے میں": faithful. It says plainly what "over the course of fifty years" says, a span, and it no longer reads as a date.
"میاں میر داراشکوہ کے دادا پیر تھے۔" as its own sentence: faithful. In the English, "and Mian Mir was his dada murshid" is a coordinate clause with no hedge of its own, so a separate plain sentence carries the same certainty. It still follows directly on the sentence that gives Mullah Shah as the prince's guide "most often", and it states the relation that sentence implies, without V3's "اس رشتے سے". In the preceding sentence, انہیں (جنہوں نے انہیں قادری راہ پر ڈالا) is the prince.
"اسی روایت کے سبب سکھ زائرین کے دلوں میں آج بھی میاں میر کی یاد زندہ ہے" for "It continues to draw Sikh pilgrims to the saint's memory": acceptable. The cause is the same (اسی روایت کے سبب is the "It" of the English, the foundation stone tradition), the people are the same (Sikh pilgrims), the continuance is kept (آج بھی), and the object is the same (the saint's memory). The English does not say the pilgrims come to the shrine, only to his memory, so turning "draws ... to his memory" into "his memory lives in their hearts" drops no fact and adds none. It changes the attraction into a lasting state, which is a matter of idiom.

The other NEW sentences, each read in place against the English: same facts, names, numbers, dates, relationships and qualifiers, nothing added. Points checked specifically. میاں میر کی والدہ is V2 round 2 note (b). "ساری محنت کا مقصد" is "the whole labour ... is to". In "شہنشاہ اور ان کے بیٹوں", ان is the emperor: had it meant the subject وہ, Urdu would use اپنے. In "جسے وہ ... سمجھتے", وہ stays Mian Mir, since the emperor cannot be dissuaded from what he himself judged unjust. "شامل سمجھی جانے لگی تھی" gives "By the 1870s it was counted within" the perfect the brief asks for. ہمارا طریقہ inside his own words is Mian Mir's way, the "his way" of the English. "ایک اور شجرے" is "another lineage". In "قادری سلسلہ جب تک قائم ہے ... دوسروں کی رہنمائی کرتا رہے گا", the line is still the one that leads, under both conditions (V2 round 2 ruling 6). "جن کے مرشد نے انہیں" now settles "whose murshid" as Mir Inayatullah's, as the English does. "میاں میر چھاؤنی کی مغربی جانب" corrects only the gender. The Bahawalpur sentence keeps "modern", "senior murid" and "set at the shrine". "اپنے دادا پیر کی قبر" for "his master": no new relation, since the English itself says Mian Mir was his dada murshid, and the precise term keeps the sentence from clashing with "اپنے مرشد ملا شاہ" just after. "میاں میر کے مقبرے تک" is "to the saint's tomb". "میاں میر کا مقبرہ ... یہ مقبرہ بھی محفوظ نہ رہا" keeps the reference to his tomb. The comma in the fairs sentence changes nothing. "میاں میر اور شاہ ابو المعالی ہم عصر تھے" drops only the additive "too"; without بھی, the sentence can no longer be misread as making Data Sahib a contemporary. "صدر بازار میاں میر" is V2 round 2 note (a).
