1. Precision, optional (The Guru's First Lessons, paragraph 3). The English says "the learning of the schools was surpassed by the inward wisdom already present in the child". کے سامنے ہیچ ثابت ہوا says the learning proved worthless before that wisdom, which is stronger than "surpassed" and sits awkwardly with the Legacy, where the faith is said to prize both learning and its transcendence. سے پیچھے رہ گیا keeps the comparison without belittling the schooling.

2. Precision, optional (Nankana Sahib, paragraph 2). The English names all three: "a Guru Nanak High School, a Guru Nanak grocery store and a Guru Nanak cloth shop". In the Urdu اسی نام کی attaches clearly only to the grocery shop, so the cloth shop can be read as unnamed, and the sentence's point (the name everywhere) weakens. Putting both shops under one predicate carries the name to each.
