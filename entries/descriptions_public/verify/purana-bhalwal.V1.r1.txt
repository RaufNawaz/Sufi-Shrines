VERDICT: PASS

ISSUES: none

NOTES:
1. [implied chain] Master and Disciple, first paragraph: "The first of the Qadiri line to come to the Punjab is held to have been Hazrat Sayyid Muhammad Ghaus Uchchi Jili Gilani ..." -> Every fact is supported, but opening the section on Shah Sulaiman's master and disciple with this man invites a reader to take him as the head of Shah Sulaiman's own chain. The team version does not link them; it gives the passage only as context "Of the order the site names", in its Setting. No claim is made, so this is not a merged chain, but the placement suggests one. Suggested fix: open the sentence with "Of the Qadiri order in the Punjab, the first master to arrive is held to have been Hazrat Sayyid Muhammad Ghaus Uchchi Jili Gilani, ..." or move the paragraph to the end of The Setting.
2. [precision] "with the drum marking the metre more strongly than in Uttar Pradesh" -> The source says the drum's articulation of the metre is "more strongly accentual" (more strongly stressed), not that the drum marks it more strongly. Suggested fix: "with the drum stressing the beats of the metre more strongly than in Uttar Pradesh".
3. [precision] "above all ʿAbd al-Qadir Gilani" -> The source has "particularly". "Above all" is slightly stronger. Suggested fix: "especially ʿAbd al-Qadir Gilani".
4. [precision, minor] "a wheel of 360 pieces of wood carrying more than a hundred small pots" -> The source lists the wheel and the pots together ("a wheel of 360 pieces of wood and more than a hundred small pots turned by a pair of oxen"); "carrying" is an interpretation of the mechanism and is acceptable. The wells clause is safe under rule 5: the team notes show the uncertain word is in "آب یاری" (irrigation), not in "چاه" (well), so dropping "irrigation depended mostly on wells" and keeping the wheel was correct.
5. [for Rauf, not a fault] "Accounts of the family's caste differ." -> This is acceptable under rule 4 and avoids a side by side pair, but on its own it tells the reader very little. The notes already put the choice to Rauf: name Qureshi on basis (b), or leave it as it is. No change is needed for V1.
6. [checked, acceptable] "is said to have lived in the village of Bhalwal" rests on the editor's legible correction, not on the uncertain Bhelowal, and it is hedged. Two framings are needed to introduce the verse, as the brief requires: "In 1875 a book on the saints of the Punjab gave Shah Sulaiman a notice of his own" and "whom the book names as the author's son". Both match the Shah Jamal pilot's accepted framing and name no author. The 1870s and 1875 anchors rest on the firm 1292 AH / 1875 CE. Nothing from Tahqiqat-e Chishti is dated. "Which falls in the Hijri year 1012" is the team version's own statement.
7. [omissions checked] Nothing important is left out. Dropped: the author's 1065 AH (rejected variant), the Mochi and Qureshi castes (see note 5), Tahqiqat's "Haji Mahmud Shah" (minor variant), Sayyid Mubarak Haqqani (ambiguous referent), the uncertain doab name, the index lines and entry number (apparatus), and the Rozehnal and Ewing passage (general, and explicitly "not of this shrine"). Each is justified under the brief.
8. No em or en dashes in the prose. Hyphens appear only in established forms (al-Qadir, al-Din, open-handed, as in the team version).
