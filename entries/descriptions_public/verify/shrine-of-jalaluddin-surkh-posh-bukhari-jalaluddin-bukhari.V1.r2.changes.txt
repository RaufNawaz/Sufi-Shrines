## Legacy

Jalaluddin Surkh-Posh Bukhari holds a foundational place in the spiritual history of the Punjab and Sindh: the Bukhara-born saint who rooted the Suhrawardi way at Uch and fathered a dynasty of saints whose influence endured for centuries.
