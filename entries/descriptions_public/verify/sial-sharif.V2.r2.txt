NOTES: Style only. I checked every round 2 change against the English, and none of them changes a fact, a hedge or a reference.
- V2 r1 issue 1 is fixed. "سیال شریف میں واقع خانقاہ" now says where the khanqah is, and the khanqah is the subject of both "آگے بڑھایا" and "بزرگوں کی تربیت کی", as in the English "whose khanqah at Sial Sharif carried the tradition forward and trained saints". "ان بزرگوں" in the next sentence still points back to these saints.
- Qawwali paragraph. "the tunes of the two" is now "دونوں خطوں کی دھنیں", which names the right noun. "can" (جا سکتی ہیں), "mainly" (زیادہ تر) and "tend to" (بالعموم) are kept, and no "only" has been added.
- "is part of the Chishti revival" is now "حصہ تھا". The revival is historical, so the past tense keeps the classification and does not suggest the line stopped belonging to it.
- "most powerful" (سب سے زیادہ اثر و رسوخ رکھنے والے), "political leaders" (اہلِ اقتدار) and "wider spread" (بڑے پیمانے پر پھیلا) keep their meaning. "usually" (عام طور پر) and "principally" (زیادہ تر) are kept. "related to ... but in tension with them" is kept (تعلقات تو تھے، مگر ... کشیدگی بھی).
- Taunsvi sentence. "yet" (تاہم) and "even while" (حالانکہ ... بھی) are kept. "their Sikh neighbours" is the Hindus' neighbours (ہندوؤں کے ہمسایہ سکھوں), and "وہ" is Taunsvi.
- Sulaymaniyya. "مزارات کی خاطر ہونے والی لڑائیوں" correctly gives battles over shrines, so "تونسہ بھی ان مزارات میں سے ایک تھا" follows. "سے بھی عبارت ہے" leans slightly towards "marked by" where the English says "included". "بھی" and the closing "محض ایک حصہ" keep it partial, so this is not a change of certainty. "اصلاح پسند مسلمانوں" follows the English grammar: "reformers" describes only the Muslims.
- "More generally" stays a framing phrase (عمومی سطح پر بات کی جائے تو) and does not become a hedge. "particularly evident" is "زیادہ نمایاں". "ایک ایسے فقیر ... جو" now attaches the relative clause to the faqir only. "الگ واقع ہے" keeps "distinct from the city". "اب تک ... چلا آ رہا ہے" keeps "remains".
- Whole text. The six headings match the English one for one, and there is no bibliography. All numbers and periods match: 1799, 1883, 1184 ہجری and 1267 ہجری, plus the eighteenth, nineteenth, twentieth and early twenty-first centuries. Every qualifier is present: "accounts of the year of his death differ", "usually", "principally", "mainly", "tend to", "In many cases", "at least", "so far" (اس وقت تک) and "only part of its story". There is no verse. The dropped glosses and italics on familiar words are deliberate under the brief. خواجہ before معین الدین چشتی, حضرت, and ؐ after النبی are conventional honorifics.
- Minor points, none of them an issue. "سالکین یہاں کھنچے چلے آتے تھے" adds "here", which the paragraph's setting already implies. "ایسی محرابیں سکھ طرزِ تعمیر کی پہچان تھیں" turns "which marked them as Sikh" into a general hallmark, but the claim is the same.
