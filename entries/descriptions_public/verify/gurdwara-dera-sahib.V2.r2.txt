VERDICT: FAIL
ISSUES:
1. [qualifier dropped, claim widened] "Tegh Bahadur was the first Guru after Guru Arjan to compose hymns." | "گرو ارجن دیو کے بعد سب سے پہلے گرو تیغ بہادر نے بھجن کہے۔" -> the English limits the claim to the line of Gurus ("the first Guru"); the new Urdu says that after Guru Arjan the first person of all to compose hymns was Tegh Bahadur, which widens it to every composer (Sikh poets, bhagats) in between. The title گرو before تیغ بہادر is only his name, not the comparison class. The old wording ("بھجن کہنے والے پہلے گرو") kept the class. -> گروؤں میں گرو ارجن دیو کے بعد سب سے پہلے گرو تیغ بہادر نے بھجن کہے۔
NOTES:
Rulings on the writer's points:
1. "پانچویں گرو کی گدی کا" for "of the Fifth Guruship": accept. Throughout this text گدی is the seat of the Guru, that is, the Guruship, so "the fifth Guru's gaddi" carries "the Fifth Guruship" without adding anything. It is grammatical, where the old "پانچویں گرو گدی کا" was not, and it is better than V3's "پانچویں گرو کا", which would have dropped the Guruship.
2. "گرو ارجن دیو کی جانشینی پر ان کے بڑے بھائی پرتھی چند کے ساتھ کشمکش ہوئی": accept. "His succession was a contest with his elder brother" is rendered as a struggle with the brother over the succession. Same persons, same relationship, nothing added.
3. "ارجن کا لاہور جانا" for "a journey Arjan made to Lahore": accept. The ایک lost with it is only the English article, and the sentence still speaks of a single journey. It stays inside "سکھ روایت کے مطابق", as in the English.
4. گرو only after his accession: accept. In para 16 the English says "defended Arjan" and "Arjan admonished", but this is after Ram Das has made him Guru, and the nearest antecedents ("گرو ارجن دیو کی بدگوئی", "گرو کے دشمن") are the same man. The referent does not change. Making Prithi Chand the agent ("پھر بھی پرتھی چند نے انہیں ستانا نہ چھوڑا") is not an added fact: the admonition was to Prithi Chand, so "still the harassment went on" can only be his, and the next sentence goes on with his attack. Moving "چند افغانوں کو ساتھ لے کر" ahead of the name changes nothing.
5. "اپنے پیروکاروں میں سے بھائی بنو کو" for "one of his followers, Bhai Bannu": accept. It is exact, and it removes the "one of his brothers" misreading. "جلد بندی کے لیے" applies the round 1 note correctly; Bhai Bannu is no longer made to have the binding done by others.
6. Refusal and torture under one "سکھ روایت کے مطابق": accept. The writer is right: in the English, "Sikh tradition holds that" governs both "the Guru refused to pay or to alter a word" and "that ... he was subjected to days of torture". The single frame governs exactly that much. "جرمانہ ادا کرنے اور ... ایک لفظ بھی بدلنے سے انکار کر دیا" reads as refusing each, as "refused to pay or to alter a word" does. بھی renders the force of "a word" (not even one), so nothing is added.
7. "اسی روایت میں بیان کیا جاتا ہے کہ ... انہیں کھولتے پانی کا عذاب سہنا پڑا": accept. This is the round 1 note (b) applied correctly. All three torments now sit under both "it is recounted" and the Sikh tradition attribution, as in the English. "انہیں" correctly gives "made to endure" its subject, the Guru.
8. Baoli Sahib sentence: accept, and "instead" is kept. "اس جانب نہیں بلکہ ... کی طرف اشارہ کر سکتا ہے": "کر سکتا ہے" governs the whole "not this but that" phrase, so the hedge of "could point instead" survives. The sentence does not claim the well rules out Dera Sahib. "اس سمادھ کا محلِ وقوع تو ... سے مطابقت رکھتا ہے" is "Its position fits", and the concessive تو only marks the "but". The well is still in the samadh's enclosure, since "اس سمادھ" is now the subject of the sentence. Optional, for a reader who might take "اس جانب" as a physical direction: "اس جانب نہیں بلکہ" -> "ڈیرہ صاحب کے بجائے" (بجائے after a noun is idiomatic; V3 objected only to the pronoun pile up in "اس کے بجائے").
9. سمادھ and سمادھی kept apart: accept and keep. The English itself writes *samadh* in the nineteenth century description (paras 58 and 60, Ranjit Singh's included) and *samadhi* elsewhere (Ranjit Singh's in the Overview and para 56, and the Guru's in paras 56, 66 and 72). The Urdu follows the English term by term. Merging them, as V3 asked in its issue 18, would erase a distinction the English keeps on purpose. The plural سمادھیں for "samadhs" is consistent.
10. "سمادھی والے کمرے سے" for "from the samadhi": accept; this is not an added specification. In the English the samadhi is the velvet covered mound at the centre of the room, and a door cannot lead from a mound. "From the samadhi" is the English's own shorthand for the room, which it calls "the *samadhi* room" in para 72 (rendered there "سمادھی والے کمرے"). The Urdu simply spells out the English's meaning, with no new fact.
11. "اندر کی جو عمارت سامنے آتی تھی": accept. It is "Inside, beyond a last security check, the inner structure". سامنے آتی تھی only places the building in the visitor's view after the check, which the English sequence already implies. Same colour, same comparison with the wall.
12. "گرو ارجن دیو کے بعد سب سے پہلے گرو تیغ بہادر نے بھجن کہے": fails; see ISSUES 1. Keeping the title گرو is fine, but the comparison class "among the Gurus" has to be stated.
13. "اپنی جگہ چھوڑ چکا ہے" for the river "has moved": accept. The river has left its old place, and the colon clause that follows gives the change of course and the distance, as in the English. It also avoids the old بدل twice.

Other changed sentences checked (all faithful): "نہایت سنجیدگی اور احترام کا مقام" (optional: احترام slightly enlarges "profound solemnity" into a doublet, as وقار did before; "نہایت سنجیدگی کا مقام" would be exact); "سب سے زیادہ" for "above all"; "الگ صحیفہ اور الگ مرکز" for "its own scripture, its own centre"; "گرو کے کاموں کے لیے" for "to the cause of the Guru"; "سکھ برادری کی یہی ترقی", whose antecedent is the community's flourishing in para 7, as in the English "That very flourishing"; "جیٹھ کے مہینے میں، یعنی مئی کے آخر یا جون کے آغاز میں", now in the English order; the Chandu Shah sentence with "دلوائی تھیں", which is "whom he had used" and is still under کہا جاتا ہے; "سکھ مذہبی فنِ تعمیر"; "اس علاقے" for "quarter"; "گوردوارے کے اندر" for "Within"; "آباد عبادت گاہ" for "living house of worship"; "بھنگ گھوٹتے"; "ایسے یاتری زیادہ تر ... پٹھان سکھ خاندان", now correctly attached to the pilgrims without rooms; "پرچم کے پائے" (its = the Nishan Sahib, the flag); "آنکھیں چبوترے سے لگاتے"; "ان پر ستم ڈھانے والوں" for "his oppressors"; "سکھ حکمران کا راج"; "سکھ خال خال ہی ملتے تھے" for "it had become rare to meet a Sikh".
Round 1 V2 precision notes the writer applied, all rendered correctly: Masand ("گرو کے وہ نائب جمع کرتے تھے جو *مسند* کہلاتے تھے"); "پرتھی چند کے بیٹے مہربان"; "جلد بندی کے لیے"; "اسی روایت میں بیان کیا جاتا ہے کہ"; "مانا جاتا ہے کہ اس نے"; "کھنڈا سے دعا مانگتے"; "جو نام رنگ سے لکھے گئے ہیں"; "1606 میں ہونے والی گرو ارجن دیو کی شہادت".
