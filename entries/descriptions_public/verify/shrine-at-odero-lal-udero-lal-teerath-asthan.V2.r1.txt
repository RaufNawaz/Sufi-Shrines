NOTES:
1. Style, optional (Legacy, paragraph 2). The English "the Sindhi god of India above all others" makes Jhulelal the foremost god of the Sindhis in India. The Urdu سب سے بڑھ کر سندھیوں کے دیوتا most naturally reads as the adverbial "above all, the god of the Sindhis". That says nearly the same thing, but the comparison with other gods is weaker. Putting the superlative on the noun keeps it.
OLD: وہ بھارت میں سب سے بڑھ کر سندھیوں کے دیوتا اور سندھی ثقافت کی مجسم صورت قرار پائے۔
NEW: وہ بھارت میں سندھیوں کے سب سے بڑے دیوتا اور سندھی ثقافت کی مجسم صورت قرار پائے۔
2. Checked and found faithful: all seven headings correspond, with دربار for "The Sanctuary", the name the sanctuary has in both texts. Odero Lal and Udero Lal stay distinct as اوڈیرو لال and اڈیرو لال, as the dictionary spells them, and Nasarpur as ناصرپور matches the existing Urdu article. Every figure the English has (50, 1947, 1851, 2016) appears with the same meaning, and no other figure is added. There are no Latin letters and no em or en dashes. The hedges are kept: کہا جاتا ہے for each of the four "it is said" or "is said to", روایات مختلف ہیں for "accounts differ", غالباً twice for "probably", ممکن ہے ... رہا ہو for "may have belonged", زیادہ تر for "most often", تقریباً 50 for "about 50", عموماً and ہمیشہ for "usually" and "always", and کسی نامعلوم زمانے میں for "at an unknown date". The legends stay legends: the persecution story inside سندھی ہندوؤں کی روایت, the heavenly voice inside ایک داستان and کہا جاتا ہے, and the camel story inside حکایتیں and کہا جاتا ہے. The glosses dropped (the living saint, darbar, Sufi, dargah, gaddi nashin, sajjada nashin, samadhis, panchayats, unity of being, and the meaning of Sab ka malik ek hai) and the italics removed are allowed by the brief. Some sentences are split or reordered (Mirkh Shah's command, the Khizr gloss, the Cheti Chand clause, the three kinds of sacred place) but they say the same thing, and each ان resolves to the same person as in the English.
