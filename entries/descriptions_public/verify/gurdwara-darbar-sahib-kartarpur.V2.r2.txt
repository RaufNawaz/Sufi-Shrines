ISSUES: none
NOTES:
Rulings on the writer's points:
1. Janamsakhi paragraph split, with "انہی کہانیوں کے مطابق گرو کے والدین..." carrying its own frame: accepted. As ruled in round 1, the English presents the whole Abode paragraph after "The janamsakhis tell" as one telling, and the next paragraph confirms it with "In the same telling". The parents of the Guru and of Mardana coming to live there is therefore inside the English frame, so giving the split sentence its own انہی کہانیوں کے مطابق keeps the frame over exactly what the English frames and widens nothing. "His parents ... and so did the parents of Mardana, the singer of his verses" is rendered with both sets of parents kept apart and the relationships correct.
2. زائرین کا قصبہ for "pilgrim town": accepted. It is a faithful rendering and adds nothing. The rewritten comparison "اسی طرح ... جیسے اس سے پہلے تلونڈی اور سلطان پور" restores "as Talwandi and Sultanpur had before it" (round 1 note b, correctly applied).
3. "ایک وہ اولین لوگ جو" for "the original inhabitants, who had come and settled when the Guru first marked out the site": accepted. The relative clause defines the group exactly as the English does. It sits comfortably with the Legends section's "اولین آبادکاروں" for Moola Keer ("one of the earliest settlers"); the two phrases describe compatible groups and set up no contradiction.
4. Naming Lehna at "The Guru's successor came to him at Kartarpur" and merging the next sentence: accepted. The English identifies the successor as Bhai Lehna in the very next sentence, so naming him one sentence earlier adds no fact. In "گرو نانک کے جانشین بھائی لہنا ... ان کے پاس آئے تھے" the pronoun ان points to Guru Nanak, as "to him" does. The merged sentence "لہنا ذات کے کھتری تھے اور اُس وقت گرو نانک کے مرید بنے جب گرو کرتارپور میں بس چکے تھے" keeps "a Khatri" and "after the Guru had settled there" intact.
5. ذلت آمیز for "humiliating": accepted. It is the exact sense, better than the round 1 تحقیر آمیز and better than حقیر ("lowly"), which would have weakened the English.
6. The Brahmin story reordered: accepted. "ان میں سے ایک کہانی اُس برہمن کی ہے جو اُس جگہ آیا جہاں ڈیرا بن رہا تھا، اور اسی کہانی میں وہاں اس کی "رسوئی" کا ذکر ہے:" now follows the English order ("a Brahmin who came to the place where the dera was being built, and of his 'kitchen' there"), وہاں ties the kitchen to the dera site, and the colon keeps the digging, the bones and the Brahmin's relenting inside "One tells". The writer rightly did not adopt the round 1 V3 wording "اس کی رسوئی کے لیے", which would have added a causal link the English does not state. The Moola Keer sentence now carries a colon too, so "In another" governs the whole story (round 1 note c, correctly applied).
7. گلوکار مردانہ for "the singer Mardana": accepted. Same person, same role; "sang his verses and drew people into the net of faith" is rendered as two coordinated actions, as in the English.
8. "سرچشمۂ فیض" under کہا جاتا ہے: accepted. The frame "it is said" governs the whole clause in the English, and in the Urdu the comma plus اور keeps the second clause inside the کہ clause, so the inspiration is reported, not asserted. سرچشمۂ فیض carries a Sufi note of spiritual blessing, a shade warmer than "inspiration", but it is a recognised idiom for a source of spiritual inspiration and adds no new fact. One small precision point on the same sentence is given below (note b).
9. "مسلم فنِ تعمیر کی بہت سی خصوصیات ہیں، مثلاً اس کی سادہ محرابیں": accepted, and "many features" is in the English ("it had many features of Muslim architecture"). The English then adds that its arches were plain, "a feature of Muslim architecture"; مثلاً presents the plain arches as one instance of those Muslim features, which carries exactly that point without restating it, and the contrast with three cusped Sikh arches is kept. This also removes the round 1 پہچان ("hallmark"), which was stronger than "a feature" (round 1 note g, resolved). The complaint remains Inderjit's report of what Sikhs from England and Canada said.
10. "ایک زائر کو اپنی پچھلی آمد پر" for "On an earlier visit, one visitor had had to walk": accepted. It restores the sense of an earlier visit by the same visitor that round 1 note d asked for. See precision note c for an optional softening of "previous" to "an earlier".
11. "اس کے علاوہ سارا سال سوکھا" for "otherwise it was parched throughout the year": accepted. اس کے علاوہ reads here as "apart from that", which is what "otherwise" means; the "moreover" reading would contradict the preceding clause about the river flowing into India and back, so context steers the reader to the right sense. It adds no fact and no season, and it fixes the antecedent problem round 1 V3 raised with اس کے سوا.

Other changed sentences checked against the English, all faithful: صحیح معنوں میں for "in a real sense"; "سرحد کی بھارتی جانب سے کھیتوں کے پار" (round 1 note a, correctly applied: the domes are seen from the Indian side, not placed on it); the station "قریب ہی ... جو اسی استھان کے نام پر" (its name is the shrine's); the Jalandhar Kartarpur kept separate, with 15 and 1594 intact; "زندگی کے آخری برس گزارنے کے لیے" and "یہاں ان کا قیام کوئی اٹھارہ برس رہا" with "by many accounts" kept; ترکِ دنیا and گرہست for "the ascetic's road" and "householder"; مہا شِوراتری; "*پنگت* یعنی ایک ہی قطار"; "جو کوئی کام کر سکتا تھا وہ"; Duni Chand as "اس کے سب سے بڑے مددگاروں میں" (اس points to the ڈیرا, as "its" does); "جانشین چنا", supported by "in favour of" and the installation that follows; برہمی for "ire" (round 1 note 8, correctly applied); "جلانے یا دفنانے کے سوال پر ان کے ہندو اور مسلمان پیروکار", with ان now clearly the Guru's; "پھولوں میں سے اپنا اپنا حصہ", which makes explicit what the English share is of (only flowers remained) and adds nothing; "اور اس کے ساتھ سنگِ مرمر کی تختی" (round 1 note e, correctly applied); the grandfather "stopped near the border" by the village Muslims, now correctly attached to روک لیا; بیرونِ ملک مقیم سکھوں for "the Sikh diaspora"; the Partition sentence with Kartarpur and Dera Baba Nanak kept apart; *درشن* یعنی عقیدت بھری زیارت; "لوٹ جانے"; the flood belief, with اسے pointing to Nanak's shrine; the snake, with ان کے بقول keeping it the granthi's report; the doha gloss and لبِ لباب for "the sum of"; "یاترا کا جیتا جاگتا مرکز"; کھینچی گئی لکیر; "گرو سے عقیدت" and "ایک مسلمان مصور کے نزدیک وہ", with وہ pointing to the Guru; "عقیدت مندوں نے بعد میں وہاں جو گوردوارے بنائے". All numbers and hedges in the changed passages match.

Precision notes (optional; none is a fidelity fault):
a. Legacy: in "سکھوں کے لیے کرتارپور وہ مقام ہے جہاں ان کے گرو نے ... اور جہاں ان کا سفرِ حیات تمام ہوا" the first ان is the Sikhs, so the second ان could be read for a moment as theirs. Sense resolves it, but naming him is cleaner: "اور جہاں گرو کا سفرِ حیات تمام ہوا"
b. Farid: the English links the inspiration to the visit ("when Nanak visited his shrine"); the new comma plus اور makes it a plain sequence. Suggested: "کہا جاتا ہے کہ گرو نانک اپنے سفروں میں بابا فرید کے مزار پر گئے تو یہ بزرگ ان کے لیے سرچشمۂ فیض بن گئے۔"
c. "اپنی پچھلی آمد پر" is "on his previous visit"; the English "an earlier visit" is indefinite. If wanted: "ایک زائر کو اپنی ایک پچھلی آمد پر"
d. In "اپنے اسی سب سے وفادار اور جاں نثار مرید" سب سے can be read as governing جاں نثار too, which makes "devoted" a superlative. If wanted: "اپنے اسی جاں نثار اور سب سے وفادار مرید کو جانشین چنا"
