- ماخذ for "meanings" of the word Sufi: accepted. The colon list in the English is a list of derivations ("some derive it from"), and ماخذ reports exactly that content. صوف، صفِ اول، اہلِ صفہ and صفا name the English items (woollen garment, first rank, people of the Bench, the word for purity) and add no fact. The Urdu adds no شریف, no Islamabad, no owner and no library, so the claim stays "a single manuscript at Gulra".
