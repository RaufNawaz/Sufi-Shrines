NOTES: (1) I checked all thirteen changed sentences from ur.r2.changes.txt against the English. None of them changes a fact, a hedge or a reference. The birth-year hedge (اس بارے میں اقوال مختلف ہیں) now governs the year alone, as it does in the English. The Sialvi sentence keeps both "in the line of" (کے سلسلے سے وابستہ ہوئے) and "his direct pir" (جو ان کے براہِ راست پیر و مرشد تھے). جن in the Khilafat sentence refers to the caliph. خلفا is a faithful rendering of "a khalifa, one of his successors". قادیان سے ہونے والے تنازع keeps the English's "controversy with Qadian". "Among others" is kept as اور کبھی دوسرے شعرا کا. بدستور carries "remain prominent to this day". (2) Public Life, paragraph 2: تاریخ میں مہر علی شاہ ... کے طور پر سامنے آتے ہیں ("appears in history as") is slightly more passive than "took on the historical persona of". The English colon also links this sentence to "different roles", but the Urdu has دائرۂ کار (scope) where the English has roles. If the reviser wants a closer link, مشائخ کا کردار ایک دوسرے سے مختلف تھا۔ ... مہر علی شاہ نے تاریخ میں ایک ایسے صوفی پیر کا کردار ادا کیا جو ... would do it. Either way the meaning is not changed. (3) Public Life, paragraph 3: in انہیں یقین تھا the pronoun refers to حسن نظامی through subject continuity. It reads correctly, though مہر علی شاہ is the last name before it. (4) Learning, paragraph 3: "the saints" before the three names is not translated. The honorifics (شیخ، پیر، بابا) make clear who they are, so nothing is lost. (5) Learning, paragraph 1: بنیاد appears in two consecutive sentences (پختہ بنیاد ... کی بنیاد). (6) "was said to be" has become کہا جاتا ہے. This changes the tense of the hedge, not its force. (7) Headings match one to one. Digits match the English prose exactly (1859, 1882, 1916, 1937 twice, 24). There are no Latin letters, no dashes and no asterisks. There is no verse.
