- V3 r1 note 3 (repetition across sections of پشتو بولنے والی دنیا، تین صدیوں سے، poets and singers gathering, birth into the Mohmand tribe): each repeats a repetition in the English (Overview and Shrine; Overview and Legacy; Overview and Shrine; The Poet and Saint and The Setting). - V3 r1 note 6 (a linking phrase into the Nath yogi paragraph): the paragraph's first sentence already announces Peshawar's religious past; any link (for example 'one instance is') would add a claim the English does not make, and the only alternative, the gloss between commas, is the English parenthesis the brief forbids.
- V3 r1 note 8 (اینیولڈسن as اینےوولڈسن): a spelling of a foreign surname, chosen in round 1 for the Danish pronunciation and recorded there; the proposed form puts ے in medial position, which is not standard Urdu orthography. Taste, not error.
- V3 r1 note 9 (the quoted saying): the English itself uses two verbs ('Sow flowers', 'the thorns you plant'), so بوؤ and لگاؤ گے follow it; تمہارے گرد for تمہارے چاروں طرف is pure taste and does not remove the repeated تمہارا.
- V3 r1 note 10 (بیان کرنے کی کوشش کی suggests an attempt that may have failed): the English says 'also tried to express', so کوشش کی is faithful; removing it would change the English's own wording of the claim.
