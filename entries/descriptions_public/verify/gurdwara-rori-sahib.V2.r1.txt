NOTES:
1. Precision, optional (The Guru at Eminabad, paragraph 2). The English "the simple bread of Bhai Lalo's honest labour" carries "honest", the word the parable turns on. The Urdu بھائی لالو کی محنت کی سادہ روٹی drops it. The sentence before (حلال روزی کماتے تھے) and the sentence after (حلال کمائی) keep the contrast, so the meaning survives, but the qualifier is cheap to restore. سچی محنت avoids repeating حلال and دیانت دارانہ, which the same paragraph already uses.
OLD: بھائی لالو کی محنت کی سادہ روٹی
NEW: بھائی لالو کی سچی محنت کی سادہ روٹی
2. Precision, optional (The Gurdwara, paragraph 2). "Ribbed" means the dome has raised ribs. دھاری دار means striped, and on a white dome it can read as painted stripes. ابھری دھاریوں والا keeps the raised ribs.
OLD: سفید، دھاری دار گنبد
NEW: سفید، ابھری دھاریوں والا گنبد
3. Precision, optional (The Guru, paragraph 1). The English says "he would visit its religious shrines, Muslim, Hindu and of any other denomination". عبادت گاہیں means places of worship, such as mosques and temples, and a Sufi shrine is not one. Elsewhere the article renders "shrines" as مقدس مقامات (سکھ مقدس مقامات، دوسرے مقدس مقامات), so the same word fits here. The agreement in the خواہ clause then changes to masculine.
OLD: وہاں کی عبادت گاہوں پر ضرور حاضری دیتے، خواہ وہ مسلمانوں کی ہوں، ہندوؤں کی یا کسی بھی اور مذہب کی۔
NEW: وہاں کے مقدس مقامات پر ضرور حاضری دیتے، خواہ وہ مسلمانوں کے ہوں، ہندوؤں کے یا کسی بھی اور مذہب کے۔
4. Checked and found faithful. All eight headings correspond. گرو نانک for "The Guru" names the same person. The paragraph count matches in every section. Every figure the English has (1469, 1539, 1520 or 1521, 1801 to 1839, 1947 twice, 1992, 1521 in the verse) appears with the same meaning. So do the counted words (four journeys, more than twenty years, a week, two companions, two minor raids, a third time, three times, two plinths, two villages, seventy eight, the twentieth century). No other figure is added. There are no Latin letters and no em or en dashes, and the asterisks are balanced. The hedges are all kept: روایت ہے for each "tradition holds"; روایات مختلف ہیں or اقوال مختلف ہیں for all six "accounts differ" (the name, the number of journeys, the date and form, the state after Partition, where Mardana died, the renaming); عموماً and زیادہ تر for the two "most often"; کہا گیا ہے or کہا جاتا ہے for each "is said"; بیان کیا جاتا ہے for "it is told"; مانا جاتا ہے for "is held" (twice) and for "is believed"; بہت سے لوگ مانتے ہیں for "many hold"; بتایا جاتا ہے for "is reported"; اکثر and خاص طور پر for "often" and "most specifically"; کے آس پاس for "around"; سمجھا گیا for "was also taken to mark"; روایت کے مطابق for "in the tradition". The janamsakhi frames stay on the Saidpur journeys, Mardana and the millet roti, the meeting with Babur, Mardana's travels, and his rubab accompaniment. The Ghavindi, Panja Sahib and Baghdad episodes stay stories. In the Legacy, "is said" governs only the miracles and not the stays, as in the English. "Bhai Bala and Bhai Mardana, a Hindu and a Muslim" keeps the right order. "His own poetry" and "his parents" correctly resolve to Mardana. بابر وانی for "Babur Bani" is the spelling of the existing Urdu article for this row. Dropping "English" from "in English translation" is right, because the Urdu lines are not in English. The verse lines are English renderings in Latin script, and the Urdu italic renderings are faithful. The dropped glosses (gurdwara, charpoy, rubab, jathas, langar, Bhai as brother, roti, kalams) and the removed italics are deliberate, as the brief allows. The causal "for tradition holds" in the Overview survives through the order of the sentences and البتہ. Some sentences are split or reordered (Bhai Lalo, the rear room, the gateway, kirtan, aarti, bani) but say the same thing.
