1. My judgment of the writer's adaptation of V3 r2 issue 4 (there is nothing here to apply): the departure from V3's wording is justified and works. The English has two subjects ("Hindu pilgrims travel…", "devotees believe…") and states the belief generally ("by travelling to the shrine of Durga Mata"). 2. "ہنگلاج کے مندر میں بھی انہی کے ایک اوتار": grammatically, کالی ماتا could also be the referent.
