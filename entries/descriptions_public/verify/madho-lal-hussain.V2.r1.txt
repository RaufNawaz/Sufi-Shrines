Rulings on the flagged points.
(a) "Red Fort in Lahore" as لاہور کے لال قلعے: accepted. It renders the English exactly, and لاہور کے rules out Delhi's Red Fort. Changing it to شاہی قلعہ would add an identification the English does not make.
(b) "beside the friend whose name he bore" as اس دوست کے پہلو میں جس کے نام کے ساتھ ان کا نام ہمیشہ کے لیے جڑ گیا تھا (the file has اسی دوست): accepted. A literal "whose name he bore" would say Madho took Hussain's name, which contradicts the Madho Lal section (Madho kept his Hindu name; his name was added to Hussain's). The rendering keeps the intended sense that the two names are joined. ہمیشہ کے لیے echoes the Overview's "for ever" and adds no fact. It could be dropped to stay closer to the English, but that is optional.
(c) جو خود ان کی داڑھی کا تھا: faithful. The English "his very own beard" carries the same ambiguity, and خود ان کی follows ان کے سامنے (Hassu), so the sense is right. Still, a reader may take ان to mean Shah Hussain, the subject of the sentence, and the story only works if the hair is Hassu's (Hussain's beard is shaved clean). Recommended: "شاہ حسین نے خاموشی سے حسو کے سامنے ایک بال پیش کر دیا، جو خود حسو کی داڑھی کا تھا".
(d) Impaling as سیخ میں پرو دینا: accepted. It matches the verse's "spike" (تمہیں سیخ میں پرویا جائے گا) and the later "impaled to death" (سیخ میں پرو کر مار ڈالا گیا). It also avoids سولی, which readers take as hanging or crucifixion.
(e) The mother kafi with three italic lines: correct. The English gives three separate renderings, two of them introduced by their own sentences ("Word for word ...", "A gloss of the same two lines ..."). They are content of the English, not the one meaning paragraph the brief limits, so all three must stay. The harmonium singer's line is also kept. The three Urdu renderings are suitably distinct (کس سے کہوں / جدائی کی ٹیس / کرب).
(f) باغبان پورہ and شالیمار باغ: correct. They match urdu-dictionary.json ("Baghbanpura" and "Shalimar Gardens").
