NOTES:
- Both round 1 V2 issues are fixed. "بارہویں صدی کے بعد علم کی دنیا میں شاردا پیٹھ کی برتری ماند پڑتی گئی، اور یہ وہ زمانہ تھا جب کشمیر سیاسی اتھل پتھل کی لپیٹ میں تھا۔" now dates the fading, not the upheavals, and keeps the upheavals as the setting ("amid"). "اور یہ مذہبی رسوم اور زائچوں کی تحریر میں استعمال ہوتا ہے۔" no longer makes the use general or near exclusive.
- I checked the round 2 V3 changes for drift in meaning and found none. "شاردا پیٹھ کی نسبت سے دیوی کا نام ... کو بھی ملا" keeps the shrine as the source of the name. "وہ مقام ہے جہاں ستی کے جسم کا ایک حصہ زمین پر گرا تھا" keeps "a part", and the sentence still sits inside "اسی داستان کی رو سے", so it stays legend. "روایت ہے کہ شاردا میں گرنے والا حصہ دیوی کا دایاں ہاتھ تھا" keeps "tradition holds". "اس مقام سے ... ہستیوں کی یادیں وابستہ ہیں" keeps the hedge in "associated in memory" and adds no "popular". "گنا جاتا تھا" for "flourished as one of the foremost" adds no certainty. "قطب نما" keeps the guiding sense of "lodestar". "مذہبی رسوم کے ساتھ افتتاح" is a fair rendering of "consecrated". "جس کا مقصد ... پھر سے جوڑنا ہے" renders "seeking to rekindle".
- I also checked every fact and qualifier against the English. Each name, title, number, date and place is present with the same meaning: 2,000, 1947, 2023, sixth to twelfth centuries, eighteen seats, Neelum, Sharda, Harmukh, Kanchipuram, Varanasi, Kupwara and Kartarpur. So is each relationship and qualifier: "roughly" (تقریباً), "by Kashmiri tradition", "the legend common to all these seats", "tradition holds", "Tradition and later accounts", "associated in memory" and "long known as". Nothing is added. Every pronoun resolves to the right referent, including ان (the goddess), the script and the Pandit community for جس کے لیے. The five headings correspond. There is no verse.
- Dropping the gloss on the Line of Control is accepted under the brief, since an Urdu reader knows the term. So is dropping the italics on Saraswati and on the Sharada script. "سطحِ سمندر سے" only makes explicit what "at nearly 2,000 metres" means, so it is not an addition. "تقریباً" for "nearly" is acceptable.
- Style only: "کپواڑہ میں" would sit a little closer to "in Kupwara" than "کپواڑہ کے مقام پر". "بجا طور پر مستحق" is slightly softer than "so richly merits". "نگہداشت" for "preservation" is acceptable. None of these changes a fact.
- Mechanics: no Latin letters, no em or en dashes, 12 asterisks (balanced) and Western digits. The only figures are those in the English. The prose length ratio is about 0.98 of the English.
