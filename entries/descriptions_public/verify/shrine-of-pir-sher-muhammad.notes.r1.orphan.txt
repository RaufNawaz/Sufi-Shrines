**English glosses deliberately dropped** (familiar to an Urdu reader): *darbar* ("or shrine"); the Sufi path ("the mystical way of Islam", given as راہِ تصوف); *naat* ("poetry in praise of the Prophet"); *qawwali* ("the devotional music of the Sufis"); *langar* ("a free communal kitchen"); *ʿurs* ("the commemoration of the saint's death", given as سالانہ عرس); *faqirs* ("or mystics"); Hind ("or India"); *taʿwidh* ("amulets"); *jagir* ("a grant of land and its revenue"); *samadhi* ("or memorial"); Tableeghi Jamaat ("a movement of Muslim preaching"); *kafi* ("a Sufi lyric"); *qitʿa* ("a short poem", given as قطعہ); chronograms ("verses whose letters give a year", given as قطعاتِ تاریخ). Kept, because each is a statement rather than a word's gloss: the Qadiri order "founded by ʿAbd al-Qadir Gilani" (جس کی بنیاد شیخ عبدالقادر جیلانی نے رکھی); the Chishti order "a Sufi order prominent in South Asia" (جسے جنوبی ایشیا میں نمایاں مقام حاصل ہے); Guru Nanak "the founder of the Sikh faith" (سکھ مذہب کے بانی); Muhammad Ilyas "the founder of the Tableeghi Jamaat"; "the Persianate world, the culture of the Persian language", rendered once as فارسی زبان کی تہذیب. No italics in the prose: every term the English italicises is ordinary Urdu. The only asterisks are the four italic meaning paragraphs. Book titles (خزینۂ معرفت، ثمرات القدس، ہیر رانجھا) stand in quotation marks instead of italics.

**Verse:**
- The 1875 chronogram by Mufti Ghulam Safdar Lahori (two hemistichs, Urdu) is copied byte for byte from the English file. No meaning paragraph is added, because the verse is already Urdu and the English has no rendering after it. The introducing sentence says only that the chronogram is on the book's subject (جس میں کتاب کے موضوع کا ذکر ہے). The English apposition "the saints of the Punjab" is not repeated there, because it would paraphrase the couplet just before it is quoted; the subject is already in the sentence before (پنجاب کے اولیاء پر 1875 کی ایک مختصر کتاب).
- The four published English translations (Waris Shah twice, translated by Usborne; Bulleh Shah's *kafi*, translated by Shackle; Bulleh Shah's "Now hell has opened its bogs", translated by Rafat) are each given as one short italic Urdu paragraph with their meaning, with no translator credit and no Latin. Each introducing sentence is kept. "Panjab" in the Shackle and Rafat renderings is پنجاب. The slashes between Rafat's lines become commas in one Urdu sentence. Before Rafat's lines the English "Another of his poems runs:" is given as بلھے شاہ کی ایک اور نظم کا مضمون یوں ہے, because what follows is a rendering of the meaning, not the poem's words.
- The Dalip Singh *qitʿa* stays a prose sentence, as in the English (its verse is not reproduced there either); it is split into two Urdu sentences.

**Renderings a fidelity checker might query:**
1. "is remembered as having received knowledge from him": کے بارے میں مشہور ہے کہ انہوں نے پیر شیر محمد سے علم حاصل کیا تھا. "Pir Sher Muhammad is remembered for a life of prayer, austerity and service, and for the reforming, sober devotion he taught": پیر صاحب کی پہچان ... زندگی ہے، اور عقیدت کا وہ اصلاحی اور متین انداز بھی جو انہوں نے سکھایا. Neither uses یاد کیا جانا.
2. "Known for his piety, his learning and the great number of disciples he guided": تقویٰ، علم اور کثیر تعداد میں مریدوں کی رہنمائی ان کی وجہِ شہرت ہے (not کے لیے جانا جاتا ہے).
3. "one of the notable saints of the Punjab in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries": پیر شیر محمد کا شمار ... کے پنجاب کے نمایاں اولیاء میں ہوتا ہے, in the present, so that the period qualifies the saints, not the counting.
4. "settled at Uch, which he is said to have entered in the Hijri year 887": the settling is plain (اوچ میں آباد ہو گئے) and the year is a sentence of its own under the hedge (اوچ میں ان کی آمد کا سال 887 ہجری بتایا جاتا ہے), so that the hedge governs only the year and the order of events stays natural.
5. "the saint's line has continued both as its custodians and as respected religious figures": پیر شیر محمد کا خانوادہ بدستور قائم ہے، اور اس کے افراد دربار کے متولی بھی ہیں اور محترم دینی شخصیات بھی. خانوادہ was chosen because, like "line", it covers a family and a spiritual house; نسل would assert descent.
6. "kept by his successors": جانشینوں کے ذمے (not اولاد).
7. "through masters who were related to, but in tension with, local political leaders": جن کے مقامی سیاسی رہنماؤں سے روابط تو تھے، مگر ان سے کشیدگی بھی رہتی تھی (the English's own "but").
8. "chiefly through Khwaja Sulaiman Taunsvi, who consolidated the order by stressing learning through the founding of institutions, the academy at Taunsa and other religious schools": three sentences; یوں ("thereby") renders "by", and the academy and schools are given as the institutions (ان اداروں میں ... شامل تھے).
9. "like those of other shrines in the Punjab": a clause of its own (اور پنجاب کے دوسرے مزارات پر بھی یہی دستور تھا), not a parenthesis between commas; "still" as بیسویں صدی کے اواخر میں بھی; "had not changed since the thirteenth century" as تیرہویں صدی سے جوں کی توں چلی آ رہی تھی (not تب سے اب تک).
10. "his assistants" kept as ان کے معاونین, leaving open, as the English does, whose assistants they are.
11. "an earlier saint": ایک اور بزرگ ... جو پیر شیر محمد سے پہلے گزرے (the verb's plural agrees with بزرگ, so the relative cannot attach to قصبہ).
12. "a manuscript ... was held there in the possession of Maulana Nusrat Naushahi of Sharaqpur": شرقپور میں وہیں کے مولانا نصرت نوشاہی کے پاس. "a work dated to after the Hijri year 1017": 1017 ہجری کے بعد کی تصنیف قرار دی جاتی ہے.
13. "Rani Jindan, too": رانی جنداں نے بھی, the English's "too", with no added link to the shrine.
14. "by the 1880s Lahore was the capital of the province": a perfect, 1880 کی دہائی تک ... بن چکا تھا, as the brief directs; "a Punjab that the British had conquered in the 1840s": فتح کر چکے تھے.
15. "In the 1870s ... numbered in the thousands" and "In the 1870s it held the tomb": plain past (تھے، تھا), "in", not "by".
16. "religious practices that he argued had been corrupted": محمد الیاس کا کہنا تھا کہ ... بگڑ چکے ہیں, as a sentence of its own, with no causal connective (no اس لیے) between his view and his urging.
17. "In 1911 one writer on the saints of the western Punjab said of its riverain folk": مغربی پنجاب کے اولیاء پر لکھتے ہوئے ایک مصنف نے وہاں دریا کنارے بسنے والوں کے بارے میں کہا; the writer stays unnamed.
18. "remembered as one of the influential Sufi masters of modern Punjab, a guide of many thousands whose teaching joined ... and whose disciples carried his influence onward": گنے جاتے ہیں for "remembered as one of", and the appositive under ان کی شہرت ہزارہا لوگوں کے ایسے رہنما کی ہے, so that the "remembered" frame governs the guide clause too.
19. "In him two lineages were united": ان کی ذات میں دو روحانی نسبتیں یکجا تھیں (نسبت feminine: دوسری).
20. No exclusive added on a claim; the one ہی (شرقپور شریف کے قصبے کی پہچان ہی اس دربار سے ہے) renders the English "its very identity", and نوجوانی ہی سے and پہلے ہی are the idiomatic forms of "from his youth" and "before his time".
