NOTES:
1. (precision) Overview, second paragraph, and judgement call 5. The relative clause "to whom God gave both outward and inward wealth" comes straight after "Shah Mahmud Ghaznavi", so the nearest antecedent is Mahmud. In the team version God gave this wealth to Ayaz. The fix splits the sentence and names Ayaz as the one who received it. "It was said" frames what is a hagiographic statement. Judgement call 5 itself is accepted: he has an entry of his own in the 1875 book (1875 is a firm date), and the team version shows that book giving him a saint's honorific and an account of his renunciation and *faiz*. So "honoured among the saints ... in the nineteenth century" is supported.
OLD: He is also known simply as Malik Ayaz, and in the nineteenth century he was honoured among the saints as Shaikh Ayaz Lahori, the slave and beloved of Shah Mahmud Ghaznavi, to whom God gave both outward and inward wealth.
NEW: He is also known simply as Malik Ayaz. In the nineteenth century he was honoured among the saints as Shaikh Ayaz Lahori, the slave and beloved of Shah Mahmud Ghaznavi, and it was said of him that God had given him both outward and inward wealth.

2. (precision) The Restorer of Lahore, second paragraph. It opens "The city stayed unpeopled for some years." straight after a paragraph that ends with Ayaz settling the city again. It therefore reads as if the city stayed empty after he resettled it. The team version ties the empty years to the sack ("After Mahmud Ghaznavi's sack ... the city stayed unpeopled for some years").
OLD: The city stayed unpeopled for some years.
NEW: After Mahmud's sack, the city stayed unpeopled for some years.

3. (precision) Legends, judgement call 6. Keeping the two temple stories as separate legends in separate paragraphs is accepted. So is saying nothing about whether they are one episode. But "sets Ayaz's counsel at Somnath" presents the Somnath story as the same counsel moved to another place, that is, as a variant of the "Onkay temple" episode. The team version expressly does not say that ("The books do not say that the two are one episode"), and the two counsels differ in content.
OLD: Another story sets Ayaz's counsel at Somnath.
NEW: Another story tells of Ayaz's counsel at Somnath.

4. (precision) Legacy, judgement call 12. "In the history of the city" has two readings, and both fail. Read as a pointer to a book (the history of Lahore), it is veiled source narration. Read as plain history, it puts one historian's division of Lahore's history in the narrator's voice as fact, although the article itself records that the refounding tradition has been dismissed. The team version gives it only as that historian's framing ("makes his settlement of Lahore the turn from which the city's later history runs"). The fix frames it as a view, the way the draft already frames other views ("has been dismissed as", "has also been regarded as").
OLD: In the history of the city, his settlement of Lahore is the turn from which its later history runs.
NEW: His settlement of Lahore has also been taken as the turn from which the city's later history runs.

5. (precision) Legacy, first paragraph. The team version reports what "should be remembered", the shrine's statement of what deserves remembering. The draft's "what is remembered" turns that into a claim that the bond is actually remembered at the shrine. That claim also sits against the same article's report that residents are largely unaware of his life and that his fame has waned.
OLD: At the shrine, what is remembered is the bond between Mahmud Ghaznavi and Malik Ahmad Ayaz, "the love and romance" of king and slave: a slave who would sacrifice his life for his king, and a loyalty so complete that he could answer for it before God on the Day of Judgement.
NEW: At the shrine it is said that what should be remembered is the bond between Mahmud Ghaznavi and Malik Ahmad Ayaz, "the love and romance" of king and slave: a slave who would sacrifice his life for his king, and a loyalty so complete that he could answer for it before God on the Day of Judgement.

   - 2: 450 AH under basis (c), with "though accounts of the year differ", is accepted.    - 4: The team version records the offices as different descriptions, and none of them excludes the others.
