## Legacy

Where other shrines mark the Guru's teachings, his travels or the sacrifices of the martyrs, Bal Lila Sahib, with its field and its tank, commemorates that the founder of the faith was once a child at play on this ground, and that even then, in the telling of the Panth, the light within him was already shining.
