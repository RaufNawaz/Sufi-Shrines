NOTES:
Rulings on the writer's doubtful choices:
1. "تیسرے گرو امر داس کے نواسے": keep. The English does not state that Guru Ram Das married Guru Amar Das's daughter (neither the public nor the team English says so), so نواسا does narrow "grandson" to the maternal line. But Urdu forces a choice, and پوتا would be false: Ram Das was not Amar Das's son, so the only way Arjan can be Amar Das's grandson is through his mother (Bibi Bhani, Amar Das's daughter). Every fact the English gives (son of Ram Das, grandson of Amar Das, with Ram Das never called Amar Das's son) is consistent only with the maternal reading. This is the correct and unavoidable rendering, not an added relationship.
2. "مسند کہلانے والے گرو کے نائبین": the spelling مسند is right for the Masands, and کہلانے والے marks it as a name, so the "seat" sense is not a risk. The real risk is the parse "[مسند کہلانے والے گرو] کے نائبین", "the deputies of the Guru called Masand", because کہلانے والے sits directly before گرو. Precision fix, recommended: "اور یہ رقم مسند کہلانے والے گرو کے نائبین جمع کرتے تھے" -> "اور یہ رقم گرو کے وہ نائب جمع کرتے تھے جو *مسند* کہلاتے تھے". Italics are optional; they mark a term the Urdu reader may not know.
3. "پرتھی چند کے فرزند مہربان": the doubt is real. فرزند is Persian, and with the izafat zer left unwritten, "فرزندِ مہربان" ("kind son") is a natural Persianate reading. The referent stays the same person, but the name is lost. Fix: use بیٹے, which takes no izafat, so that مہربان can only be a name: "کہ پرتھی چند کے فرزند مہربان اپنا الگ صحیفہ" -> "کہ پرتھی چند کے بیٹے مہربان اپنا الگ صحیفہ". The "it is said" hedge (کہا جاتا ہے کہ) correctly governs the whole claim about the reasons and the rumour.
4. "گرو ارجن دیو کی برسی منانے کے لیے جمع ہوئے تھے" for "had gathered to commemorate it": acceptable. "It" is the martyrdom (or "his memory", the subject of the clause before the colon). A gathering on a particular day to commemorate a martyrdom is its anniversary, and the English itself speaks of "the anniversary of the Guru's martyrdom" in the Overview and in Devotion and Pilgrimage. So برسی adds nothing. If a closer wording is wanted: "گرو ارجن دیو کی برسی منانے کے لیے" -> "گرو ارجن دیو کی شہادت کی یاد منانے کے لیے".
5. "اس کا سنہری گنبد حضوری باغ کے اُس پار بادشاہی مسجد کے گنبدوں کا جواب ہے": acceptable. "کا جواب ہونا" carries the same figurative architectural sense as "answers" (a counterpart or match), and the faint "rival" sense in Urdu is also present in the English metaphor. "Gilded" as سنہری is fine (the English later calls it "a golden dome"). Optional and softer: "کا جواب ہے" -> "کو گویا جواب دیتا ہے".
6. "جہاں آج یہ گوردوارہ کھڑا ہے": not an addition. آج only renders the English present tense "stands" naturally (the earlier sentence is set in 1606), and the Setting section says plainly that the river has since moved. Keep it, or write "جہاں اب یہ گوردوارہ واقع ہے" if a lighter word is preferred.

Hedges in The Martyrdom of 1606, checked clause by clause:
(a) Manner of death. "Tradition holds that the Guru's end came in the River Ravi ... and that his body was never recovered" is "روایت ہے کہ گرو ارجن دیو کا انجام اسی دریا میں ہوا اور ان کا جسد کبھی نہ مل سکا": one frame over both clauses, as in English. "Accounts of the manner of his death differ" is kept ("اگرچہ ... روایات مختلف ہیں"; اگرچہ is a fair link for the English semicolon). "It is believed that he lost his life at the place" is "مانا جاتا ہے کہ": correct. "Guru Arjan died in 1606" is plain in both: correct, and the frame is not widened. "Accounts of the torments, and of where he was held and for how long, differ; ... most often said to have lasted five days" is fully kept ("زیادہ تر یہی بیان ہوتا ہے").
(b) The torture details. In English, "Sikh tradition holds that" runs on through the colon and covers the iron plate, the sand and the water as well, with "it is recounted" added. The Urdu ends the "سکھ روایت کے مطابق" sentence at "اذیتیں دی گئیں۔" and opens a new one with "بیان کیا جاتا ہے کہ", which covers all three torments. The details stay hedged, so certainty is unchanged, but they lose their explicit attribution to Sikh tradition. Recommended precision fix: "بیان کیا جاتا ہے کہ گرو کو گرم لوہے کی تختی پر بٹھایا گیا" -> "اسی روایت میں بیان کیا جاتا ہے کہ گرو کو گرم لوہے کی تختی پر بٹھایا گیا".
(c) Chandu Shah's role. The instigation is inside the "سکھ روایت کے مطابق" sentence, as in English. The facts that accounts differ over responsibility and over his name and office are kept. "Is traced to a refused marriage" is "کو بتایا جاتا ہے", and the colon carries it over the explanation, as in English. "Remembered as Chandu's ally" is "حلیف مانا جاتا ہے". The rosary is "روایت ہے کہ". His killing is "کہا جاتا ہے کہ", which covers the relative clause "whom he had used in tormenting the Guru", as the English hedge does. All correct.
(d) The river. "Which then flowed beside the fort" is moved out of the tradition sentence and stated plainly ("اُس زمانے میں دریائے راوی قلعے کے ساتھ بہتا تھا۔"). This is a non restrictive relative in the English, given in the narrator's voice, and The Setting states the same thing plainly ("the Ravi, which flowed next to the fort"). It is not a widened certainty. "The river beside which the Guru died has moved" is plain in both versions.

Other precision notes (optional, none is a fidelity fault):
- "to bind the Adi Granth": "جلد بندھوانے" is causative ("to have it bound"), which narrows what Bhai Bannu did. Neutral wording: "آدی گرنتھ کی جلد بندھوانے لاہور بھیجا" -> "آدی گرنتھ کی جلد بندی کے لیے لاہور بھیجا".
- "it is held to have hastened the development of the Khalsa": "اسی نے" is emphatic ("it alone/this very thing"). "مانا جاتا ہے کہ اسی نے" -> "مانا جاتا ہے کہ اس نے".
- "Men prayed to the Khanda": "کھنڈا کے سامنے دعا کرتے" is "before" rather than "to". It is a slight softening that a reader would not misread. Keep it, or write "کھنڈا سے دعا مانگتے".
- "painted after Guru Nanak's picture": "نقش کیے گئے" can mean engraved. "جو نام نقش کیے گئے ہیں" -> "جو نام رنگ سے لکھے گئے ہیں".
- Overview: "اس مقام کی یادگار ہے جو 1606 میں گرو ارجن دیو کی شہادت سے وابستہ ہے" lets 1606 attach to وابستہ ہے. Clearer: "جو 1606 میں ہونے والی گرو ارجن دیو کی شہادت سے وابستہ ہے".
- "only people carrying special cards made for them by the ETPB": "for them" is dropped. This is negligible: "کے بنائے ہوئے خصوصی کارڈ" already implies it.

Style: headings match one for one, nine sections. The three verse paragraphs are byte identical to the English. There are no Latin letters and no em or en dashes, and every figure the English gives is present. The glosses dropped (gaddi, kos, diwan, bhagat, shalok, kafi, darshan, mala, bhang, baradari, langar, langur) are of familiar words and are deliberate.
