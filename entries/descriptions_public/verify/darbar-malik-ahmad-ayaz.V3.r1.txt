Overall the prose is good: the Mahmud succession paragraph, the huma story and the Rumi paragraph read naturally, hedges are kept, and punctuation is clean (no Latin, no dashes, asterisks balanced, verse untouched). Taste and points to check against the source, not counted as issues:
