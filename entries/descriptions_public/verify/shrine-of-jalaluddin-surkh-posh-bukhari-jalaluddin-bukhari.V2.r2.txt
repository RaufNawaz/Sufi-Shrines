Rulings on the flagged points.
(1) "انیس جمادی الاول 690 ہجری مطابق 1291": accepted. "ہجری مطابق" plus a bare year is the standard Urdu way to give a year equivalence. With no day or month attached to 1291, it reads as the English "the year that corresponds to 1291" and does not claim that the day itself fell in a stated Gregorian month. The next sentence keeps "ان کے سالِ وفات کے بارے میں بیانات مختلف ہیں", and طویل عمر گزار کر renders "full of years".
(2) "ایک بار جب لشکر جودھپور کی طرف بڑھا تو": accepted. It renders "on one march toward Jodhpur" without naming whose army it was, as the English does. The paragraph opens with "اوچ لشکروں کی گزرگاہ پر واقع تھا", so the bare لشکر reads as an army and is not tied to Mirza Pir Muhammad. Making this a separate sentence matches the English semicolon.
(3) دین و معاشرت for "the religion and society of the region": accepted. دین و معاشرت is the usual pairing for religious and social life and keeps both nouns. The subject (his descendants, above all Makhdum Jahaniyan) is as in round 1.
