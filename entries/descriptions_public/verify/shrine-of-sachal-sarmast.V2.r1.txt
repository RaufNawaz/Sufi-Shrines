[precision, corrects meaning] The Shrine, paragraph 4: Nimano Faqir's own master was a Sakhi Qabul Muhammad of Daraza | خود نمانو فقیر کے مرشد دڑازہ کے سخی قبول محمد تھے۔ -> the English "a" deliberately marks this man as one Sakhi Qabul Muhammad among several (the notes keep him apart from the early twenty first century sajjada nashin Sakhi Qabul Muhammad Faruqi, named two sentences later, and from the 2002 author). Without ایک the Urdu reads as a known, definite figure and invites the reader to take him for the Faruqi of the next sentences. Restore the indefinite, as the Urdu already does in the Radha Krishin Lal paragraph (دڑازہ کے ایک مرشد سخی قبول محمد).

[precision, ambiguous reading] The Poetry, Bedil's line: The ordinary men read Kanz Quduri, the lovers wisdom from God | عام لوگ کنز قدوری پڑھتے ہیں، عاشق خدا سے حکمت -> with the verb elided, عاشق خدا can be read as the izafat عاشقِ خدا ("the lover of God"), which breaks the contrast between the two groups.
