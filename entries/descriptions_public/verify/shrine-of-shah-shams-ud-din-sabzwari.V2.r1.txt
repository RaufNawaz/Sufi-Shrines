(1) "One devotee of the Tableeghi Jamaat" | "تبلیغی جماعت کے ایک پُرجوش پیروکار": پُرجوش is a fair reading of "devotee" and not counted as an addition, but a plainer عقیدت مند پیروکار or simply پیروکار would sit closer to the English without characterising the man. (2) "An Indian tradition holds" | "ہندوستان میں ایک روایت چلی آتی ہے": same meaning; fine as written. (3) Dropped glosses (pir, daʿwa, darbars, Fatiha, ʿurs, qawwali, langar, shirk, Tableeghi Jamaat) and removed italics are of terms familiar to an Urdu reader, per the brief, and are not omissions. Headings correspond one to one; no verse in the article; all figures (711, 1175) and centuries match; every hedge ("it is said", "it is told", "tradition holds", "it was said", "not settled", "no settled connection", "open question") is carried with the same scope.
