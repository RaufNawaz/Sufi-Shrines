NOTES: The article now reads fluently and idiomatically almost throughout, and the round 1 fixes sit well. The writer's own wordings read naturally on fluency grounds: "معافی چاہتے ہوئے" is fine; "بی بیوں کے مرقد کی دیوار" is fine; the convert prince in the singular in the narrative and in the honorific plural once he is the keepers' ancestor reads as a natural shift, and each section is internally consistent. "اس کی ایک اہم تاریخی کتاب" is understandable, but "اس کی" makes the روایت the owner of the book; "اس روایت پر ایک اہم تاریخی کتاب" would be tighter. Taste items: "ایک کرامت کے ذریعے بے آبروئی پر موت کو ترجیح دی" makes the miracle the means of the preference rather than of the death, though readers will follow it; "قدیم قلعہ" and "قدیم ترین" in successive sentences of the opening;
