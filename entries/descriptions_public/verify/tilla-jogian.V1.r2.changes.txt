Changes applied by the coordinator, exact replacements, each OLD occurring once.

1. V1r1 1

NEW: The Salt Range, on whose eastern heights the hill stands, is the line of mountains dividing the plains of the Punjab from the slightly elevated plateau of the Potwar. There is no irrigation in the range.

2. V1r1 2
OLD: The 'unstruck music' is the mystical sound heard at the climax of the yogic process, the 'circle of nectar' is the ecstatic state that is the goal of yoga, and the 'tenth door' is the hidden aperture in the skull that is opened at that climax.
NEW: The 'unstruck music' is the mystical sound heard at the climax of the yogic process, the 'circle of nectar' is a traditional image for the ecstatic state that is the goal of yoga, and the 'tenth door' is the hidden aperture in the skull that is opened at that climax.
