VERDICT: PASS
ISSUES: none
NOTES:
Rulings on the writer's points:
1. "ان کہانیوں کی بدولت زمین کا یہ ٹکڑا *پنتھ* یعنی سکھ برادری کے مقدس مقامات میں شمار ہونے لگا، اور اس کی تعظیم میں یہاں ایک استھان تعمیر ہوا": correct. The English actually reads "These stories fixed this patch of ground in the sacred geography of the *Panth*, the Sikh community, and a shrine rose to honour it." "came to be counted among the sacred places of the Panth" is the plain sense of "fixed in the sacred geography"; the causal link (کی بدولت) is the English's own ("These stories fixed"). On the building, "a shrine rose to honour it" is rendered as "اس کی تعظیم میں یہاں ایک استھان تعمیر ہوا": same fact, no date, builder or certainty added, and the shrine clause stays coordinated with اور rather than made a consequence of the stories, as in the English. The sentence stays inside the "Tradition holds" passage exactly as before.
2. "روایتاً موجودہ عمارت کو اٹھارہویں صدی کے وسط کی بتایا جاتا ہے اور اسے دیوان کورا مل کی فیاضی سے منسوب کیا جاتا ہے": correct. The English "traditionally associated with the middle of the eighteenth century and the munificence of Diwan Kaura Mal" hedges both. Sentence initial روایتاً scopes over the whole coordinated predicate, and each clause is in any case itself a reported passive (بتایا جاتا ہے، منسوب کیا جاتا ہے), so neither the date nor the patron is stated as fact. اسے points to the building. The zeugma V3 flagged is gone. "کی بتایا جاتا ہے" for "associated with" amounts to a traditional dating, which is what the English means.
3. "خاندان یہاں 1965 اور 1971 کی جنگوں کے بعد پیدا ہونے والی بیگانگی کے دور میں بھی آئے اور 1992 میں بابری مسجد کے انہدام کے بعد بھی": acceptable. Both causes and all three dates are there. The writer rightly did not take V3's "دو مرحلوں میں", which would have added a count of waves the English does not give; بھی ... بھی says only that families came in both settings, as "following ... and ..." does. "پیدا ہونے والی" is the natural Urdu for "the alienation after the wars" and adds no fact. "کے دور میں" (during the period of) for "following" (in the wake of) is a slight shift but it fits a lasting condition; not a fault.
4. "اور کہا کہ لوگوں میں مقام صرف اور صرف ہمارے اعمال سے بنتا ہے": correct, and better for fidelity than V3's "اپنے" would have been, because ہمارے keeps the quoted words "our actions and our actions alone". "لوگوں میں مقام" covers "his position among men"; the generic "a man" is implied by the reported speech without changing the claim. Still inside "in the same stories" (انہی کہانیوں کے مطابق).
5. "نانک پاکپتن سے ہو کر ایک بار پھر تلونڈی آئے": acceptable. "سے ہو کر" leans slightly towards "by way of Pakpattan", where the English "after Pak Pattan" gives only the order; but the English sequence also implies he came on from there, so no new fact of substance. The exact form, if the writer wants it, is V3's "پاکپتن کے سفر کے بعد نانک ایک بار پھر تلونڈی آئے". The frame انہی کہانیوں کے مطابق still governs the whole sentence, the semicolon clause included, as "In the same stories" does.
6. "مہتا کالو بھٹی خاندان کے حساب نویس تھے اور رائے بلار کے ماتحت کام کرتے تھے": correct. The English relation is "the accountant of the Bhatti family under Rai Bular": he keeps the accounts of the Bhatti family, and he is under Rai Bular (its head). The new wording keeps both and removes the misreading V3 flagged (the family being under Rai Bular). "کام کرتے تھے" adds no fact.
7. "رائے بھوئے کے حساب نویس بیدی خاندان کے سربراہ شو رام تھے": correct. Shiv Ram, head of a Bedi family, was accountant to Rai Bhoe; Shiv Ram and Rai Bhoe are now clearly two people. Still under اسی بیان کے مطابق, as "In the same telling" frames it. Precision note only: the English says "a Bedi family", and without a comma the run "رائے بھوئے کے حساب نویس بیدی خاندان" could, on a hard reading, make the family the accountants. If the writer wants to remove that possibility: "اور رائے بھوئے کے حساب نویس شو رام تھے، جو ایک بیدی خاندان کے سربراہ تھے؛". Not a fault.
8. "بیمار اور نڈھال": correct. It was V2 round 1's own suggested shorter form for "sick and failing"; نڈھال (enfeebled, sinking) is the right strength, and the stronger قریب المرگ is gone.
9. "سفید پروں": correct, as V2 round 1 recommended for "white feathers".

Other changed sentences checked against the English:
"یوں نانک نے صوفیانہ فکر سے گہری واقفیت حاصل کی": the pronoun fix is right, since نانک now names the person who gained the knowledge. Precision note only: یوں (thus, in this way) makes explicit a causal link that the English only implies by its "and"; to match exactly, drop it: "اور نانک نے صوفیانہ فکر سے گہری واقفیت حاصل کی". Not a fault, since the English juxtaposition plainly invites that reading.
"اصلاح پسندوں کے ایک پُرامن جتھے": correct for "a peaceful body of reformers".
"ایک موقع پر انہوں نے یہ بھی بتایا": انہوں can only be Surjeet, the subject of the clause before it; "he explained" is kept.
"رُت بدلنے کے موقع پر منایا جاتا ہے": correct for "commemorates the change of season".
"آنے جانے والے مسافروں اور مہمانوں کی خدمت": correct for "those who come and go, the traveller and the guest".
"تلونڈی کا گاؤں راوی اور چناب کے درمیان": correct; dropping دریاؤں loses nothing, since راوی and چناب are unambiguous, and the comma removes the "between the rivers and Delhi" misreading.
"بچپن میں کبھی اسی زمین پر کھیلا کرتے تھے" and "گرو نانک کے ابتدائی زمانے سے وابستہ مقامات کا سلسلہ مکمل ہو جاتا ہے": correct for "was once a child at play on this ground" and "completes the geography of Guru Nanak's beginnings". The frame "پنتھ کی روایت کے مطابق" still governs only the inner light, as "in the telling of the Panth" does.

Style: no Latin letters, no em or en dashes, Western digits only in the changed lines.
