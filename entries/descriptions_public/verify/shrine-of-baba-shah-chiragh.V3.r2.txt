1. Round 1 V3 issues: all five are fixed. Issue 1 (دادا ... نام لیا جاتا ہے calque) now reads دادا کا نام زیادہ تر ... بتایا جاتا ہے, natural. Issue 5 (باقی and حصہ echoing) is fixed: the land is the subject and تھوڑی ہی رہ گئی تھی agrees in the feminine with the understood زمین.

6. Change 7: عمارت and تعمیر sit close together in one clause, but they are different words and the clause reads smoothly.
7. No changed sentence has Latin letters, em or en dashes, or a new ambiguity, and each fits the sentences around it.
