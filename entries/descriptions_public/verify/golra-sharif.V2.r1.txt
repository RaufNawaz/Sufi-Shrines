ISSUES: none
NOTES: (1) The Saint, paragraph 1: "a Sayyid family ... descended from the Prophet Muhammad" is rendered سادات کے ایک ایسے گھرانے without the descent clause. This counts as a dropped gloss of a word familiar to Urdu readers (سید/سادات), not an omission. (2) Learning and Poetry, paragraph 1: "the loved poetry of the tradition" becomes صوفیانہ روایت کی محبوب شاعری. In context this is a fair reading, but plain اس روایت would follow the English more closely. (3) Book titles سیف چشتیائی and البابِ ثلاثہ are not italicised, unlike the English. This is consistent with the brief and does not change the meaning. (4) Headings match one to one: تعارف، صاحبِ مزار، عوامی زندگی اور مریدین، علم اور شاعری، مزار، ورثہ. All dates (1859, 1882, 1916, 1937, 24) and hedges ("accounts of the year differ" اقوال مختلف ہیں; "was said to be" کہا جاتا ہے; "is traced as far as" رشتہ ... جوڑا جاتا ہے; "are recorded there" درج ہے) are carried over. There is no verse.
