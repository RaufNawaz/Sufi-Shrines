Rulings on the consensus choices:
- Hanuman's standing beside Rama: accepted. "devoted servant" is the site's account, and the two mystical readings are rightly placed as readings, not as rivals to "servant".
- The god's name: accepted.
- Pakistan's Hindus: accepted. "between 6 and 10 million, though estimates vary widely" carries the source's own caveat. "most of them belong to underprivileged tribes and castes" is the true common ground of "over 90 percent" (Pakistan) and "a majority" (Sindh, scheduled castes).
- Partition migrants: accepted. "a kind of consensus that they numbered around 1 million" supports "most often put at around a million". There is one clause that accounts differ, the lower reckoning is not set beside it, and the sentence reports the figure as the one most often given, not as fact.

It is consistent with the Karachi riots, the Rochaldas narrative and the 1992 attacks, and Sorley's "rapprochement" concerns mystical ideas, not the absence of violence.

- Time anchors: accepted, except N2.
