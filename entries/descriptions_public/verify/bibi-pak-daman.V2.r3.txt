Rulings asked for:
"وقار کا احساس" for "dignity": faithful. "A place of comfort and of dignity" is a place where women find both, and وقار کا احساس is how Urdu says dignity found or felt. It is closer to the English than round 2's عزتِ نفس (self respect).
"ظلم کے آگے سر جھکا کر عزت نہ گنوائی جائے": faithful, and better than round 2. "A refusal to bow before injustice at the cost of one's honour" means not losing one's honour by bowing to injustice, which is exactly what this says. Round 2's order (عزت گنوا کر ظلم کے آگے سر نہ جھکایا جائے) could be read the other way round. عزم for "refusal" and ظلم for "injustice" were accepted in earlier rounds.
"ان میں سے ہر ایک کے دور میں": faithful. Its antecedent is "یکے بعد دیگرے آنے والے حکمران", the rulers in succession, so "each of them" is the English "every ruler", and the old land is still continued under all of them.
