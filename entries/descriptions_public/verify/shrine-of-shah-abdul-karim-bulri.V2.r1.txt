(1) The Shrine, paragraph 2: "1940 تک صاحبِ مزار کا ذکر مشہور سیّد عبدالکریم کے نام سے ہونے لگا تھا" uses the inceptive "ہونے لگا تھا" ("had begun to be spoken of"). It is a fair rendering of "By 1940 he was spoken of as". A plain perfect ("... کے نام سے معروف ہو چکے تھے") would follow the brief's pattern for "by" more closely. (2) The Saint, paragraph 4: "اس بنا پر بھی کہ" makes the lack of parallels a stated ground for setting the reading aside. That matches the English, where "with no parallels" is part of the same judgement, but a plain "اور" would sit closer to it. (3) Second verse rendering: "پنہارن کا سنبھلا ہوا گھڑا" keeps the jar, the carrier and "poised" but drops the preposition "on". It is acceptable as it stands.
