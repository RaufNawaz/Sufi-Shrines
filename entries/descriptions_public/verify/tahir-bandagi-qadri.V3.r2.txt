[new ambiguity, change 9] "سلسلۂ قادریہ کے وابستگان میں ہوتا ہے، اور اس سلسلے میں وہ اپنے زمانے کے قطب مانے جاتے ہیں" -> In Urdu prose, اس سلسلے میں is first of all the stock idiom for "in this regard / in this connection". Because سلسلہ has just been used in its Sufi sense, the clause now reads two ways: "in this order he is the qutb of his age" and "in this regard he is held to be the qutb of his age".
