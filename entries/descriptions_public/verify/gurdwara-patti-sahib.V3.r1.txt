- Repetition across sections (structural, fixing would remove sentences, so left as taste): پٹی as a wooden slate is explained in the introduction and again at the start of گرو کے پہلے سبق; "گرو سے وابستہ بڑے یادگاری دنوں پر زائرین ... آتے ہیں" appears in the introduction and again at the start of عقیدت اور تہوار; Talwandi's renaming as Nankana Sahib is stated under گرو نانک and again under ننکانہ صاحب; ساکھیاں is glossed in the introduction and جنم ساکھیاں glossed again later; the Aurangzeb-era followers' "worship" is described twice (service to travellers, then recitation), and the two statements sit uneasily together.
