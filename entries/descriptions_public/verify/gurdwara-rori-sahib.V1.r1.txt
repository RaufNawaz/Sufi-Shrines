"The Muslim minstrel" (the site's) and Khalid's "Muslim companion" already give what is certain.
   Candidate (b), Rizvi's return from eastern India about 1520: do not restore. Rizvi's return from the east would put a second, incompatible sequence beside it. It is not important to this site, and the site's "around 1520 or 1521" already carries the time.

Minor points (precision fixes in exact wording, to apply):
(a) Paragraph 21 (the Lalo paragraph). "Mardana has his own part in the janamsakhi telling of the story." Coming after "In the best loved telling", this reads as a second telling set beside the first. Replace with: "In the janamsakhis, Mardana has his own part in the story." In the same paragraph, "The Guru's teaching that there was no Hindu and no Muslim was questioned at the court of Nawab Daulat Khan Lodhi at Sultanpur," is an episode of the Guru's life in the narrator's voice, inside a paragraph about the janamsakhis. Replace with: "The Guru's teaching that there was no Hindu and no Muslim is said to have been questioned at the court of Nawab Daulat Khan Lodhi at Sultanpur,".
(b) Paragraph 47 (Mardana in the janamsakhis). Replace "Turned away from house to house in an inhospitable village, he asks why two villages received opposite blessings." with "In one of them, turned away from house to house in an inhospitable village, he asks why two villages received opposite blessings." Replace "His parents settled at Kartarpur, and he and Nanak spent a lifetime on the road." with "In the same stories, his parents settled at Kartarpur, and he and Nanak spent a lifetime on the road."
(c) Paragraph 31. Replace "whose patronage adorned so many of the sites connected with the Gurus, though accounts of when it was built differ." with "whose patronage adorned so many of the sites connected with the Gurus, though accounts of its date and its form differ." The site's several storeys and dome are disputed along with the date, and this stays one clause naming no rival.
(d) The rubab gloss comes after the term's first use. Replace "In the janamsakhis, Mardana, the *rubab* player, set out with Nanak on his travels." with "In the janamsakhis, Mardana, who played the *rubab*, a traditional stringed instrument, set out with Nanak on his travels." Then, in paragraph 55, replace "take up his *rubab*, a traditional stringed instrument, and provide" with "take up his *rubab* and provide".
(e) Paragraph 45: "several non-Sikh saints" uses the quotation's hyphenated word outside quotation marks. Replace "which contains the poetry of several non-Sikh saints, among them" with "which contains the poetry of several saints who were not Sikhs, among them".
(f) Optional. "*kirtan*, the singing of hymns in the gurdwaras" ties the gloss to this setting. "*kirtan*, the devotional singing of hymns" is the plain dictionary sense. "Monolithic" in the Setting is descriptive enough to stay.
