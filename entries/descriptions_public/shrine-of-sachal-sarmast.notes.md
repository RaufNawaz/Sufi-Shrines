## Consensus choices

- **Death year: 1826**, "Accounts of the year of his death differ; it is most often given as 1826", and "(1739 to 1826)" in the Overview. Basis (b): the site's 1827 is (a), but every other source that gives a year is against it (Schimmel in three books, and Rafat, all 1826; Sorley gives none), so (a) gives way, as on shrine-of-mauj-darya-bukhari. - **Birth year: 1739**, no clause. Basis (a) and (b): the site, Schimmel and Rafat. Sorley's span (1739 to a year the team version says cannot be read) rests on an uncertain reading (rule 5) and does not contradict 1739.
- **Languages: seven**, with "Accounts of how many languages he wrote in differ, but he bears the title *Shair-e-Haft-Zaban*, the poet of seven languages", and the site's list. Basis (a): the only other count is Schimmel's four (two books, one author), which is a subset of the seven, not every other source against it (the Mithankot precedent). Her four are not listed. The Overview keeps "in seven languages" (the clause stands in The Poetry, as Mithankot's birth year clause did).
- **His name:** "His given name was Abdul Wahab, which is also given in a longer form as Khwaja Salah al-Din Ḥafiẓ Abd al-Wahhab." Not treated as a conflict (the longer name contains Abd al-Wahhab), so both kept as one name in two forms; the spelling 'Sacchal' is a minor variant, dropped. Judgement call 3.
- **The meeting with Shah Abdul Latif:** told once, as a legend ("it is told as a legend", "it is said"), in the more detailed telling (aged about seven, Shah Abdul Latif coming to pay homage to the local *murshid*, his exclamation quoted), with the site's "cherished as the passing of a torch". Schimmel's plain "who met him when still a boy" is not used as fact; only her "took up and developed" his tradition and "younger contemporary" are kept. Judgement call 4.
- **Daraza:** the site's "near Ranipur in the Khairpur district", with "northern Sindh" (Boivin's glossary) and "not far from Khairpur". "Village" and "town" avoided (minor).

- **The masters of Daraza:** no numerals for Sakhi Qabul Muhammad (the team version gives none). Nimano Faqir's master and the present *sajjada nashin* Sakhi Qabul Muhammad Faruqi are kept in separate sentences, not joined; the 2002 author Dr. Sakhi Qabool Muhammad Faruqi is kept apart from both, as in the team version.

- **Dalpat Sufi:** left out entirely (see Left out).
