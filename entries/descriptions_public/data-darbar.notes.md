Basis (a), the tablet on the tomb (the shrine's own date, which also falls inside the site account's 465 to 469 AH), with (b) behind it (Lahori's author, Kanhaiya Lal, Sharib, the Tazkirat al-Asfiya as Chishti cites it, Ernst and Lawrence's 465/1073). Left out: the site's "between about 1072 and 1077" as a stated range, Nicholson's 465 to 469, Rizvi's "after 1089", Schimmel's 1071, Ernst and Lawrence's 1072, 1074 and ca. 1071, Rozehnal's 1072 and 1074, Sharib's 456 "in the view of some", Jami's 495, the editor's "alive in 490" (uncertain figure), the publisher's 400 to 465, Chishti's chronogram figures (all uncertain). Lahori's "at the end of that year" kept, since it belongs to the chosen year.
- Birth: "born about 1009 in Ghazni". Basis (a) (site, "around the turn of the eleventh century") and (b) (Rizvi, "about 1009"); Sharib's Ghazni with no year agrees. The editor's 400 AH is an uncertain reading (rule 5), so no real conflict remains.
- Arrival: "Accounts differ over when he came and in whose company; by the usual reckoning he arrived around 1039 (431 AH), in the age of the Ghaznavid sultans." Basis (a), the site's "usual reckoning", with Kanhaiya Lal's 431. Left out: Rizvi's c. 1035; every companion (Sultan Masud in Kanhaiya Lal and in Sharib's "some"; Sultan Mahmud in Bhandari, with the Sultan crediting the conquest of Lahore to his arrival; Ahmad Jamawi Sarakhsi, an uncertain name, and Abu Sa'id Hujwiri in Sharib's "others"), since no position on the companion is the shrine's and none has a majority.
- Whether he stayed in Lahore: treated as minor; only the common ground is given ("He made Lahore his home, and there he died"), with Rizvi's uncontested "kept in contact with Sufis in Transoxiana, Khurasan and Syria" even after settling. Left out silently: Lahori's "lived in Lahore all his life", Kanhaiya Lal's thirty four years, Rizvi's two long journeys, his presence at Khuttali's death in 1065 and his return after 1067, Schimmel's "last years".

Lahori's Shaikh Ismail Muhaddith claim and the editor's redating also left out.

Gurgani, Khwaja Muzaffar and al-Ashqani from the saint's own book; Sharib's "Shaikh Abu al-Qasim" kept as "a teacher of his", not identified with Gurgani or Qushairi.
