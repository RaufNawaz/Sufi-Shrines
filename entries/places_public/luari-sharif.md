## Overview

Luari Sharif is a village close to Badin in southern Sindh. It is the home of a group of scholars of the Naqshbandi order, one of the major Sufi orders. The official book devoted to the Naqshbandis of Luari Sharif was written by a Hindu follower.

## A Home of Naqshbandi Scholars

The Naqshbandi order, or Naqshbandiyya, is a major Sufi order, a *tariqa* or path of the mystical tradition within Islam. In southern Sindh, close to Badin, the village of Luari Sharif is the home of a group of its scholars.

Named with these scholars is Makhdum Muhammad Hashim (1692 to 1762), "the main author". He was a *qazi*, a judge of Islamic law, in Thatta.

The Naqshbandis of Luari Sharif are also tied to a Hindu devotee. The official book devoted to them was written by a Hindu follower.
