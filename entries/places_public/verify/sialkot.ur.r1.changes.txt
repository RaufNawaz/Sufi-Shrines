REJECTED (not applied as written)

- V3 issue 4 and V2 note 3 (unify شاہ دولا into شاہ دولہ): rejected. The English deliberately keeps the team's two spellings "Shah Daulah" and "Shah Daula" (an open question for the project lead whether they are one man), and the Urdu keeps them distinct on purpose (شاہ دولہ / شاہ دولا). Unifying would settle in the Urdu what the English leaves open.

- V3 issue 5 (کرار -> مولا): rejected. The English introduces him as "Maula Karar" and then calls him "Karar" ("Karar later hid from Nanak"); the Urdu follows the English. Replacing it with مولا would name him by a part of the name the English does not use alone.

- V3 issue 16, main fix (کشمیر کے حکمران حسین خان): rejected as written. The English says "aggrieved with the Khan who ruled Kashmir" and names Husain Khan only for the reign in which he left; it does not identify the two. Reworded instead (see the change above) to keep "Khan" without the stray-title reading.

NOTES not applied (taste, or would change what the English says)

- V3 note: سالباہن / سالیواہن may be one figure: kept as two, since the English gives Raja Salbahan and Raja Salivahan as separate traditions. «ایک تیسری روایت کے مطابق» not applied: the English says "It is also believed", not a tradition; taste.
- V3 note: body repeats the introduction, and the tense shift «پُررونق شہر تھا۔ یہ عین سرحد پر واقع ہے»: not applied; the English repeats the same matter and has the same shift ("was a bustling city ... It lies right on the border").
- V3 note: glossing مردانہ and پراتن: not applied (V3 itself left them; a gloss would add information the English does not give).
- V3 notes of taste: «عین بازار کے چوک میں» (the present wording is closer to "in the centre of the market square"), «قدیم آثار» for «آثارِ قدیمہ کے کھنڈر» (the English has "archaeological ruins"), «تجدید اور مرمت» redundancy (the English has "renewed and repaired"): not applied.
