NOTES:

1. Rule 4, not from the fixes, and missed in rounds 1 and 2: the number of Hindus in the Punjab. The team lists this as a disagreement ("How many Hindus live in the Punjab"), but the public notes record no choice for it. The public text gives the variants side by side, two of them in one paragraph of Communities. That paragraph's first sentence says the majority of Pakistan's non-Muslims are "Hindus living in the regions of Southern Punjab, inner Sindh and Baluchistan". Its next sentence ends "most of the country's Hindus are scattered in other provinces, with only a few in Punjab". Elsewhere the text has "a significant Hindu population still lives there" (Southern Punjab; also in the Overview) and "Most of the Hindu community lives in Sindh and southern Punjab". Each clause is supported, so this does not meet the stated FAIL grounds. It does breach rule 4, and the reader gets a contradiction. Basis (b), what most of the team's passages give, is that many Hindus live in Southern Punjab. Exact fix: OLD "while Hindus are a smaller community there, so political parties can ignore them; most of the country's Hindus are scattered in other provinces, with only a few in Punjab." NEW "while Hindus are a smaller community there, so political parties can ignore them." Then record in the notes: "- How many Hindus live in the Punjab: a significant population in Southern Punjab, basis (b); 'only a few in Punjab' (A White Trail p. 87) dropped." I recommend the coordinator apply this before V2 and V3, with a one line confirming check.
