- "Not everyone welcomed him: a number of mystics and scholars settled in Lahore were strongly hostile to his views" goes slightly beyond the source, which speaks of hostility to the author's views, not to his welcome. Suggest: "Not everyone shared his views: ...".

- "Sufis and their families came from Lahore to Delhi" generalises from two cases: the Khwaja, and the father, Ahmad, of another figure. Suggest: "and Sufis and their kin moved from Lahore to Delhi", or simply "and men of the Sufi circles went from Lahore to Delhi".

- "Kanhaiya Lal, himself a historian of the city" points at the source. Consider "ran through its Executive Engineer, Kanhaiya Lal" alone.
- "The stone had two purposes." The team says Lal "states the purpose twice", not that there were two purposes. The paragraph does show two uses, Amritsar and the Hazuri Bagh baradari, so this is borne out, but the opening sentence could simply be dropped.

- In "The people of Lahore, Hindus and Muslims, raised a great outcry", the word for outcry is the one marked uncertain. The protest itself is stated plainly in the team Overview, so this is safe in substance. Plainer wording would be: "The people of Lahore, Hindus and Muslims, protested and wished the holy place saved from demolition, but no one listened." The Pattharan-wali explosion and the half mile clearance are fine as kept: their uncertain words are unspecified and the facts are plain.
- "Until 1972, Chunian was part of Lahore district": the team's quotation reads "Chunian district was part of Lahore". Suggest "Until 1972, Chunian was part of Lahore".
- Sums: the hospital's "one lakh and a number of thousands" and the Medical School's "one lakh and thirty two thousand" (the second figure uncertain) could both be kept safely as "more than a lakh of rupees", for example "The hospital and the Medical School each cost more than a lakh of rupees." This is optional.

That passage is not in team_P1, and the public text states Bhandari's version (deputy installed, Ghurid rule began). Confirm they appear in those parts.
