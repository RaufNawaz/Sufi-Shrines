Overview, "Sufi Hazrat Abdul Sattar Shah of Jhok Sharif, a mystic of Islam" | "یہیں کے صوفی بزرگ حضرت عبدالستار شاہ": "a mystic of Islam" is a gloss of "Sufi" and is rightly dropped (صوفی is familiar to an Urdu reader). بزرگ is a conventional reverential word, not a new fact. یہیں کے points unambiguously to Jhok Sharif, the subject of the preceding sentence.
2. Second section, the sentence glossing "Sufi" and "Hazrat" for the English reader is dropped. Both are familiar Urdu words, so under the brief this is deliberate, not an omission.
3. Headings correspond: Overview = تعارف; "Bedil's Visit and the Cup of Unity" = "بیدل کی آمد اور وحدت کا جام". The Bibliography is left out, as the brief requires (the site shows the English bibliography).
4. Names follow urdu-i18n/urdu-dictionary.json: جھوک شریف، دڑازہ. Bedil = بیدل throughout. The Sindhi phrase *جام وحدت جو* stays italic, which is right for a term that is not ordinary Urdu. Its meaning "وحدت کا جام" matches "the cup of unity". Tenses match the English: the visit is in the past (تشریف لے گئے، سفر کیا), and "speaks of" is in the present (ذکر کرتے ہیں).
5. Style only: "بیدل نے جن مقامات کا سفر کیا" might read slightly better as "بیدل جن مقامات پر گئے". This is a matter for V3 and does not affect fidelity.
