ADAPTED (recorded above with the change): V3 issue 8 (possessive kept as اپنے ... سمیت, since the English says "her two children"); V3 issue 12 (گنتیوں for "counts", not بیانات); V3 issue 13 (guards kept here, since the English states them in Holy Places as well as in the State section; split and reworded instead of removed); V3 issue 16 merged with V2 issue 1 (no causal کے باعث or کے نتیجے میں).

NOT APPLIED, with reasons:

V2 note (b), دادا for "grandfather". Kept. Urdu has no neutral word for grandfather (دادا is paternal, نانا maternal; جدِ امجد reads as "forefather"), and unstating the relationship would drop a fact the English states. دادا is the reading the material supports: the Persian of Bhandari prints خانه جد ابادری, most plausibly جدِ پدری (paternal grandfather), though the print is uncertain; and the English places Nanak's father's Bedi family (Shiv Ram, succeeded by his son Kalyan Chand Das Bedi) in Talwandi. V2 itself found دادا acceptable.

V2 notes (c), (d), (e): verifier marked them acceptable; no change.

V3 note a (اودم and اودھم سنگھ). No change: the English has two spellings, "Surjeet and Udam" and "Udham Singh, originally from Nankana", so the Urdu keeps two names as the English does. (The dictionary's Udham Singh is the samadh of Raja Udham Singh, another person.)

V3 note b (کے کے کھنہ). No change: the repository's Urdu writes initials as plain spaced letters (ایم اے، ایس ایم); punctuating them or moving the name into a comma clause would break that style or cut the name off from its postposition. Not a meaning error; V2 found the name correct.

V3 note d (مرکزی دھارے میں). Not applied: the English says "syncretism in the mainstream", and V3's alternatives (عام مذہبی زندگی، عوامی سطح) narrow or change that; taste, not meaning.

V3 note e (repeated points; تقسیم کے بعد against تقسیم کے وقت). Not applied: the English itself repeats these points, and it says "after Partition" in History and "at Partition" in Communities, so the two time phrases rightly differ.

V3 notes f, g, h, i (بلکہ شاید، مسلسل اور بلا تعطل، سربراہی، اسی گاؤں میں): pure taste, skipped as the brief directs. Note j: confirmation only.
