NOTES: style only. (1) "wandering" is rendered سیاحت in both sections. In a Sufi context this is the usual word and keeps the meaning. A reader could take it as ordinary travel, though, and سیر و سیاحت or آوارہ گردی would not be better, so no change is proposed. (2) In the second section, "one of the stops" has become a relative clause (جن مقامات پر ٹھہرے، ان میں یہ جگہ بھی شامل تھی). The meaning is the same, there is no added certainty, and it avoids repeating پڑاؤ from the overview. (3) Headings correspond (تعارف; شاہ عبداللطیف کا پڑاؤ اور لاہوت کا نام). Both titles stay in italics as titles of works, and their transliterations are consistent: ہنگلاج اے لاہوت، لاہوت لامکان. Both authors are present and attributed correctly: ابڑو for the first title, نظامانی for the second. The Bibliography is correctly absent. There is no verse and there are no numbers.
