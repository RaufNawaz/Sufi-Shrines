REJECTED: V3 r1 note: the two college names may be the same institution
REASON: The English gives both names ("the new Dayaram Jethmal Science College", "the D. J. Sindh Government Science College"). The Urdu follows it; V3 marks this as not a fluency fix.

REJECTED: V3 r1 note: «نانک» inside the quotation before he is introduced
REASON: The English has the same order (the quotation, then "Guru Nanak, the founder of the Sikh faith"). The quotation stays as it is.

REJECTED: V3 r1 notes not applied as matters of taste
REASON: Repetition of «... کا شہر ہے» and of the introduction in the body (the English introduction summarises the body the same way); «زندہ چشتی سلسلہ»; «مینارِ خاموشی» against «دخمہ»; «اسلامک سینٹر» against «اسلامی مرکز» (the English capitalises it as a name); the Sehwan fakirs sentence (V3 leaves it); «ایک شیخ» kept as vague as the English; the unnamed society (the English does not name it).
