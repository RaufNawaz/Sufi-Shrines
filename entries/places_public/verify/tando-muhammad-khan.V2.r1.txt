1. Headings correspond (Overview = تعارف; Tando Saindad and Mohsin Shah = ٹنڈو سائیں داد اور محسن شاہ). There is no Bibliography section, which is correct by design. The file has no verse.
2. Every fact is present with the same meaning: the place lies near Tando Muhammad Khan; Mohsin Shah was its gaddi nashin; the gloss of gaddi nashin (the successor who heads a Sufi lineage at its centre); he was a Sufi on the mystical path of Islam; he was affiliated to the Qalandariyya, an order of wandering dervishes. Nothing is added. The English hedges nothing, and the Urdu adds no hedges and no certainty.
3. Removing the italics on گدی نشین and قلندریہ follows the brief. The glosses of gaddi nashin and Qalandariyya are kept, which is allowed.
4. Paragraph 2, "ٹنڈو محمد خان کے نزدیک ہی": the ہی ("right near") is a little stronger than English "near". It is harmless, but plain "کے نزدیک" (or "کے قریب", as in paragraph 1) would match exactly.
5. "آوارہ گرد درویش" for "wandering dervishes": آوارہ گرد can sound like "vagabond, loafer" in everyday Urdu. سیلانی درویش or جہاں گرد درویش would avoid that faint pejorative tone. This appears in paragraphs 1 and 3.
6. Saindad (سائیں داد) is not in urdu-i18n/urdu-dictionary.json. The spelling is reasonable, but nothing in the dictionary confirms it. Tando Muhammad Khan (ٹنڈو محمد خان) matches the dictionary.
