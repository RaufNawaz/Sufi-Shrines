REJECTED / NOT APPLIED

R1. V3 #9, in part: علاقے for ضلع
REASON: The English says 'from the district' (and 'the home district of pilgrims' later). Changing it to 'area' would drop the English's district; the missing antecedent is fixed by naming ضلع عمرکوٹ instead (see the change above).

R2. V3 #12, in part: عمرکوٹ کے نام میں جو عمر ہے
REASON: Adds a relationship the English does not give (that the town's name comes from this Omar). The ambiguity is fixed with سے منسوب and vowel marks instead.

R3. V3 #13, in part: simple past پہنچے
REASON: The English is present perfect ('have walked'); English is the authority for tense. Kept as perfect: پہنچے ہیں (V3's verb and نزدیک applied).

R4. V3 NOTE: write شیو as شِو
REASON: urdu-i18n/urdu-dictionary.json spells Shiva شیو (derivedBareNames/Shiva, saints/Shiva (Mahadev)); the dictionary spelling governs, and the context (قوم ... کی پجاری) excludes 'shave'.

R5. V3 NOTE: سومرو rather than سومرہ
REASON: The dictionary has no entry for the Somras; V3 itself calls it taste and the file is consistent (سومرہ singular, سومروں plural), so the spelling is kept.

R6. V3 NOTE: comma before اور elsewhere (قصبہ ہے، اور / گروہ تھے، اور)
REASON: Taste, not a fault of meaning, agreement or referent; removed only in the Overview sentence already being changed.

REASON: Taste (repetition for emphasis is clear and correct); not applied.

R9. V3 NOTE: خیال کیا جاتا ہے for خیال ہے
