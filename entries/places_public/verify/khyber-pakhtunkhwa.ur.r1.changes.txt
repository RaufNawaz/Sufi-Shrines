REJECTED OR NOT APPLIED

R1. V3 issue 9, its word change غم پسندی to غم و اندوہ (word order part applied).
   REASON: The English names the term gham pasandi and glosses it 'loving grief'. غم و اندوہ means 'grief and sorrow' and would drop both the named term and the 'loving'. V3 cannot see the English; what it read as 'fondness for grief' is exactly the English meaning.

R2. V3 note: drop the FATA label (فاٹا یعنی وفاق کے زیرِ انتظام قبائلی علاقوں) for consistency.
   REASON: The English names FATA and spells it out at that point; dropping it would drop a name the English gives.

R3. V3 note: former names, کہا جاتا ہے to کہا جاتا تھا.
   REASON: The English says 'also called' in the present; V3 itself left it. Changing it would change a fact's time.

R4. V3 note: مثبت to خوش گوار or امید افزا.
   REASON: Taste; the English says 'positive', which مثبت renders exactly. Not applied under the stopping rule.

R5. V3 note: مصیبت جھیل چکی تھیں to سیلاب سے متاثر ہوئی تھیں.
   REASON: Taste; the current wording renders 'had suffered' faithfully. Not applied.

R6. V3 note: facts repeated across sections.
   REASON: Structural, from the English article's shape; the Urdu must carry the same facts in the same sections.

R7. V2 note: سرحد کہتی تھیں for the appositive.
   REASON: V2 says the meaning is kept; taste only.

R8. V2 note: Communities 'them' read as the Hindus.
   REASON: V2 accepts it; no change needed (V3 issue 5's rewording keeps Hindus as the referent).
