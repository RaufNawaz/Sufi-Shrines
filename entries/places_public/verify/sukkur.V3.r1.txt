NOTES:
- "تیرتھ یعنی مقدس زیارت گاہ" glosses a word most Urdu readers know. It is defensible for a general audience on first use, so I leave it as taste.
- Sukkur is called both "شہر" and "قصبہ" (for example "قصبے سے دس کوس", "یہ قصبہ ایک سفر کی منزل"), and Rohri is called "قصبہ" in the introduction but "ان شہروں میں شامل" in the history section. Choosing one term for each place would read more cleanly.
- "علن فقیر" is more often written "الن فقیر" in Urdu print. The article is consistent with itself, so this is taste.
- The article repeats material almost word for word: the triangle (sections 1 and 4), Rochaldas's 1879 birth in Rohri (sections 1 and 4), and the bus-and-train sentence about Alan Faqir (sections 1 and 5). That is a structural matter, not an error.
