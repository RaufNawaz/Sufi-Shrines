Overview sentence 2 and Holy Places: "شیخ صابر کی خلوت کا غار محفوظ ہے" now says the retreat is a cave, as the English does. غار is masculine, so "خلوت کا غار" is correct and "محفوظ ہے" needs no agreement. Both places use the same wording. In Holy Places, ان follows حضرت فرید الدین directly and is unambiguous. The comma after ہوا matches the introduction. Using حضرت at first mention in both sections is consistent and adds no fact.
3. Overview sentence 3: "زائرین کے مقررہ سفری راستے پر ... بڑی منزلوں میں" renders "main points on a route of pilgrimage". بڑی is the right strength for "main". Dropping ایک leaves the route neither definite nor indefinite, which is normal in Urdu. مقررہ was already accepted in round 1 because the History section supports it.
4. History: the split removes the false contrast of جبکہ and the clash of tenses. "دہلی اور پاکپتن بھی اسی راستے کے بڑے پڑاؤ تھے" matches "Delhi and Pakpattan were main points on it too", and بھی renders "too". پڑاؤ is masculine plural, so بڑے and تھے agree. Using پڑاؤ after منزلوں avoids repetition without changing the meaning. Taste only: the next three sentences use راستے / اسی راستے / یہی راستہ one after another. This is across sentences, not inside one, and یہی راستہ correctly points back to the same route.
5. "خلد آباد میں برہان الدین غریب کے سب سے اہم جانشین شیخ زین الدین شیرازی نے 1352 میں یہی راستہ اختیار کیا" is plain Urdu order, and the name now sits next to نے. "In Khuldabad" attaches to the successor, as in the English. A reader will not take it as the place where the route was taken. سب سے اہم for "main" is unchanged from round 1, where V2 accepted it.
6. "پاکپتن شریف میں بھگدڑ بھی مچ چکی ہے": "mob" is not lost, because بھگدڑ already means a crowd's stampede. بھی still renders "also".
7. "2001 کا سانحۂ پاکپتن اسی قصبے کے نام سے موسوم ہے": the English "too" is no longer rendered. This is not an omission of fact, because the sentence still adds the tragedy as a further item and اسی carries the emphasis. Any reader who wants "too" back could use "بھی", but that would bring back the double بھی that V3 removed. I would leave it as is.
8. Taste: the History section's first mention keeps "زائرین کے ایک مقررہ سفری راستے", with ایک, while the introduction now has no ایک. This is acceptable for an indefinite first mention in that section, so it is not an issue.
