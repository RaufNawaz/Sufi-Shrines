The one English sentence, "In Sargodha district lies a tomb to which a man's ancestors were attached.", is rendered in full by "ضلع سرگودھا میں ایک مقبرہ واقع ہے جس سے ایک شخص کے آبا و اجداد وابستہ تھے۔": place (ضلع سرگودھا), "lies" (واقع ہے, locating only), "a tomb" (ایک مقبرہ, unnamed), "a man's" (ایک شخص کے, an unspecified man, grammatically masculine, not made Malik's or a family's), "ancestors" (آبا و اجداد), "were attached" (وابستہ تھے, the past tense and the word kept, not turned into devotion, discipleship or lineage). Nothing the notes deliberately leave out (Malik, the village, Sial Sharif, any location within the district, shrine, Sufi, saint) appears in the Urdu. No Latin letters, no dashes, no numbers. Style only: ایک آدمی would mark "a man" as male slightly more explicitly than ایک شخص, but ایک شخص is the natural Urdu for an unnamed individual and does not change the meaning; no change needed.
