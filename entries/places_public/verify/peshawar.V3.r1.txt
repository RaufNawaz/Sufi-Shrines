NOTES:
- Structural repetition. The same three items each appear three times in a short article: the woman's quotation about shrines on the Frontier (lines 5, 35, 57), the Diwali line (5, 47, 63), and the Sikh families and Hindu pilgrims (5, 41/43, 63). The introduction and the last section, "کتابوں میں پشاور", both act as summaries, so a native reader feels the article going in circles. Whether to trim is an editorial decision, not a language fix, but one quotation of each would read better.
- "پانچ ہزار کفار تہِ تیغ کیے گئے": the word is the Persian chronicle's. It is attributed ("کے بیان کے مطابق"), but quotation marks around "کفار" would make it plain that this is the source's word and not the site's voice.
- Possible further glosses of familiar words: "قلندر وہ سیلانی درویش ہیں..." (line 31), "وسطی ایشیا کی سرزمین توران" (line 23), "جنوبی ایشیا کے ممتاز صوفی سلسلے" (line 17). Educated Urdu readers know all three terms. They are harmless but give the prose a translated feel.
- Punctuation inside and outside quotation marks is inconsistent. Full-sentence quotations end "۔"" in lines 35, 47 and 59 but ""۔" in line 31. One convention would be better (full-sentence quotation: ۔ inside; quoted fragment: ۔ outside).
- "شاہد اللہ فریدی بھی پشاور میں رہے۔" and "پشاور یونیورسٹی میں دیال تقابلِ ادیان پڑھاتے ہیں۔" are names that appear with no introduction. A reader is left asking who these people are. No fact can be added here, but the editor should know that both sentences stand oddly alone.
- "اور فصلیں اور غلہ وافر تھے": "اور فصلوں اور غلے کی فراوانی تھی" would read more smoothly.
- "جمرود کہلانے والے چشمۂ جمشید سے": "چشمۂ جمشید سے، جسے جمرود کہتے ہیں،" would be smoother, but the original is correct.
- "یہ پہلی بار 1951 میں سلسلے کی جانب سے شائع ہوئی": "سلسلے" is slightly vague. "سلسلے ہی کی جانب سے" or the order's name would be clearer.
- "سب ایک ہی حقیقتِ الٰہی کے مختلف مظاہر کے سوا کچھ دکھائی نہیں دیتے": acceptable, though "... کے سوا کچھ نہیں رہ جاتے" is more idiomatic. It is inside a quotation, so I have left it.
- "سرجیت سنگھ کے بزرگ ... یہاں منتقل ہوئے": "یہاں آ بسے" is warmer and more natural for families.
- Line 61, second sentence: three clauses joined by "اور". It could be split after "آتا ہے۔"
