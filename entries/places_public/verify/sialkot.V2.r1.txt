NOTES (style only):
- City and Its Country, para 5: "جمدھر اور کٹاری جیسے خنجر" reads as "daggers such as jamdhar and katari", which hints that these are examples from a larger set. The English names exactly these two. A closer option is "جمدھر اور کٹاری نام کے خنجر اور برچھیاں"; it is optional.
- City and Its Country, para 3: "شہر کے گرد باغ ہی باغ تھے" is slightly stronger than "Gardens surrounded the city". It is idiomatic and acceptable, though "شہر کے گرد باغات تھے" would be the neutral version.
- Holy Places, para 5: the Urdu follows the English in using both شاہ دولہ and شاہ دولا in consecutive sentences. In Urdu script the two spellings can read as two different people. If the English is unified, unify the Urdu too (شاہ دولہ, as in the Books section).
