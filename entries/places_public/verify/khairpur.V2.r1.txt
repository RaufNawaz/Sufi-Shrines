1. Headings correspond (Overview | تعارف; The Emirate and Its Amirs | امارت اور اس کے امیر; The Landscape Around the Town | قصبے کے گرد و نواح); no bibliography, as the brief requires. All facts carry over with the same meaning: the emirate in Sindh, the princely states, Kayasht migration for work (with the "caste of scribes" gloss kept), staunch Shia amirs, nineteenth-century imambaras ("beautiful" kept as خوبصورت), patronage of all religious buildings despite their faith, the Darazi silsila and the Jalalani tariqa's dargah near Khairpur in northern Sindh, Daraza not far off, Halani between Nausharo Firuz and Khairpur, and Hingorja near Khairpur as Bhagwan K. Ajwani's birthplace. The dropped glosses of imambara, silsila, dargah and tariqa are familiar words, so they are deliberate and not omissions. The spelling دڑازہ matches urdu-dictionary.json. No verse.
2. Style: "did not narrow their patronage" comes out as ان کی فیاضی کا دائرہ تنگ نہیں کیا ("generosity"). The meaning holds, but ان کی سرپرستی کا دائرہ would be closer to the English and would match the سرپرستی that follows in the same sentence. The wording could be: تاہم ان کے ذاتی شیعہ عقیدے نے ان کی سرپرستی کا دائرہ تنگ نہیں کیا
3. Style: in بھگوان کے اجوانی the initial کے can be read as the postposition ("Bhagwan's Ajwani"). The usual way to write an initial, بھگوان کے۔ اجوانی, would remove that reading.
4. Style: روزگار کی تلاش میں جا بسے ("went and settled in search of a livelihood") for "migrated for work" is an acceptable idiomatic rendering of migration and adds nothing.
