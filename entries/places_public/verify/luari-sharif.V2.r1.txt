ISSUES: none
NOTES: (1) In the last paragraph, "The official book devoted to them" is rendered "ان بزرگوں کے بارے میں". It points to the same people, the Naqshbandis of Luari Sharif, but بزرگ adds a mild note of reverence that the neutral English does not have. If a strictly neutral term is wanted: "ان نقشبندیوں کے بارے میں". (2) In the second section, "a major Sufi order, a tariqa or path of the mystical tradition within Islam" is folded into one phrase, "اسلام کی صوفیانہ روایت کے بڑے طریقوں میں سے ہے". "Major", "Sufi" and "tariqa" are all kept, and the dropped gloss of a familiar term follows the brief. (3) The English says twice that a Hindu follower wrote the official book, once in the Overview and once at the end. The Urdu keeps both statements with different verbs (تصنیف کی تھی, then کے قلم سے نکلی), which is acceptable variation.
