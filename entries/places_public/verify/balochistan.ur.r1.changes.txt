== REJECTED (wholly or in part), with reasons ==

R1. V3.8 (tense: make both clauses past, or both present)
   REASON: The English itself has present 'Its pastoral tribes move between the plains ... and the mountains' against past 'the pastoralists of the western Punjab, who stayed on the plains'. Either fix changes when something held (V3 cannot see the English). The Urdu keeps the English tenses: میدانوں کا رخ کرتے ہیں / میدانوں ہی میں رہتے تھے.

R2. V3.15 (جیلانی -> شیخ عبدالقادر جیلانی)
   REASON: The English says only 'Gilani'; the fuller name is an identification the English does not give, which the brief's override rule forbids. جیلانی is the dictionary's spelling of the nisba (SAINTS: Sheikh Abdul Qadir Gilani = شیخ عبدالقادر جیلانی), so the spelling is consistent.

R3. V3.23, the dropping of قانونی طور پر in 'وہاں کے ویزوں کی حیثیت ... مبہم تھی'
   REASON: The English says the status of visas was 'legally ambiguous'; dropping 'legally' drops a qualifier. Repetition reduced instead with قانوناً (see changes).

R4. V3.18, the emphatic عین متوقع and subjunctive لطف اٹھائیں
   REASON: The English asserts that the poets delight and says 'as might be expected', not 'quite expected'. The rest of V3.18 applied (see changes).

R5. V3.14, یکسر in V3's new sentence
   REASON: V2.2 finds یکسر ('entirely') an overstatement of 'reshaped'; the merged sentence uses صورت بدل دی.

R6. V3.16, عقیدت مندوں کے بقول
   REASON: The English says 'devotees claim'; کے بقول ('according to') is weaker, so دعویٰ is kept. The split applied.

R7. V3.17, آبادی کا بڑا حصہ
   REASON: The English says 'most of the Hindus'; بڑا حصہ is 'much of' (brief). زیادہ تر ہندو used; the ambiguity V3 found is removed.

R8. V3.20, سرائیکی خطے (singular)
   REASON: The English has 'the Siraiki speaking regions' (plural); سرائیکی علاقوں kept. The rest of V3.20 applied.

R9. V3 NOTE: پچاس سے زیادہ in words
   REASON: The English spells out 'more than fifty'; no change, as V3 itself advises.

R10. V3 NOTE: گواٹی -> گواتی
   REASON: The English writes 'guāṭi' with a retroflex ṭ, which is ٹ; the Urdu follows the English. No dictionary entry.

R11. V3 NOTE: ایک برادری -> اسی برادری
   REASON: The English says 'the conceptual worldview of a community', not of the Brahui; اسی برادری would make the identification the English does not make. The suggested ٹھہرتی ہے for ہے is taste, skipped.

R12. V3 NOTE: drop ضرور from اندازہ ہے کہ ... ضرور آباد رہے ہوں گے
   REASON: The English has both 'must' and 'it is reckoned' ('There must, it is reckoned, have been many Mekranis'); ضرور renders 'must'.

R13. V3 NOTE: ایم ایم اے -> متحدہ مجلس عمل
   REASON: The English says 'the MMA'; the expanded name is a fuller name than the English gives. The note on سب سے بڑی جماعت بن کر ... برتری is taste: both halves render the English 'gained the upper hand as the largest party'.

   REASON: Taste; the official name is the one the English gives and the dictionary has no entry. Kept.
