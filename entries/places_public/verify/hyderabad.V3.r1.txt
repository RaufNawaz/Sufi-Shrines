NOTES:
- Place names: the text has "ٹنڈو جہانیاں"، "محلے جہانیاں" and "ٹنڈو جہانا". If the first two are the same place, one form should be used throughout. If "ٹنڈو جہانا" (Pir Qutb Din Darya's mazar) is a different place, it is worth making that obvious, because a reader will take it for a misspelling.

- Inside the quotations (which I have left as they are): "حیدرآباد میں آ بسے" reads better as "حیدرآباد آ بسے"، and "طالب علم کی حیثیت سے داخلہ" is redundant. Change these only if the quotation is our own translation and not fixed wording.
- "سپاہی ملانی لین پر": "لین میں" is slightly more idiomatic for a lane. "حیدرآباد کی سول لائنز میں" would read more naturally as "حیدرآباد کے علاقے سول لائنز میں".
