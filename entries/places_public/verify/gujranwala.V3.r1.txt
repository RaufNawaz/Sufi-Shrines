NOTES:
- The body repeats the introduction almost word for word: Ranjit Singh and the jagir, the 75 km distance, Eminabad, the Sikh communities, and the heart of Punjab. This seems to come from the structure of the source rather than from the Urdu, so I have not counted it as an error. A native reader will still notice the echo. Varying the wording between the introduction and the body (as issues 1 to 6 already do in part) makes it less obvious.
- Under "تاریخ", the sentence "یہ شہر صوبائی دارالحکومت سے 75 کلومیٹر دور واقع ہے۔" comes abruptly after the Ranjit Singh sentences and is a geographical fact rather than a historical one. It could move to the start of the paragraph or be tied in, without changing the fact, for example: "صوبائی دارالحکومت سے 75 کلومیٹر دور واقع اس شہر کی تاریخ رنجیت سنگھ سے جڑی ہوئی ہے۔"
- Using "قلب" in the introduction and "دل" in the last paragraph is acceptable variation.
- The plural "سکھ برادریاں" after "ان شہروں" can be defended, because it refers to the communities of those cities. The singular "سکھ برادری" is also possible. This is a matter of taste.
- Punctuation, digits, and the absence of Latin letters and dashes are all correct. There are no asterisks and no verse.
