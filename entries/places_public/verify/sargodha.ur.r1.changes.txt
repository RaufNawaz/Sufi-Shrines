1. V3 issue 1, applied in V3's own wording.
WHY: «سے ... وابستہ تھے» for a family and a tomb reads as a calque of "associated with"; the idiomatic Urdu is «کا تعلق تھا», and plain «ہے» reads more naturally than «واقع ہے» in a short sentence. V3's further point, that «ایک شخص» has no referent, is not a fault to fix: the English deliberately leaves the man unidentified (public notes: "a man's" identifies no one), so the Urdu keeps him unnamed; V3's corrected sentence itself keeps «ایک شخص». The repeated ایک is in V3's own sentence and is two separate indefinite nouns.
