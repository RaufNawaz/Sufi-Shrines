NOTES:
- Shikarpur is called "شہر" in the introduction and the heading "تاجروں کا شہر", but "قصبہ" in the body. You can defend this (a city today, a town in the period the sources describe), but a reader may find the switch unexplained. It is a matter of taste whether to align.
- The opening sentence mixes tenses: "ایک شہر ہے، جہاں ... بولا جاتا تھا جو ... کہلاتا ہے". It follows the sources, but splitting it into two sentences ("... ایک شہر ہے۔ یہاں سندھی کا شمالی لہجہ بولا جاتا تھا جو اترادی یا سرائیکی کہلاتا ہے۔") would soften the jolt.
- "وہ خود کپڑے کے تاجر تھے": "خود" adds emphasis that nothing in the text sets up. I have left it because dropping it might weaken a contrast the source intends.
- "ان کا بہت آنا جانا رہتا تھا": "اکثر آنا جانا رہتا تھا" is slightly more polished.
- The article repeats the introduction's material (the Bania majority, the triangle with Rohri and Sukkur, the trade hub) in later sections almost word for word. That is how the article is structured, but varying the wording would help.
- "آبادیاتی ساخت" is correct for "demographic composition", but some readers may hear "نوآبادیاتی" in it. "آبادی کی ساخت" would rule that out.
- Punctuation, digits, script and asterisks are all clean: no Latin letters, no dashes, Urdu ، and ۔ used throughout.
