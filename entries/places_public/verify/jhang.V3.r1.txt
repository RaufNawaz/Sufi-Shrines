NOTES:
- «اساطیری داستان»: «اساطیری» suggests mythology; «لوک داستان» is the more familiar term for the Heer story. A matter of taste.
- «ہیر کا باپ»: «والد» suits a reference work better, but «باپ» is acceptable in a retelling of a legend.
- «رواجی قانون سے مراد مقامی رسم و رواج کا غیر تحریری قانون ہے۔»: the gloss is justified because the term is not common. «رسم و رواج پر مبنی غیر تحریری مقامی قانون» would read a little more smoothly.
- The article is short, and the introduction, paragraph 2 and the last paragraph all repeat the same three points (the Sials, Chuchak, the grave). That structural repetition is an editorial decision rather than a language error; issues 3 and 6 reduce it without dropping any fact.
