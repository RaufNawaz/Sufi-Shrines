ISSUES: none
NOTES:
1. Poets and Singers ¶1 and Sukkur in Books ¶3: English "One scholar of Islamic mysticism called him" and "A scholar of Islamic mysticism says nothing of Rohri" become اسلامی تصوف کے ایک مطالعے میں / اسلامی تصوف پر ایک تحقیق (a study, not a scholar). Both refer to the same source, and no fact changes. A person-noun would force a grammatical gender the English leaves open (the brief requires عالمہ for a woman scholar), so this is acceptable. If a person is preferred, the feminine is accurate: اسلامی تصوف کی ایک ماہر نے انہیں ... کہا ہے.
2. Sukkur in Books ¶2: inside the quotation, English "from where he turned southwards" (he = Guru Nanak) becomes جہاں سے وہ ... مڑ گئے. After مسافر this reads naturally as the travellers. They travelled together, so the meaning is effectively the same, and the quotation is left alone.
3. History ¶2: English "On the sixth they reached Sukkur" becomes چھ صفر کو یہ سالار سکھر پہنچے. Calling the three named commanders سالار and repeating صفر are fair clarifications. Neither adds a fact.
4. Holy Places ¶2: English "His darbar ... is in Ulhasnagar, and another stands in Rohri" becomes ان کا ایک دربار الہاس نگر میں ہے اور دوسرا روہڑی میں. The pairing ایک ... دوسرا is idiomatic, and it reads close enough to "his darbar ... another".
5. Overview ¶1 and Communities ¶3: "was the birthplace" becomes جائے پیدائش ہے, and "was the home" becomes وطن رہا ہے. For a permanent attribute this is natural Urdu, and the time frame does not change.
6. Glosses of familiar words were dropped as the brief allows: Muslim mystic, the first month / the second month of the Islamic year (محرم، صفر), local landholders, Sufi order, Sufi shrines, district subdivision. The glosses kept for فوجداری and تیرتھ are appropriate. The janamsakhi gloss became a separate sentence and keeps its meaning. Every qualifier is kept: "is reported" (بتایا جاتا ہے), "It is related" (بیان کیا جاتا ہے), "roughly a century" (لگ بھگ), "nearly a hundred" (تقریباً), "call modest" (کے بقول). All numbers and dates match: 25 Muharram, 63 kos, 5 Safar, ten kos, 25 kos, Dec 1939 to Feb 1940, 1882 to 1942, 1879, 1872, seven years. The headings correspond.
