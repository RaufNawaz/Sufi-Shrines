Taste and points for the editor to check. The introduction repeats some sentences from the body word for word ("یہ ملک لگ بھگ تین سو برس عرب حاکموں کے ماتحت رہا", "ان سے عقیدت کو مسلمانوں اور ہندوؤں دونوں کی سرپرستی حاصل تھی", the sentence on the Hindu elite leaving). That is acceptable in a summary, but light rewording would read better. "سب سے بڑھ کر" comes up very often; vary it here and there. "نومبر 1221 کے سات برس بعد" reads oddly without the event the date refers to, but adding it would add a fact.
