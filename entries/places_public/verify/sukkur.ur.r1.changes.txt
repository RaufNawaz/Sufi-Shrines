REJECTED OR NOT APPLIED

V3 r1 issue 11, in part: نظمیں for گیت. The English says Janullah "composed songs" on the martyrdom of Shah Inayat; poems would change the kind of work. The calque V3 named (لکھے) is fixed by کہے.

V3 r1 issue 12, in part: روہڑی کا ذکر ایک گویّے کے سفر کے سلسلے میں بھی آتا ہے. In Poets and Singers the English states a fact about the place ("Rohri also stands at one end of a singer's road"), not what a text mentions; ذکر آتا ہے would make it a statement about a source. Reworded instead as روہڑی ایک گویّے کے سفر سے بھی جڑا ہوا ہے, with the next sentence (between Rohri and Sehwan Sharif) carrying the end of the route.

V3 r1 issue 13, in part: ذکر ... کرتی ہے. Same meaning given as بتاتی ہے, so that ذکر is not repeated in the next sentence, which now ends ذکر آتا ہے (issue 14).

V3 r1 note 1 (تیرتھ یعنی مقدس زیارت گاہ): taste, by V3's own account; V2 judged the gloss appropriate. Not applied.

V3 r1 note 2 (one term each for Sukkur and Rohri, شہر or قصبہ): not applied. The English itself calls Sukkur "a city" in the present and "the town" in the Mughal passage ("ten kos from the town") and in the janamsakhi passage ("names the town"), and calls Rohri a town in the overview and one of "the cities of the riots" in History; the Urdu follows the English in each place. Issue 15 (دونوں مقامات) removes the one clash inside a single passage.

V3 r1 note 3 (الن فقیر for علن فقیر): not applied. The name is not in urdu-dictionary.json; this file spells it علن throughout, as do sehwan.ur.md and lal-shahbaz-qalandar.ur.md.

V3 r1 note 4 (repeated material across sections): structural; the English repeats the same material. Not applied.

V3 r1 note 6 (link منزل گاہ کے فسادات to the Sukkur riots): V3 itself notes linking them would add a fact; the English does not link them. Not applied.

V3 r1 notes 7 to 9 (Kandri and orphanage sentences, tense after وطن تھا, چیف آفیسر): no change asked or needed; the sentences follow the English.

V2 r1 note 1 (a study for "a scholar"): the inconsistency V2 and V3 both saw is fixed by using اسلامی تصوف پر ایک تحقیق in both places. A person noun (اسلامی تصوف کی ایک ماہر) was not used, since it would give a gender the English prose leaves open; V2 judged the study wording acceptable.
