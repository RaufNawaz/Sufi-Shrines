## Overview

Jhang is the land of the Siyal Jats, the family of Hir, Jats being an agricultural caste. In the myth of Hir, which is several hundred years old, her father Cucak Siyal was the powerful Jat chief of Jhang Siyal, "a vast agricultural territory situated to the southwest of Lahore in Pakistan". Villagers in the district of Jhang identify Hir's tomb to this day.

## Hir, Her Clan and Her Tomb

In the myth, Hir was the beautiful and witty daughter of Cucak Siyal, the powerful Jat chief of Jhang Siyal. She belonged to the family of the powerful Siyal Jats of Jhang.

Siyal is the name of Hir's clan, "powerful landowners of the Panjab". The Siyal tribe is more than usually considerate toward its women: it is "one of the few tribes in the Panjab that allow women to inherit property under customary law", the unwritten law of local custom.

The myth of Hir is several hundred years old, and yet villagers in the district of Jhang identify her tomb to this day.
