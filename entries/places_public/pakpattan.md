## Overview

Pakpattan, also called Pakpattan Sharif, *sharif* meaning noble, is the town in Pakistan where the saint Farid al-Din died. Near the place where he died, the cave retreat of Shaykh Sabir is preserved, *shaykh* being a title meaning elder or master. In the century after the death of Mu'in ad-Din, Pakpattan stood with Ajmer and Delhi among the main points on a route of pilgrimage. Its name also recalls the Pakpattan tragedy of 2001.

## History

In the century after Mu'in ad-Din's death, Ajmer became one of the main points on a pilgrimage itinerary, a set route of travel for pilgrims, and Delhi and Pakpattan were main points on it too. In 1352 the route was followed by Shaykh Zayn ad-Din Shirazi, the main successor to Burhan ad-Din Gharib in Khuldabad.

Pakpattan Sharif has also been the scene of a mob stampede. The town gives its name, too, to the Pakpattan tragedy of 2001.

## Holy Places

Two saints are tied to the town. Farid al-Din died in Pakpattan, and near the place where he died the cave retreat of Shaykh Sabir is preserved.
