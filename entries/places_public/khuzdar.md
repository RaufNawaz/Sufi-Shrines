## Overview

Lahoot, in Khuzdar, was one of the stops on the wandering of Shah Abdul Latif. The name of Lahoot also stands in the titles of two works, Abro's *Hinglāj ai Lāhūt* and Nazamani's *Lāhūṭ lā Makān*.

## Shah Abdul Latif's Stop and Lahoot's Name

Lahoot lies in Khuzdar. It was one of the stops on the wandering of Shah Abdul Latif.

The name of the place is carried in the titles of two works. One is *Hinglāj ai Lāhūt*, by Abro; the other is *Lāhūṭ lā Makān*, by Nazamani.
