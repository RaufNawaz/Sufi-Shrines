## Working rule on unspecified uncertain words

Where the team version flags "one word uncertain" (or "two words") in a sentence without saying which word, and still states the content as fact, the content is kept in plain form and the uncertain word is not reproduced where it can be identified. Where the flag names the word that carries the fact (a name, a figure, a year, a key noun), the fact is left out. Affected kept sentences include: Shah Abu Ishaq Qadri's seven bighas ("two words of the sentence uncertain"), Shiv Gar's grandson disciple living in the shivala with his wife ("one word uncertain"), the Khoja mosque rent ("one word uncertain"), Sayyid Sarbuland's room ("one word uncertain"), the Sunehri Masjid shops ("one word uncertain"), the Kashmiri Bazaar mosque salaries ("one word uncertain"), Bhauri Sarkar's endowment ("one word uncertain"), the Wilbagh Rai offering ("two words uncertain"), the Thibi Wala offering ("one word uncertain"), the Sunehri Masjid fatwa ("one word uncertain"), the Badshahi eastern cells ("a word illegible", the reason only), Jahangir's tomb embankment ("one word uncertain"), Sarfaraz Khan building "a mosque" ("the last word uncertain": kept because the emperor's response, "so devout a man", rests on it, and the team gives it as the sense; V1 may prefer it dropped).

- Lehna Singh and Ranjit Singh and the monuments (Chishti): given as nineteenth century tradition ("it was held that"), with the Ranjit Singh period stripping stated plainly, as the team does.
- Timing: Lal's present tense rendered "in the 1880s" / "by the 1880s"; "last year's rains" as "the rains of the early 1880s"; "two years earlier" for the bazaar straightening as "In the early 1880s"; Lal's "within the last ten years" as "within the ten years before the 1880s". - Lal's own judgements carried with "as a historian of the city put it in the 1880s" (brief rule 2 device, no surname): the picture baba's portrait as "a means of his own advancement"; the Dai Anga "kothi that ought to be called a mosque".
