## Overview

Gujrat district has a place in the tale of Sohni and in the family of Ranjha. In the tale, the district is where her father, the potter Tulla, lived. Takht Hazara, "now Gujrat District of Pakistan", was the home of Ranjha's father and of the Ranjha clan of Jats, Jats being an agricultural caste.

## Sohni's Tale and Ranjha's Family

The tale of Sohni begins in the district: "Once upon a time there was a well-to-do potter called Tulla who lived in the Gujrat district of the Panjab." In the tale, Tulla had a beautiful daughter called Sohni, a name which means "the beautiful one".

Ranjha's family belonged to the district too. Ranjha was the youngest of the eight sons of Chaudhry Muazzam, Chaudhry being an honorific title. His father, also known as Mauju, was of the Ranjha clan of Jats of Takht Hazara, "now Gujrat District of Pakistan".
