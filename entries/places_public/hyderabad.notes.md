## Consensus choices (rule 4)

- **Tando Jahaniyya and Tando Jahana (team disagreement 1, team question 1).** Public, Holy Places: "Qutub Ali Shah was a famous Sufi from Hyderabad, and his *dargah* is in Jahaniyya, a neighbourhood of the city" in one paragraph, and "The city also holds the shrine of Qutb Din Darya, a *pir* ... His shrine is called Tando Jahana, and it stands in the Civil Lines at Hyderabad" in the next. Team: "Neither book says whether the two names mean one shrine." Basis: the two are given as two separate statements, each as its source gives it, with no claim that they are one shrine or two, and no "accounts differ", because the team version does not show that they conflict (the saints differ and no book identifies the places). Never merged: Qutb Din Darya is not made the saint of Jahaniyya, nor Qutub Ali Shah of Tando Jahana. The team's signpost "Abbas places another Sufi shrine in the city" is not carried over as "another", which would assert they are different; the public says "The city also holds the shrine of".
- **What Dalpat Sufi's place was (team disagreement 2).** Public: the *astano* on the Sipahimalani Lane that became a pilgrimage centre, "known as Guru Sehwani's place".
