## Overview

Quetta is among the places of Hindu settlement in Pakistan, and the city is also home to Hazara Shias, Shia Muslims of the Hazara people. Hindu communities in Quetta, as in Sindh, were attacked after the demolition of the Babri Mosque. An attack on the Hazara Shias of Quetta killed 84 people and was followed by nationwide protests.

## Communities

Hindus are among the communities of Quetta, one of the places of Hindu settlement in Pakistan. After the demolition of the Babri Mosque, Hindu communities in Sindh and Quetta were attacked.

The Hazara Shias are another of the city's communities. An attack on the Hazara Shias in Quetta resulted in the death of 84 people. Nationwide protests followed, with "calls for action against the culprits, Lashkare-e-Jhangvi, a Sunni extremist organization".
