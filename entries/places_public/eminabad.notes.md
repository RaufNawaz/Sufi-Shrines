## Consensus choices (rule 4)

- **How the town came by its name (the team's one listed disagreement).** Public, History: "Babur destroyed the cities of Eminabad and Lahore, and when he took over Saidpur, he renamed it Eminabad. Accounts of how the town came by its name differ, but this is the account generally given." Overview: "Babur, by the account generally given, renamed it Eminabad when he took the city." Team: "Khalid identifies the Saidpur of Guru Nanak's time with Eminabad, writing that 'When Babur took over the city of Saidpur, he renamed it Eminabad.'"; disagreement: "Bhandari's chronicle says that a town and pargana of that name were established by royal order, in the reign of an emperor it does not name, by Muhammad Amin, a *karori*, though only the spelling links the chronicle's place to Eminabad". Basis: neither (a) nor (b) applies (no shrine position; one book on each side). The Babur account is the only one the team version ties firmly to this town; the chronicle's rests, in the team's own words, on spelling alone, and a spelling only link may not be stated as fact about this place. So the Babur account is given as the established one, with "accounts differ" in one clause, and the chronicle's Muhammad Amin founding is dropped as the rival variant (rule 4: no side by side variants). "Most often" is not used, since only one source gives each account.
- **The woman who fed Babur.** Team: "A woman, whose name the printed text gives as Eni though the reading is uncertain, offered Babur roasted grain when he had not eaten for days, shortly before he reached Saidpur, and the city, Khalid relates, was renamed in her honour". Shortly before Babur reached Saidpur, when he had not eaten for days, a woman offered him roasted grain, and the city, it is said, was renamed in her honour." The uncertain name Eni is dropped (rule 5); the story's point stands without it. It is framed as tradition, and kept as an addition to the Babur account, not as a rival to it: the team notes call the two "Khalid's and compatible with each other".

## Urdu (coordinator, 31 October 2026)
