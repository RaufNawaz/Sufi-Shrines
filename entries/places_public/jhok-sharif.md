Jhok Sharif is one of the places that Bedil visited, along with Daraza. Sufi Hazrat Abdul Sattar Shah of Jhok Sharif, a mystic of Islam, speaks of *jam wahdat jo*, "the cup of unity".

## Bedil's Visit and the Cup of Unity

Among the places Bedil visited were Jhok Sharif and Daraza.

Sufi Hazrat Abdul Sattar Shah of Jhok Sharif speaks of *jam wahdat jo*, which means "the cup of unity". A Sufi is a follower of the mystical path within Islam, and Hazrat is a reverential title for saints and scholars.
