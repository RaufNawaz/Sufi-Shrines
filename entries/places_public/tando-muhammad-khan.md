## Overview

Near Tando Muhammad Khan lies Tando Saindad. Mohsin Shah, the *gaddi nashin* of Tando Saindad, or holder of its seat, was a Sufi, a mystic of Islam, affiliated to the *Qalandariyya*, an order of wandering dervishes.

## Tando Saindad and Mohsin Shah

Tando Saindad lies near Tando Muhammad Khan. Its *gaddi nashin* was Mohsin Shah. The *gaddi nashin* is the holder of the seat: the successor who heads a Sufi lineage at its centre.

Mohsin Shah was a Sufi, a follower of the mystical path within Islam. He was affiliated to the *Qalandariyya*, a Sufi order of wandering dervishes.
