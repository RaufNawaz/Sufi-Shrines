## Overview

Las Bela is a district of Balochistan close to Hinglaj, the Hindu temple of the goddess Hinglaj Devi. In Las Bela the Tumbel spread the Goddess's fame. The district is also where a body known as the HSM was founded in 1986, and Lohanas from Las Bela, members of a Hindu trading caste, have chiefly made it up ever since.

## Las Bela and Hinglaj

Las Bela is a district close to Hinglaj. The shrine itself lies within the Hingol National Park. In Las Bela the Tumbel spread the Goddess's fame.

Las Bela is also where the HSM began. The body was founded on 5 January 1986 at Bela, in Las Bela district, Balochistan, and from its beginning it has consisted mainly of Lohanas from the district of Las Bela. The Dewanis, a Sindhi Lohana family, also come from Las Bela.
