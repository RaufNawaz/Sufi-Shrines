## Overview

Gujranwala is a city of the Punjab, 75 kilometres from the provincial capital, and together with Faisalabad and Sahiwal it is counted as the heart of the Punjab. Its history is tied to Ranjit Singh, who started from a small fief around the city. Gujranwala has replaced Eminabad as an important trading centre, and it is one of the cities whose Sikh communities have newly become assertive.

## History

The city's history is bound up with Ranjit Singh. He started from a small fief around Gujranwala, a fief being an estate of land held from a ruler. The city lies 75 kilometres away from the provincial capital.

Ranjit Singh also allotted land around the cities of Gujranwala and Lahore to Baba Sahib Singh Bedi, Baba being a title of respect.

## Trade and Community

Gujranwala has replaced Eminabad as an important trading centre.

Gujranwala is one of the cities whose Sikh communities have newly become assertive, Sikhs being followers of the faith founded by Guru Nanak.

Together with Faisalabad and Sahiwal, Gujranwala is counted as the heart of the Punjab.
