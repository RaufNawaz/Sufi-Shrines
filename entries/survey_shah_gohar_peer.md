## 8. اس جگہ کی تاریخ کیا ہے؟ براہِ کرم ہمیں تفصیل سے بتائیں کہ یہ جگہ کیسے اور کیوں قائم ہوئی اور وقت کے ساتھ اس میں کیا تبدیلیاں آئیں۔

**Answer:**
This Darbar is famous by the name of Hazrat Shah Gohar Peer. The shrine of Hazrat Shah Gohar Ali is located here. After the demise of Hazrat Shah Gohar Ali, his shrine was built by Sultan Mahmud Ghaznavi. In 1972, the Auqaf Department took responsibility, and with the passage of time, taking care of the construction and arrangements is the responsibility of this institution. In 1983, a mosque was constructed by the name of the shrine, which is called by the name of Hazrat Shah Gohar Peer.

## 9. اگر یہ جگہ کسی تاریخی یا روحانی شخصیت سے منسلک ہے تو براہِ کرم ہمیں اس شخصیت کی زندگی کے بارے میں تفصیل سے بتائیں۔
*English gloss: If this place is linked to a historical or spiritual figure, please tell us in detail about that figure's life.*

**Answer:**
Hazrat Shah Gohar Peer was born on 11 Rabi-ul-Sani 729 Hijri in Uch Sharif. His original name was Syed Ali Gohar, but people called and knew him by the name of Shah Gohar Peer. He belonged to the genealogical lineage of Hazrat Imam Hussain (R.A). His family lineage was from the Ismaili Sadaat of Uch Sharif. His father's name was Syed Ul Hassan Kabeer. His father was also a pious Sufi and a scholar of Deen (religion). Shia, Sunni, and people of other sects were his disciples. He received his early education from his father and started walking in his father's footsteps. During his life, he acquired all his spiritual knowledge from his father. His family occupation was to invite people to Islam, and he was world-famous by the name of "Kuffar Shikan" (the destroyer of disbelief). He took the oath of allegiance at his father's hand and began preaching the religion of Islam. When his father saw his rising spirituality, he bestowed the mantle of spiritual succession (khilafat) upon him and asked him to set out to convert non-Muslims to Muslims. He possessed a deep consciousness of the remembrance of Allah (Zikr-e-Ilahi). His outer appearance was that of a beautiful dervish. He began spreading the call of Islam; he first invited the people in Kashmir to Islam and significantly increased the number of Muslims entering the fold of Islam. Whichever non-Muslim met him would only leave from there after becoming his disciple. Seeing his spiritual grace and knowledge, people used to wait for him. When he migrated towards Uch Sharif, on the way, he arranged his stay at a place in Lahore which is today famous by the name of Mahmood Booti, but within just a few days, he changed his mind and established a permanent camp here. He began entering people into the fold of Islam in Lahore. People of every class, rich and poor alike, would come to him and leave spiritually benefited. People would see him riding a lion and a snake and would be left astonished. Before his arrival, there was a canal at his camp site which suddenly dried up after his arrival, which deeply impressed the people. He did not manifest his spiritual greatness to the people and would remain busy in the remembrance of Allah in secrecy and by constantly changing locations. Many other saints (Auliya Karam) were also with him, who were part of this preaching mission with him from the very beginning. He planted a Sukh Chain (Pongamia) tree with his own hands which exists to this day, and on its branches, written language in firm script is still present today. Witnessing his miracles, people would accept Islam and request him to pray on their behalf. The emperors and Maharajas of that era would also come to him to seek spiritual blessings. Another famous story from his life is that at one moment he would be seen in one place, and at the same moment, he would be seen somewhere far away. One day he went to Delhi, where he had a vision of Hazrat Ali Murtaza (R.A) and was bringing his sacred footprints on a snake when one blessed footprint remained there, and he brought one to Uch Sharif, which is still a place of pilgrimage today. On account of all these miracles, people kept asking him questions, seeing which he adopted a Qalandari appearance so that people would consider him ecstatic (mast) and not pay close attention. He considered doing good for the people as his duty. It is said that anyone going to his shrine did not even need to state their request; he would understand it and pray in their favor, which Allah Almighty would accept. He converted a very large number of non-Muslims to Muslims during his life. Afterwards, due to his declining health, he gradually weakened but did not let anyone know that his departure was drawing near. He passed away from this world on 21 Ramzan 825 Hijri. When the news of his demise became known, a very large number of people gathered, and many people also arrived from Uch Sharif. At that time, the King built a grand shrine for him, which is still known today for its grandeur and antiquity.

## 11. لوگ اس جگہ کو روحانی طور پر اہم کیوں سمجھتے ہیں؟
*English gloss: Why do people consider this place spiritually important?*

**Answer:**
Hazrat Shah Gohar Peer was a very pious Sufi and scholar, and he belonged to a Syed family, which is of the Ahl-e-Bait (the family of the Holy Prophet). It is stated in history that in those times, people used to eagerly wait for a single glance from you, arriving empty-handed and returning with their hands filled with blessings. Even the Mughal emperors of that era used to praise your miracles. The tree planted by your own hands continued to give sugar (sweetness) for a long time after your passing, and the tree planted by your father's hands, which is in Uch Sharif, used to give milk. In this way, the members of your family used to obtain one thing or another from trees, which is also a source of great fame. The saints (Auliya Karam) who came after you wrote many books about you. Whosoever saw you became yours forever.

## 12. اس جگہ پر کون سی مذہبی یا روحانی سرگرمیاں باقاعدگی سے ہوتی ہیں اور یہ کب ہوتی ہیں؟ (مثلاً قوالی، نماز، مراقبہ، دعائیں، لنگر، دھمال، محفل) ساری سرگرمیوں کے بارے میں تفصیل سے بتائیں-
*English gloss: Which religious or spiritual activities happen regularly at this place and when do they happen? (e.g. qawwali, prayer, meditation, supplications, langar, dhamal, mehfil) Describe all the activities in detail.*

**Answer:**
Every week on Thursday, gatherings (mehfils) are held here. Although people keep coming here every day, a larger crowd is present on Thursdays, during which free food (langar) is distributed, Naats are recited, Qawwalis are performed, and lamps (diyas) are lit. Right in front of the shrine, there is a mosque named after Hazrat Shah Gohar Peer, where the five daily prayers and the Friday congregational prayer are offered. In the surroundings of your shrine, there is a residential area and a graveyard, due to which there remains a rush of people, and people keep distributing food offerings (niyaz).

## 16. اگر اس جگہ کے بارے میں کوئی اور اہم معلومات ہوں تو براہِ کرم یہاں بتائیں۔
*English gloss: If there is any other important information about this place, please tell us here.*

**Answer:**
It is written in the books compiled about him that the graves immediately adjacent to his grave are those of his lion and snake, which he used to ride. The condition of the shrine is gradually deteriorating due to a lack of proper care; whatever arrangements are made here are done by the local people and the pilgrims who love him. When Hazrat Shah Gohar Peer established his camp here, a water canal used to flow through this area before that, which closed on its own after his arrival. Hazrat Shah Gohar Peer planted a Banyan (Bor) tree with his own hands, upon which writing formed by its own branches is still present today in that very same manner and holds great popularity among the people. The Auqaf Department is not carrying out its maintenance work properly. However, Hazrat Shah Gohar Peer himself did not write any book because there was no such custom of writing in that era, but those who came after him and the people who had seen him wrote books about him. The dome of this shrine is the largest compared to all other shrines in Lahore.
