# Shrine of Makhdoom Abdul Rahim Girhori
shrine-of-makhdoom-abdul-rahim-girhori / researched 16 August 2026 / batch H

## Verified findings

- **Location: shrine complex ~8 km west of Pithoro taluka, Umarkot district.** "Located about 8 km west of Pithoro taluka in Umarkot district is the shrine complex of a great Naqshbandi mystic, Makhdoom Abdul Rahim Girhori." — Zulfiqar Ali Kalhoro, "Swamis and Sufis," *The Friday Times*, 28 September 2018, https://thefridaytimes.com/2018/09/28/swamis-and-sufis (established press). Accessed 16 August 2026.

- **Dates and caste.** "Makhdoom Abdul Rahim Girhori (1739-1778 AD)" — same article — and he "belonged to the Mangrio caste." Zulfiqar Ali Kalhoro, "Swamis and Sufis," *The Friday Times*, 28 September 2018 (as above). Accessed 16 August 2026.

- **Teacher and order.** "he became a disciple of Khwaja Muhammad Zaman (1713-1775 AD) of Luari Sharif, a celebrated Naqshbandi mystic" — same article, describing Girhori as one of Khwaja Muhammad Zaman's khalifas (deputies). Zulfiqar Ali Kalhoro, "Swamis and Sufis," *The Friday Times*, 28 September 2018. Accessed 16 August 2026.

- **Literary works.** The article lists his compositions in Sindhi, Arabic and Persian, including *Sharh Abayat Sindhi*, *Fath-al-Fazal*, *Risala Gul Numa*, and *Kalam Girhori*. Zulfiqar Ali Kalhoro, "Swamis and Sufis," *The Friday Times*, 28 September 2018. Accessed 16 August 2026.

- **Death/martyrdom, and the shrine's shared Hindu–Muslim following.** "He fell in battle against a Hindu ascetic" and "Makhdoom Abdul Rahim Girhori killed the Swami and destroyed the Marhi but he himself lost his life in the encounter." "Today, the shrine complex of Shaheed (martyr) Makhdoom Abdul Rahim Girhori is frequented by both Muslims and Hindus." Zulfiqar Ali Kalhoro, "Swamis and Sufis," *The Friday Times*, 28 September 2018. Accessed 16 August 2026.

- **Administrative location of "Girhor Sharif" as a place-name (independent corroboration, different district framing).** A separate Dawn news report situates the shrine town as "Girhor Sharif town of Sindhri taluka" rather than Pithoro/Umarkot: "MIRPURKHAS: A shutdown was observed in Girhor Sharif town of Sindhri taluka on Tuesday" and refers to "the custodian of the Shaheed Abdul Raheem Girhori shrine." — "'Armed attack' on Girhor Sharif custodian stirs sectarian unrest," *Dawn*, 10 June 2015, https://www.dawn.com/news/1187162 (established press). Accessed 16 August 2026. This article gives no biographical detail on the saint himself, only on the shrine's custodian (Syed Asif Ali Shah) and a 2015 attack on him.

## Conflicts

- **Administrative location.** *The Friday Times* (2018) places the shrine complex "about 8 km west of Pithoro taluka in Umarkot district." *Dawn* (2015) instead locates "Girhor Sharif town" in "Sindhri taluka," datelined from Mirpurkhas. Pithoro and Sindhri are both talukas historically associated with Mirpurkhas/Umarkot-area administrative boundaries in Sindh, but the two sources name different talukas and imply different current districts (Umarkot vs. Mirpurkhas). Not resolved here — report both as given.
