## Follow-up: Encyclopedia Sindhiana (subject search) and Sindhi Adabi Board digital library — 5 September 2026

**Encyclopedia Sindhiana:** the earlier pass's single attempt used a search-engine-supplied article-title URL that resolved to the encyclopedia's homepage rather than the article. This pass instead went through the encyclopedia's own web portal (http://encyclopediasindhiana.org — the "Sindhiana Web Portal" linked from the Sindhi Language Authority's official site, https://sl.sindhila.org) and used its alphabetical browse feature (`alpha.php?Alpha=آ`) to list every entry beginning with the Sindhi letter آ. This surfaced a genuine entry: "آسودارام سَنت" (category: شخصيتون / "Personalities").

Fetching that entry (`article.php?Dflt=آسودارام سنت`) returned a full article, entry no. 3866, sourced by the page itself to "انسائيڪلوپيڊيا سنڌيانا پهرين جلد جو ضميمو" ("Encyclopedia Sindhiana, First Volume Appendix"). Quoted verbatim (original Sindhi, as fetched):

"سنت آسودارام ضلعي سکر جي تعلقي پنوعاقل ۾ 1895ع ڌاري ڳوٺ عالم خان جتوئيءَ ۾ ڄائو ... کيس ننڍي هوندي ئي سندس والد پنھنجي مرشد سنت سترام داس وٽ ڇڏي آيو، جتي 13 سال رهيو ... 1940ع ۾ مرشدن جي حڪم سان پني عاقل ۾ آستان جي اڏاوت ڪرايائين ۽ 1942ع ۾ اڏاوت مڪمل ڪري اتي رهائش اختيار ڪيائين ... سنت آسودارام صاحب 4 سيپٽمبر 1960ع ۾ 64 ورهين جي عمر ۾ پرلوڪ پڌاريو."

Translated to make the content usable (not a substitute for the Sindhi original above): Sant Asudaram was born around 1895 in Alam Khan Jatoi village, Panno Aqil taluka, Sukkur district, to a father the entry names as لڌڙو مل (approx. "Ludhro Mal"). As a child his father left him with his murshid (spiritual guide), Sant Satram Das, where he stayed 13 years, then returned to his village on his murshids' orders. In 1940, on his murshids' instruction, he had an aastan (hermitage/shrine) built in Panno Aqil, completed it in 1942, and took up residence there. He died on 4 September 1960 at age 64; his samadhi (tomb) was built at the same aastan, where devotees from Sindh and India come to pay respects, and an annual mela is held there. The entry also names an ashram bearing his name in Lucknow, India, and a "Sakhi Baba General Hospital" established in Panno Aqil.

Citation: "آسودارام، سَنت," Encyclopedia Sindhiana (انسائيڪلوپيڊيا سنڌيانا), entry no. 3866, First Volume Appendix, published by the Sindhi Language Authority (a statutory body of the Government of Sindh), http://encyclopediasindhiana.org/article.php?Dflt=آسودارام سنت, accessed 5 September 2026. Reliability class: official body (statutory provincial language/culture authority) reference work.

Note on an existing unverified lead: the file's "Unverified leads" section flagged a search-engine summary reporting an Encyclopedia Sindhiana page giving Asudaram's death as "4 September 1960 at age 64" under a mismatched article title ("دردي صاحب خان جروار"). That death date and age are now independently confirmed by a direct fetch of the correct article; the mismatched title in the earlier lead was evidently a search-indexing artefact, not evidence the fact was wrong.

This also corroborates, from a now-citable source, part of what two below-the-bar devotee sites already claimed: Shiv Sakhi Ashram's page and Sacho Satram's page (both still outside the reliability bar, not independently re-verified here) described Asudaram as a disciple sent by Satramdas of Raharki to found the Panno Aqil darbar in 1940 — the encyclopedia entry independently gives the same guru relationship and the same 1940 founding year.

**Sindhi Adabi Board digital library:** searched via the Board's own site search (https://sindhiadabiboard.net/?s=آسودارام). Result: "Nothing Found - Sorry, but nothing matched your search terms. Please try again with some different keywords." A genuine, checked-and-silent negative — the Board's digitized catalog does not surface anything under this name.

### Revised verdict
**PARTIAL** — upgraded from NOTHING RELIABLE FOUND. One source now meets the reliability bar (an official body's published encyclopedia entry), giving a full and specific biography: birthplace and approximate birth year, father's name, guru/lineage (Sant Satram Das), the 1940–42 founding of the Panno Aqil aastan, and a death date (4 September 1960, age 64). Still short of STRONG (≥2 independent verified sources) — no second citable source was found this session, and the devotee-site accounts that corroborate parts of it remain below the reliability bar on their own terms.

This entry matters more than any other in the audit: per the assignment brief, it is **the

absence here is expected on chronological grounds as well as descriptive ones.
