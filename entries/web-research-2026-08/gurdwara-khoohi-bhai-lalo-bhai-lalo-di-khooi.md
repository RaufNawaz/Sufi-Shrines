# Gurdwara Khoohi Bhai Lalo (Bhai Lalo di Khooi)
gurdwara-khoohi-bhai-lalo-bhai-lalo-di-khooi / researched 16 August 2026 / batch B

Sheet location on file: Eminabad, Gujranwala District, Punjab, Pakistan (32.0415, 74.2470).

## Verified findings

1. **Bhai Lalo's identity and the founding story.** "Bhai Lalu, a resident of Said pur (Eminabad), was a carpenter of Getaura caste. Guru Nanak stayed at his house when came he to Eminabad." — Iqbal Qaiser, *Historical Sikh Shrines in Pakistan* (Lahore: Punjabi History Board, 1998), full text, p. 58 of the scan, at https://archive.org/download/HistoricalSikhShrinesInPakistan/HistoricalSikhShrinesInPakistan_djvu.txt (details page https://archive.org/details/HistoricalSikhShrinesInPakistan), accessed 16 August 2026. Reliability class: published book (citable).

2. **Naming of the gurdwara and the well.** "Lalu's house, where Gur Dev Ji had stayed, was made a Gurdwara. This is called G[u]rdwara Bhai Lalu Di Khoi (the well of Lalu). The well from which Gur Dev Ji had been drinking water is still intact." — same source as above, p. 58–60 of the scan. Reliability class: published book (citable).

3. **Post-1947 history and 1989 restoration.** "After 1947, a refugee family had occupied the Gurdwara. In 1989 Sardar Surjit Singh Panesar purchased it with the money raised by the foreign Sangat and restored it. The well inside the premises was covered with a platform." — same source, p. 60 of the scan. Reliability class: published book (citable).

4. **Official district-government mention of the Bhai Lalo tradition in Eminabad** (though attached to a neighbouring, differently-named gurdwara — see Conflicts). "Gurdwara Rori Sahib — the sacred shrine which marks the site where, according to tradition, Guru Nanak … had stayed with Bhai Lalo" (paraphrase of page content returned on fetch, entry for Eminabad) — District Gujranwala official government website, "Important Places," https://gujranwala.punjab.gov.pk/important-places, accessed 16 August 2026. Reliability class: official body (citable).

## Conflicts

- **Two distinct Eminabad gurdwaras, one Bhai-Lalo tradition.** Iqbal Qaiser's book treats Gurdwara Rori Sahib (marking the site of Guru Nanak's captivity and grinding-wheel labour under Babur) and Gurdwara Bhai Lalu Di Khoi (the well at Bhai Lalo's own house) as two separate structures in Eminabad. The official Gujranwala district government "Important Places" page names only Gurdwara Rori Sahib among Eminabad's Sikh sites and attributes the Bhai Lalo association to it, without naming Khoohi Bhai Lalo separately. The two sources are not reconciled here; this file does not resolve which gurdwara the "stayed with Bhai Lalo" tradition most properly belongs to.
- **Transliteration of the well's name.** The sheet's file_slug uses "Khooi"; Qaiser's book (as OCR'd) renders it "Khoi," and most web sources render it "Khuhi." These are attested alternate Latin transliterations of the same Punjabi word (کھوہی/کھوئی, "well"), not necessarily different places, but no single canonical spelling was found in a citable source.
- **Coordinates.** Non-citable community sources (SikhiWiki, etc.) give 32°2'32.02"N, 74°15'35.95"E for the gurdwara, which does not exactly match the sheet's recorded 32.0415, 74.2470 (close but not identical). Since the source giving those coordinates is a wiki, this is not verified and is not treated as a citation — flagged only as a discrepancy to check independently.

## Verdict

PARTIAL — one independent, directly-quoting published-book source (Qaiser) with strong, specific coverage (founding story, post-1947 history, 1989 restoration); the only other citable source (Gujranwala district government page) corroborates the general Eminabad/Bhai Lalo tradition but attaches it to a differently-named neighbouring gurdwara rather than independently confirming the Khoohi-specific facts.
