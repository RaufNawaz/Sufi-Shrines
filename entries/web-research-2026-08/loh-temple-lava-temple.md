## Conflicts

- **Historical dating vs. legend:** *Dawn* (2018) reports historians (MacLagan, Chaudhry) leaning toward Sikh-period construction based on architectural style and ground level, explicitly noting they "seem to be unable to offer an exact date," while oral legend links the site to the mythic figure Loh/Lava, son of Rama, pre-dating any historical record. The two accounts (legendary origin vs. Sikh-period structure) are reported as found, not reconciled.
- **Restoration timeline:** the Walled City of Lahore Authority's own page states conservation "in 2021"; a January 2026 wire report via *The Tribune* (India) describes conservation as newly "completed" in collaboration with the Aga Khan Cultural Service, noting the site "had earlier undergone partial restoration in 2018." Three different restoration dates (2018 partial, 2021 per WCLA's page, 2026 "completed" per the 2026 report) appear across sources and are not reconciled here.
- **Whether the temple was ever used for worship:** *Dawn* (2018) states plainly: "Who built the Lava Temple? When? What was housed inside the Temple? Were any acts of worship ever performed there? These and many other questions remain unanswered, and we can only speculate." This directly complicates any claim (e.g. in the WCLA page or press coverage) that presents the Loh/Lava identification as settled fact.
