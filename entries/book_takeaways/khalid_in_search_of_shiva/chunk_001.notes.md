## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- Method: the book is "a product of several journeys, spread over a few years", for articles, research, social visits and recreation; travels are "not narrated in a chronological order" but combined "to suit a particular theme" (p. 6, Introduction, folio not stated).
- The practices "all represented an alternative version of practised Islam"; their common thread "was their connection to antiquity"; they "defied the dogma of the exclusivity of the two religions, Islam and Hinduism" and show "much borrowing and lending between different religious traditions" (p. 6, Introduction, folio not stated).
- Frame: the traditions are read "in the backdrop of the rising tide of religious puritanism and extremism in Pakistan; the creation of Pakistan, the two-nation theory and its economic development. All these factors affect how these shrines are perceived and survive" (p. 6, Introduction, folio not stated).
- Scope limit: research limited "around my hometown of Lahore"; "several areas and shrines that are unexplored" (p. 6, Introduction, folio not stated).
- Ch. 1: violent protests "end up reinforcing the very fact that they are protesting"; *Innocence of Muslims* "became one of the most watched movies on YouTube" (p. 7, ch. 1, folio not stated). "Muslims today face an intellectual challenge which can only be countered through reason, rationality and intellectual discourse" (p. 7, ch. 1, folio not stated).
- The effigy-hanging as "the voice of reason being dragged to death" (p. 7, ch. 1, folio not stated).
- Front matter: author "has an academic background in Anthropology from the Lahore University of Management and Sciences (LUMS)", travel writer and freelance journalist "since 2008", author of *A White Trail*; "This is his second book" (p. 1, front matter, folio not stated). Publisher "Rupa Publications India Pvt. Ltd 2015", "First impression 2015", eISBN "978-81-291-3902-3" (p. 3, front matter, folio not stated). Dedication to his teachers (p. 4, front matter, folio not stated).
