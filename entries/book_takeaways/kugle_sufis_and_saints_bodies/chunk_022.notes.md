## Doubts

**HEADLINE CAUTION. No page list in this chunk may be relied on without checking the printed book.**
An index is almost entirely bare numerals, and a locator run has no surrounding words to constrain
it, so every list above is reported exactly as printed and none has been normalised or repaired.
`221-2`-style truncations and lost or inserted spaces are endemic here (see the inventory), and a
single misread digit sends a reader to the wrong page. **Treat every locator above as unverified.**

1. **THE COLUMN TRAP — the largest error risk in this chunk, and it is live on both index pages.**
   The index is set in two columns and the extraction interleaves them: one output line carries the
   tail of a left-column entry and the head of a right-column one. The clearest instance is at the
   top of p. 365, where `Sultanpurl, Abd al-Nabl, 218-19` is followed on the same line by
   `66, 75, 78, 79,108,132,137,155,157,` — **that number run belongs to `Time`, the right-hand
   column's first entry, not to Sultanpurl, whose own list is the two digits `218-19`** (p. 365,
   folio 344). Another, which the brief anticipated: `Womb (rahim), 82, 93, 94, 237-38` and
   `Zuhur al-Haqq, Hajji, 138, 302 (n. 17)` share one line and are two separate entries in two
   separate columns (p. 366, folio 345). **Every list above was reassembled column-wise, not
   line-wise, and any reader re-deriving a list from the raw chunk must do the same.**
2. **The watermark cuts entries in half.** The EBSCO copyright line falls mid-column on p. 365
   (splitting `Temptation`, whose list resumes four lines later at `144,176-77,...`) and on p. 366
   (between `Wealth and property` and `Weber, Max`). `Temptation, 35-39, 91-92, 95,132,141, 144,
   176-77,185,189,190, 202, 205, 230-31, 235-36, 248, 251, 276, 283, 294` (p. 365, folio 344) is a
   reassembly across that break, not a printed run.
3. **`Zakariya, 138` is left unmapped and must stay unmapped until checked** (p. 366, folio 345). The
   headword is a bare name with no epithet, no dates and no place. It *could* be the archive's
   Bahauddin Zakariya of Multan (**shrine-of-bahauddin-zakariya**) — the row that would take it if a
   check of printed p. 138 identified him — or it could be the Qur'anic prophet or another figure
   entirely. **The index does not say, so nothing is added from general knowledge.** Note that
   `Zuhur al-Haqq, Hajji` carries the same locator 138, which does not help identify either.
4. **`Al-Tirmidhl, al-Haklm` is NOT the archive's Tirmizi row, and the refusal is deliberate**
