**What this chunk is.** Thirteen pages of pure back-matter apparatus. **pp. 518–527 (folios
487–496) are the `INDEX OF NAMES AND PLACES`**, continuing from PDF 510 and running to its end on
p. 527 (the I–Z stretch: the chunk opens mid-`I` at `Intiha?nama` and closes at `Zulaykha`).
**pp. 528–530 (folios 497–499) are the `INDEX OF SUBJECTS`**, which **begins on p. 528** with that
title standing alone above the `A` run and continues past the chunk's end. The task's structure
table is confirmed page by page from the running headers; there is **no bibliography matter
anywhere in pp. 518–530** (see `## Doubts`).

**Archive yield.** **32 archive ids**, all reached by the person rule (18 September) and the
many-row person sub-case (22 September), on **27 index entries and cross-references** that name a
person who is an archive row's principal figure. **Zero toponym mappings**: nine toponym-key hits
fire in this chunk and **all nine are declined** under brief §6(ii), because a bare index entry is a
pointer rather than a statement about the place. The declined ids are listed in full under
`## Doubts`, one line per place, so the decision reverses in one step.

**THE LOCATOR RULE, applied everywhere below.** Every number after a comma in an index entry is
**this book's own printed folio**, not a PDF page. **Not one of them has been converted into a
`(p. N)`, and 31 has been added to none of them.** Locator strings are quoted verbatim, exactly as
the text layer prints them, damage and all; the page reference on every bullet is the **PDF page the
entry is printed on**. Readers should assume every quoted locator is digit-damaged — see
`## Doubts` for the measured damage classes.

**Folio regime.** All thirteen pages carry a folio and **`folio = PDF − 31` holds on every one**.
Twelve are running-header folios and the parity self-check passes on all twelve: leading (verso)
folios 488, `49°` [text-layer?: 490], `4Q2` [text-layer?: 492], 494, 496, 498 are even; trailing
(recto) folios 487, 489, 491, 493, 495, 499 are odd. The thirteenth, **p. 528, is the
section-opening page of the `INDEX OF SUBJECTS` and prints its folio `497` alone at the foot**,
below the last entry and with no running header — the brief's foot-folio rule, confirmed again. The
foot was checked on every page before any `folio not stated` was considered, and none was needed.

## Doubts

**1. The locator rule, and what it cost.** Every number quoted above from an index entry is the
book's own printed folio. **None was converted to a PDF page and 31 was added to none of them.**
This is the single largest thing a human must not undo: an entry like `Multan, 67, 82, 344, 352,
353, 354, 433` (p. 521, folio `49°` [text-layer?: 490]) tells you seven folios of this book, in a
digit class this run measured as the second worst of the seventeen chunks, and those folios are
useless as facts until someone reads the pages they point to. The page reference on every bullet is
the PDF page the entry is **printed on**.

in this chunk, so the total-and-mechanical exemption does not apply and every damaged token above
