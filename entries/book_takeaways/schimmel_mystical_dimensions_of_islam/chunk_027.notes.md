# schimmel_mystical_dimensions_of_islam — chunk 027 — pp. 500–517

**What this chunk is.** Eighteen pages of pure back matter, and the structure given in the task is
confirmed page by page from the running headers and section titles actually printed:
**`A D D E N D U M TO` / `THE BIBLIOGRAPHY`, pp. 500–504** (folios 469–473); **p. 505, which prints
only `This page intentionally left blank`** (folio not stated); **`INDEX OF KORANIC` /
`QUOTATIONS`, pp. 506–507** (folios 475–476); **`INDEX OF PROPHETIC` / `TRADITIONS`, pp. 508–509**
(folios 477–478); and **`INDEX OF NAMES` / `AND PLACES`, pp. 510–517** (folios 479–486), which
begins on p. 510 and is **still running at the chunk's end**, cut off inside the letter I at
`Interior Castle (St. Theresa), 61` (p. 517, folio 486). **There is no bibliography proper anywhere
in this chunk** — the Addendum is already under way on p. 500 and ends on p. 504 with
`Weischer, Bernd Manuel, and Wilson, Peter L. Heart's Witness: The Sufi Quatrains of Auhaduddin
Kirmani. London, 1978.` (p. 504, folio 473). The older claim that the bibliography ran to PDF 527 is
**contradicted by the pages themselves**; see `## Doubts`.

**Archive yield.** **Thirteen archive rows**, every one of them reached through the **person rule**
and every one of them resting on an **index entry**, not on running prose: `bhit-bhit-shah`,
`rahman-baba-mausoleum-rehman-baba-shrine`, `shrine-of-akhund-darweza-baba`,
`shrine-of-shah-abdul-karim-bulri`, `shrine-of-makhdoom-abdul-rahim-girhori`,
`shrine-of-bahauddin-zakariya`, `mazar-of-bulleh-shah`, `shrine-of-fariduddin-ganjshakar`,
`data-darbar`, `dargah-roza-sufi-shah-inayat-shaheed`, `shrine-of-sachal-sarmast`,
`shri-panchmukhi-hanuman-mandir-karachi`, `tomb-of-qutbuddin-aibak`. **Zero toponym mappings**:
every place in this chunk is either a publisher's imprint city or a bare index entry, and under
brief §6 (ii) **neither fires ruling (a)**. The ids declined are listed, one line each, under
`## Doubts`, so a reversal is one step.

**The locator rule, and what it means for every number below.** Not one number quoted in these notes
is a PDF page. The index entries print **this book's own printed folios**, the `AM no. N` citations
print **hadith numbers in another book** (see the headnote finding on p. 508), and the Addendum's
parenthesised numbers are **years of publication**. Every locator string below is quoted **verbatim
as printed, damage and all**, is named in words as the book's own index locator, and the page
reference attached to it is **the PDF page the entry is printed on**. Nothing has been converted,
and 31 has been added to nothing.

**Folio regime — checked on every page, zero violations.** `folio = PDF − 31` holds on all sixteen
non-blank pages. Four of them are **section-opening pages that print the folio alone at the foot**,
exactly as brief §1 predicts, and the foot was checked before any `folio not stated` was considered:
`469` at the foot of p. 500, `475` at the foot of p. 506, `477` at the foot of p. 508, `479` at the
foot of p. 510. The other twelve print the folio in a running header and the **measured parity
self-check passes on all twelve**: leading (verso) folios 470, 472, 476, 478, 480, 482, 484, 486 are
even; trailing (recto) folios 471, 473, 481, 483, 485 are odd. Running headers are apparatus and are
not quoted below as content. **p. 505 prints no folio and none was invented.**

## Arguments and interpretations

- **Schimmel states, in her own voice, why the Addendum exists and what the field did after 1975:**
  `Since this book first appeared in 1975, many new works about various aspects of Sufism have been
  published, particularly in connection with Ibn 'Arabl.` [text-layer?: Ibn ʿArabī] (p. 500,
  folio 469).
- She records a German edition of this same book, in her own translation: `A somewhat enlarged
  German edition of this book, Mystische Dimensionen des Islam (Cologne, 1986), appeared in my own
  translation.` (p. 500, folio 469). This is the only first-person sentence in the whole chunk.
- The Ibn ʿArabī claim is borne out by the Addendum's own contents on the same and following pages:
  `Addas, Claude. Ibn 'Arabl ou La Quete du Soufre Rouge. Paris, 1988.`,
  `Chittick, William. The Sufi Path of Knowledge: The Spiritual Teachings of Ibn al-'Arabi. Albany,
  1989.`, `Chodkiewicz, Michel. Le Sceau des Saints: Prophetie et saintete dans la doctrine d'Ibn
  'Arabl. Paris, 1986.` (p. 500, folio 469), `Austin, R. W. G. The Bezels of Wisdom. New York, 1980.`
  (p. 500, folio 469) and `Deladriere, Roger. La Profession de Foi d'Ibn 'Arabl. Paris, 1978.`
  (p. 501, folio 470).
- **A siglum the brief could not place is resolved here, by the book itself.** The headnote to
  `INDEX OF PROPHETIC TRADITIONS` reads, entire: `(The AM citation gives the location of the full
  Arabic text of the tradition in Badicuz-Zaman Furuzanfar's Aftadlth-i Mathnawl [Tehran, 1334
  sh./ig55].)` [text-layer?: Badīʿuz-Zamān Furūzānfar's Aḥādīth-i Mathnawī; `ig55` = 1955] (p. 508,
  folio 477). **`AM` is therefore a hadith-number citation in Furūzānfar's collection, not a page of
  this book and not a year** — it is the one token brief §3 listed as unplaced. See `## Doubts` for
  the internal inconsistency in how the index prints it.
- The Addendum also notes an ongoing project rather than a finished book, under `Nurbakhsh`:
  `A Sufi encyclopedia in many volumes is currently be-` / `ing translated from Persian into
  English.` (p. 502, folio 471).

## Doubts


**Structure and boundaries — checked against what the eighteen pages actually print.**

- **The task's corrected map holds exactly, on every boundary.** `A D D E N D U M TO` /
  `THE BIBLIOGRAPHY` opens on p. 500 and its running header carries that title on pp. 501–504; the
  last Addendum entry is `Weischer … London, 1978.` on p. 504 (folio 473); p. 505 prints only
  `This page intentionally left blank`; `INDEX OF KORANIC` / `QUOTATIONS` occupies pp. 506–507 and
  closes with `Sura 112, confession of God's unity, 176` (p. 507, folio 476); `INDEX OF PROPHETIC` /
  `TRADITIONS` occupies pp. 508–509; `INDEX OF NAMES` / `AND PLACES` opens on p. 510 and runs to the
  chunk's end.
- **The superseded claim that the bibliography ran to PDF 527 is contradicted by these pages and I
  am not smoothing it.** No bibliography entry of the main bibliography appears anywhere in
  pp. 500–517; p. 500 is already the Addendum, and pp. 506 onward are indexes. A human should
  confirm that no worker downstream is still reading the uncorrected figure.
- **The brief's foot-folio rule for section-opening pages is confirmed four times over** —
  `469`/p. 500, `475`/p. 506, `477`/p. 508, `479`/p. 510 — and the foot was checked on each before
  `folio not stated` was considered. Only p. 505 needed it.
- The `INDEX OF NAMES AND PLACES` **continues past this chunk**; its entries for the letters J–Z
  (including, on the evidence of the two cross-references above, the main `Sachal Sarmast` and
  `Qutbuddln Aybek` entries, and any `Multan`, `Lahore`, `Sehwan` or `Uch` place entries) are **not
  visible here** and must be read in chunks 028–029.

**Archive mapping — what a human should weigh.**

- **Every one of the thirteen mappings rests on an index entry and on nothing else.** Not one of
  them is supported by a sentence of running prose anywhere in this chunk. Under the 18 September
  person ruling that is a legitimate mapping, but the material each row gains is thin by design: a
  headword, a death date where the index gives one, and a list of folios elsewhere in the book.
  **The value of this chunk is navigational** — it tells the consolidator which folios of this book
  to go back to for each figure.
- **Thinnest four, in order.** `tomb-of-qutbuddin-aibak` and `shrine-of-sachal-sarmast` rest on
  **cross-references with no locator at all**; `shri-panchmukhi-hanuman-mandir-karachi` rests on the
  two-word entry `Hanuman, 394`; `shrine-of-akhund-darweza-baba` rests on `Akhund Darwaza (d. 1638),
  398`. Any of the four could reasonably be demoted by the consolidator.
- **Three printed death dates disagree with the archive rows, and both readings are kept:**
  `^bdu^r-Rahman (Rahman Baba)` is printed `(d. 1709)` against the row's 1711 (p. 510, folio 479);
  `Bullhe Shah` is printed `(d. 1752)` against the row's 1757 (p. 513, folio 482);
  `Fariduddin Ganj-i Shakar` is printed `(d. 1265)` against the row's 1266 (p. 514, folio 483). Two
  more are hedged by the book itself: `Baha^uddln Zakariya Multani (d. ca.` / `1267)` and
  `Hujwiri … (d. ca. 1071)`. **Given this chunk's measured digit damage, none of these three
  disagreements should be treated as a substantive variant without a look at the printed page.**
- **`Bullhe Shah (d. 1752)` and `Abdu3l-Lat!f Bhita3!, Shah (d. 1752)` print the identical year.**
  Two pages apart, in the same index, in a chunk where `!` stands for `1` ten times. Flagged.
- **`Bilawal, 395` (p. 513, folio 482) was NOT mapped, and this is a real judgement call.** The
  archive has `shah-noorani-shrine-syed-bilawal-shah-noorani`, principal figure Syed Bilawal Shah
  Noorani. The index prints the bare word `Bilawal` with no qualifier, no date and no place, and its
  locator `395` sits in the run of folios the index elsewhere assigns to Sindhi poets
  (`Bullhe Shah, 388, 395`; `Bulrri, 397`; `lnayat Shahid … 395, 397`). **The chunk gives nothing to
  decide identity on**, and RULE 2 forbids supplying it. Recorded under `## Other`; a human should
  rule.
- **`Ayaz, 190, 292` (p. 512, folio 481) was NOT mapped, for the same reason.** The archive has
  `darbar-malik-ahmad-ayaz`. The index prints `Ayaz` with no gloss whatever — no Maḥmūd, no Ghazna,
  no Lahore — and prints a **second, clearly different** `Ayaz, Sheikh, 390 n` four lines away.
  Identity cannot be established from this chunk; a human should rule.
- **`lmaduddin (disciple of Multani), 353` (p. 517, folio 486).** Its locator falls inside
  `Baha^uddln Zakariya Multani`'s `352-54`, which is why it is recorded under that row — but **the
  index does not say which Multānī**, and the inference is mine from the locator, not the book's
  statement. Weigh accordingly.
- **The two Quṭbuddīns and the two Farīduddīns are both present in this chunk and were kept apart.**
  `Aybek. See Qutbuddln Aybek` (p. 512, folio 481) vs. `Bakhtiyar Kaki, Qutbuddin (d. 1235), 346`
  (p. 513, folio 482); `Fariduddin Ganj-i Shakar` (p. 514, folio 483) vs. the long
  `Attar, Fariduddin (d. 1220)` entry (p. 512, folio 481). Neither pair was merged.
- **`lnayat Shahid, Shah (d. 1718)` vs. `lnat, Mian Shah (d. early i8th century)`** — two entries,
  four lines apart on p. 517 (folio 486). Only the first was mapped, on the strength of the epithet

- **No Sikh, Hindu-temple or Nanakpanthi row is reachable from this chunk** other than `Hanuman`.
  The index has not yet reached the letters under which Nanak, Ranjit Singh, Shiva or Valmiki would

**Damage inventory — this chunk, measured classes. This is the worst-damaged chunk of the
seventeen, and every locator quoted above is suspect.**

- **`°` inside a number — the run measured 18 firings in this chunk, and it is UNRESOLVABLE.** This
  run has proved `°` stands for **both 0 and 8** in this book (header `44°` = folio 440, header
  `45°` = folio 458), and **no `°` in these notes has been silently resolved**. Instances carried
  above include `2°8` (p. 506, folio 475), `25°` (p. 511, folio 480), `4°7` and `4°3` (p. 511, folio
  480), `4°i` and `37°` (p. 513, folio 482), `43°` (p. 514, folio 483), `34°` (p. 516, folio 485),
  `39°` and `29°` (p. 517, folio 486).
- **`°` in this chunk is worse than ambiguous between two digits — it also stands for letters.** It
  renders the **ʿayn** in `°Ad, 198` [text-layer?: ʿĀd] (p. 510, folio 479) and it renders the
  **letter o** in `l^e eYe °f certainty, 142` [text-layer?: the eye of certainty] (p. 507, folio

  single most important thing on this page for a human to check.
