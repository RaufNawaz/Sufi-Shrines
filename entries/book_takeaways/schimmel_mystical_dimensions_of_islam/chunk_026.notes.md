of Prophetic traditions and no Index of Names and Places appears anywhere in this chunk — they lie

at any point, per the task).

**How this chunk was handled.** Per the task, the bibliography is **not transcribed**. Recorded here,
quoted as printed and with the PDF page each is printed on, are (1) every entry concerning **South
Asian Sufism, Sindhi / Punjabi / Pashto / Urdu / Siraiki poetry, or shrines, saints and pilgrimage**;
(2) every entry whose **author or subject is the principal figure of an archive row**; (3) the one
item that is not a bibliography entry at all (the blank-page line). **134 entries are recorded.** On a
hand count of the twenty-three pages (no tool used; approximate) the bibliography runs to roughly
**550 entries**, so **roughly 415–420 entries were passed over** — European and Middle Eastern
scholarship on Arabic, Persian, Turkish, Egyptian, Andalusian, Balkan and Southeast Asian Sufism

South Asian, Sindhi/Punjabi/Pashto/Urdu and no shrine-or-pilgrimage subject and no archive figure as
author or subject.

**Archive yield.** **Eight archive rows by the person rule** (18 September), all on author-or-subject
namings, plus **one place-level bullet under ruling (a)**. **Every publisher's imprint city was
declined** under brief §6(ii) — `lahore` 20 times, `karachi` 13, `hyderabad` 12 as I count them,
`peshawar` once — and the declined ids are listed one line per place under `## Doubts` so a reversal

**Folio regime.** Every one of the twenty-three bibliography pages prints a running header and
**`folio = PDF − 31` holds on all twenty-three**, with the measured parity self-check passing on all
