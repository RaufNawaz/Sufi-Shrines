## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- **Schimmel dates the first real orders against Abū Saʿīd's destroyed tomb**: `By that time, the
  first real Sufi fraternities had come into existence—namely, the Suhrawardiyya and the Qadiriyya,

  (p. 276, folio 245) — quoted in full above. This is the chunk's central claim about Suhrawardī

  rites are given as the reason `why the Badawiyya never crossed the borders of its homeland`
  (p. 280, folio 249).

  the reason for Maqqari's (d. 1631) somewhat unfair statement: "Faqirhood as it is current in the

  unfair` in her own voice.

  order` (p. 282, folio 251).

  Hikam's content as `a "dialectic of the mystery of God Who is both obscure and transparent."`
