## Doubts

- **The page carries two ISBN-shaped strings, and the run brief accounted for only one of them.**
  The brief named the foot-of-page barcode rendering "9   780226V00922" as the measured string;
  the page also prints, on its own line above the barcode, "ISBN 0-226-70092-5" (p. 290, folio not
  stated). That second string is undamaged *in appearance*, but appearance is not verification: a
  text layer that turned a barcode digit into a capital V can equally have altered a digit in a
  clean-looking line without leaving a mark. **I have not reported either string as the book's
  ISBN, have not reconciled them against each other, and have supplied nothing from general
  knowledge.** A human must check both against the scan image before either enters the source line.
- **The reviewers' quotations are already abridged by the publisher, with the elisions rendered
  erratically by the text layer.** Four ellipses appear as runs of widely spaced periods —
  "conversations. .                   .   .   This work is a genuine tour de force"; "Superbly
  documented.       .   .   .   Sufi Music"; "... A welcome addition"; "practice in India and
  Pakistan.           .   .   .   The whole is a most impressive work" (p. 290, folio not stated).
  Each marks text the jacket cut, so **no endorsement on this page is a continuous sentence from
  its review**, and none should be cited as one.
- **One sentence is word-order damaged in the text layer.** The Chen endorsement is printed as
  "but also how music                          —perhaps   all musics   — informed by," with the
  word "is" displaced onto a line of its own below it, before "shaped by, and interacts with the
