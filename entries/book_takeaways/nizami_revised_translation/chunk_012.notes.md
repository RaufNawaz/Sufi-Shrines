## Doubts
- darbar-malik-ahmad-ayaz: person-level identification. The book's Ayaz is Mahmud's favourite (per the brief, Anecdote XIV); this index entry says only "(xi)". It does not mention Lahore, a governorship or a tomb. The meaning of the "(xi)"-type markers is not explained in this chunk.
- Bare Lahore mentions, no bullet (they do not count as material): "Lahore, 117" (p. 192, folio 174); "Jalandar (near Lahore), 117" (p. 191, folio 173). The Lahore material is in chunk 008's Notes, not here.
- "Sind, 20" (p. 197, folio 179) is an index locator. Per the brief it points to Lamghan's "district of Sind" (12th-century geography). No province bullet.
- False friends, not mapped: "Sindibad-nama" (p. 197); "Ibn Sfna. See Avicenna" (p. 197); "Kamilu's-Sina'at" (p. 191); "Abu Sa'd b. Hindu of Isfahan" (p. 196). No "Muslim", Kashfu'l-Mahjub, Hujwiri, Multan, Uch, Punjab, Karachi or Peshawar in this chunk.
- "Muhammad … — Iqbal (fl. 1921), 28 n." (p. 194, folio 176): only the name matches, and the index gives no identifying gloss. Not mapped to mazar-e-iqbal (RULE 2).
- Text damage (text_layer): there are digit errors in locators and dates (`i`/`I`→1, `o`→0, `n`/`in`/`no` for 111/110, "son." etc.). Macrons are mangled (`f`/`l`/`i`/`d` for long vowels), so all marked names carry `[text-layer?]`. Two-column interleaving scrambles many entries (e.g. Daqiqi, Hamid, Qanun, Ta'rikh entries), and entries were read only where their own line is coherent. Stray scan marks (• » « * ' ^ t) were dropped silently.
