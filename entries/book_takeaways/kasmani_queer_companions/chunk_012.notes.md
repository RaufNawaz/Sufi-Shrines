# kasmani_queer_companions — chunk 012 — pp. 217–225

The last chunk of the book. It contains the **tail of the References** (p. 217, folio 200, from
`Werbner, Pnina, and Helene Basu` to `Zayed, Ahmed A.`) and then **the whole Index**, pp. 218–225,
which opens on p. 218 with the word `Index`, three blank lines and the rubric `Note: Page numbers in
italics refer to figures.` (p. 218, folio not stated) and closes at the foot of p. 225 with the
`Zaheda (fakir)` entry, whose last subheading is `terms of address used for, 95` (p. 225, folio 208).
**The book ends there.** There is no colophon, no about-the-author page and no further matter in this
chunk.

**THE LOCATOR NUMBERS IN THIS CHUNK ARE THE BOOK'S PRINTED FOLIOS, NOT PDF PAGE INDICES, AND THEY
MUST NEVER BE CONVERTED.** When the index says `Lal's burial, 146` it means printed page 146, not PDF
page 146. Every index locator below is quoted exactly as printed and is left in the book's own
numbering. A reader who wants the PDF page for an index locator must apply the book's folio relation
themselves and say that they have done so; **nothing in these notes is converted, and nothing should
be converted at consolidation either.** The same holds for the note-form locators (`169n53`,
`177nn31 – 32`): the number before the `n` is a folio of the endnotes, not a PDF page.

**Folio. Every page in this chunk except p. 218 prints its folio, and every printed folio was read off
its own page.** p. 217 `200      References`; p. 219 `202       Index`; p. 220 `Index         203`;
p. 221 `204       Index`; p. 222 `Index         205`; p. 223 `206       Index`; p. 224
`Index          207`; p. 225 `208       Index`. Odd PDF pages print the folio first, even PDF pages
print it last, exactly as briefed, and **p. 225 is odd and prints folio-first (`208       Index`),
confirming the parity rule right to the last page of the book.** All eight agree with PDF − 17 with
no exception. **p. 218 prints no folio anywhere in its text layer** — I read the whole page, both
columns and the trailing lines, not just the head — and it is therefore cited throughout as
**(p. 218, folio not stated)**. The −17 relation would give 201 and p. 219's printed `202`
corroborates that the Index begins at folio 201, **but 201 is not printed and is not asserted here**;
a suppressed running head on the opening page of a section is the ordinary typographic explanation
and needs no rule change. This is a measured result for the "does the index print folios" question:
**seven of the index's eight pages print one.**

**The text layer interleaves the index's two columns line by line, and this chunk documents it
precisely.** On every index page, one physical text line carries the left column's line followed by
whitespace followed by the right column's line. The consequence is that **an entry which runs over
the foot of the left column continues at the TOP of the same page's right column, and in the
extracted text those continuation lines appear ABOVE the entry they belong to.** This happens on
four of the eight index pages and is reconstructed below with the splice point named each time.
See Doubts for the two locator strings this endangers.

**Route is text_layer**, so damaged strings carry `[text-layer?]`, never `[OCR?]`. There are no
transcriber flags in the source; everything marked below was found by reading.
