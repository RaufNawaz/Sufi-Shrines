# rizvi_history_of_sufism_india_1 — chunk 028 — pp. 484–491

**This chunk is the tail of the book's alphabetical index and contains nothing else: no running prose, no footnotes, no plates, no appendix, no colophon.** What follows is a finding aid, not a transcription. It opens at the top of a verso, mid-S, with `Salar Shahu, 313` (p. 484, folio 460) and closes with `Zurkan, 33` (p. 491, folio 467). **The alphabetical range covered is Salar Shahu → Zurkan** — the back half of the S-run (from "Sal-") through to the end of Z. **This is where the book ends.** p. 491 is the final PDF page of the 491-page volume; its last printed line is the headword `Zurkan, 33` (p. 491, folio 467), and after it there is no end-matter, no publisher's colophon, no errata and no "end of index" rule — the index simply stops. **The index is therefore covered to its end by this chunk, and the consolidator needs no further chunk for it.**

**Correction to the chunk brief's folio regime, with the evidence, because it governs every citation below.** The brief predicted that index rectos print only the word `Index` with no number and are therefore "folio not stated". **They do print their folio.** The index is set in two columns, and tesseract reads column 1, then the header furniture, then column 2, so on a recto the folio surfaces in the middle of the OCR rather than at its head. Read off the page, one by one: `460` (p. 484), `461` (p. 485), `462` (p. 486), `463` (p. 487), `464` (p. 488), `465` (p. 489), `466` (p. 490), `467` (p. 491). ## Doubts

**The source carries no transcriber apparatus — no `[OCR?]`, no `[illegible]` and no `[folio N]` lines — so every inline flag above and below is mine, and items 13–19 are the damage-class inventory for pp. 484–491 (folios 460–467), per interim convention (b), applied in both forms.** An index of transliterated names and dense digit-strings is this route's worst case and the damage below is heavier than chunk 023's.

**Archive-mapping doubts, most consequential first:**

1. **`Shaikh Baha‘u’d-Din, 263` (p. 485, folio 461) must not be read as `shrine-of-bahauddin-zakariya`, and this is the trap most likely to be sprung by a consolidator working fast.** The index gives the headword no nisba, no dates, no Multan and no order — all (not stated). Two things weigh against the identification and neither is decisive on its own, which is why the entry is unmapped rather than either claimed or dismissed: **the index has no `Zakariyya` headword at all** (the Z-run runs `Zakat, 146, 221` straight into `Zakhiratu'l-Muluk, 293, 295-97`, p. 491, folio 467), and **folio 263 sits inside a cluster of fifteenth-century men in the same column** — `Shaikh Abu'l-Fath (b. 1370, d. 1454), 262-63`, `Shaikh Adhan Jaunpuri, 263`, `Shaikh ‘Ali (d. 1499-1500), 263`, `Shaikh Muhammad ‘Isa, 263, 367` (p. 485, folio 461) — whereas the index's own `Suhrawardiyya` entry puts that order's main treatment at `190-240` (p. 487, folio 463). **I cannot see folio 263 and say nothing about what is on it. The evidence here is an index headword only.**
2. **`Shaikh Kabir b.
