- Syed Ismail's Friday preaching: "دو پچاس[OCR?]" the first Friday, "سو پچاس[OCR?]" the second, "پانچ سو[OCR?]" Hindus the third became Muslim; "مہتاب" as his chronogram, value "چار سو اٹھتالیس[OCR?]" (p. 223).
