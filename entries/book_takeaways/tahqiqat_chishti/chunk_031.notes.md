# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 031 — pp. 346–355

The chunk opens mid-section (p. 346) with a damaged description of a well, an enclosure, a "قبرستان بھٹیاریاں [OCR?]" and a faqir Ghulam Rasul. **The heading of that section is in the previous chunk (030).** Five titled sections follow. "حال میدان زین خان" (pp. 346–348) covers the Maidan Zain Khan outside Mochi gate and the takia of Rukn al-Din Sain, with its Chishti faqirs, sajjada nashins, death dates, urs dates, grant and silsila. "احوال مکان شاہ کمیٹہ [OCR?] صاحب" (pp. 348–349) covers a small shrine at the corner of Shah Abul Maali's tomb, which the book says it wrote from oral report only. "احوال مکان ڈھایہ بیوی وڈی [OCR?]" (pp. 349–351) covers Haji Ismail Shah and Bibi Wadi, with the tree-and-chadar karamat involving Hazrat Mauj Darya Bukhari. "احوال تکیہ لوہاراں" (pp. 351–352) is a short takia entry. "احوال مقبرہ عالیہ حضرت بی بی پاک دامناں[OCR?] صاحبہ[OCR?]" (p. 352) gives the Bibis' names and the story of their coming to Lahore. Pages 353–355 describe graves, a mosque and inscriptions around a "خانقاہ" with no new heading, so they are read as still in the Bibi Pak Daman section. The chunk breaks off mid-sentence on p. 355. Six archive rows are mapped.

## Legends and miracles (karamat)

- **Bibi Wadi and Mauj Darya Bukhari** (pp. 350–351):
  - A tree at her house was too tall for anyone to climb. When she wanted to dry her chadar, the tree "فی الفور" bowed (the passage on p. 350 is garbled). After she put the chadar on it, it rose again so high that it could be seen from Mauj Darya's khanqah (pp. 350–351).
  - Seeing the chadar, Mauj Darya Bukhari came home angry, thinking she had climbed the tree and been seen by outsiders (p. 351).
  - She smiled and answered that it was not the custom of her ancestors for women to climb trees: "بلکہ یہ درخت اللہ کی حکم سے پست ہوگیا" (rather this tree bowed by God's command) (p. 351).
  - He told her to take the chadar down the same way. She asked the tree to bow again, and "بمجرد[OCR?] فرمانے کے درخت پست ہوگیا" (p. 351).
  - She said: "یہ کرامت تم کو ورثہ اپنے اجداد کرام کا ہے۔ ایسی خفگی فرمانا لازم نہیں تھا" (p. 351).
  - Then, "بحالت خفگی", it was declared that from that day there should be no marriage between the Bukhari and Gilani sayyids: "حتی الآن[OCR?] موقوف ہی ہے" (it remains stopped to this day) (p. 351).
- **Shah Kamaita**: a garbled line says something happened whenever someone tried to settle or build at the ruined grave ("جب کوئی آبی [OCR?] او دی [OCR?] کرتا تہا تو ابد ابہو نچتی [OCR?] تہی"). It is too damaged to say what (p. 349).
- **Bibi Pak Daman**: the Bibis were told by Imam Husain that leaving was "حکم مرتضوی", to be confirmed "مراقبہ کر کے" (p. 352).

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- **Shah Kamaita**: the author states his source. "کسی کتاب میں حال انکا تحریر نہیں صرف زبانی … جو حال سنا سو تحریر ہوا" (his account is not written in any book; only what was heard by word of mouth has been written) (p. 349).
- **Rukn al-Din takia**: the information on Darya Khan's garden is attributed to Chaudhri Rakha, "چودہری رکھا میاں کرتا ہی کہ میں نے یہی دیکھا ہی" (Chaudhri Rakha says he saw it himself) (p. 348).

- "چنانچہ اوس روز سے فیمابین سادات گیلانیان و بخاریان درمیان[OCR?] رشتہ داری موقوف ہوگئی اور حتی الآن[OCR?] موقوف ہی ہے" — "So from that day marriage ties between the Gilani and Bukhari sayyids stopped, and they remain stopped to this day." (p. 351)
- "حتی کہ لاہور میں آ پہنچیں اور یہاں بمقام خانقاہ[OCR?] ایک ٹیلہ تھا۔ اوس پر آ ٹھہریں[OCR?]" — of the Bibis: "Until they reached Lahore; at the khanqah's site there was a mound, and they stopped on it." (p. 352)
- "کسی کتاب میں حال انکا تحریر نہیں صرف زبانی … جو حال سنا سو تحریر ہوا" — of Shah Kamaita: "His account is written in no book; what was heard orally has been written down." (p. 349)

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** Damage is heavy on pp. 346–350 (dense `[OCR?]`) and pp. 352–355 (dense `[illegible]`). Pages 351 and part of 352 are cleaner. Every quotation above keeps its flags, and readings offered in brackets are mine. The printed folios wander (348, 350, 351, 352, then 347–351 again from p. 351). They are not cited, per the brief.
- **Opening fragment (p. 346)** belongs to a section headed in chunk 030. Its subject cannot be placed from this chunk.
- **bibi-pak-daman, extent.** No new heading appears between p. 352 and the chunk's end. The khanqah, mosque and graves on pp. 353–355 are read as still in the Bibi Pak Daman section only because of that, and because of the "ٹیلہ"/"خانقاہ" wording (pp. 352, 355). This should be checked against the PDF. The heading's "پاک دامناں[OCR?]" is flagged.
- **bibi-pak-daman, names.** "بی بی حاج[OCR?]", "عقیل[OCR?]", "عباس[OCR?]" and "شہر بانو[OCR?]" are flagged (p. 352). The text calls the five both "صاحبزادیاں حضرت عقیل[OCR?]" and "ہمشیرگان حضرت مسلم". Neither is reconciled here.
- **shrine-of-mauj-darya-bukhari.** The name is printed three ways: "میج [OCR?] دریا بنجارہی [OCR?]" (p. 349), "مکسیتہ [OCR?] میراں محمد شاہ دریا کارہی [OCR?]" (p. 350) and the clean "حضرت موج دریا بخاری" (p. 351). The mapping rests on the clean p. 351 form plus "میراں محمد شاہ". "روحہ [OCR?]" is read as زوجہ (wife), which is my reading, not printed. Who declares the marriage ban on p. 351 is ambiguous ("آپ نے بحالت خفگی فرمایا" follows "بیوی صاحبہ … رنجیدہ ہوئی تھیں"). It reads most naturally as Bibi Wadi, but this is not certain.
- **darbar-abul-muali-qadri.** It is mapped on the name "شاہ ابوالمعالی صاحب" plus its position outside Mochi gate (pp. 346, 348, 352). The chunk gives no "Qadri", full name or date, and "ابوالمعالی[OCR?]" is flagged on p. 352. The chunk does not describe the tomb itself here. Weigh accordingly.
- **madho-lal-hussain** is mapped on a flagged bare name ("مادھو [OCR?] لال حسین"), used only as a location for another saint's mazar (p. 348).
- **shrine-of-fariduddin-ganjshakar** is mapped by the person rule, on a bare name in a silsila (p. 348).
- **ranmal-sharif** is mapped by the person rule on the bare name "حضرت نوشہ صاحب" (p. 349). The chunk gives no "Ganj Bakhsh", place or date. The identification is by name only; weigh accordingly.
- **Shalimar is not mapped.** "ساون بھادوں شالامار [OCR?]" (p. 347) is a comparison for a feature at the takia, not material on any site. The `shalimar gardens` toponym (madho-lal-hussain) is not attached.
- **Anarkali is not mapped.** "انارکلی کی طرف" (p. 346) is a road direction, so the 3 `anarkali` ids are not attached.
- **Uch is not mapped.** "اوجی [OCR?] سید گیلانی" / "اوج [OCR?] سی" (p. 350) is a non-archive figure's origin, and both words are flagged, so the 5 `uch sharif` ids are not attached. This is recorded for the consolidator to weigh against ruling (a).
- **No Lahore-in-general bullet.** "لاہور" appears only as the place the Bibis reached (p. 352), where descendants live (p. 354), in "لاہوری" nisbas and in "کوتوال بلدۂ لاہور" (p. 355). None of this is material on the city as such, so the 35 `lahore` ids are not attached.
- **No province bullet.** "فنانشل کمشنر بہادر ممالک پنجاب" (p. 349) is an official's title used as a landmark, not a statement about Punjab.
- **"مہاراج" (p. 355)** is unnamed, so samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh is not mapped. "عہد سکہاں [OCR?]" (p. 348) is carried as printed.
- **Dates, all flagged numerals; do not compute**:
  - The sajjada death years "سنہ ۹۸ [OCR?]", "سنہ ۱۳ [OCR?]" (twice) and "سنہ ۱۶ [OCR?] ۱۳ [OCR?]" (p. 348).
  - Shah Kamaita: "۳ [OCR?] ربیع الاول سنہ ۱۹ [OCR?]" and Ilahi Shah's "سنہ ۱۲ [OCR?]" (p. 349).
  - Inscriptions: "۱۰[OCR?]" and "۱۱۶[OCR?]" (p. 353).
  - "۳۰[OCR?] سال" (p. 354).
- **Name inconsistency.** The third sajjada is "محمد عبد الخالق" on p. 347 but "محمد ابوالخالق" on p. 348.
- **Held identity questions**: neither shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh nor langer-makhdoom is named in this chunk.
- **Darbars on the site but not in the index**: neither Shah Gohar Peer nor Mian Qurban Ali Shah is named. "حضرت بی بی گوہر" (p. 352) is one of the Bibis, not Shah Gohar Peer.
