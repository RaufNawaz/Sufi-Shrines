# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 028 — pp. 313–323

The chunk opens mid-account of the khanqah and tomb complex of Hazrat Mian Mir. **No section heading appears in this chunk**, so the heading is presumably in an earlier chunk. It first describes the buildings: the vaulted chamber over the khanqah, the tomb floor and the marble that Maharaja Ranjit Singh removed, the tomb of Khwaja Bihari, a mosque and cistern, and the ruined tank and baradari of Nadira Begum (pp. 313–315). It then tells the history of the land and the village: Dara Shikoh's purchase of the land, the destruction of Hashim Pura, the walled garden of Mulla Shah becoming a refuge, and the settling of the village of Mian Mir (pp. 315–317). After that it gives the custodian family's genealogy, the division of the shrine's income into shares, and the succession of numberdars down to the time the book was written (pp. 317–320). Two titled pieces close the chunk. The first is "نقل مہورت[OCR?] اس حال کی جو وقت حجر[OCR?] حصہ تقسیم جسس[OCR?] تحریر پر ہوئی تہی", a Persian petition-and-testimony document with the seals of the sajjada nashins of other Lahore shrines (pp. 320–321), followed by a note on a wedding fee (p. 321). The second is "نقشہ مزارات", a table of the graves in and around the rauza (pp. 322–323). Pages are badly damaged, and pp. 314, 318 and parts of 316 and 320 are largely garbled.

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- The author gives a motive for Ranjit Singh's removal of the marble: "بسبب بقصد[OCR?] مذہبی". The phrase is flagged, and its sense ("for a religious purpose") is uncertain (p. 313).
- The author presents Dara Shikoh's purchase as a scrupulous one, made so that no one could say he took the land by force (p. 316).

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** Damage is very heavy: dense `[OCR?]` on almost every proper name and numeral, scattered `[illegible]`, and `[margin]` notes. Pp. 314 and 318, and parts of pp. 316 and 320, are near-unreadable word salad. Facts were taken only from legible runs, and every quotation keeps its flags. The folios printed in this chunk (308–316) are not cited, per the brief.
- **Section heading missing.** The chunk opens mid-account of Mian Mir's complex. The heading should be in an earlier chunk, so check against chunk 027.
- **Zero Lahore-in-general bullet.** Lahore appears only as a destination ("لاہور مین آکر سکونت"), an official ("باطلم[OCR?] لاہور") and "نواحی لاہور" in the seal list. None of these is material on the city as such, so the 35 `lahore` ids are not attached.
- **Multan is not mapped.** "ملتان[OCR?]" (p. 316) is a flagged word in a garbled sentence about someone's posting as hakim. It is not Multan material, so the 8 `multan` ids are not attached.
- **Amritsar** (p. 313) is outside Pakistan and maps to no row.
- **Mochi Darwaza** (p. 317) is named only as a residence. It is not mapped to darbar-abul-muali-qadri, whose location text mentions Mochi Darwaza.
- **samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh** is mapped by the person rule. The material concerns Ranjit Singh's removal of marble, not the samadhi. The attachment is person-level, so weigh accordingly. The name is flagged both times ("سنگ[OCR?]", "سنگہ[OCR?]").
- **madho-lal-hussain** is mapped on a flagged name, "مادھو لال حسین [OCR?]" (p. 321), and the garbled "ما دہو لال[OCR?] حسن[OCR?]" (p. 318). It is a sajjada nashin's seal only, with no content about the shrine.
