# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 045 — pp. 494–504

The chunk opens mid-section on p. 494 with the end of an account of a "مکان" that has a mosque "مسمی[OCR?] ولی شاہ". This part covers a table of its dimensions, two decayed kothas, its khadims and Wali Shah's Qadiri shajra. **The heading of that section is in the previous chunk (044).** Four titled sections follow. The first is "احوال مزار نواب جعفر خان مرحوم" (p. 494), describing the tomb near "موضع ساہو گڈھی[OCR?]" and its inscriptions (pp. 494–496). The second is "احوال مکان مذکورہ یعنی تہ خانہ" (p. 496), about the grave of Jafar Khan's teacher (pp. 496–497). The third is "احوال مکان بنگلہ" (p. 497), with an excursus on Nawab Khalil Allah Khan (pp. 497–498). The fourth is "احوال مکان سمادہ گورو ارجن صاحب" (p. 498). It opens with a list of Lahore's twelve gates, places the samadh, and then gives a long account of Guru Ram Das's family, Prithi Chand's and Chandu Lal's enmity, Guru Arjan's imprisonment and death in Lahore, and his verse letters to Guru Ram Das (pp. 498–504). The chunk breaks off mid-sentence (p. 504). The archive rows touched are the Lahore rows (place-level, for the twelve gates), Ranjit Singh's samadhi, and the Sikh Guru rows by the person rule.

## Practices, institutions, economy

- **Wali Shah mosque** (p. 494): built as "بطور مسجد", with a dimension table. One phrase reads "جو سرکار کا سالہ[OCR?] ہے", but it is garbled.
- **Jafar Khan tomb** (pp. 494–496):
  - Layout: a raised chabutra and a stair "میانہ" in the south wall. The enclosure wall is of white chuna-gach brick. There is a cupola ("بطور بالا قبہ") with "کار منوت[OCR?]" stucco work, a red outer face and white floral work (pp. 494–495).
  - Damage: about "دو گز" of the south wall west of the door has fallen. The north wall "عہد مہاراجہ شیر سنگھ سے لے سبب بہ باران گر گئی ہے" (fell from rain, from Maharaja Sher Singh's time). Half of the east wall has also fallen (p. 495).
  - Two minarets of the mosque have collapsed: "دو مینار یاں[OCR?] مسمار ہو گئی ہیں" (p. 494).
  - Inscriptions: "آیت الکرسی بخط عربی" in red on a long tablet over the great arch (p. 496), followed by further Quranic verses quoted as printed (p. 496). The kalima "لا الہ الا اللہ محمد رسول اللہ" appears in stucco in two lines and is repeated on other arches (p. 496). Some of it was copied "حسب تحریر" (as written) (p. 496).
  - The ceiling has floral work "رنگ سفید ولاحوردی[OCR?]" with hexagonal roundels (p. 496).
- **Tahkhana** (p. 497):
  - A brick platform "طول دس گز اور عرض ساڑھے سات گز", hollow beneath like a tahkhana.
  - Its inner chamber "جسکو زبان پنجابی میں بھورہ کہتے ہیں" (called "bhora" in Punjabi).
  - Two chuna-gach graves on top, and a wall with a mihrab marking a mosque.
- **Bangla** (p. 497):
  - Odd form: square below, triangular sloping dome above, painted black.
  - Four doors, three of them closed with mud.
  - No grave inside now. "ساکنان گڈھی" (residents of the Garhi) use it to keep grass ("کھاس[OCR?]").
  - East of it runs "سڑک آہنی ملتان" (the iron road to Multan).
- **Sikh practice**:
  - Guru Ram Das served in Guru Amar Das's langar (p. 498).
  - Guru Hargobind's order that Chandu Lal's name not be spoken and that it be cursed three times (p. 503).
  - The "مندر[OCR?] مڑھی[OCR?]" marker at the cremation place (p. 503).
  - The succession dispute over "گدی" and "ولی عہد سجادہ نشین" (pp. 498–499).

## Legends and miracles (karamat)

- **Wazir Khan's cure** (pp. 499–500), attributed to Wazir Khan's own account before Jahangir:
  - Ill in Lahore with "درد شکم" (stomach pain), he got relief whenever a Sikh of Arjan passing toward the river recited Guru Arjan's bani aloud.
  - He then went to Guru Arjan at Amritsar.
- **People sent against the gurdwara at Amritsar**: they were on the road when "قدرت الہی سے" (by divine power) something befell "وہ دونوں". The passage is garbled (p. 500).
- **Chandu Lal's failed attempts**: his efforts to have Arjan arrested failed, "قدرت الہی سے پیش اسکی نہ چلتی تھی" (p. 501).
- **Prison torture**: sitting in boiling cauldrons and on a hot "توا[OCR?]", and days without food. Arjan refused to let his Sikhs take revenge, saying the stain would stay on Chandu Lal's forehead "تا قیامت" (pp. 501–502).
- **Chandu Lal's daughter-in-law**: she bribed the guards and brought him water. He told her "جو کریگا[OCR?] سو بھریگا[OCR?]" and, at her request, said "اچھا تو ہی[OCR?] پہلے[OCR?] مرجائے گی". Chandu Lal later found her dead (pp. 502–503).

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** Damage is heavy: dense `[OCR?]` flags and many `[illegible]` gaps, worst on pp. 494, 498, 500, 502 and 504. Every quotation keeps its flags. Folio markers (488–498) are printed but not cited, per the brief.
- **Opening fragment (p. 494)** belongs to a section headed in chunk 044. The place of the Wali Shah mosque is not stated in this chunk.
- **Lahore ids: place-level.** The 35 `lahore` ids are attached for the twelve-gates list and the subadari of Lahore (pp. 497–498). This is place-level, not site-specific. Gates named in the list (Bhati, Shah Alami) are not mapped separately to `bhati gate` or `shah alam market` rows. Darbar-malik-ahmad-ayaz is not reached by any toponym.
- **samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh**: the name appears in a garbled sentence about the location of Guru Arjan's samadh (p. 498). It is mapped by the person rule on a bare name. The chunk does not clearly say anything about Ranjit Singh's own samadh.
- **Guru Arjan rows (3), Guru Hargobind rows (6) and Guru Nanak rows (18)** are person-level, not site-specific; weigh accordingly. The chunk does not identify Guru Arjan's samadh with any archive row by name. Guru Nanak is a bare name in a garbled succession line (p. 498).
- **gurdwara-guru-ram-das-ji**: mapped by the person rule. The chunk names no Lahore site of his. "ساکن لاہور" is attached to a garbled "ہر داس" line, and whose residence it is is not certain (p. 498).
- **"قلعہ شاہ" (p. 498)** is not mapped to the `lahore fort` rows. The chunk names only the fort as a location marker.
- **Marriage match contradiction.** The text reads "انکو سکھیے[OCR?] وہ ناطہ قبول کیا" (p. 500), yet Chandu Lal's enmity and later demand "اگر ناطہ قبول کرو" (p. 501) imply the match was refused. A negation may be lost in transcription; check the PDF.
