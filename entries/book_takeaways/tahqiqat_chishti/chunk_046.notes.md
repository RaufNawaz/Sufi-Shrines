# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 046 — pp. 505–516

The chunk opens mid-section, in a Sikh-history narrative whose section heading is in an earlier chunk. It begins with Guru Arjan's composition of the fourth pauri and his installation by Guru Ram Das, then covers Prithi Chand's hostility, the birth of Guru Hargobind, the attempts on the infant, Chandu Lal at Jahangir's court, Khusrau's visit and Guru Arjan's summons to Lahore (pp. 505–510). A subheading "حال بعد وفات گورو ارجن صاحب" (p. 510) follows Guru Hargobind: the Akal Takht[OCR?], the two swords, his audience with Jahangir, his confinement at "قلعہ گوالیار", his release, and the punishment and death of Chandu Lal in Lahore (pp. 510–513). Next come a Sikh place at "مزنگ[OCR?]", "گوردوارہ ہذا", its gaddi-nashins and its endowments (pp. 513–514). A table titled "در باب احوال گورو پرنالی گوروان سکھان" gives birth and death data for the ten Gurus (pp. 515–516). The chunk ends with a new subheading, "حال عمارت ڈیرہ گورو ارجن صاحب کا بیان" (p. 516), on the building of Guru Arjan's Dera, which breaks off in a degraded Gurmukhi line. Damage is extreme on pp. 506, 508, 510 and 511–514. Eight Guru-figure bullets map 28 archive ids, almost all at person level.

## Legends and miracles (karamat)

- Guru Arjan's letters to Prithi Chand were praised so highly that "ہماری عقل ان پر نہین پہونچتی" (our understanding cannot reach them) (p. 505).
- The "میر" sent to arrest Guru Arjan was killed, and this was popularly attributed to "برکت گورو ارجن صاحب" (p. 509).
- A belief at the gaddi: the childless receive children, and a pregnant woman bears "فرزند نرینہ" (a male child). The line is garbled (p. 507).
- Poisoned milk given to an animal killed it at once, which proved the plot (p. 508).
- Hargobind, single-handed, defeated "ہزارہا[OCR?] سپہ[OCR?]", and a light ("روشنی[OCR?]") appeared. He explained that the one killed had sought "جیون مکت[OCR?]" and that "اسکی روح کو معراج ہوا". The passage is heavily damaged (p. 511).
- Jahangir's three dreams led to Hargobind's release from Gwalior (p. 512).

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory**:
  - Damage is extreme. Pp. 506, 508 and 510 are largely garbled `[OCR?]` strings, and pp. 511–514 are dense with `[illegible]`.
  - Every quotation keeps its flags. No repairs were made.
  - The printed folios (500–510) and the stray "۵۰۰[OCR?]" at the foot of p. 505 are not cited.
- **Section heading not in this chunk.** The chunk opens mid-narrative. "گوردوارہ ہذا الشریف" (p. 513) and its gaddi-nashins and endowments (p. 514) are not named here. Because of the p. 516 subheading "حال عمارت ڈیرہ گورو ارجن صاحب", the section may be on Guru Arjan's Dera, but the chunk does not say so. The endowment material is therefore NOT attached to gurdwara-dera-sahib. Check against the heading in chunk 045.
- **gurdwara-dera-sahib is mapped by name.** The book's "ڈیرہ گورو ارجن صاحب" (p. 516) is matched to the row "Gurdwara Dera Sahib" (Guru Arjan Dev, Lahore). The chunk does not itself say "Lahore" in this subsection, only "قلعہ", so weigh accordingly.
