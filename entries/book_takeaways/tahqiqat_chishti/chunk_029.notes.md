- **Shah Rahmat Ullah Qureshi — building of the tomb**:
  - His murids included countless masons ("معمار لوگ بیشمار مرید تھے"). All masons were engaged on "مسجد بادشاہی", and "کسیکو اجازت نہ تھی کہ کسی اور جگہ یہ کام کرے" (no one was permitted to work elsewhere) (p. 329).
  - So they agreed to build the tomb in a single night (p. 329).
  - When the women of the mohalla began grinding at their mills before dawn, the masons stopped out of fear of the king, and "وہ گنبذ نا تیار رہ گیا" (the dome was left unfinished) (p. 329).

## Legends and miracles (karamat)

- **Muhammad Salih**: a merchant who was his khadim had goods on a ship and vowed a sum to the saint if it came through safely. "اوستی وقت آپ یہاں تشریف لائی" (at that moment he arrived there) and drew the ship out (p. 328). When the merchant came to Lahore with "[illegible] و سو روپیہ" (… hundred rupees), the saint refused it and told him to build a mosque, which is this mosque (p. 328).
- **Shah Rahmat Ullah Qureshi — the "angels" story**: seeing the half-built tomb in the morning, people said "آج رات فرشتی یہ عمارت بنا گئو میں" (angels built this building last night). "بلکہ آج تک یہ ہی نقل مشہور سی" (and this tale is current to this day) (p. 329). The author rejects it; see Arguments.
- **Shah Rahmat Ullah Qureshi — the barakat at the grave**: "اب حضرت کی قبر [OCR?] … یہ برکت مشہور ہے". Whoever goes there with a hope and makes a vow has it fulfilled, and then brings dark jaggery (p. 330).
- **Haji Jamil and Ghulam Rasul**: Ghulam Rasul, a rich but childless merchant and Haji Jamil's friend, wished to go on hajj. The Haji told him "ہمنے سات حج کیو[OCR?] … ایک حج مقبول ہم سے لے" (we have made seven hajj … take one accepted hajj from us) (p. 333). When Ghulam Rasul insisted, he was told to wear fine clothes and perfume and sleep that night. He then saw himself at Madina[OCR?] and Mecca, and an elder told him "نامہ اعمال مین ایکہ حج …" (one hajj [is entered] in your record of deeds). The chunk ends mid-sentence (p. 333).

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- **The author's own correction (Shah Rahmat Ullah Qureshi)**: "مگر اصلی معاملہ یہ ہی جو بندہ نی تحریر کیا ہے" (but the real matter is what this servant [the author] has written). He sets the masons' night work against the popular story of angels (p. 329).
- **The author's dialogue with the sweepers**: their belief is "بہ روز قیامت والیان بہشت صرف مہتر لوگ ہونگی" (on the Day of Judgement only the mehtars will be the people of paradise). When he asked about other peoples, they allowed that God might admit them "کیونکہ ہم لوگوں کے پیروں سا کی خاک یہ بہشت میں جاسکی میں" (because the dust of our feet [can] go to paradise). The author calls such talk "لایعقل" (senseless) and quotes the Persian verse "ما [illegible] در پرورش است   کس نگوید کہ دوغ من ترش است" (p. 330).

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** Damage is heavy. The grave register (pp. 324–325), Dolu's dharamsala (p. 326), the Naqiban continuation (p. 332) and the Qadam Rasul description (pp. 332–333) are dense with `[OCR?]` and `[illegible]`. One `[margin] [illegible]` is on p. 325, and a `[margin]` falls inside the Muhammad Bakhsh sentence (p. 331). Every quotation keeps its flags. Folio markers (318, 320–327) are printed but are not cited, per the brief.
- **Grave-register title is in chunk 028.** It is mapped to shrine-of-mian-mir because the rows themselves name his rauza, enclosure, murids, khadims and sister. Whether every row is at his site is not stated in this chunk.
- **Bibi Jamal appears twice** (rows "۱۷" and "۲۱", p. 325). The first gives "سنہ ۱۰۵۵ [OCR?]" and a location "روبرو مقبرہ خواجہ بہاری [OCR?] … متصل قبر روضہ باغ ملا شاہ [OCR?]". The second gives no date, "بیرون باغ ملا شاہ بطرف غرب" ("ایضاً"), and says the structure is now ruined. They may be the same grave entered twice or two sites; this is not resolved.
- **Register numerals.** All dates are flagged. Some look short or odd ("سنہ ۱۳۴ [OCR?]", "سنہ ۱۱۸ [OCR?]"), and serial "۱۴ [OCR?]" is printed twice. Do not compute or convert any of them.
- **"شاہ دولاں [OCR?]" / "متی شاہ دولہ [OCR?]" (p. 324)** is NOT mapped to shrine-of-hazrat-shah-daula-daryai (Gujrat). The book places this grave by the Mian Mir enclosure with "سنہ ۱۲۶۶ [OCR?]" and makes no identification. The name match is noted only for the consolidator.
- **Lahore-in-general bullet** rests on one damaged sentence ("پیشتر [OCR?] لاہور کے تین [OCR?] حاکم", p. 326). Attachment is place-level; weigh accordingly. Lahore also appears only as the location of named non-archive sites, and those are not attached.

Attachment is person-level, not site-specific, and the evidence is a bare name. "لنگ" is flagged. The consolidator should weigh this; it is recorded per the protocol, not re-litigated.
