# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 037 — pp. 404–413

The chunk opens mid-section on the khanqah of "حضرت حامد قاری صاحب". **The section's heading is not in this chunk** (it is presumably in chunk 036). The rest of that section covers the saint's Suhrawardi chain, the line of his successors, the family of Mian Muhammad Bakhsh (whom the author calls his own "جد[OCR?] ماجد[OCR?]"), and a list titled "تفصیل قبور خاندان میان محمد بخش صاحب…" (p. 405). It then describes the khanqah buildings and inscriptions, a Muhammad Shah grant and its later fate under the Sikh and British governments, and a long sajjada-nashini dispute that ends in a signed agreement (pp. 404–411). It closes with the date of death, the urs and "قطعہ تاریخ حامد" (p. 411). Three titled sections follow: "احوال مقبرہ خان دوراں شاہجہانی" (pp. 411–412), "حال دیوڑھی باغنچہ علی مردان خان مرحوم" (pp. 412–413) and "احوال مقبرہ علی مردان خان" (p. 413), which breaks off mid-description. None of these sites is an archive row. Two archive rows are touched by the person rule only: Bahauddin Zakariya, named in a silsila chain, and Ranjit Singh, named as a period.

## Practices, institutions, economy

- **Hamid Qari's khanqah: enclosure, well and mosque** (p. 405):
  - A brick enclosure wall "قد آدم بلند" (a man's height), entered from the south.
  - A masonry well with a wheel ("چاہ پختہ چرخی دار").
  - Two masonry bathhouses ("غسل خانہ").
  - A one-domed masonry mosque on the west side, all white inside.
- **Mosque inscriptions** (pp. 405–406):
  - The main mihrab carries "لا الہ الا اللہ محمد رسول اللہ" (p. 405), with "یا فتاح[OCR?]" also written there (p. 406).
  - The north mihrab carries the couplet "محمد عربی کہ[OCR?] آبروی[OCR?] ہر دو سرا[OCR?] ٭ کسی کہ خاک درش[OCR?] نیست[OCR?] خاک بر[OCR?] سر او[OCR?]" (p. 406).
  - The names "محمد و ابو بکر و عمر عثمان و علی" are written in the middle (p. 406).
  - A "سال تاریخ" line is mostly illegible (p. 406).
- **Platform, room and the saint's grave** (p. 406):
  - East of the mosque's courtyard there is a raised platform, "طول آٹھ گز اور عرض ساڑھے چار گز" (eight gaz long and four and a half gaz wide).
  - A damaged line about a room ("کوٹھہ") on the mosque's north side names "میاں احمد دین[OCR?] صاحبزادہ [illegible]" next to the words "فقیر[OCR?] بٹھایا ہوا". Who placed whom there is not clear.
  - Hamid Qari's grave is inside an inner enclosure with lamp niches ("چراغداں[OCR?]"), with further graves to its east and west.
- **Grounds** (p. 406):
  - Trees: "سوٹرا[OCR?]", "کریر[OCR?]" and "کیکر" (large and small).
  - Two platforms outside on the west, one carrying two graves; on the other the occupant is "[OCR?] معلوم[OCR?] الاسم[OCR?]".
  - The north wall of the outer enclosure has partly collapsed, enough for a person to get in.
- **Grant and jagir** (p. 407):
  - Muhammad Shah's farman "پاس فقیر[OCR?] کے مامون[OCR?] صاحبان کی موجود" (is held by the author's maternal uncles).
  - Later rulers kept the "معافی" in force, and the jagir money reached Muhammad Bakhsh's family year after year.
  - "بعہد مہاراجہ صاحب", the jagirdar "بابو باج[OCR?] سنگھ المشہور[OCR?] بابو رای[OCR?]" kept paying it.
  - Under "عملداری سرکار انگریزی", "بابو گیان سنگھ" refused to pay and offered instead cultivable land with a choked well ("چاہ مسدودہ[OCR?]"). This was entered in the settlement ("ہند[OCR?] و لسبت[OCR?]", read as bandobast).
  - The three brothers restored the well. Gyan Singh later sold it.
  - The sajjada nashins, "نا واقف قانون سرکار" (unfamiliar with the government's law), kept silent, but now say they will file a claim at the time of settlement.
- **Sajjada-nashini dispute** (pp. 407–411):
  - In "سن بارہ سو [illegible]" Sharaf-ud-Din claimed the sajjada-nashini. The case went before the "[illegible] صاحب بہادر". Fazl-ud-Din and Khair Din obtained a ruling that the sajjada nashins and owners are Muhammad Bakhsh's descendants (p. 407).
  - The author ("کمترین … مورخ[OCR?]") visited and found "مسمی رجبا[OCR?] درویش" living there for seven years by order of Ahmad Din. He summoned both parties (p. 407).
  - Fazl Din's side said Muhammad Bakhsh had appointed Sharaf-ud-Din mutawalli out of friendship. Ahmad Din answered that Muhammad Bakhsh had only got his permission for a grave and "اب باحق مالک بن بیٹھی ہیں" (p. 407).
  - Ahmad Din then posed three questions: deeds of descent, a "تولیت نامہ", and a shajra (p. 408).
  - His own account of the post-Hamid line (Malik Sahib, Ghulam Mustafa, and Sharaf-ud-Din's appointment) is given under Other saints (p. 409).
  - Ahmad Din says Muhammad Bakhsh's family put faqirs there: "خاکی شاہ" first. When the place was becoming desolate ("ویران"), they asked Sharaf-ud-Din to post a servant-darwish of his own, whose upkeep they would provide (p. 409).
  - A damaged line mentions the urs: "مرور[OCR?] عرس خود بخود و ہاں علا[OCR?] آتا[OCR?]". Hafiz Bakhsh later raised a claim to the property "ایام جہلم[OCR?] میں" (p. 410).
  - Outcome: by "دستور قدیم", Ahmad Din is "قابض و متصرف" (in possession). His khadim stays by his wish, Muhammad Bakhsh's family keep serving the present darwish, and their burials there are not to be obstructed. An "اقرار نامہ" was drawn up (p. 410).
  - Signatories: Ahmad Din and his sons "محمد عطا نسیم[OCR?]", "امام الدین" and "محمد دین"; and Fazl-ud-Din, Khair Din, Faiz Bakhsh, Chiragh-ud-Din, Ramzan and Amir Bakhsh (p. 411).
- **Urs**: "بتاریخ ١٨[OCR?] جمادی الثانی ہر سال عرس آپ کا ہوتا ہی رہے[OCR?]" (p. 411).

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- The author places himself in the account. He is the grandson of Mian Muhammad Bakhsh (p. 404), he went to the site as "مورخ" and summoned both parties "احتیاطاً" (as a precaution) (p. 407), and he says he wanted to enter the true state of affairs in the book ("حال پختہ درج کتاب کرنا منظور تھا") (p. 407).
- He reports Ahmad Din's side at length, including Ahmad Din's view that Muhammad Bakhsh's family had "باحق مالک بن بیٹھی ہیں" (made themselves owners) (p. 407). He records the final agreement without ruling on it himself (p. 410).
- On Khan Dauran's tomb he gives the attribution as hearsay, "افواہاً معلوم ہوا" (p. 411). He adds "واللہ اعلم" to the burial story (p. 412).

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** Damage is heavy throughout, with dense `[OCR?]` and `[illegible]` flags. The worst passages are the dispute pages (pp. 408, 410), the mosque inscriptions (p. 406) and the opening of the Khan Dauran story (p. 412). Every quotation above keeps its flags. Folio markers are printed (pp. 405–410, 413) but are not cited, per the brief.
- **Section heading for Hamid Qari is not in this chunk.** The chunk begins mid-sentence on p. 404.
- **shrine-of-bahauddin-zakariya is mapped on a flagged name in a silsila chain**: "بہار[OCR?] الحق زکریا[OCR?] ملتانی" (p. 404). Person rule, single row. The evidence is a chain entry only; weigh accordingly. "ملتانی" is a nisba, not material on Multan, so the 8 `multan` ids are not attached.
- **samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh is mapped on a bare name used as a period** ("بعہد رنجیت سنگھ", p. 413), and the surrounding clause is broken by "[illegible]". Person rule; this is person-level, not site-specific. "بعہد مہاراجہ صاحب" (p. 407) does not name the maharaja, so it is not mapped.
- **No Lahore-in-general bullet.** Lahore appears only as a reference point ("لاہور کی شرق رویہ بفاصلہ دو کوس", p. 405), in damaged fragments ("شہر لاہور", pp. 408, 410) and as where Khan Dauran stayed (p. 412). None is material on the city itself, so the 35 `lahore` ids are not attached. This follows the chunk 015 practice; recorded for the consolidator to weigh against ruling (a).
- **Hamid Qari's death date is given three ways, and the first two do not clearly name him**:
  - "وفات عہد محمد شاہی[OCR?] تاریخ[OCR?] [illegible] ماہ جمادی الثانی[OCR?] سنہ[OCR?] گیارہ سو[OCR?] [illegible]" (p. 404).
  - "بتاریخ سترہ[OCR?] ماہ[OCR?] جمادی الثانی ۱۱۶۷ [OCR?] اوہنوں نے وفات پائی" (p. 409). The subject of "اوہنوں" is unclear: the sentence runs through "خادم مولوی تیمور[OCR?]", "میان اسماعیل صاحب" and "خان بہادر".
  - "[illegible] جمادی سنہ ١١٦٦ [OCR?] ھ" (p. 411), and the chronogram's "سنہ ١١٦٦ [OCR?]" (p. 411).
  - Urs: "١٨ [OCR?] جمادی الثانی" (p. 411). All are flagged numerals. Do not compute or reconcile.
- **Two different successor lines after Hamid Qari**: Maulvi [illegible] Muhammad, then Hafiz Rahmat, then Muhammad Bakhsh (p. 404), against Malik Sahib, then Ghulam Mustafa, then Sharaf-ud-Din (p. 409). The book gives both as the parties' claims. Kept side by side.

- **"درس میان وڈا صاحب" / "خانقاہ میاں وڈا[OCR?]"** (pp. 407, 409, 411) is not an archive row. It is carried as printed.
