# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 001 — pp. 1–16

**This chunk is the book's table of contents and the opening of its preface. It has no shrine chapters.** Pages 1–7 are the table of contents ("نمبر صفحہ | مطلب"). The run printed here starts at printed number "۱۳۷" and ends at "۸۷۰[OCR?]". Page 8 carries an accession stamp and an illuminated "بسم اللہ الرحمن الرحیم", and opens the hamd. The hamd runs on into a "مناجات" (pp. 9–11), then "در نعت سید المرسلین…" (pp. 11–12), a "غزل من مصنفہ" (p. 12) and "در مناقب … علی المرتضیٰ کرم اللہ وجہہ و صحابہ کبار" (pp. 12–13). The section "احوال مصنف مع سبب تالیف کتاب[OCR?]" begins on p. 13. In it the author names himself and gives a history of his Chishti family of maulvis, from Shah Jahanabad and Aurangabad to Lahore, under the heading "حال مولوی بہا[OCR?] الحق برادر مولوی ضیاء[OCR?] الحق صاحب" (p. 15), with a family tree. That history breaks off mid-sentence on p. 16. All archive-row material below comes from **ToC lines**. Each is cited at the PDF page on which the line is printed, and the book's own page number is quoted as text. **No ToC number is a PDF page.**

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- **Why the preface praises Ali.** "از انجا کہ اصل ہر فرع فقر یعنی چہار پیر چودہ[OCR?] خانوادہ کا ذات عالی درجات جناب علی کرم اللہ وجہہ کی ہے". Since Ali is the root of every branch of faqr (the four pirs and fourteen[OCR?] khanwadas), the author found his manaqib fitting to adorn the preface (p. 12).
- **Choosing a match by shari'a.** The family judged that Lahore's two respectable qaums were "خوجہ[OCR?]" and "لہار[OCR?] ترکھان[OCR?]". Being "نہایت[OCR?] متشرع و دیندار", they consulted "حسب شریعت نبوی" about matches among Muslim qaums. The sentence breaks off at p. 16's end (p. 16).

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** `[OCR?]` and `[illegible]` flags are dense throughout. On ToC pp. 2–7 nearly every printed number is flagged. The preface on pp. 9, 11 and 13 is mostly `[illegible]`, including whole lines. Every quoted word above carries its flag. Every ToC number is printed text, not a PDF page.
- **The ToC is incomplete and out of order.** It begins at "۱۳۷", so entries for anything printed before ۱۳۷ are not in this chunk. Its sequence also breaks: "۲۱۹[OCR?]" before "۲۱۶[OCR?]" and "۲۱۷" (p. 1); "۲۴۶[OCR?]" before "۲۴۲[OCR?]" (p. 2); "۵۴۸[OCR?]" before "۵۴۵[OCR?]" (p. 4); "۶۹۲[OCR?]–۶۹۸[OCR?]" before "۶۶۷[OCR?]", "۶۶۹[OCR?]" and "۶۷۰[OCR?]" (p. 5); "۸۳۴[OCR?]" before "۸۳۲[OCR?]" (p. 7). These are misreadings or printing order, not resolved here. **No ToC number may be used to locate a PDF page.**
- **"سید علی ہجویری[OCR?] چراغ شاہ[OCR?]" (p. 6).** This single ToC line joins Hujwiri and "Chiragh Shah". It is mapped to both **data-darbar** and **shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh** as the book names them. Whether it is one entry on two men, an epithet, or a misreading is for the PDF and the body chapter. The held identity question on **shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh** (whether "Shah Chiragh" conflates two men) is not resolved here.
- **"تکیہ سید چراغ شاہ چشتی" (p. 1).** This takia of a "Chishti" Syed Chiragh Shah is left under Other: a takia that is not shown to be the archive row. It is a candidate for **shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh** under the same held question. Weigh it with the p. 6 line once the body chapter is read.
- **"سید عبدالرزاق مکی[OCR?]" (p. 1).** "سید عبدالرزاق" matches the index's principal figure of **shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh** (Syed Abdul Razzaq). "مکی[OCR?]" echoes **peer-makki**. Both candidates are flagged and the line is not mapped. The body chapter must decide.
