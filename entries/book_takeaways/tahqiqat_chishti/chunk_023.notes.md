# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 023 — pp. 258–268

This chunk opens mid-section, part-way through a legend cycle of "حضرت سید احمد صاحب". The book names him as "حضرت سید احمد سخی سرور" only near the end of his section (p. 266). His section's heading, and whatever the book says before p. 258, are in the previous chunk (022). The legends run through pp. 258–266: a quarrel with his brothers over land and harvest, a journey to the ruler of Multan, a horse brought back to life, his marriage to the king's daughter, and then his martyrdom and the burial of his wife and infant son. Then come the shrine's custodians (Bharai, Dholi[OCR?], mujawirs), the book's two written sources, his spiritual lineage, and a death year with a chronogram headed "تاریخ وفات سخی سرور صاحب" (pp. 266–267). A new section follows, "خانقاہ [illegible] المشہور دیوان صاحب" (p. 267). It describes, in architectural detail, the enclosure and graves of "حضرت صدر دیوان صاحب" outside "دروازہ شاہ عالمی" in Lahore, and ends with a list of sajjada nashins (pp. 267–268). The chunk has no material on Lahore in general and none at province level.

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- The author separates his sources. The Sakhi Sarwar legend is reported as "مسموع" from the Bharai[OCR?]. The pir-murid link to Khwaja Maudud Chishti is attributed to "کتاب معراج[OCR?] الاولیاء[OCR?]" (p. 266).
- The author describes the Bharai as having declined from honoured guests to beggars (p. 266).

## Doubts

- **The chunk opens mid-section.** The heading for Sayyid Ahmad / Sakhi Sarwar, and anything said before p. 258, are in chunk 022.
- **The sakhi-sarwar mapping rests on the name.** The chunk names "سید احمد سخی سرور", "سخی سرور صاحب" and, in the chronogram, "سلطان عالم". It never names Dera Ghazi Khan or any shrine location for the row. The martyrdom and grave are at "مقام نگاہی[OCR?]", which the book does not tie to the row's site. The consolidator should weigh this.
- **The Multan bullet is place-level.** All of it comes from inside the Sakhi Sarwar legend (ruler, fort, jail, gate), so weigh it accordingly. The unnamed "ولی کامل" in Multan near the fort (p. 260) is too damaged to identify. It is not mapped to any Multan row.
- **shrine-of-bahauddin-zakariya and tomb-of-qutbuddin-aibak are person-level only.** Each is a bare name used as a lineage source or a dating point. Nothing in the chunk is about either site.
- **Who is "father"?** The "بابا" dies on p. 258, yet Sayyid Ahmad later consults "اپنی باپ" (p. 263) and Zain al-Abidin keeps acting through p. 264. The chunk never says outright who the father is or whose death sets off the land claim.
- **Who dies first at the p. 264/265 break?** Daud is named just before the break, with a damaged margin. The rider martyred first on p. 265 is "آپ" and is not named, so who that is stays unresolved. "بہتر[OCR?]" (number killed) is flagged.
- **The brothers' kinship term is damaged.** "مسیری[OCR?] ہماشوں" / "مسیری[OCR?] سہا[OCR?] ہیوں[OCR?]" is not read here.
- **Numerals and dates are carried as printed.**
  - Death year: "ششمہ[OCR?] ہجری" (p. 267).
  - Aibak's accession: "۶۰۲[OCR?]" / "چھ سو دو[OCR?]" (p. 267).
  - Margins "۳۴[OCR?]" (p. 266) and "۲۳[OCR?]" (p. 268).
  - "دو ہزار[OCR?]" (p. 263) and "[illegible] ہزار گز[OCR?]" (p. 267).
  - None is converted.
- **Sajjada nashin clause (p. 268).** "مہاراجہ[OCR?] [illegible] سنگھ[OCR?]": the ruler's name is illegible, so this is not mapped to samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh.
- **Not treated as province material.** "ملک پنجاب" (p. 266) is part of a citation of a register ("یادگار[OCR?] چشتی[OCR?]"), not a statement about the province. "ملک ہندوستان" (p. 267) is not a province.
- **No Lahore-in-general bullet.** Lahore is named only as the place Sadr Diwan came to (p. 268), and his khanqah is not an archive row. Shah Alami gate is a location, not a toponym bullet.
- **Damage.** Very heavy across the chunk. pp. 258, 260, 262 and 264 are mostly `[OCR?]`, and pp. 266 and 268 have many `[illegible: ~N words]` gaps. Names flagged in this chunk include the Multan ruler "گہر[OCR?] سہاں[OCR?]", the amir "ن خان[OCR?]", "شہ[OCR?] د لیو[OCR?]", "رسخالی[OCR?]", Kursi Kot[OCR?] and Nigahi[OCR?]. No name above has been repaired.
- **Folio markers wander.** They read 252, 253, 254, 256, 258, then 240 and 241 at pp. 266–267, and pp. 261, 263, 265 and 268 have none. This is recorded only as damage evidence; no folio is cited.
