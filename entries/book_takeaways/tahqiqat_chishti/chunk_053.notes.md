## Practices, institutions, economy

- **Urs of Sayyid Mahmud**:
  - "قدیم الایام سے تاریخ ۱۱[OCR?] ربیع الثانی عرس حضرت سید محمود صاحب کا [illegible] سے ہوتا ہے" (from olden days the urs of Sayyid Mahmud has been held on 11[OCR?] Rabi al-Thani) (p. 596).
  - A few people gather, "خصوصاً لوہار[OCR?] اور [illegible] فروش", stay the night and hold qawwali (p. 596).
  - Later "ناچ راگ رنگ" (dance and music) began (p. 596).
  - Now "ہر روز مقرر ہی عرس ہوتا ہے". Faqirs gather, cook "نان گوشت" and distribute it (p. 596).
  - On p. 599 the descendants are said to hold the "ہر روز عرس علیحدہ" (separately) (p. 599).
- **Custodianship**:
  - "اس خانقاہ پیر الہی شاہ فقیر خادم انکا بطور سجادہ نشین ہے" (the faqir Ilahi Shah, its khadim, acts as sajjada nashin) (p. 602).
  - The book's chain of khadims: Ilahi Shah was khadim of Sayyid Hamid Shah, Hamid Shah of Sayyid Ghulam Muhyi al-Din, then Sayyid Nur, then Sayyid Abd Allah Qadiri, then Shah Muhammad Hazuri (p. 602).
  - A faqir "مسمی آبی[OCR?] شاہ" was present at the enclosure (p. 595).
  - Ilahi Shah draws a "وظیفہ" (p. 599).
- **Burial fees** (p. 600):
  - A new grave inside the enclosure pays "پانچ روپیہ", and one outside pays "ایک یا دو".
  - Of this, "ایک روپیہ حق [illegible] الٰہی شاہ لیتا ہے اور بقیہ اولاد حضرت کو ملتا ہے" (Ilahi Shah takes one rupee as his due; the rest goes to the descendants).
  - "چلہ کو غرب رویہ پانچ کنال زمین مسافر خانہ کرکے مشہور ہے" (five kanals west of the chilla are known as the musafir khana). Ilahi Shah takes the fee for graves there, and also what comes "ہمراہ لاش" (with the corpse).

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- The author says he will give first the present state of the khanqah's buildings, then what he learned "زبانی افواہ[OCR?]" (orally) from the descendants and "خواندین[OCR?] قدیمی" (the old families) (p. 596).
- He explains why the khanqah bears Jan Muhammad's name rather than Sayyid Mahmud Shah's: the murid Abd al-Samad built the tomb (p. 594).
- He cites "کتاب تذکرۃ الامرا" for Amir Khan's rank (p. 602).
- He records that "تحقیقات" (inquiries) showed Sayyid Mahmud Shah died in Rabi al-Thani (p. 595), and gives his ownership finding (p. 601).

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** Damage is heavy throughout: dense `[OCR?]` and `[illegible]` flags, pp. 589–590 almost unreadable, and pp. 597–598 largely broken. Every quotation keeps its flags. Folio markers are printed but not cited, per the brief.
- **The Lahore-in-general bullet** is place-level: Sikh-state history set in the city and fort. The book's units "سرکار لاہور" and "ریاست لاہور" (p. 591) mean the Sikh state and are carried as printed, not mapped to modern boundaries.
- **Lahore Fort** (loh-temple-lava-temple, samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh): mapped as place-level from "محاصرہ قلعہ لاہور" (p. 589). Both ids are also in the Lahore bullet.
- **samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh by the person rule**: the evidence is the margin heading on his death (pp. 589–591) and his seal on a parwana (p. 600). The samadhi itself is not mentioned. "اکال سہائی[OCR?]" is flagged.
- **shrine-of-mian-mir rests on a bare name in a broken sentence**: "جو میاں میر کو حاصل ہے" (p. 593). It cannot be told from the chunk whether this means the saint or a place named Mian Mir. Weigh accordingly.
- **Multan (8 ids)** is place-level. The material is the office of "ناظم ملتان" and the killings of Agnew[OCR?] and Kahn[OCR?] Singh (p. 592). The chunk does not say where the killings happened.
- **mausoleum-of-waris-shah** is mapped by ruling (a) from "قلعہ شیخوپورہ" (p. 591). The match is only through the row's "Sheikhupura District". The row is at Jandiala Sher Khan, and the book names only the fort. This is place-level; the consolidator should weigh it.
- **Province bullet**: the book's period is the Sikh state under Dalip Singh, 1848–49 as stated (p. 592), with flagged years. Province-level, weigh accordingly.
  - The Sutlej battles, Ferozepur, "دوآبہ[OCR?] جالندھر", Kashmir, "امرتسر" and "چنار گڑھ" are not given the Punjab tag (ruling (c)(3)/(4)). Saadullahpur and Chillianwala[OCR?] are not located by the book.
  - The farman's "صوبہ نجاب[OCR?] دار السلطنت لاہور" (p. 600) is a Mughal suba wording tied to one land grant, so it is not put in the province bullet.
- **Not mapped, recorded for the consolidator (possible deviation from ruling (a))**:
  - **"شاہ پور"** occurs in the farman's two-column seal block: "شاہ پور رمضان صوبہ | نجاب[OCR?] دار السلطنت" (p. 600). toponym_map's "shahpur" → shah-yousuf. It is not mapped because the reading comes from a column-split transcription that may belong to a compound place-name within Lahore's suba. Weigh it.
  - **"پشاور[OCR?] لاہور کی فوج"** (p. 591) is flagged and its sense is unclear (perhaps the Peshawar and Lahore troops). The 10 Peshawar ids are not attached.
  - **"تحصیل لاہور"** (p. 595) is one man's residence and is not attached to the `lahore district` rows.
- **Date inconsistencies (do not compute)**:
  - On p. 598 the digits "۱۲۴۶[OCR?]" are followed by the words "بارہ سو اٹھالیس" (twelve hundred forty-eight).
  - Jan Muhammad Hazuri's death is "۱۰۵۰[OCR?]" (p. 594), while the descendants' deaths fall in the "۱۲۳[OCR?]"–"۱۲۵۸[OCR?]" range (pp. 595–596). Whose "سنہ ۱۲۴۱[OCR?]" is (p. 596) is lost.
