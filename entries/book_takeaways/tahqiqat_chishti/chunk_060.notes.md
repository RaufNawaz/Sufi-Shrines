# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 060 — pp. 664–673

The chunk opens with a badly damaged fragment (p. 664) about a house or mosque of "رحیم شاہ", a takia, a mujawir and a grave. **The heading of that section is in the previous chunk (059)**. The titled sections then run in this order. "حال مکان [illegible] مسماۃ نکائین[OCR?] وجہ[OCR?] مہاراجہ رنجیت سنگھ والدہ مہاراجہ کھڑک سنگھ" covers an enclosure of Sikh royal samadhs (pp. 664–665). "بقیہ احوال شاہ شرف صاحب لاہوری" gives the family background of Shah Sharaf as told by the sajjada nashin of Shah Jamal's khanqah (pp. 665–666). "حال شوالہ [illegible] بدھو[OCR?]" describes a shiwala and is almost wholly illegible (pp. 666–667). "احوال تالاب لکھپت و جسپت رای لاہوری" and "حال موجودہ عمارت تالاب لکھپت رای" cover the tanks of the diwans Lakhpat and Jaspat Rai, with the rival diwan Dila Ram and a faqir's prophecy (pp. 667–670). "حال مقبرہ حلیم[OCR?] شاہ [illegible: 2 words] رحمۃ اللہ علیہ" and "حال حسبی و نسبی حضرت شاہ بدر رحمتہ اللہ علیہ" cover a tomb held by darzis (tailors) and Shah Badr's Qadiri genealogy (pp. 670–672). The last full sections are "احوال شوالہ باوا[OCR?] جیتن[OCR?] گر" (pp. 672–673), "مکان مستہاراں[OCR?] والہ" (p. 673) and "احوال تکیہ بہوری[OCR?] شاہ فقیر" (p. 673). The heading "شوالہ السنت[OCR?] کہ متصل تکیہ بہوری[OCR?] شاہ" closes the chunk with no text under it. Three archive rows are named in passing (Shah Jamal, Madho Lal Hussain, Ranjit Singh), and Baba Nanak is named once, which brings in his 18 gurdwara rows by the person rule.

## Legends and miracles (karamat)

- **The faqir's prophecy at Lakhpat Rai's tank**:
  - When the three tanks were made, the servants stopped a faqir from using them. He came at last to Lakhpat Rai's tank, where Lakhpat himself was present. Lakhpat, displeased with his men, ordered that no one stop the faqir (p. 669).
  - The faqir then said: "اب یاد رہے[OCR?] کہ یہ تالاب ہمیشہ آباد رہیگا اور ان دونو تالاب [illegible: 3 words] نام و نشان بھی نہ رہیگا" (remember[OCR?]: this tank will always stay full, and of those two tanks … not a trace will remain) (p. 669).
  - The book adds that this tank is full and the other two are deserted (p. 669).

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- **The author on new-Muslim Shaikhs**: those who became Muslim keep the name of their former Hindu caste, "جس قوم ہندو سے وہ مسلمان ہوئی ہوتی ہیں وہ ہی نام قوم قائم رکھتی ہیں". He gives this as context for Shah Sharaf's origin (p. 665).
- **The author's method**: for Shah Sharaf he reports what the Shah Jamal sajjada nashin "بیان کرتا ہی" (relates), "بندہ در پی تحقیقات" (the writer, pursuing inquiries) (p. 665).
- **Two genealogies for Shah Badr**: the author records one shajra as the family gives it (p. 671) and a second as the darzis' (p. 672). He adds "بعضے کہتے ہین" (some say) something about the sajjada and descent, but that sentence is garbled (p. 672).

## Citable passages

- "شیخ امیر بخش سجادہ نشین خانقاہ حضرت شاہ جمال مرحوم بیان کرتا ہی" — "Shaikh Amir Bakhsh, sajjada nashin of the khanqah of Hazrat Shah Jamal, relates…" (p. 665)
- "چار دیواری میں اب دم تحریر زراعت[OCR?] ہوتی ہے" — of the royal samadh enclosure: "inside the enclosure, at the time of writing, farming[OCR?] is done." (p. 665)
- "اب یاد رہے[OCR?] کہ یہ تالاب ہمیشہ آباد رہیگا" — the faqir at Lakhpat Rai's tank: "Remember[OCR?], this tank will always stay full." (p. 669)
- "تاحال بارہ بیگہ زمین متعلقہ اس خانقاہ کے سرکار سے معافت ہے" — "To this day twelve bigha of land belonging to this khanqah are revenue-free from the sarkar." (p. 671)
- "بروز عرس دال گوشت نان بطور بھنڈارہ تقسیم کرتی ہین" — "On the urs day they distribute dal, meat and naan as a bhandara." (p. 671)

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** Damage is heavy: pp. 666–668 and 670 are mostly `[illegible]`. Pp. 671–673 are legible in outline but carry dense `[OCR?]` flags, with garbled, non-word strings (e.g. p. 672's darzi lineage, p. 672's Bawa Jitan Gar location, p. 673's Bawa names). Every quotation keeps its flags. Folio markers are printed but not cited, per the brief.
- **Opening fragment (p. 664)** belongs to a section headed in chunk 059. Its subject (Rahim Shah's house or takia, Karim Ali Shah, the grave of Sher[OCR?] Ali Shah) is not placeable from this chunk.
- **madho-lal-hussain is mapped on a bare name.** "مادھو لال حسین" sits between illegible runs in a sentence about a mohalla's name (p. 664). The relation to the shrine is not stated; weigh accordingly.
- **shah-jamal**: the book says only "خانقاہ حضرت شاہ جمال مرحوم", with no location (p. 665). There is one Shah Jamal row in the index. The mapping is by name, in a Lahore book; the row gives the material only through its sajjada nashin's testimony.
- **samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh**: mapped by the person rule because Ranjit Singh is named (pp. 664–665). The chunk describes the samadhs of his wife or wives and of Kharak Singh's mother and wife, **not** Ranjit Singh's own samadhi; weigh accordingly. The heading word "وجہ[OCR?]" (read as wife) is flagged. Whether "نکائین[OCR?]" and "[illegible] کنور[OCR?] زوجہ رنجیت سنگھ" are one woman or two is not clear from the text (p. 665).
- **Guru Nanak's 18 rows**: person-level, not site-specific. The mention is a bare name ("بابا نانک") inside a damaged phrase, as an example of the Bedi caste name (p. 665). No gurdwara or place is named. Weigh accordingly. "دہرم سنگھ[OCR?] نانک پنتھی[OCR?]" (p. 669) is a Nanakpanthi, not Nanak, and is not mapped.
- **"حضور ہی[OCR?] باغ"** (p. 668) is not mapped to samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh by its Hazuri Bagh location. The word is flagged, and the sentence ("سردار سیوا[OCR?] سنگھ[OCR?] و حضور ہی[OCR?] باغ میں [illegible: 4 words]") is too damaged to say what was there.
- **"موضع مزنگ[OCR?]"** (p. 669) is not mapped to the two `mozang` rows (gurdwara-chhevin-patshahi-mozang, shrine-of-shah-inayat-qadiri). It is flagged, and it is named only as the location of a tank and of the zamindars who bought its land, not as material on Mozang's tradition. Recorded for the consolidator to weigh against ruling (a).
- **"سید عبد الرزاق بن غوث الاعظم"** in Shah Badr's genealogy (p. 671) is **not** mapped to shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh, whose principal figure is "Syed Abdul Razzaq (Shah Chiragh)". The chunk does not connect this genealogical name to Shah Chiragh or to any shrine. This is a name coincidence that I have not resolved (it is also a held identity row).
- **"ملتانی[OCR?]"** (p. 669) is a flagged nisba of an unnamed nawab, not material on Multan. The `multan` rows are not attached.
- **No Lahore-in-general bullet.** Lahore appears only as a reference point ("بیرون شہر لاہور", "دو میل لاہور سے", gates "روشنی", "تکسالی"), so the 35 `lahore` ids are not attached. No province material appears.
- **Section heading vs grave occupant**: the tomb section is headed "حلیم[OCR?] شاہ" (p. 670), but the text names "اہل قبر حضرت شاہ بدر صاحب" (p. 671). Whether these are one person, or "حلیم[OCR?]" is a misreading, is not settled by the chunk.
- **Flagged numerals; do not compute**: "سنہ ۱۲۱۱[OCR?] ہجری" (p. 666) in an illegible context, so what it dates is unknown. Also "۱۹[OCR?] گز" (p. 666), "بیس[OCR?] برس", "آٹھ[OCR?] بیگھہ", "ایک سو پچاس[OCR?] گز" (p. 669), "سولہ[OCR?] قدم" and "۱۲[OCR?] ماہ ربیع الاول" (p. 671).
- **Shah Sharaf's relation to Amir Bakhsh's family** turns on an `[illegible]` word before "شاہ شرف صاحب خسر حقیقی شیخ روشن علی کی تھی" (p. 665). The feminine "کی تھی" suggests the subject is someone of Shah Sharaf's household, not Shah Sharaf himself. Not resolved.
- **Two darbars on the site but not in the index** (Shah Gohar Peer; Mian Qurban Ali Shah): neither is named in this chunk.
