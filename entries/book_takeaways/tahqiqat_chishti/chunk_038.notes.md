# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 038 — pp. 414–424

The chunk opens in the middle of a section whose heading is in the previous chunk (037). It describes a large domed tomb with eight two-storey arches and a pool (pp. 414–415), and then gives the "مرآۃ الہند" account of "علی مردان خان" with Mufti Ghulam Sarwar's chronogram (p. 415). ## Practices, institutions, economy

- **Shah Shams-ud-Din Qadiri — custodianship and observance**:
  - A khadim "جو مکاں [illegible] لاہوری [OCR?] رہتا ہی" says "حضرت کی مرضی نہیں کہ کوئی یہاں رات کو شب باش [OCR?] ہوے [OCR?]" (the hazrat does not wish anyone to stay the night here) because "ہیبت [OCR?] آتی ہی" (awe comes on one) (p. 416).
  - A lamp is lit in the morning, and fatiha is said "ہر دو وقت [OCR?]" (at both times) (p. 416).
  - "کوئی عرس کلاں یہاں نہیں ہوتا" (no large urs is held here) (p. 416).
  - The khadim is linked to the rauza of "حضرت شاہ اللہ اسحاق [OCR?]" in "موضع [illegible]" (p. 416).
- **Shah Shams-ud-Din Qadiri — patronage**: the tomb was built through Shah Jahan while he was still a prince, and a garden around it lasted until "عہد محمد شاہی [OCR?]" (p. 416).
- **Bahadur Khan tomb — reuse**:
  - Used as a dance house by "صاحبان مالیتان[OCR?]", and now has railway employees' sheds (p. 421).
  - All lower doors are now bricked up except one in the south corner (p. 421).
  - The sarkar paid cash to empty it of Mehr Nura's[OCR?] heirs (p. 423).
- **Babu Baj Singh[OCR?] section — zamindars' petition**: zamindars of the surroundings complained to the "سرکار [OCR?]" that "پیر کبیر [OCR?] کے روضہ کو خرابت [OCR?] کرنا ہمارا [illegible] ہمت نہیں [OCR?]" (to ruin the rauza of the Pir Kabir[OCR?] is beyond our courage). After that something "کا موقوف [OCR?] ہوا" (was stopped) (p. 420).
- **Masjid Qassab Khana wala — Sikh period**: Gulab Singh "[illegible] توڑا" (broke) the south wall of the mosque (p. 424).
- **Hazrat Muhtaram — inscriptions**: "اللھم اغفر لامۃ محمد" on the arches, "اللھم ارحم امۃ محمد", "اللہ محمد [OCR?] علی عثمان" over the west door, and "افضل الذکر [OCR?] لا الہ الا اللہ محمد رسول اللہ" in nastaliq (p. 419).

## Legends and miracles (karamat)

- **Musa Kahokar[OCR?]**: Shah Muhammad Muqim, faced with the cost of a large wedding procession that a nawab expected, came to the hazrat in distress. He was given "ایک ہانڈی [OCR?] یعنی دیگچی [OCR?]" (a pot) and told to ask it for whatever he needed, and "قدرت الہی" (by divine power) whatever was asked for was there (pp. 417–418).
- **Musa Kahokar[OCR?], second episode**: an army ("یہ لشکر [OCR?] ہزار ہا [OCR?] لوگ") and a promise that the asker would be granted something "اوسی سال حساب الہی سی" (that same year by divine reckoning). The passage is too damaged to state more (p. 418).
- **Babu Baj Singh[OCR?] section**: the man who built at the deorhi "قدرت الٰہی سے وہیں[OCR?] قتل ہوا", and the village was deserted (p. 421).

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- The author draws on "کتاب مرآۃ الہند" for Ali Mardan Khan (p. 415) and for Bahadur Khan's death (p. 423).
- For the tomb inscription of Hazrat Muhtaram, the author computes a chronogram: "اگر [illegible] کے اعداد سی جو گیارہ سو سات [OCR?] ہوتی ہی میں [OCR?] پانچ [OCR?] دور کر دے". The result is cut by the page break (p. 419).
- The author records the ignorance of Shah Bilawal's sajjada nashins about the Rasul Shahiyan tomb (p. 417).
