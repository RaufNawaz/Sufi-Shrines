# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 054 — pp. 603–614

The chunk opens with a badly damaged passage (p. 603) about the takia and graves of Fazil Shah, his chain and his sajjada nashins. **Its heading is in the previous chunk (053).** Titled sections follow in this order:
- "حال تالاب سید سمریہ" (pp. 604–606): the tank of Sayyid Abd al-Khaliq, the lineage of the narrator Sayyid Qutb Shah, the madrasa, the jagir, the tank's healing reputation and the family's later history.
- "احوال مسجد دایہ انگا" (pp. 606–608): the mosque inside the railway station compound and its builder, Shah Jahan's wet nurse.
- "احوال حضرت شیخ حسو تیلی" (pp. 608–610): the khanqah and its fittings, the urs of the Teli community, a karamat, the disputed khadim chain, the death date and Madho Lal Husain as his contemporary.
- An untitled, very damaged passage on Hazrat Huzur Ali[OCR?] and Lal Husain (p. 611).
- "حال گورستان حکیم حاکم شاہ" (pp. 611–612).
- "حال موضع جیو [OCR?] متعلقہ ضلع لاہور" (pp. 612–614): the founding of a village, then Shah Hakim's death, karamat and graves.
- "احوال تکیہ گنج علی شاہ" (p. 614), on Hammam Daryam Shah[OCR?] and Daryam Shah Lahori.
- "حال مفصل رسوم مروجہ خاندان فقرای جلالیہ" (p. 614), which breaks off mid-sentence.

Two archive rows are touched: Madho Lal Husain and, through a chain of khadims, Shah Jamal.

## Practices, institutions, economy

- **Fazil Shah's takia:**
  - A faqir stayed and held an urs. On the urs day there was a "حلقہ فقرا" (p. 603).
  - The place became "بالکل خراب و ویران" (wholly ruined and deserted). It is now in the care of a named person (name illegible), and fatiha is held (p. 603).
- **Tank of Sayyid:**
  - Women take sick children there, bathe them and distribute "شیرینی" or bread. This is done especially for children with "خارش" (itch) and wasting "سایہ زدہ" (shadow-struck) children (p. 604).
  - In Abd al-Khaliq's lifetime the sick were told to bathe in the tank and were cured. The custom continued after his death (p. 605).
  - A garbled line on a fixed day ("یوم الثلثی[OCR?]"), and "شہر بھر" coming (p. 605).
  - Jagir granted "سرو اسطی[OCR?] مدہ[OCR?] معائش": "موضع راوال[OCR?]" "قریب ہزار و ام[OCR?]", and "موضع صالح پور[OCR?]" (p. 605).
  - The author's own visit: "نہ تو کوئی نشان مدرسہ باقی ہے" (no trace of the madrasa remains). The land is now cultivated, and the tank has no "نام نشان". People still go and take earth from the ground to bathe sick children (p. 606).
- **Dai Anga mosque — architecture:**
  - "کار کاشی[OCR?]" on the arches and Quranic inscriptions "بخط عربی" (p. 607).
  - "چار مینار" (four minarets) (p. 607).
  - Two minarets survive on the east (p. 606).
  - Now used within railway premises (pp. 606–607).
- **Hasu Teli khanqah:**
  - Enclosure "دو قد آدم بلند" on one "بیگہ" of ground, with a west gate (p. 608).
  - A brick chabutra "طول اسکا الیس[OCR?] گز اور عرض الھارہ[OCR?] گز ارتفاع ایک گز" (p. 608).
  - A water trough for birds (p. 608).
  - Graves of "دس عدد تیلیوں" (ten Telis) (p. 608).
  - Trees: kikar and others (p. 608).
  - "[OCR?]چہ بیگہ زمین مزروعہ معہ چاہ" (p. 608).
  - Faqir "حسین شاہ" lives there and built the whole enclosure; before that it was "صرف ایک چبوترہ" (just a platform) (p. 608).
  - Urs "تیسری شوال کو مقرر". "تمام تیلی قوم" (the whole Teli community) attends and stays the night. Sweet dishes are cooked and distributed, there is "راگ و رنگ" all night, and the next day "نان و گوشت" and "نوالی[OCR?]" (qawwali) (p. 608).
  - Fatiha is held on the death day (p. 610).
- **Hasu Teli custodianship dispute:**
  - Husain Shah calls himself a khadim of the family, but "وغیرہ لوگ اسکو اس خاندان کا خادم نہیں کہتے" (others do not accept that he is) (p. 610).
  - The son of "امام بخش[OCR?]" says "میرا باپ کسی کا مرید نہ تھا" (my father was no one's murid) (p. 610).
  - The book's "اصلی حال":
    - Mian Khan was the first mujawir;
    - his khadim was Hamrah Shah, who made his elder son khadim;
    - that son was incapable, and the place fell into neglect;
    - "یہ حسین شاہ مالک بن بیٹھا اور روپیہ خرچ کرکے" took it over (p. 610).
- **Village Jiyo[OCR?] and Shah Hakim:**
  - "معافی" (revenue-free grant) of wells and cultivated land; "انعامداری سرکار خالصہ ضبط ہو گئی" (the inam was confiscated by the Khalsa sarkar) (p. 613).
  - "بعہد ملکہ معظمہ [OCR?] اٹھارہ سو [illegible]", Ali Pur was "جاگیر راجہ و میاں [illegible]" (p. 613).
  - A kikar inside the enclosure "سو سال سے کھڑا ہے" (has stood for a hundred years) (p. 613).
- **Jalali faqirs — rite of bai'at:**
  - At bai'at the murshid takes a piece of cloth, rolls it, sets one end alight and places it on the murid's right arm. The mark stays "بطور مہر" (as a seal) (p. 614).
  - Then a "پچکول [OCR?]" and an agate or "سلیمانی [OCR?]" item are given; the text breaks off (p. 614).

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** Damage is heavy throughout, with dense `[OCR?]`/`[illegible]` flags. The following are largely unreadable:
  - p. 603;
  - the Dai Anga history (p. 607);
  - the Hasu Teli karamat (p. 609);
  - p. 611;
  - the Hakim Shah graveyard (pp. 611–612);
  - the Jiyo[OCR?] village founding (p. 612).

  Every quotation keeps its flags. Folios are not cited.
- **Page order.** The printed folio markers on pp. 611–613 do not continue those on pp. 603–610: the text reads 605 on p. 610 and 406 on p. 611. The p. 611 passage opens mid-story. Its link to the Hasu Teli section, which ends mid-sentence on Lal Husain on p. 610, is by content only. Check against the PDF.
- **madho-lal-hussain** is mapped because the book names "حضرت مادہو لال حسین" (p. 610) and "حضرت لال حسین" (p. 611). No site or date is given. The p. 611 passage is too damaged to state its point.
- **shah-jamal** is mapped on a bare name in a khadim chain (p. 610). The book gives no place (no Ichhra) and no date. The chain passes through Shihab al-Din Suhrawardi, but the book does not identify this Shah Jamal with the Lahore row. "حضرت سا چمال[OCR?]" (p. 609) is a flagged reading. The chain itself is disputed in the book, and Husain Shah's claim is contested (p. 610). Weigh accordingly.
- **No Lahore-in-general bullet.** Lahore appears as the location of named sites, as a mohalla ("محلہ مولیان", "محلہ سوامی من[OCR?]"), as a gate ("دروازہ شاہ عالمی") and as "مطبع لاہور". There are two possible city-level lines:
  - "جب پشور[OCR?] [illegible] ساکنان [illegible] لاہور" (p. 606);
  - "شہر بھر ولی لاہور" (p. 605).

  Both are too damaged to say what they concern. So the 35 `lahore` ids are not attached.
- **"گورستان میانی"** (p. 603) is not mapped to the 4 `miani sahib graveyard` rows. It is the location of a grave in a damaged sentence, not material on the graveyard. Recorded for the consolidator.
- **"ضلع لاہور"** (p. 612) is not mapped to the 2 `lahore district` rows. It locates a named village and is not material on the district.
- **"علاقہ گجرات"** (p. 606) is not mapped to shrine-of-hazrat-shah-daula-daryai. It is only where a non-archive family moved.
- **"شہید گنج[OCR?]"** (p. 606) is a flagged locality name for Qutb Shah's residence. It is not mapped to gurdwara-shaheed-ganj-singh-singhnian.
