# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 007 — pp. 85–96

This chunk is almost entirely about the khanqah of Hazrat Madho Lal Husain. It opens in the middle of a succession narrative (pp. 85–87), covering Sain Azmat Shah, Wahid Shah, Ashraf Shah ("Subah Shah"), Karim Husain and the present sajjada nishin Hasan Ali Shah, and the shrine's dependent takias and khanqahs. Four titled sections follow, in this order: "بیان سیر گردی[OCR?] و [illegible] و معافیات متعلقہ خانقاہ حضرت مادھو لال حسین", on the shrine's grants, lands, fairs, bhandaras and debts (pp. 87–90); "بیان سرگروہی[OCR?] سجادہ نشین مزار حضرت مادہو لال حسین", on the headship (sargirohi) and a dispute over it (p. 90); "بیان تعمیر مزار حضرت مادہو لال حسین", on how the tomb came to be built, with legends (pp. 90–91); and "حال عمارت موجودہ خانقاہ حضرت مادہو لال حسین", a long description of the standing buildings (pp. 91–96), which is still running when the chunk ends. The author says the sajjada nishin and his khuddam came to his house and dictated the account (p. 87). The damage is very heavy: pp. 86, 88 and 92 are mostly `[illegible]`.

## Practices, institutions, economy
- Custodianship passes by appointment and dastar, with a guardian (sarparast / "نائب سجادگی") named during a minority (p. 85). The head of the faqirs, the sargiroh, receives "حق سرگروہی" (p. 90).
- The fair calendar was reset by solar date, to 14 Baisakh, three years before the inquiry (p. 89). The fairs, bhandaras, costs, lands, sales and debt are detailed under madho-lal-hussain (pp. 89–90).
- Successive states figure as patrons: Mughal grants, the "ناظمان لاہور", Sikh rulers, and Ranjit Singh to Dalip Singh. Land was lost at the English settlement (pp. 88–89, 95).
- A local official, the tehsildar, ordered a safety wall around the well after a death at the fair (p. 94).

## Legends and miracles (karamat)
- Azmat Shah was told "عالمِ رویا میں حضرت مادھو لال حسین" that the boy was too young and "پنجاب میں صوبہ ہوگا", and that Wahid Shah should be made his na'ib until he came of age (p. 85).
- Subah Shah prayed through Madho Lal Husain for the Maharaja, and "مہاراج ہمیشہ با مراد و [illegible] آتو تھے". The book adds "صدہا کرامات" of his were well known (p. 86).
- A jogi[OCR?] opposed the burial. A voice came "حضرت کی لاش سے", from the body, telling him to dig, and a musalla and a Quran were found. The jogi became the Hazrat's khadim, embraced Islam, and was named Khaki Diwan (pp. 90–91).
- A red dastar was left as an amanat for "مادہو" and handed to him when he came, and he went "زندہ زمین میں", alive into the earth (p. 91).
- Mu'izz-ud-Din[OCR?] regained the throne "بامداد[OCR?] حضرت" after his vow at the mazar (p. 91).
- Through the mercy of a majzub faqir buried at the khanqah (died "سن بارہ سو اکیس[OCR?]"), Lahore was spared by Ahmad Shah Abdali (p. 91).
- A carpenter seeking offspring was told he would have four sons, named "الغو[OCR?] حبنا[OCR?] موسیٰ سرو[OCR?]". Musa and Habna[OCR?] are said to be alive (p. 92).
- Husain said that whoever passes through the "بہشتی دروازہ" on [illegible] day will be "بہشتی" (p. 93).
- Of Sadr Diwan Sahib, "کہتے ہیں کہ اونہوں نے حضرت حسین کو اپنی بستان پر [illegible] میں [illegible] کیا تھا" (p. 95).

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)
- The author's judgement is that faqiri has nothing to do with caste or occupation: "فقیر کو ذات اور پیشہ سے کچھ علاقہ نہیں" (p. 85).
- The author argues that the old building deserves the sarkar's care, as Jahangir's[OCR?] tomb receives (p. 95).
- The author names the sajjada nishin as his oral source (p. 87).

- "بوقت بندوبست سرکار انگریزی باعث کسی سہو کے یہ [illegible] ضبط ہوگئی[OCR?]" means "at the English sarkar's settlement, through some error, this was confiscated" (p. 89).

## Doubts
- **Damage inventory.** There are many `[OCR?]` and `[illegible]` flags throughout. Pp. 86, 88 and 92 are mostly illegible, so the grants narrative (p. 88) and the carpenter legend (p. 92) are fragmentary. All numerals are flagged or partly illegible: "بارہ سو[OCR?] [illegible]" (p. 86), "بارہ سو ستر[OCR?]" (p. 87), "بارہ سو اکاسی[OCR?]" / "۱۲۸۱[OCR?]" (p. 87), "بارہ سو اکیس[OCR?]" (p. 91), "تیس[OCR?]" and "دس[OCR?]" years, "ایک ہزار چوہتر[OCR?]", "چالیس[OCR?]", "نو[OCR?] [illegible] سو", "بجس[OCR?]", and the land figures. None of these has been converted.
- **Folio markers** appear on pp. 89–94 and 96 but are not cited, per the brief. The printed sequence skips one number between p. 90 and p. 91, and p. 95 has no marker.
- **Whose death and which date.** It is unclear whose death is dated tenth Rajab 12[..] (p. 86), and which event "بارہ سو ستر[OCR?] … ششم[OCR?] رمضان" (p. 87) refers to: Subah Shah's death or Karim Husain's accession.
- **"صوبا / صوبہ".** The p. 85 dream phrase "پنجاب میں صوبہ ہوگا" seems to play on the title Subah, but this is not resolved.
- **The "Punjab" province bullet is weak.** Both statements (pp. 85, 95) are incidental to the shrine. It is province-level and should be weighed accordingly. "سرکار" at p. 95 is carried as printed.
- **samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh is mapped on the principal figure only.** The samadhi is not mentioned, and everything recorded under that id is about his relations with the Madho Lal Husain shrine.
- **The Lahore-in-general bullet** rests on two Ahmad Shah Abdali passages (pp. 88, 91). The attachment is place-level, not site-specific. The second passage is a legend.
- **"حضرت میراں بادشاہ" (p. 90) is not mapped.** The chunk gives no location or full name. It is not placed against any archive row.
- **"شیخ ارزانی[OCR?]" with a mazar in "ملتان[OCR?]" (p. 94)** names a single tomb, not Multan in general, so no Multan toponym bullet is made. "پنج ارزانی[OCR?]" (p. 95) may be the same name and is unresolved.
- **The builder's name is printed two ways:** "حضرت [illegible] بلاول[OCR?] صاحب" (p. 91) and "حضرت رنگ[OCR?] لادل[OCR?] صاحب" (p. 94). They may be the same person, and this is unresolved.
- **Places in district Amritsar** (pp. 85, 89) and "[illegible]گڑھ[OCR?] ضلع لاہور" (p. 89) are endowment villages. They are named as locations, not as toponym material.
- **"مقبرہ شاہ جہانگیر[OCR?]" (p. 95)** is OCR-flagged and is not an archive row.
