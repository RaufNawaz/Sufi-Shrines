# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 058 — pp. 646–654

The chunk opens mid-story, with no heading, on the fall of "مہر محکم" (Mehr Muhkam): Maharaja Ranjit Singh's entry into Lahore, his attachment to the tawaif "موراں" (Moran), the quarrel between Moran and Mehr Muhkam, and the destruction of "نوان کوٹ" and Mehr Muhkam's ruin (pp. 646–648). **The heading of that section is in an earlier chunk.** Four titled sections follow. "حال مقبرہ سید نواب صدر جہان مرحوم" (p. 648) covers that tomb west of Lahore, Sadr Jahan's Akbar-period jagir and title, his descendants' taluqas and genealogy, and a smaller rauza with six graves (pp. 648–649). "حال خانقاہ سید عبدالقادر ثانی" (p. 649), then "حال حضرت سید عبدالقادر ثانی" (p. 650), cover the khanqah between the city and "موضع مزنگ", the forts of his descendants, Mir Yaqub, the saint's arrival, a karamat, his death, and a succession dispute over the sajjada decided under Shah Jahan with Alamgir. Building descriptions of the khanqah and of Mir Yaqub's mazar follow (pp. 652–653). "حال دہرم سالہ جہنگر شاہ" (p. 653) describes a dharamsala beside the Lahore fort, an initiation rite, shabad verses, and the story of the fakir "ستہرا" and "حیدرا مل" before Alamgir; the chunk breaks off mid-sentence (p. 654). Mapped: the Lahore-in-general bullet (35 ids), samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh, tahir-bandagi-qadri, darbar-abul-muali-qadri, and Guru Nanak's 18 rows by the person rule.

## Practices, institutions, economy

- **Abd al-Qadir Thani — division of income before the dispute**: "ایک حصہ بخرچ لنگر" (one share for the langar), a second share distributed among dependents, and the rest split "نصفا نصف" between the brothers Sayyid Haji and Sayyid Sultan Akbar; offerings of the murids during the saint's life went to Sayyid Haji alone (p. 651).
- **Sajjada dispute**: in "بعہد شاہجہانی", Sayyid Muhammad Maruf, with access to Alamgir, claimed a share against Fath Muhammad, saying "میں یہی پوتا ہوں" (I too am a grandson); the case came before "حضرت شاہجہان بادشاہ غازی"; Shah Jahan said he would come in person "بمزار معلی شیخ عبدالقادر ثانی" and give the "دستار سجادگی" (pp. 651–652). Outcome under Legends.
- **Jagir lost**: "جب ملکہ[OCR?] پیران ہوا اور عہد خان بہادر بھی بگڑ گیا تو وہ جاگیر[OCR?] بھی ابار[OCR?] ہو گئی" (p. 651).
- **Abd al-Qadir Thani's khanqah — building**: a "چار دیواری" "بالمثال[OCR?] عمارت شاہجہانی" (like Shah Jahani work), an arched gateway, small domes, a platform with a mazar of "سید عبدالعلا[OCR?]", and a platform with graves of his descendants; the description is heavily damaged (p. 652).
- **Mir Yaqub's mazar — building**: an enclosure to the north, a stair, "پانچ خورو[OCR?] کبندیان[OCR?]" (five small domes), and a tall "چراغدان" with a small white dome (p. 653).
- **Sadr Jahan's tomb**: descendants' graves "پختہ"; a "تکبہ[OCR?]" and a mosque; the descendants are "متولی" (p. 648). A list of taluqas held by named descendants (p. 649).
- **Dharamsala initiation rite**: the newcomer places an offering before the guru, the place is cleaned, water touched by the feet ("پاؤن سکہ[OCR?]") is mixed with "قند سیاہ" (dark sugar) and given to drink while shabad is recited; the rest is shared out, and "افقیر[OCR?] … دگنا حصہ پانا ہے" (the fakir gets a double share) (p. 653).

## Legends and miracles (karamat)

- **Abd al-Qadir Thani**: a slip of paper written "یا شیخ عبدالقادر جیلانی[OCR?] … مدد" was shown; on seeing it "نعرہ رباں[OCR?] بہاگ نکلا" (fled crying out). Who or what fled is garbled (p. 650).
- **Alamgir's stomach-ache**: the night before Shah Jahan's visit, Alamgir had "درد شکم" so severe he nearly died, and was told by ilham that whoever is "سجادہ نشین ہمارا" stays so, and "اگر عزل و نصب کرلگا[OCR?] تو تیری ہی سلطنت میں عزل و نصب ہو جاویگا" (if you depose and appoint, deposing and appointing will befall your own kingdom). Next morning Sayyid Muhammad Maruf was told to give up the claim and "سید میر محمد فتح صاحب سجادہ نشین مقرر رہیں" (pp. 651–652).
- **Mir Yaqub**: something is "مشہور" about sick people being cured; garbled (p. 650).
- **The seli of Haidra Mal**: before Alamgir, the seli was put on the head; afterwards Muslims and others came to take threads from it; the passage is heavily damaged (p. 654).

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** Very heavy throughout: most lines carry several `[OCR?]` flags, pp. 646, 650, 652 and 654 are largely unreadable, and the shabad verses (pp. 653–654) are garbled. Every quotation keeps its flags. Folio markers are printed but not cited, per the brief.
- **Lahore-in-general bullet: place-level.** The attachment to all 35 `lahore` ids is for the city's history (Ranjit Singh's entry and conquest, Akbar's enthronement); it is not site-specific. The passages are garbled; weigh accordingly.
- **samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh: person-level.** The samadhi is not named; mapped by the person rule from Ranjit Singh. The opening story's subject (Mehr Muhkam) begins in an earlier chunk.
- **Guru Nanak's 18 rows: person-level**, not site-specific; no gurdwara is named. "با نا نک" (p. 654) is in a garbled line.
- **tahir-bandagi-qadri mapped on a flagged name** ("طاہر بندگی[OCR?]", p. 649; "طا مر سدگی ہو[OCR?]", p. 650). The chunk gives no location, date or "Qadri" for him. Bare name; weigh accordingly.
- **darbar-abul-muali-qadri mapped on the printed "شاہ المعالی"** (p. 652), not "ابوالمعالی"; in a badly damaged sentence. Bare name; weigh accordingly.
- **Not mapped, gates and quarters as locations**: "دروازہ بھائی"/"بھائی دروارہ[OCR?]" (p. 646; printed "بھائی", not repaired) is not attached to the `bhati gate` rows; "موضع مزنگ" (p. 649), the location of Abd al-Qadir Thani's khanqah, is not attached to the `mozang` rows; "قلعہ لاہور" (p. 653), the landmark for the dharamsala, is not attached to the `lahore fort` rows. Recorded for the consolidator to weigh against ruling (a).
- **"چراغ شاہ" / "سید چراغ شاہ"** (pp. 649, 650) are descendants in family genealogies, not the saint of shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh; not mapped. (The row appears only in the Lahore place-level bullet; the held identity question is not touched by this chunk.)
- **"ضلع امرتسر"** (p. 650) and **"ضلع گورداس پورہ"** (p. 654) are outside Pakistan; no province bullet.
- **"برہما بیشن ؛ ہا دیوکان"** in the shabad (p. 653) is not mapped to the Shiva rows; the printed form is not "مہادیو".
- **Chronology not checked**: Abd al-Qadir Thani's death "۱۰۹۲[OCR?]" (p. 650) and a dispute after his death decided "بعہد شاہجہانی" before Shah Jahan with Alamgir (p. 651). Carried as printed; do not compute.
- **Mir Yaqub's death given two ways**: "سہ محرم شنبہ ۱۹۶[OCR?]" (p. 650) and "تیسری محرم سنہ [illegible] بروز سہ شنبہ" (p. 653); the year "۱۹۶[OCR?]" is incomplete.
- **Sadr Jahan's death** "ایک ہزار انیس[OCR?]" (p. 649) and the distance "حہ در[OCR?] میل" (p. 648) are flagged.
- **"مہان سنگہ[OCR?]"** and **"گورو رہمر[OCR?] رائے"** are carried as printed and not identified.
- **Two darbars on the site but not in the index** (Shah Gohar Peer; Mian Qurban Ali Shah): neither is named in this chunk.
