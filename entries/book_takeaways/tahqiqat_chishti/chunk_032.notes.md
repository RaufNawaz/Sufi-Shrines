# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 032 — pp. 356–364

The chunk opens mid-description (p. 356) of a walled family graveyard of "امام الدین[OCR?] خالصہ[OCR?] مرحوم" / "نواب صاحب", with the graves of the Nawab's mothers and a Persian chronogram dated "۱۲۶۶[OCR?] ہجری". **The heading of that section is in an earlier chunk**, and the chunk does not say whether this enclosure belongs to the Bibi Pak Daman precinct. From p. 357, without a new heading, the text tells the legend of the "بی بی صاحبان": the raja's demand, the conversion of the raja's child, the earth opening to receive the Bibis, the four hafiz who stayed, and the mass conversion (pp. 357–358). It then gives the site's building history from "سلطان محمود غزنوی" and "بادشاہ اکبر" onward (p. 359). A long walk-through of the surrounding graveyards, the mujawars' dalans, the deorhi, Baba Khaki's grave and mosque follows (pp. 359–361). Next comes the tomb of "سید سلطان جلال الدین حیدر [OCR?] بخاری" with its Akbar-period and Mauj Darya Bukhari material and a chronogram by Mufti Ghulam Sarwar (pp. 361–362). Then the layout of the Bibis' graves, the names of those buried, the sajjada nashin mujawars, their division of income and their lineage back to Baba Khaki (pp. 362–364). A new section, "حال بی بی حظیمہ[OCR?] المسہو[OCR?] بہ سوی[OCR?] تنو[OCR?]", begins on p. 364 and is cut off at the chunk's end. ## Legends and miracles (karamat)

- **Bibi Pak Daman — the raja's child**: the elder Bibi's single glance made the raja's child fall senseless, and the child then converted (p. 357).
- **Bibi Pak Daman — the earth opens**: the earth split at the Bibis' prayer and took in all the Bibis and maidservants (p. 357).
- **Bibi Pak Daman — the convert's marriage**: guided by an ilham at the mazar, the convert married; his wife's face became like the full moon; "چھ سات ہزار[OCR?]" people converted on seeing this karamat (p. 358).
- **The Bibis seen afterwards**: a damaged line says they were once seen ("نظر آتے تھے") and then vanished ("ناپید ہو گئی") (p. 358).
- **Yaqub Khan's grave**: "قدرت الٰہی سے" the place was his lot; he was killed near Begumpura and buried there. The book frames this as divine ordering (p. 359).

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** Damage is heavy, and pp. 361–364 are near-total: almost every token on pp. 361–364 carries `[OCR?]`, and the Mauj Darya and income passages (pp. 362–364) are largely garbled. Every quotation keeps its flags.
- **Folio markers are not cited.** The printed folio markers in this chunk run irregularly (the sequence repeats and skips between p. 359 and p. 364), which confirms the brief's rule.
- **bibi-pak-daman mapping.** The section heading naming the shrine is not in this chunk. The chunk says "بی بی صاحبان" throughout; the only "پاکدامان" is flagged ("حضرات پاکدامان[OCR?]", p. 364). The mapping rests on the continuous narrative and that phrase; weigh accordingly. The chunk gives no count of the Bibis.
- **Bibi Pak Daman — the raja's child: daughter or son?** p. 357 has "اوسکی بیٹی کا نام بکرماجیت[OCR?]" and "راجہ کی لڑکی", but the same figure is also "سردار ولی عہد" and "کنور[OCR?]", the verbs are masculine ("بہوش میں آیا[OCR?] تو اٹھ[OCR?] پڑا[OCR?]"), and on p. 358 "کنور" converts. Carried as printed; this is not resolved here.
- **Bibi Pak Daman — numbers.** The companions are given as "سات سو چار[OCR?]" and later "سات[OCR?] اشخاص" (p. 357); the converts as "چھ سات ہزار[OCR?]" (p. 358). All are flagged numerals.
- **Bibi Pak Daman — the four hafiz.** p. 358 says the four remained and that their graves exist, then says "وہ سبھی آپکے ساتھ ہی زمین میں سما گئی". The subject is unclear and may be the maidservants. Internal tension, not resolved here.
- **Bibi Pak Daman — the principal grave.** It is named "بی بی حاج [OCR?]", true name "بی بی رقیہ [OCR?]", and called daughter of Ali (p. 363). Both names are flagged.
- **Dates for Bibi Pak Daman.** The four hundred years of Hindu rajas and the Mahmud Ghaznavi and Akbar building phases (p. 359) are the book's own chronology; no year is given. "۱۲۶۶[OCR?]" (p. 356) belongs to the Nawab's mother's grave, and its link to the precinct is not stated.
- **shrine-of-mauj-darya-bukhari** is mapped by name (person rule). The chunk does not mention his own shrine or its location. Every statement about him sits in garbled text (p. 362); the firman's location "وٹالہ [OCR?]" and "قلعہ حضور گڑھ [OCR?]" are flagged and not identified.
- **shrine-of-mian-mir** is a thin mapping. "میاں میر[OCR?]" is a road destination on p. 360, flagged, and could be a locality name rather than the shrine. Weigh accordingly.
- **No Lahore-in-general bullet.** "لاہور" occurs only as a road's origin (p. 360) and as where Mauj Darya's descendants live (p. 362), and neither is city-level material. The "قتل عام" at the time of "قتل احمد شاہ[OCR?]" (p. 359) names no city. So the 35 `lahore` ids are not attached.
- **Uch is not mapped.** "انکا مقام [OCR?] اوچ [OCR?]" (p. 362) is the home of a non-archive saint (Jalal al-Din Haidar Bukhari) and is flagged. Following chunk 015's handling of Peshawar, the 5 `uch sharif` ids are not attached. Recorded for the consolidator to weigh against ruling (a).
