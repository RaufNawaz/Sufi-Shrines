# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 056 — pp. 625–635

The chunk opens mid-section (p. 625): **its heading is in the previous chunk (055)**. It covers decayed graves, a takia with fakirs' graves, a domed rauza "باغبان پورہ میں" and a Shahjahani mosque that once stood in the garden of Husain Ali Khan, with a note from "کتاب مراۃ الہند" (pp. 625–626). ## Legends and miracles (karamat)

- **Sa'dullah Khan's birth**:
  - His pregnant mother craved a "ولایتی انار" (foreign pomegranate). The father went with her silver earrings to Kabuli fruit merchants who had come to "سیالکوٹ" with a royal "ڈالی".
  - The merchant, "قیافہ شناس", divined a fortunate son in the womb. He gave the fruit free in return for a written undertaking ("اقرار نامہ").
  - The book tells this as a story ("کہتے ہیں" framing elsewhere), not as karamat (p. 631).
- **The Shah of Iran's letter**:
  - The Shah objected to the name "شاہ جہاں". Shah Jahan threatened Asaf Khan with death if he did not answer (p. 632).
  - Sa'dullah answered before the emperor by abjad: "جہان" and "ہند" have equal value, printed "ا ن[OCR?] ۱[OCR?] ۵۰[OCR?]" and "۵[OCR?] ۵۰[OCR?] ۴[OCR?]", so the name stands (p. 633).
- **Dari[OCR?] Shah's hand**, mauled by the lion over the gate (p. 627). Told as an incident, not karamat.

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- **Husain Ali Khan as shahid**: the author disputes "مراۃ الہند" calling him "شہید". He writes "شہید وہ ہو ما ہی جو کافر کے ہاتھہ سے مارا جاوے" (a shahid is one killed at the hand of a kafir), so he cannot be called shahid (p. 626).
- **The "mosque" in Mian Khan's garden**: the author argues that "اگر اسکو مسجد کہیں تو سجدہ بطرف مشرق ہوتا ہے" (if it is called a mosque, prostration would face east), because its back is to the east. He compares its form to "مسجد علی" (p. 628).
- **The women's graves**: in the rauza the author judges "یہ قبور زنانی معلوم ہوتی ہیں" (p. 625).
- **The ruin of Mian Khan's tomb**: "افسوس اور مقام عبرت ہے". He cites the couplet "ہر کہ آمد عمارت نو ساخت / رفت و منزل بدیگری پرداخت" (p. 629).

## Doubts

- **Damage inventory.** Damage is heavy, especially pp. 625, 628, 630 and 632–634. On those pages architectural descriptions and the Sa'dullah narrative are often illegible mid-sentence. Every quotation above keeps its flags. Folio markers (623–633) are printed and not cited. **p. 631 carries no folio marker**, and the folios jump from 628 (p. 630) to 630 (p. 632). This is recorded, not resolved.
- **Opening fragment (p. 625)** belongs to a section headed in chunk 055. Whose death "عہد محمد شاہی میں" refers to cannot be told from this chunk.
- **samadhi-of-maharaja-ranjit-singh** is mapped by the person rule only. Every mention names Ranjit Singh as the ruler in whose time stone was taken. The samadhi itself is not mentioned. "رنجیت سکہ[OCR?]" (p. 630) is flagged. "عہد مہاراجہ" (p. 627, the lion over the gate) names no maharaja and is not attached.
- **The 8 Shiva rows** are attached by the many-row person rule from "شیو جی کا لنگ[OCR?]" at a non-archive dharamsala in Lahore (p. 635). This is person-level, not site-specific; weigh accordingly. "لنگ" is flagged.
- **Nath jogis are not mapped to Gorakhnath rows.** The chunk names only "[illegible] ناتھ", "پریم[OCR?] ناتھ", "ترت[OCR?] ناتھ" and "پہول[OCR?] ناتھ". Gorakhnath is not named, so gorakhnath-goraknath-temple and tilla-jogian are not attached.
- **No Lahore-in-general bullet.** Lahore appears as the location of named non-archive tombs, havelis and the dharamsala. The nearest city-level phrases are "عمارات سعد اللہ خانی دہلی و آگرہ و لاہور میں موجود ہیں" (p. 633, about one man's buildings) and a garbled "ریاست لاہور" (p. 630). So the 35 `lahore` ids are not attached. Flagged for the consolidator.
- **Baghbanpura is not mapped to madho-lal-hussain.** "باغبان پورہ" (p. 625) locates a non-archive rauza, and "محلہ باغبان[OCR?] پورہ[OCR?]" (p. 634) appears in a fragment about the Shah Alami–Mochi gate stretch. These are mohalla locations, not material on Madho Lal Hussain. It is not clear from the chunk whether the two refer to the same place.
- **Gates.** "دروازہ شاہ عالمی", "موچی دروازہ" and "اندرون دروازہ دہلی" are named as locations only and are not mapped.
- **Chiniot is not mapped to langer-makhdoom.** "ساکن چنیوٹ[OCR?]" (p. 631) and the jami mosque "بمقام چنیوٹ" (p. 633) concern Sa'dullah Khan's family and a non-archive mosque. Neither Langer Makhdoom nor its figure is named. This follows the chunk 015 precedent for a family residence; flagged for the consolidator against ruling (a). The held identity question on langer-makhdoom does not arise here.
- **Other places not mapped:**
  - Multan: "ساکن ملتان" and the jogi's arrival "شہر وارد ملتان", p. 635. These are a person's origin and an episode, not Multan material, so the 8 `multan` ids are not attached.
  - Sialkot: fruit merchants came to "سیالکوٹ", p. 631. The 5 `sialkot` ids are not attached.
  - Amritsar and Haridwar are outside Pakistan. Delhi and Agra have no rows.
- **No province-level material.** "بعملداری سکھاں", "سرکار" and "مہاراج" are carried as printed. "Punjab" is not named.
- **Parvez's parentage** is printed "شاہزادہ پرویز بن شاہجہان" (heading) and "خلف شاہجہان بادشاہ" (p. 626). It is carried as printed and not checked (RULE 2).
- **Mian Khan / Sa'dullah Khan relation is contradictory in the chunk:**
  - The headings say Mian Khan is "پسر نواب سعد اللہ خان" (pp. 627, 630).
  - p. 630 also has "حویلی نواب سعد اللہ خاں ولد نواب میاں خاں", which reverses the relation.
  - p. 630 further says "میاں خاں لاولد فوت ہوا", while Hidayat Ali's line follows.
  - Not resolved.

- **Sa'dullah's mother** is said to die when he was five (p. 631), yet the merchant's toll is waived "حسب وعدہ والدہ" (p. 634). The p. 634 passage is heavily illegible, so the sense is uncertain.
- **Names flagged in the takia section:** "نیکہ[OCR?] جیبی[OCR?] شاہ", "جیمی[OCR?] شاہ" and "جیمہ شاہ" (p. 625) may be one name read three ways. They are not reconciled. "داری[OCR?] شاہ" is flagged at every occurrence (p. 627).
- **The two darbars on the site but not in the index** (Shah Gohar Peer; Mian Qurban Ali Shah) are not named in this chunk.
- **Held identity questions:** shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh and langer-makhdoom are not named in this chunk.
