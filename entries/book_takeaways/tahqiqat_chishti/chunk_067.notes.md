# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 067 — pp. 731–740

The chunk opens mid-section (p. 731) on the architectural description of a walled garden (باغ) with a hidden mosque ("مسجد خفیہ"), a well, tanks and a dalan; the section heading and the garden's name are in the previous chunk. P. 732 continues with the garden's size, its custodians and a tomb in it that "Parsi people" honour, then a short titled excursus "مختصر حال پارسیان" (pp. 732–733), which turns into a rebuttal of Lala Amin Chand's *Safarnama* on the Wazir Khan mosque. The next titled section, "اصل حال مسجد وزیر خان" (pp. 733–738), gives the life of Hakim Ilm-ud-Din, titled Nawab Wazir Khan, the building of the mosque, a full copy of its waqfnama dated "۱۰۵۱[OCR?]" hijri, his part in Shah Jahan's accession, and the state of his descendants; it ends with a long, badly damaged defence of the "خاندان فقیر صاحبان" of Lahore against an author's remarks (pp. 738–739). The last titled section, "حال عمارت مسجد وزیر خان مرحوم" (pp. 739–740), begins the description of the mosque's buildings from Delhi Gate inward and of the octagonal tomb/takia of "میراں سید معروف صاحب"; the chunk ends on an `[illegible]` line (p. 740). The text is extremely damaged throughout (almost every word on pp. 732, 738–740 carries `[OCR?]`); only what can be read is reported. Folio markers are printed but not cited (brief rule).

## Practices, institutions, economy

- Waqf of the Wazir Khan mosque (pp. 736–737): endowed shops on both sides of the road with upper rooms, a large sarai, hammam and other properties inside Lahore; inalienable ("لا تباع و لا توہب ولا یرہن"); management to the founder for life, then his son "محمد سعید جان[OCR?]" and descendants; final beneficiaries "الفقرا المسلمین"; posts of imam/khatib, muezzin and madrasa teachers paid from the rents (figures damaged: "بہ نارہ[OCR?] روپیہ", "جہار[OCR?]"); shops outside the eastern gate rent-free for "صحافاں[OCR?] کتب اسلامیہ"; family members' shares of rent; rule "کما ہو الحکم فی المذہب الحنفی" (pp. 736–737).
- Delhi Gate complex (p. 739): a pakka sarai with hammam south of the gate, now named "...گنج"; east of the mosque a chowk bazar; a large arched gateway with three shops on each side and a shah-nashin above.
- Chowk under Sikh rule: "بعہد سکھاں" it was spoiled and shops built in it; now "حسب الحکم سہر کا[OCR?]" restored to its original form (p. 740).
- Garden tomb (p. 732): a lamp is lit daily; the Parsis honour the grave.

## Legends and miracles (karamat)

- The cure of the Begum's sciatica by a hidden lancet, with a Persian couplet "خون فاسد بہ نشتر فساد" spoken by the ruler (p. 735) — told as history, not as karamat.

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- The author disputes Lala Amin Chand twice: on Parsi burial (Miani Sahib evidence; possibly a Bombay custom) (p. 733) and on the Wazir Khan / Sunehri mosques, arguing that Chaghtai histories show the founders were years apart (p. 733); he says an author must investigate before writing (p. 738).
- He presents the Wazir Khan account explicitly as a correction, "اصل حال مسجد وزیر خان" (p. 733).

## Doubts

- Damage inventory: very heavy `[OCR?]` throughout; pp. 732, 738, 739 (second half) and 740 are largely unreadable word salad; one `[illegible]` closes the chunk (p. 740) and a date "سنہ [illegible]" on p. 740. Section headings and the Wazir Khan story (pp. 733–737) are the most legible parts.
- Lahore bullet is place-level (ruling (a)), not site-specific; weigh accordingly.
- Miani Sahib bullet is place-level (toponym "miani sahib graveyard", 4 rows); the material is only about the Parsi graveyard there and the author's cross-reference.
- The garden/tomb of pp. 731–732 cannot be identified from this chunk (heading in chunk 066); the deceased's name is illegible.
- "ساکن چہروٹ[OCR?]" (p. 734): Wazir Khan's home town as printed; not mapped. If a reviewer reads it as Chiniot it would be a toponym for **langer-makhdoom** — not applied here because the printed form does not support it securely.
- "میران باد شاہ" / "سید محمد سحاق[OCR?]" (p. 735) and his "brother" "میراں سید معروف" (p. 740): not mapped. Not to be confused with shrine-of-miran-hussain-zanjani-zanjani-sahib or shrine-of-mauj-darya-bukhari (Meeran Muhammad Shah) — the book's name matches neither.
- "شاہ بردہ[OCR?]" (p. 738), site of Jahangir's tomb, is quoted as printed; not resolved.
- Which Begum: p. 734 "...بیگم" (name damaged); p. 735 names "نورجہان بیگم" as giving the jewels; the book's identification is kept as printed.
- "تمام ملک بجات[OCR?]" (p. 738) may or may not be "Punjab"; too damaged — no province bullet written.
- The author whose remarks on the "Faqir sahiban" are rebutted (pp. 738–739) is printed as "ہمارا مہ[OCR?] رحمت[OCR?] سیک[OCR?]"; unidentifiable.
