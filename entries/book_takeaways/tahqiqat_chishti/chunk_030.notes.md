# tahqiqat_chishti — chunk 030 — pp. 334–345

The chunk opens mid-section (p. 334) with a badly damaged account of money given away to faqirs, a house of one Ghulam Rasul bought by a merchant from Delhi, a "قدم رسول" in that house, a house that "ریل کے اسٹیشن مین گیا", and a chain of khadims and pirs. **The heading of that section is in an earlier chunk.** Titled sections follow in this order: "مکان خانقاہ و مفصل[OCR?] شاہ" (p. 335; the saint is written "فضل شاہ" in the body), covering Fazl Shah's origin, Raja Dina Nath's patronage, the tomb, the yearly mela, his gaddi nashins, his Naushahi chain and his death (pp. 335–337, 339); "احوال مکان گھوڑی شاہ" (p. 339), a takia outside Kashmiri gate with the grave of Sayyid Kamal Shah Chishti; "احوال مکان چوبرجی" (p. 339, continued pp. 342–345), on the Chauburji as the gateway of Zeb-un-Nisa's garden, its tile-work, inscriptions and Mian Bai; "احوال مکان بخاریاں والہ" (p. 340), a takia and mosque north of Fazl Shah's takia; "حال باغیچہ بخشی کنہیا لعل صاحب" (p. 345), a walled garden beside Data Ganj Bakhsh's tomb held by his mujawars; and "حال تکیہ بھٹیاریاں والہ [OCR?]" (p. 345), where the chunk breaks off. pp. 338 and 341 are suppressed duplicate photographs and are not noted. None of the titled sections is an archive row; five archive rows are touched in passing.

## Practices, institutions, economy

- **Fazl Shah's tomb — buildings** (p. 335):
  - East-facing wooden door with "طاق تختہ"; a small west door like a window ("بطور کھڑکی"); a room on the north; brick wall of a man's height all round.
  - Trees along the inner wall: "سرو کیلہ امرود".
  - A kotha with a south "سایبان"; a structure with "چار برجیان چہوٹی چہوٹی"; a mosque with "تہ خانہ"; a ghusalkhana, "سقاوہ" and "سبیل" with four lotas for wudu; a pakka chabutra; a three-door dalan.
  - "ایک بڑا ملند[OCR?] چبوترہ" with "تہ خانہ مقبرہ فضل شاہ صاحب" beneath; on it his brick grave, white "جو نگ[OCR?]" (lime) (p. 335).
- **Fazl Shah's tomb — other graves**: several graves on platforms; named are "سید گوکل شاہ سیلاکے[OCR?]" and "محمد بخش … سیلاکے[OCR?]" (p. 336).
- **Fazl Shah — mela**: "میلا معہ محلس[OCR?] انکا سال سال ماہ ساون کی اکیسویں ہوتا ہی" (a mela with majlis every year on the 21st of Sawan) (p. 336). Something with "گوشت" is distributed (garbled) (p. 336). Kashmiri people are mentioned in connection with the urs (garbled) (p. 336).
- **Fazl Shah — patronage and income**: "صدہا[OCR?] روپیہ کا حج[OCR?] ما ہواری[OCR?]" (hundreds of rupees monthly) for his family; clothes and gold were kept for them (p. 336). After the raja's death "سب کجہ[OCR?] منذ[OCR?] ہو گیا"; the raja's khalifa and relatives still give something (p. 337).
- **Fazl Shah's son Buland Shah**: no fixed allowance now; lives by selling household goods ("گھر با رہ بیچ کر"); the raja had given him "چار پانچ منزل" (p. 337).
- **Fazl Shah — grave fittings**: a small green door in the grave enclosure; a stone floor; a "جراغدان" at the head of the grave (p. 337).
- **Fazl Shah — qawwali and mujra**: "ہمیشہ بیان قوال آتی مین … کبھی کبھی مجرا یعنی رقص طوایفان بھی ہو جاتا ہی" (p. 339).
- **Fazl Shah's takia — demolition and complaint** (p. 340): a faqirs' kotha was bought for "سترہ رویہ" and "مکان لشکر گرا دیا"; the faqirs complain "ہمارا امت[OCR?] بقتماں[OCR?] ہوا ہم عرصی[OCR?] دین گے" (we will submit a petition).
- **Ghori Shah takia**: a pakka well with wheel; a small cultivated plot (vegetables, flowers); mustard sown at the head of the grave; a platform for faqirs; townspeople come to smoke "حقہ تماکو" and bhang ("بنگ") is kept (p. 339). A banyan tree ("درخت بوڑہ") "ایکسو برس کا" (p. 339).
- **Bukhari mosque** (p. 340): inscriptions "ھو اللہ" and the kalima; couplets with "مسجد حمید"; a verse naming "ابو بکر و عمر عثمان و حیدر".
- **Chauburji — structure** (pp. 342–344):
  - Of four minarets, three remain; the one at the north-west corner has fallen (p. 342).
  - Gateway one storey outside and two inside; seats ("چبوترہ") two gaz long on either side; all walls "گلکار" (p. 342).
  - Minarets two storeys outside, three inside; upper burjis gone; from four gaz up, "کاشی" work; "۷۵ [OCR?] طاقچہ" in each minaret (p. 343).
  - A long inscription below the parapet with Ayat al-Kursi in "خط ثلث", copied in full by the book (p. 343).
  - The gate is high enough that "ہاتھی مع سواری اسکے اندر چلا جاوے"; most of the "چینی" work is now lost (p. 343).
  - A "بسنتی [OCR?] کاشی [OCR?]" inscription of four parts, three remaining; verse ending "ز لطف صاحب زیب النسا [OCR?] بیگم دوران" (p. 343).
  - A second inscription in which "میاں بائی [OCR?]" is read (p. 344).
- **Chauburji — surroundings**: the river used to flow west of it in the rains; "سرکار انگریزی نے [illegible] کاٹ ڈالا" (p. 342); "اب وہاں ریل مرور [OCR?] ہے" (p. 344). Land with "لارس [OCR?]" was exchanged by "سرکار" and came into the "باغ سرکار" (p. 344). A "بارہ دری" was demolished "عملداری انگریزی" (p. 344). The date of the buildings is not clear to the author (p. 344).
- **Bakhshi Kanhiya Lal's garden** (p. 345): high wall; two small burjis over the gate; a seating platform with two brick rooms; three rooms, a dalan with "بالاخانہ" and a "چوبارہ" inside; a pakka well "مع چرخ چوب جس میں ایک بیل چلتا ہی"; a trough for cattle outside; beds of pomegranate, "سیب [OCR?]", mulberry, banana, lemon trees; "اب اسمیں کرایہ دار رہتی میں" (tenants now live in it). Custodianship and the mujawars are under data-darbar above.

## Legends and miracles (karamat)

- **Fazl Shah**: "صدہا[OCR?] کرامات انکی کے لوگ معتقد مین" (people believe in hundreds of his karamat) (p. 336).
- **Fazl Shah's last words** (p. 337), two days before death:
  - To Buland Shah: stay with Jani Shah and you will live well.
  - To Jani Shah: "جو میری مانو والا ہوگا" will bring him offerings at home and serve him.
  - To Kakku[OCR?]: "میری قبر پر جا رو بکشی[OCR?] کیا کر اوقات بسر تیری ہوگی"; "چنانچہ آجتک کام اوسکا چلا جاتا ہی" (and so his work goes on to this day).
