  folio 175), then `yunus`, `yusuf`, `zakariya`, `zulaika` (p. 201, folio 176), where the Glossary
  ends. **There are no headwords at all under U, V or W.**
- **ABSENT as Glossary headwords, all twelve tested: `revenue`, `sajjada nashin`, `shrine`, `tomb`,
  `ʿurs`, `wakf`, `ziarat`, `samaʿ`, `sama`, `zikr`, `Sufi`, `saint`.** Each would fall inside a
  range this chunk covers in full (e.g. `revenue` between `ravan` and `roda`; `sajjada nashin` and
  `sama`/`samaʿ` between `sahti` and `sammi`; `shrine` between `shirin` and `sial`; `tomb` between
  `tegh bahadur` and `Turk`; `wakf`, `ziarat` and `zikr` in the empty W and the two-entry Z run).
  None is there.
- **"Sufi" and "saint" occur only inside definitions, never as headwords** — "Shams Sufi saint who
  was hanged…", "Sharaf Sufi saint of Panipat…", "Sarmad outspoken Sufi executed…", "also used by
  Sufis as the equivalent of Pir" (all p. 200, folio 175). The distinction matters for the test.
- **PRESENT as headwords in R–Z, and this is the whole of it**: `shah` and `shaikh` (titles),
  `qazi` and `quduri` (Islamic law), `Qibla` (prayer direction), `sirat` (eschatology), `savan`
  (calendar), `Turk` (communal category), `qarun` (a Qur'anic figure). **Not one of these names a
  built shrine, a shrine office, an endowment or a shrine income.**
- **THERE IS NO INDEX IN THIS VOLUME.** The back matter ends with the Bibliography at folio 180
  (p. 205) and is followed by four pages the transcriber records as `[blank page]` (pp. 206–209,
  folio not stated). No index headwords exist to test. **A human should confirm this against the

  cannot be excluded from inside chunk_007, though the folio sequence 175–180 running unbroken from
  Glossary into Bibliography and stopping leaves no room for one after it.
- **Result, stated plainly for the queue:** this third test agrees with Sorley and Schimmel. The
  built shrine and its economy have **no headword** in this book's apparatus, in either half of the
  Glossary, and the book has no index at all.

### Transcription damage and structure

- **Damage class 1 — the repeated-author dash is lost.** Where the Bibliography uses a long dash for
  a repeated author, the text layer renders it as three spaces and a period: printed "   . 2009."
  [reading: "———. 2009."], "   . 1999.", "   . 1992.", "   . 1993.", "   . 1995.", "   . 2011."
  (pp. 203–204, folios 178–179). **Seven entries are affected and none of them names its own
  author**: one under Quraeshi (2009), one under Rinehart (1999), and five under Shackle (1992,
  1993, 1995, 1999, 2011). Every such entry has been given its author explicitly above, and each is
  flagged `[text-layer?]` where quoted. **A machine reading this bibliography will otherwise
  attribute six works to nobody.**
- **Damage class 2 — a dropped letter.** "blackmith's [text-layer?]" [blacksmith's] (p. 200,
  folio 175, in the entry for `roda`). This is the only clear dropped-letter fault in the chunk.
  Unlike the Risalo volume in this queue, **the fi/fl/ff ligatures survive intact here** — "fish"
  (p. 201, folio 176), "Kāfīāñ", "Ferozsons", "Faqir" are all whole. The two books' text layers
  behave differently; do not assume the ligature fault on this book.
- **Damage class 3 — inconsistent apostrophes, same page.** Straight and curly apostrophes alternate
  within single lines of p. 200, folio 175: "Hir's" (straight) beside "Farhad's" (curly), "Ram's"
  (straight) beside "Hir's sister-in-law" (curly). Cosmetic, but it will defeat an exact-string
  search; noted so a searcher does not conclude a term is absent.
- **Damage class 4 — ʿayn rendered as a left single quote**, in "Qānūn-e ‘ishq" (Ruhtaki 1889,
  p. 202, folio 177) and "Mathnawī-yi Nayrang-i ‘Ishq" (Shackle 1999, p. 204, folio 179), and in
  "Sanan (San‘ān)" (p. 200, folio 175). Recorded as printed.
- **p. 200 (folio 175) IS STRUCTURALLY MANGLED AND SHOULD BE RE-RENDERED AS AN IMAGE.** It is a
  two-column glossary page and the text layer has interleaved the columns line by line, so the first
