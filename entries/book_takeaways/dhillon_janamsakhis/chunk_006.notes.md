## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- The author holds that Multan's conversions "were by and large voluntary, spurred on by the renunciation char [that] the Sufi saints practised" and the Sufi tenet of "the equality of all human beings" (p. 167, folio 183).
- The author suggests that each Muslim conquest's mosque-building was "Perhaps, in reaction to che [the] face [fact] chat [that] Multan had once been a stronghold of the Hindu religion" (p. 166, folio 182).
- The author's moral: the bowl and petal are "a symbol of the way we should lead our lives in relation co [to] those around us" (p. 171, folio 187).
