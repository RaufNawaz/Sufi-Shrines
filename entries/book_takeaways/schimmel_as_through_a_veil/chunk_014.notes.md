# schimmel_as_through_a_veil — chunk 014 — pp. 318–341

**This chunk is the heart of the book's BIBLIOGRAPHY.** It contains no prose argument: it is an
unbroken alphabetical author list running from `Corbin, Henry` (p. 318, folio 300) to
`Thomas, Gerald` (p. 341, folio 323). Its value to the archive is exactly what a finding aid is
worth — exact titles, editors, translators, publishers, **places of publication** and years of the
sources Schimmel actually used, a large minority of them Lahore, Karachi, Hyderabad/Sind, Multan,
Islamabad and Delhi imprints that an archive bibliography can cite directly.

**Folio regime confirmed on all 24 pages, no exceptions.** Every page in pp. 318–341 prints its
number inside the running-header line, and `folio = PDF − 18` (BRIEF §1) holds throughout. The
parity self-check passes on all 24: leading (verso) folios are even — `3 00` (p. 318), `3 02`
(p. 320), `3 04` (p. 322), `3 06` (p. 324), `3 08` (p. 326), `3 10` (p. 328), `3 12` (p. 330),
`3 14` (p. 332), `3 16` (p. 334), `3 18` (p. 336), `3 20` (p. 338), `3 22` (p. 340) — and trailing
(recto) folios are odd — `30 1` (p. 319), `3 03` (p. 321), `3 05` (p. 323), `3 07` (p. 325), `3 09`
(p. 327), `311` (p. 329), `3 13` (p. 331), `3 15` (p. 333), `3 17` (p. 335), `3 19` (p. 337), `32 1`
(p. 339), `3 23` (p. 341). **No page in this chunk uses "folio not stated."** The three-digit space
split of BRIEF §3 hits nearly every header folio, and it is closed silently here per that ruling.

**Chunk boundaries.** The bibliography begins before this chunk (the first page opens directly with
`Corbin, Henry`, mid-alphabet, p. 318, folio 300) and this chunk ends mid-entry: the last printed
line is `Thomas, Gerald. The Tall Tale and Philippe d'Alcripe. An Analysis of` (p. 341, folio 323),
which continues into chunk 015. Two entries straddle a page break inside the chunk: `Gabrieli` /
`"Religious Poetry in Early Islam"` across pp. 320–321 and `Hujwiri` across pp. 324–325.

**Archive mapping — every toponym bullet in this chunk is imprint-level.**

- **The Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and Hyderabad/Sind bullets rest on places of publication, not on

  and citable printed fact — it is often the only Pakistan-side detail an entry carries — and it is
  **not** evidence about any shrine. Flagged so a consolidator can weigh the bullets down or drop
  them wholesale. The attachment is in any case **place-level, not site-specific**.
- The **only** exceptions, where the toponym is genuinely the subject and not the imprint, are
