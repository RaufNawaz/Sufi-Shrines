# schimmel_as_through_a_veil — chunk 016 — pp. 365–382

**This chunk is the end of the book.** It contains three distinct things: (1) the whole of the
book's terminological index, printed under the heading `Index of Technical Terms` (p. 365, folio not
stated) and running from the headword `'Abduhu` to the headword `Zuhdiyyat, ascetic verse` (p. 377,
folio 359); (2) a genuine blank leaf at p. 378 (folio not stated); and (3) four pages of appended
non-book matter in Sindhi under the bilingual banner `The Reading Generation` (pp. 379–382, folio
not stated). `Zuhdiyyat, ascetic verse` is the last printed entry of the book proper.

**This is NOT an index of proper names, and that is the single most important negative in this
chunk.** Every headword in pp. 365–377 is a technical term, a motif or an image — `Dargah`,
`Qawwiili`, `curs`, `Rose`, `Nightingale`, `Ocean` — and the running head on all twelve paginated
pages reads `Index of Technical Terms` (or `Index of Technical Tenns` [text-layer?: Terms] on
pp. 367, 371 and 376). **No headword in this chunk is a person's name**, so the names this worker
was told to expect — Shah Abdul Latif, Sachal, Sultan Bahu, Shams, Waris Shah and the like — do not
occur as index entries here. If the book carries an Index of Proper Names it lies outside pp.
365–382. Persons appear in this chunk only inside a definitional gloss (`Bayezid Bistami`,
`I;Iusamuddin Chelebi`, `Avicenna`), and none of those is an archive row's principal figure.

**The index is strictly alphabetical, not classified**, so it carries no subject taxonomy of the
kind that would list an archive site as a shrine. It does, however, have two structures worth
recording, both described under `## Arguments and interpretations`: nested sub-entries under a
headword (`Dhikr` → `dhikr-i jali` / `dhikr-i khafi` / `dhikr-i 'aini`, p. 367, folio 349), and a
distributed `Love as …` sub-entry that recurs under scores of unrelated headwords.

**Folio regime confirmed, folio = PDF − 18, with the parity self-check passing on all twelve
paginated pages.** The folio sits inside the running-header line: leading and even on the verso
(`348        Index of Technical Terms`, p. 366), trailing and odd on the recto
(`Index of Technical Tenns             349`, p. 367). Every leading folio in the chunk is even
(348, 350, 352, 354, `35 6`, 358) and every trailing one odd (349, `35 1`, 353, 355, 357, 359), so
no reading is discarded. Two are split by the three-digit space of BRIEF §3 — `35 1` (p. 369) and
`35 6` (p. 374) — and are closed silently. `folio not stated` is used for p. 365 (the index's own
opening page, which prints its title instead of a header), p. 378 (blank) and pp. 379–382 (appended
matter, unpaginated).

**No index locator is carried into any note below as a page reference.** The locators are destroyed
exactly as BRIEF §3 describes — `Ishrilq, "illumination": 2700 113` (p. 369, folio 351),
`Parbi, "Eastern" language: 2700 113` (p. 373, folio 355), `Pantheism … 29011 169` (p. 373, folio
355), `Namu Amida Butsu: 27� 14` (p. 372, folio 354). Where a fact below concerns an entry, the
page cited is the PDF page the entry is **printed on**, never a number inside the entry. See
`## Doubts`.

## Arguments and interpretations

- **The index is the book's own statement of what it takes its subject matter to be, and its
  centre of gravity is the Prophet, not the saints.** Muhammad-related headwords and glosses run
  to dozens — `ljaqiqa mubammadiyya, the Archetypal Muhammad, principle of the world`
  [text-layer?: ḥaqīqa muḥammadiyya] (p. 368, folio 350); `Nar Mubammad, the light of Muham­mad`
  [text-layer?: Nūr Muḥammad] (p. 372, folio 354); `Na't, na'tiyya, poetry in honor of the Prophet`
  (p. 372, folio 354); `Seal of the Prophets, of Prophetship` (p. 375, folio 357); plus a run of
  "surname of the Prophet" entries (`Jasimun`, p. 369, folio 351; `Nasimun`, `Qasimu11`, `Wasimun`,
  `Rasillun karim`, pp. 372–377, folios 354–359). By contrast **`saint` is not a headword at all**
  and appears only inside two glosses (`curs`, p. 377, folio 359; `Munaqib6`, p. 372, folio 354)
  and one sub-entry (`Lion … saint as`, p. 370, folio 352).
- **A distributed `Love as …` taxonomy runs right through the index and is the best finding aid it
  offers.** Love is glossed as an occupation or agent under scores of otherwise unrelated
  headwords: `Baker` (p. 365, folio not stated), `Bleacher`, `Bottlemaker`, `Carpenter`, `Camel`,
  `Cupper` (p. 366, folio 348), `Dyer` (p. 367, folio 349), `Falcon`, `Garden`, `Ghiizi` (p. 368,
  folio 350), `Imam` (p. 369, folio 351), `Key-seller`, `Kitchen`, `Ladder`, `Light`, `Lightning`,
  `Lion` (p. 370, folio 352), `Magnet`, `Mi<rilj`, `Mortar`, `Mother` (p. 371, folio 353),
  `Mu?baf`, `Musician` (p. 372, folio 354), `Ocean`, `Peacock`, `Physician` (p. 373, folio 355),
  `Rain`, `$auma'a`, `School` (p. 374, folio 356), `Shtlhsuwtlr`, `Shibna`, `Tailor` (pp. 375–376,
  folios 357–358), `Tannar`, `Torrent`, `Trap`, `Tree` (p. 376, folio 358), `Unicom`, `Washerman`,
  `Water`, `Weaver`, `Wine`, `Worm` (p. 377, folio 359). Recorded here because it shows the book
  organises its material by image rather than by person or place.
- **The index preserves a three-stage path and its fruit metaphors, but inconsistently.**
  `Sharf"a, the divine law` is `sym­bolized in the prune` (p. 375, folio 357), `Tariqa` is

- **Hindu and Turk are treated as a matched symbolic pair, and `Turk` is also glossed as an ethnic

  designa­tion for Muslim` (p. 376, folio 358).
- **The index admits non-Islamic and non-Indian comparanda as headwords**, which shows the book's
  comparative reach: `Bhakti` (p. 366, folio 348), `Guru` (p. 368, folio 350), `Neti neti, negative
  description of the Divine in the Upanishads` (p. 372, folio 354), `Tantric tradition` (p. 376,
  folio 358), `Prema, unfulfilled love` (p. 373, folio 355), `Yang and yin` (p. 377, folio 359),
  `Koan (in Buddhism)` (p. 367, folio 349), `Namu Amida Butsu` (p. 372, folio 354), `Eros`,

## Doubts

**Locator damage — the governing doubt for this whole chunk.**

- **Every locator in pp. 365–377 is unusable and none has been carried into a note above.** The
  note-reference `n` renders as `0`, `"`, `}` or `9` exactly as BRIEF §3 measures, and the
  three-digit space of §3 then splits the page number as well. Specimens printed on the pages
  cited: `2900 169` under `Pantheism` (p. 373, folio 355); `2700 113` under `Ishrilq` (p. 369,
  folio 351) and again under `Parbi` and `Sharq` (p. 373 and p. 375, folios 355 and 357); `22001`
  is not in this chunk but `2211166` under `cfd`, `220064` under `ljadith` and `24� 120` under
  `Alif` are (pp. 368, 368 and 365). **A consolidator must not lift any of these.** The only safe
  fact from an index entry is *that* the book indexes the term, plus the wording of the gloss.
- **The plain page-number locators are damaged too, not only the note references.** `Arrow: 1 15,
  2500 266` (p. 365), `Baker: Love as, 1 18` (p. 365), `Dance: … 1o8, 1 1 1` (p. 366), `Fish: 1o8,
  1ow` (p. 367), `Elephant: u2` (p. 367), `Crocodile: … love as, 1 14` (p. 366) — `0`→`o`, `8`→`B`
  (`Mirror: 75, So`, p. 371; `Nightingale … So`, p. 372), `1`→`l`/`u`, and the three-digit space
  all apply. Treat **no** number anywhere in this index as readable.
