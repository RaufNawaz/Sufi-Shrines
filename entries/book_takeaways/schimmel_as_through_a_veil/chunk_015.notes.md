# schimmel_as_through_a_veil — chunk 015 — pp. 342–364

**What this chunk is.** Twenty-three pages of back matter, entirely apparatus. It carries the last
three pages of the **Bibliography** (pp. 342–344, running head `Bibliography`, letters T–Z), then the
**`Index of Selected Quotations`** (pp. 345–347), a genuine blank (p. 348), then the
**`Index of Proper Names`** (pp. 349–364, running head `Index of Proper Names`, two columns per page,
running from `Abbasids (750--1258)` on p. 349 to `Zulaikha` on p. 364). The Bibliography's last entry
is `Zimmer, Heinrich . Maya. Der indische Mythos. Zurich: Rascher, 1952.` (p. 344, folio 326). There is
no running prose anywhere in the chunk.

**Folio regime.** Folio = PDF − 18 throughout, confirmed page by page against the printed
running-header line with the BRIEF §1 parity self-check (leading folio even, trailing folio odd):
p. 342 prints `3 24       Bibliography` (leading, even → folio 324); p. 343 prints
`Bibliography       325` (trailing, odd → folio 325); p. 350 prints `33 2      Index of Proper Names`
(leading, even → folio 332); p. 363 prints `Index of Proper Names                345` (trailing, odd →
folio 345); p. 364 prints `346       Index of Proper Names` (leading, even → folio 346). **Zero parity
violations.** Three pages carry no header folio and are written `folio not stated`: **p. 345**, which
opens `Index of Selected Quotations`; **p. 348**, transcribed `[blank page]` and listed as a genuine
blank in BRIEF §3; and **p. 349**, which opens `Index of Proper Names`. The three-digit split of
BRIEF §3 hits the headers themselves (`3 24`, `33 2`, `33 7`, `34 1`, `3 29`); the gap is closed
silently for the computed folio, as the brief directs.

**The locators in both indexes are destroyed and no locator is carried anywhere below.** Every page
reference in this file is the PDF page the index entry is *printed on*, never a number printed inside
an entry. See `## Doubts`.
