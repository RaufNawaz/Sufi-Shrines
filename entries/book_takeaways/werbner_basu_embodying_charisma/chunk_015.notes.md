## Doubts

- **STANDING LINE, to be carried into consolidation and never dropped: no locator on these five pages
  may ever enter a shrine entry as a PDF page reference.** Every number printed beside a headword on
  pp. 250–254 (folios 239–243) is a **printed page number of this book**. The conversion is folio + 11.
  A locator of "154" means folio 154, which is PDF p. 165 — not PDF p. 154, which is folio 143. Every
  locator recorded above is named as a folio and converted in brackets, and no bare `(p. N)` anywhere
  in this file refers to anything but the index page it was read from (pp. 250–254, folios 239–243).
- **SECOND STANDING LINE: every one of the five archive mappings in this chunk rests on an index
  headword and nothing else.** An index headword is a bare name with no statement about it; it tells
  the archive that the book mentions a person or place somewhere, and it supplies no fact that an entry
  can cite. `darbar-ghamkol-sharif-zinda-pir`, `bari-imam`, `shrine-of-hazrat-shah-daula-daryai`,
  `shrine-of-fariduddin-ganjshakar` and `mazar-e-iqbal` are all recorded on that basis, under the
  author-only/bare-name ruling of 18 Sep 2026, and the consolidator should treat every one as thin and
  supersede it with whatever the chapter chunks carry for the same rows (pp. 250–254, folios 239–243).
