# boivin_hindu_sufis_south_asia — chunk 013 — pp. 250–253

**The last four pages of the book, and the tail of the Index.** It picks up exactly where chunk 012
broke off: chunk 012 ended at the foot of p. 249 (folio 236) inside the `Sindhi Hindus` entry at
"syncretism of 15–16", and this chunk opens at the head of p. 250 (folio 237) with the top-level
entry `Sindhis`. The stretch covered is S (from `Sindhis`) through Z, ending at `ziyarat 37, 191`.
**Pass 1 on this book is complete with these notes.**

**Running heads and folios: every folio cited below was read off the page that carries it, none
computed.** Three of the four pages carry a running head with the section name and the folio, and the
parity chunk 012 measured holds: an odd folio is set last, after the section name (`Index    237`,
p. 250; `Index    239`, p. 252); an even folio is set first, before it (`238    Index`, p. 251). The
head reads `Index` throughout, never the book's title. **No page in this chunk prints anything at its
foot** — confirming chunk 012's finding that in the back matter the folio lives only in the running
head. **p. 253 is the exception and an odd one: it carries the single line `240` and nothing else** —
no section name, no index text, no other content — so it is cited as `(p. 253, folio 240)`, with the
caveat under Doubts that the extracted text gives no positional evidence whether that `240` sits in a
running head or a footer. **The measured relation printed folio = PDF page − 13 held on all four
pages of this chunk** (250 → 237, 251 → 238, 252 → 239, 253 → 240). Nothing printed contradicts it,
and nothing below was derived from it.

**The interleaving bites on almost every line.** The four pages carry **119 content lines, of which
112 (94%) set two unrelated columns side by side**; 4 lines are left-column only, 2 are right-column
only (each at a letter break where the other column is blank), and the last is p. 253's bare `240`.
As chunk 012 found, it is worse than simple adjacency: **in reading order the right column continues
the left**, so a right-column top is a continuation of the left-column bottom, and on one page break
the right column's headword is not repeated at all. The most dangerous single line in this chunk is
p. 251 (folio 238) `surs 58, 79, 83     urs 44, 46, 110, 131–7, 165, 167–9, 190` — **two
near-homographs side by side, one a poetic form and one the shrine festival**; read across, it would
manufacture an identification the book nowhere makes. Two more worked examples are set out under
"Column reconstruction" below. **I reconstructed the two columns separately by horizontal position
and then verified every column break and page break against alphabetical continuity. All six breaks,
plus the incoming break from chunk 012, checked out**; the checks are listed individually below so
the consolidator can see the evidence rather than take the reconstruction on trust.

**Index locators are printed folios, not PDF pages**, and are labelled as folios everywhere below.
Locators of the form `203 n.12`, `198 n.1` and `204 n.7` are folio-plus-note references and are
reproduced exactly in that form.

**This chunk maps seven archive rows**: `lal-shahbaz-qalandar`, `shrine-of-sachal-sarmast`,
`dargah-roza-sufi-shah-inayat-shaheed`, `shrine-of-bahauddin-zakariya`, `gurdwara-dash-mesh-pita`,
`sadh-belo-sadh-belo-island-temple` and `shrine-at-odero-lal-udero-lal-teerath-asthan`. The check was
run against all 169 rows of `shrine_index.tsv` under the author-only ruling of 18 September 2026; the
near-misses considered and refused are set out under Doubts. **The chunk's single most important
finding is that the index does carry a top-level `urs` entry with seven shrine subentries** — which
corrects, rather than confirms, chunk 012's interim reading that the index uses `death anniversary`
instead.

## Doubts

**Damage inventory, text_layer route.** The dominant class is column interleaving, quantified above:
**112 of 119 content lines (94%) set two unrelated entries side by side**, and on one page break the
right column's headword is not repeated on the following page. No fragment was genuinely
unassignable, so no column `[text-layer?]` flags were raised; all six column and page breaks were
verified against alphabetical continuity and all six checked out. Remaining damage classes, all
minor: (i) one lost opening quotation mark, marked inline —
`'Tasawwuf or Theosophy' [text-layer? the opening mark is absent from the extracted text]` (p. 251,
folio 238); (ii) **all italics are lost** — the index sets book titles and *see* / *see also* in
italic and the extracted text carries no styling, so the title entries above are reconstructed as
titles from context, not from the page's own marking; (iii) abnormal internal spacing in "Yarhen,
festival at Sufi mandir   110" (p. 252, folio 239), which does not affect the reading; (iv) **no
dotted or underdotted characters appear anywhere in these four pages**, whereas chunk 012's range
carried several (e.g. "Shah, Nur Maḥmad", "Ḥafiẓ, Khwaja Muḥammad"); whether the page prints
diacritics in this range is undetermined and I have not supplied any.

**Folios.** Every folio cited was read off its own page. p. 253's "240" is the one uncertain case:
it is the only content on the page, so **the extracted text gives no positional evidence whether it

`(p. 253, folio 240)` because it is printed, not because −13 predicts it. The −13 relation held on
all four pages and nothing printed contradicted it.
