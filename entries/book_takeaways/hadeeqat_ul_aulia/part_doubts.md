## Doubts and review points

*Consolidated from 628 "Doubts" findings filed by the chunk workers of chunks 001–032. Ordered by
how much damage the doubt would do if ignored, not by chunk order. Every item carries the PDF page,
the printed folio where the notes give one, and the chunk it came from. Where several chunk workers
raised the same doubt independently, the item says so — that repetition is a severity signal, not a
duplication. Nothing here is resolved: where the evidence points one way the item says so and stops
(CLAUDE.md RULE 2; the decision is Rauf's, RULE 5).*

**Folio caution that applies to every citation below.** The relation `folio = PDF index − 12` holds
from p. 14 to p. 352. The transcription's `[folio N]` markers for **pp. 176–197 are ten too high**,
and chunks 016, 017, 018 and 019 recorded inflated folios for pages in that range. Where an item
cites a folio in that stretch it is written "folio N as printed" and the true value is N − 10. See
Apparatus item A26 for the proof and the amendment this needs.

**Group counts.** Blocking 21 · Identification unresolved 27 · Numerals and dates needing the page
image 102 · Illegible and lost 24 · Internal contradictions 45 · Apparatus problems 27. Total 246
merged items from 628 filed findings.

**The three most urgent, in the compiler's judgement.** (1) **B1, `data-darbar`** — the row's death
year is the one this edition calls "غلط" in terms, and the editor offers no replacement. (2) **B7,
`shrine-of-miran-hussain-zanjani-zanjani-sahib`** — the row's date and the row's site come from two men

`darbar-malik-ahmad-ayaz`** — three death years are in play and the row's 1041 is none of them, and it
falls inside a governorship the editor dates elsewhere. Close behind, and cheapest to fix: **A26**, the
ten-too-high folio markers for pp. 176–197, which nine chunk workers raised and which mis-points every
folio citation in chunks 016–018.
