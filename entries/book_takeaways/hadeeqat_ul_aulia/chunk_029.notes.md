# hadeeqat_ul_aulia — chunk 029 — pp. 307–318

**No numbered tazkira entries in this chunk. It is end-matter of the edition in three parts:** the
editor's own list of publications, "فہرست تصانیف و مقالات محمد اقبال مجددی" (pp. 307–308, folios
295–296); a **second appendix**, "ضمیمۂ ثانی" (pp. 309–310, folios 297–298), in which the editor adds
corrections keyed to entry numbers and printed pages ("شمارہ ۸۳ ص ۱۱۷" etc.); and the opening of the
**index of persons**, "اشاریہ — رجال" (pp. 311–318, folios 299–306), letters آ through س, breaking off
mid-س at "سرخ بخاری اوچی" (p. 318). The index is the chunk's chief value for the archive: **it gives
printed-page locators for about fifteen archive figures**, and its numbers are demonstrably the
printed folios — six names that chunk 022 placed at folios 219–221 are indexed here at exactly those
folios (see Arguments). It also settles chunk 022's last doubt: **"بی بیاں پاک دامن" is indexed at
folios 187 and 235** (p. 314), the second of which lies past the end of the numbered entries. The
appendix carries one hard dating anchor for the edition: the editor obtained material **"اپنے قیام
کراچی ۴، ۳ دسمبر ۱۹۷۵ء کے دوران"**, during his stay in Karachi on 3–4 December 1975 (p. 310).

**Folio markers: all twelve pages run folio = PDF index − 12**, p. 307 → folio 295 through p. 318 →
folio 306, with no break. Where I convert an index folio to an estimated PDF page below I write "est.
p. N" and mean folio + 12 on the assumption that the same offset holds at that point of the book; this
is arithmetic on the chunk's figures, not something the chunk states, and must be verified against the
PDF before use.

## Arguments and interpretations

- **The index's numbers are printed folios, and this can be shown from within the notes.** Chunk 022
  placed the following at p. 231 (folio 219), p. 232 (folio 220) and p. 233 (folio 221): Sayyid Hasan
  Shah Bukhari, Sayyid Ahmad Shah son of Munawwar Ali, Maulana Jan Muhammad Fazil Lahori (all folio
  219); Khalifa Ahmad al-Din and Hamid al-Din (folio 220); Mufti Chiragh Din and Shaikh Ahmad Shah
  Kashmiri (folio 221). The index here reads **"حسن شاہ بخاری | ۲۱۹"** (p. 316), **"احمد شاہ بن منور علی
  | ۲۱۹"** (p. 312), **"جان محمد فاضل لاہوری | ۲۱۹"** (p. 315), **"احمد الدین لاہوری | ۲۲۰"** (p. 312),
  **"حمید الدین لاہوری | ۲۲۰"** (p. 317), **"چراغ دین مفتی | ۲۲۱"** (p. 316), **"احمد کشمیری تارہ بلی |
  ۲۲۱"** (p. 312). Seven for seven. **A consolidator can therefore convert any index number to a PDF
  page by adding the folio offset (12 wherever the offset has been checked).**
- **The index is not exhaustive, so absence from it proves nothing.** Baha al-Din Zakariya is named in
  the author's pedigree at folio 220 (chunk 022, p. 232) but his index line stops at 172 (p. 314);
  Ahmad Shah Durrani drives chunk 022's entry no. 159 at folios 215–216 but "احمد شاہ ابدالی | ۱۴۰"
  (p. 312) lists only 140. **Use the index to find material, never to rule it out.**
- **The book continues well past its 170th entry.** Chunk 022 ended the numbered entries at folio 223
  (p. 235) and asked what follows. The index cites folios **225–228** (majzubs), **232–270** (Lahore
  pirs, women at 235–243, Sultan Bahu at 252, the author's ancestor Rahmat Allah at 259, Chiragh Shah
  Lahori at 262, Roshan Mufti Chiragh Din at 270) (pp. 312–318). **So at least forty-seven further
  printed pages of biographical matter exist after entry no. 170** — est. pp. 237–282 — and the
  archive's **bibi-pak-daman** and **garh-maharaja-shorkot** material lies there. Whether this is the

- **The edition's editorial work spans at least 1974 to December 1975.** Chunk 022 noted the editor's

  ۱۳۰", "ص ۲۷"), i.e. it corrects footnotes already set in type.
- **The editor corrects a genealogical tradition and explains how the error arose.** Following Abu
  al-Hasan Faruqi, he replaces the twenty-eight-link pedigree of *Zubdat al-Maqamat* and *Hazarat
  al-Quds* with thirty-two, and quotes the mechanism: "میزان الاعتدال اور تقریب التہذیب میں عبداللہ بن
  عمر بن حفص بن عاصم بن عمر کا ذکر ملا اور خیال ہوا کہ عبداللہ بن عمر بن حفص کے صاحبزادے کا نام ناصر
  ہوگا اور مشابہت کی وجہ سے عبداللہ بن عمر کو ابن الخطاب سمجھ لیا گیا" — a like-named Abdullah bin Umar
  was mistaken for Ibn al-Khattab's son (p. 309, folio 297). **The correction is attributed, sourced to
  a 1392 AH Delhi imprint, and the reasoning is quoted — the editor's standard for pedigrees.**
- **The editor sides with the author against a later tazkira-writer on a death year.** "جو مفتی صاحب کے
  عین مطابق ہے اس لئے فقیر محمد جہلمی کا مندرجہ سنہ ۱۱۶۶ھ درست نہیں ہے" (p. 310, folio 298): Shah
  Ghulam Ali Dehlvi's 1160 AH is accepted because it agrees with "مفتی صاحب" — the author — and Faqir
  Muhammad Jhelami's 1166 is rejected. **Calibration for the archive: the editor treats the author's
  dates as evidence, not as claims to be checked, at least where an independent source agrees.**
- **The index folds the editor's sources into the author's subjects.** One "رجال" list holds Storey,

- **The editor dates saints in CE in his own bibliography and in AH in the appendix.** "ف ۱۶۵۳ء", "ف
  ۱۸۳۴ء", "ف ۱۷۱۸ء", "ف بعد ۱۷۳۷ء" (p. 307) against "ف ۱۲۷۰ھ" (p. 307), "ف ۱۳۴۳ھ" (p. 308), "متوفی

## Doubts

- **darbar-abul-muali-qadri: the index makes Khair al-Din the father, the archive makes him the
  saint.** "خیر الدین (والد شاہ ابوالمعالی) | ۴۵" (p. 317, folio 305) against the archive row's
  principal figure "Hazrat Syed Muhammad Khair ul Deen, known as Shah Abul Muali Qadri". **Either the
  row has conflated father and son, or the index's gloss is wrong; the book's entry at folios 33–46
  (est. pp. 45–58) must be read before the row's name field is touched.** The chunk itself decides
  nothing.
- **ranmal-sharif: the editor's death year is 1653 CE; the archive's is 1654.** "حضرت حاجی محمد نوشہ گنج
  بخش قادری ف ۱۶۵۳ء" (p. 307, folio 295). The editor gives no month, no Hijri year and no source in this
  list; a one-year gap of this kind is typical of a Hijri-to-CE conversion, but that is a guess. **Record
  both, and check the book's own entry under ن.**
- **Two archive rows could take "حیات شاہ عنایت قادری قصوری ؒ (ف بعد ۱۷۳۷ء)" (p. 307, folio 295), and the
  chunk cannot choose.** The archive has **shrine-of-shah-inayat-qadiri** (Mozang, Lahore; d. c. 1728;
  Qadri Shattari) and **dargah-roza-sufi-shah-inayat-shaheed** (Jhok Sharif, Sindh; d. 1718). The
  editor's title gives "Qadiri" and "Qasuri" (of Kasur) and a death **after 1737 CE**. "Qadiri" points
  to the Lahore row, but the archive says Lahore where the editor says Kasur, and **the archive's c. 1728
  is incompatible with "after 1737"**; the Sindh row's 1718 is further off still. **Left unmatched. If a
  human confirms the Lahore identification, the row's death year needs a qualifying note.**
- **Two Uch rows could take "جلال بخاری سید | ۱۴۶" (p. 315, folio 303).** The archive has
  **shrine-of-jalaluddin-surkh-posh-bukhari-jalaluddin-bukhari** and **shrine-of-makhdoom-jahaniyan-
  jahangasht**, both a Sayyid Jalaluddin Bukhari of Uch. The index has a separate, explicit line for the
  first ("سرخ بخاری اوچی", p. 318); this bare "جلال بخاری سید" at folio 146 — the first Multan folio —

  folio 304) contains the word "Jahaniyan" but the personal name is Kamal; **do not merge it with
  Makhdoom Jahaniyan without reading folio 254.**
