# hadeeqat_ul_aulia — chunk 031 — pp. 328–342

**Back matter, not entries: the tail of the edition's index of persons (letters م continued, ن, و, ہ,
ی — pp. 328–332), the complete index of places (اماکن, آ through ہ — pp. 333–336), a Third Appendix
(ضمیمہ ثالث, p. 337) presenting three family documents about the imamat of the Wazir Khan Mosque and
the offerings of the mazar of Sayyid Ishaq Gazruni, with three largely illegible facsimiles
(pp. 338–340), and the opening of a Fourth Appendix (ضمیمہ رابع, "تعلیقات جدیدہ", pp. 341–342) that
gives the editor's account of the author's last works and, per the editor, his death on the Hajj of
"۱۳۰۷ھ/۱۸۸۹ء", then begins an Arabic manuscript passage on the founder of Hujra Shah Muqim.** Eight
archive rows appear in the persons index by name — **tahir-bandagi-qadri**, **shrine-of-mian-mir**,
**shrine-of-makhdoom-jahaniyan-jahangasht**, **shrine-of-mauj-darya-bukhari**,
**shrine-of-syed-musa-pak**, **shrine-of-mian-umar-baba-chamkani**, **ranmal-sharif** and, less
securely, **shrine-of-hafiz-muhammad-jamal-multani** and **shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh** — and the
place index gives folio leads for the Uch, Multan, Taunsa, Mithankot, Chah Miran, Sharaqpur, Mozang and
Hasan Abdal rows. **What the index gives is the book's own name for each saint and the printed pages
on which he appears; it gives no facts about him.** The appendices carry the chunk's substance: a
Mughal licence of 1125 AH for a mosque imam, a father's deed passing a mosque office and a shrine's
"فتوحات" to his son, and the editor's dating of the author's death.

**Folio markers: fourteen of the fifteen pages run folio = PDF index − 12**, p. 328 → folio 316 through
p. 342 → folio 330. **p. 338 carries no folio marker** — it is a full-page facsimile plate, and on the
offset it would be folio 326; the marker resumes at p. 339 → folio 327, so the relation is not broken,
only unprinted (or untranscribed) on that page. **The page numbers inside both indexes are printed
folios, not PDF indices**: "منور علی شاہ نقشبندی | ۲۱۸" (p. 330) and "محمد محمود بن عبد السلام | ۲۲۰"
(p. 329) match the folios chunk 022's notes recorded for those passages (folios 218–219 and 220, PDF
pp. 230–232). **To reach an indexed passage, add 12 to the index number.** The conversions given below
are that arithmetic and nothing more.

## Practices, institutions, economy

- **A Mughal licence for the imamat and khitabat of a Lahore mosque, 1125 AH.** Document 1: "اجازت
  نامہٴ امامت و خطابت مسجد وزیر خان برائے ملا محمد حنیف ولد محمد لطیف بمہر صدر الصدور افضل خان فدوی
  بادشاہ غازی محمد فرخ سیر ۱۱۲۵ھ" — a licence of imamat and khitabat of the Wazir Khan Mosque for
  **Mulla Muhammad Hanif son of Muhammad Latif**, under the seal of the **Sadr al-Sudur Afzal Khan,
  servant of Badshah Ghazi Muhammad Farrukh Siyar, 1125 AH** (p. 337, folio 325). Its facsimile
  (p. 338, no folio marker) shows two seals at the head and the legible fragments "دار السلطنت لاہور
  [OCR?]", "بالتماس [OCR?] ملا محمد حنیف [OCR?]" and "وزیر خان [OCR?] مرحوم" (p. 338). **The
  appointment of a mosque's imam is here a state act, sealed by the imperial Sadr — the archive's
  clearest document of Mughal involvement in a Lahore religious office.**
- **A father's deed passing a mosque office and a shrine's offerings to his son, witnessed by Lahore
  notables.** Document 2: "تحریر و مہر حافظ غلام محمد معروف بہ امام گاموں بن حافظ محمد صدیق مذکور جس
  میں بتایا گیا ہے کہ مسجد وزیر خاں کی امامت ، خطابت اور مزار حضرت سید اسحق گازرونی کی فتوحات وغیرہ میرے
  صاحبزادے اللہ بخش کو ملیں — اور بطور گواہ دیگر سربرآوردہ اصحاب لاہور کے دستخط اور مواہیر بھی ثبت ہیں"
  — the writing and seal of Hafiz Ghulam Muhammad (Imam Gamon) stating that **the imamat and
  khitabat of the Wazir Khan Mosque and the "فتوحات" (offerings/income) of the mazar of Sayyid Ishaq
  Gazruni should go to my son Allah Bakhsh**, with the signatures and seals of other leading men of
  Lahore as witnesses (p. 337, folio 325). Its facsimile (p. 339, folio 327) shows "several round
  seal impressions with witness signatures in the right margin", the fragments "حضرت میران سید اسحق
  کازرونی [OCR?]", "حافظ غلام محمد [OCR?] ولد حافظ محمد صدیق مرحوم [OCR?]", "حافظ اللہ بخش [OCR?]
  خلف", a date "۱۲۴۳ [OCR?]", and marginal witnesses "گواہ شد … شاگرد حضرت … غلام شاہ [OCR?]", "شیخ
  فیض بخش [OCR?]", "مولوی عبد الرحمن [OCR?]", "[illegible] عافیہ [OCR?]", "حافظ محمد [illegible]",
  "گواہ شد [illegible]" (p. 339, folio 327). **Mosque office and shrine income are transmitted together
  in one instrument, from father to son, and the transfer is made public by the seals of the city's
  ulama** — the archive's most exact description of how a Lahore custodianship was handed on.

- **A family record kept for two centuries and held by a railway pensioner.** The three documents span

  عبد المجید بیگ … بن خان صاحب مرزا عبد العزیز مرحوم ریٹائرڈ ریلوے انسپکٹر ، گوالمنڈی لاہور" (p. 337,
  folio 325). **Custodial families' private papers as the source for shrine history — here surfaced
  only because the editor was shown them after the book went to press.**
- **A hujra as the seed of a town.** Of Hujra Shah Muqim: "البانی للحجرۃ المتبرکۃ فی الاصل ھی موضع
  خلوتہ و معبدہ وبنیانھا الی الان قائمۃ ثم سمی بھا مسکنہ من قبیل تسمیۃ الکل باسم الجزء" — the founder
  built the blessed Hujra as his place of seclusion and worship; its building still stands; his
  dwelling-place was then named after it, "the whole by the name of the part" (p. 342, folio 330).
  **A place-name derived from a saint's cell, stated by a member of the family itself** in an Arabic
  grammar's preface.
- **The author's works, their compilers and their presses.** "۲۱۔ دیوان وصال سرور — مرتبہ مفتی غلام
  صفدر فوقانی [OCR?] بن مفتی غلام سرور … یہ کتاب مطبع صدیقی، فیروز پور سے ۱۸۹۰ء کو طبع ہوئی" — compiled
  by his son Mufti Ghulam Safdar Fauqani[OCR?], printed at **Matba Siddiqi, Ferozepur, 1890 CE**;
  "۲۲۔ کلیات سرور — مرتبہ حکیم مفتی محمد انور بن مفتی مفتی غلام سرور، مطبوعہ، مطبع اسلامیہ لاہور،
  ۱۹۱۰ء" — compiled by his son **Hakim Mufti Muhammad Anwar**, printed at **Matba Islamia, Lahore,
  1910 CE**; the Kulliyat unites "دیوان نعت سروری (۱۲۹۰ھ)" and the Diwan Wisal-e Sarwar (p. 341,
  folio 329). **A family of muftis publishing its own devotional verse through two presses across
  twenty years; one son styled Hakim, a physician, like the author's father in chunk 022's notes.**
- **A Muslim mufti revising a Hindu historian's books.** "مفتی غلام سرور نے اپنے دوست اور مورخ لاہور
  رائے بہادر کنہیالال کی مندرجہ ذیل تالیفات پر نظر ثانی کی تھی: رنجیت نامہ، ہیرو رانجھا، مناجات ہندی،
  یادگار ہندی، نصیحت نامہ، بندگی نامہ، تاریخ پنجاب، تاریخ لاہور، اخلاق ہندی اور دیوان ہندی" (pp. 341–342,
  folios 329–330) — **ten titles, including a *Tarikh-e Lahore* and a *Tarikh-e Punjab*, revised by
  the author for his friend.** Source given by the editor: "(دیوان وصال سرور، مقدمہ ص ۲۰۔ ۲۱)"
  (p. 342, folio 330).
- **Letters to sons as a published textbook.** "انشائے صفدری دراصل خود مفتی غلام سرور کے لکھے ہوئے وہ
  خطوط ہیں جو انہوں نے اپنے فرزندوں غلام صفدر اور مفتی غلام حیدر کو ان کے زمانہ طالب علمی میں لکھے تھے۔
  … غلام صفدر نے انہیں مرتب شکل میں جمع کر کے انشائے صفدری نام رکھا جو کئی بار طبع ہو چکی ہے" — the
  author's letters to his sons **Ghulam Safdar and Mufti Ghulam Haidar** in their student days,
  compiled by Ghulam Safdar as *Insha-e Safdari*, "printed several times"; the author also revised
  his son's *Ma'lumat-e Fauqani (Tahqiq al-Huruf)* (p. 342, folio 330).

## Arguments and interpretations

- **The editor dates the author's death, and it is the only such statement in the chunk.** "اس دیوان
  میں وہ نعتیں شامل ہیں جو مفتی غلام سرور نے اپنے سفر حج (۱۳۰۷ھ/۱۸۸۹ء) کے دوران لکھی تھیں اور اسی سفر میں
  مفتی صاحب کا وصال ہو گیا تھا۔ گویا ان کی آخری تصنیف ہے" — the Diwan contains the na'ts Mufti Ghulam
  Sarwar wrote during his Hajj journey of **1307 AH / 1889 CE**, "and on that same journey the Mufti
  passed away", so it is his last composition (p. 341, folio 329). **This is the editor's "تعلیقات
  جدیدہ", sourced to the Diwan's introduction** ("دیوان وصال سرور، مقدمہ ص ۲۰۔ ۲۱", p. 342), not the
  author's text. Any archive citation naming the author can now carry a death on the Hajj of
  1307/1889 **as the editor's statement**.
- **A dated na't collection of 1290 AH fixes the author's activity in the year chunk 022's notes
  inferred for the book's composition.** "دیوان نعت سروری (۱۲۹۰ھ)" (p. 341, folio 329). Chunk 022's
  notes reasoned from internal deaths (1288–1290 AH) and one "827 years" figure to a composition in
  the early 1290s; **the editor's 1290 AH for a separate work by the same author is an independent
  datum consistent with that** — it is not a date for the Hadiqa.
- **The editor treats descendants' introductions as primary sources.** "دیوان وصال سرور اور کلیات سرور
  کے مقدموں میں مفتی غلام سرور لاہوری اور ان کے اجداد و اخلاف کے متعلق درجہ اول کی بہت سی معلومات جمع کر
  دی گئی ہیں" — the introductions to the two collections gather "much first-rate information" on
  the author, his ancestors and his descendants (p. 341, folio 329). **For the consolidator: the
  editor points to two printed sources (1890, 1910) for the author's family beyond this book.**
- **The editor appends documents after press rather than withhold them, and says so.** "یہ کتاب پریس
  میں جا چکی تھی … سرسری جائزہ کے بعد تین دستاویزات کے عکس اس کتاب میں شامل کرنے کا فیصلہ کیا گیا"
  (p. 337, folio 325); and he refers the reader to his own entry no. 240 (pp. 263–264) for the
  fuller account. **The Third and Fourth Appendices are therefore later than the indexes that precede
  them** — the order of the back matter is chronological by editorial stage, not logical.
- **The editor uses a manuscript in a private Lahore library to correct a family's history.** The
  *Kifayat al-Nahw* of Sayyid Inayat Allah Husaini, seen "جناب خلیل الرحمن داؤدی (لاہور) کے کتب خانے
  میں", is introduced with "جس سے اس خاندان پر مفصلہ ذیل امور واضح ہوتے ہیں" — from which the following
  matters about the family become clear (p. 342, folio 330). The matters themselves fall in the next
  chunk; **what this chunk shows is the method**: an Arabic preface quoted in full, its chronogram
  noted ("ویستفاد تاریخ وفاتہ من تعداد اعداد عبدالقادر"), and the value left for the reader.
- **The index's cross-references are identity claims by the edition.** "میر محمد | دیکھئے میاں میر"
  (p. 329, folio 317); "میراں بادشاہ لاہوری | (دیکھئے اسحاق گازرونی)" (p. 331, folio 319); "محمد حفیظ
  شاہ | دیکھئے جھولن شاہ" and "محمد سعید | دیکھئے میراں بھیکھ" (p. 328, folio 316); "محمد مبارک علوی :
  | دیکھئے امیر خورد" (p. 329) and "میر مبارک | دیکھئے امیر خورد" (p. 331); "وجیہ الدین | دیکھئے پیر
  زہدی لاہوری" (p. 332, folio 320); "اکبر آباد | (دیکھئے آگرہ)" (p. 333, folio 321); "ہندی (ر ۔ ک
  کنہیا لال) | ۷۵" (p. 332). **Eight equations of a popular name with a personal one, made by the
  indexer** — each usable in an entry only as "the edition identifies X with Y".
- **The book's numbering runs to at least 240 and its text to at least folio 264.** "مسلسل شمارہ ۔۔ ۲۴۰
  ، صفحہ ۲۶۳ ۔ ۲۶۴" (p. 337, folio 325); the highest index page-numbers are "مکرم قریشی | ۲۶۴" (p. 330),
  "مرگ نینی [OCR?] محمد سلطان | ۲۶۳" (p. 330), "ہوشیار پور | ۲۶۲" (p. 336), "موسیٰ کھوکھر شیخ | ۲۰ ،
  ۲۶۱" (p. 330), "امرتسر | … ۲۵۸" (p. 333), "نظام الدین بودیاں والا | ۲۵۷" (p. 331), "ہادی راہنما پیر |
  ۲۵۵" (p. 332), "محمد شیرازی پیر | ۲۵۴" (p. 328). **This answers the question left open at the end of
  chunk 022's notes — whether entries continue past no. 170: they do, to no. 240 and folio 264 (PDF
  p. 276).** Whether the Bibis of **bibi-pak-daman** have an entry in that range is not shown by this
  chunk: the ب section of the place index (p. 333) has no "بی بی پاکدامن" entry, and the persons
  index's ب section lies outside the chunk.
- **The place index is selective, and its gaps are worth recording.** The whole اماکن index is in this
  chunk (آ–ہ, pp. 333–336). As transcribed it has **no entry for پاکپتن, سیالکوٹ, جھنگ, اوکاڑہ, گولڑہ,
  سیال شریف, چنیوٹ, بھلوال or کوٹ مٹھن under that spelling** (the پ, س, ج, ا, گ, چ, ب and ک sections
  are all present), while it does list "تونسہ", "مٹھن کوٹ", "ڈیرہ غازی خان", "شرقپور", "چمکنی", "اوچ"
  and "ملتان". **An absence in the index is not an absence in the book**; but the consolidator should
  not expect the index to lead to every archive site.

## Doubts

- **Nothing in this chunk is a fact about an archive saint beyond his name and page-numbers.** The
  index entries under "Shrines and figures in the archive" locate passages; they do not describe
  them. **Every "co-indexed on folio N" line above is a lead drawn from shared page-numbers and must
  not be turned into a relationship** (a footnote can cite a modern author on the same page as a
  medieval saint).
- **shrine-of-hafiz-muhammad-jamal-multani: the index has "محمد جمال ملتانی" without "حافظ"** (p. 328,
  folio 316). The ج section of the persons index (outside this chunk) may carry a "جمال ملتانی" or
  "حافظ جمال" entry that is the real match, and Multan has more than one Muhammad Jamal. **Read PDF
  p. 120 before attaching it to the row.**
- **shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh: only a son is indexed, and "شاہ چراغ" is a common title.** "مصطفیٰ ابن
  شاہ چراغ لاہوری | ۵۶" (p. 330, folio 318). The archive's figure is Syed Abdul Razzaq; the chunk gives
  no personal name for the father. **Could equally be another Shah Chiragh of Lahore. Check PDF p. 68
  and the ش section of the index.**
- **shrine-of-mian-mir: two "میر محمد" entries.** "میر محمد | دیکھئے میاں میر" (p. 329, folio 317) and,
  separately, "میر محمد | ۱۲۱" (p. 331, folio 319). Folio 121 is co-indexed with "آگرہ", "بخارا", "کابل",
  "سرینگر", "وخش", "معین الدین بن حضرت ایشاں | ۱۲۰ ، ۱۲۱ …" and "محمد صالح کمبوہ | ۱۲۱" (pp. 329–336) —
  a Central Asian / Naqshbandi page. **Do not add folio 121 (PDF p. 133) to Mian Mir's page-map
  without reading it.**
- **shrine-of-miran-hussain-zanjani-zanjani-sahib: "چاہ میراں | ۷۳" is a locality, not the saint.**
  (p. 334, folio 322). The persons index in this chunk also has "میراں بادشاہ لاہوری" = Ishaq Gazruni
  (p. 331) — a different "Miran" of Lahore. **Which Miran the well is named for is not stated here.**
  Chunk 022's notes recorded an editor's "مدفون چاہ میراں" for Husain Zanjani at PDF p. 196 (folio
  184); folio 184 is **not** in the index's Chah Miran entry, so the index is incomplete or the two
  spellings differ. Read PDF p. 85.
- **Mozang: two archive rows share the locality.** "مزنگ محلہ | ۲۰۴" (p. 336, folio 324) could bear on
  **shrine-of-shah-inayat-qadiri** or **gurdwara-chhevin-patshahi-mozang**; a Muslim tazkira makes the
  former likelier, but the chunk does not say. **Leave unassigned until PDF p. 216 is read.**
- **shergarh: "شیر گڈھ |" has no page number** (p. 335, folio 323). Seven other entries share the
  defect: "محمد رومی سید |" (p. 328), "احمد پور (پنجاب) |", "ایبٹ [OCR?] |" (p. 333), "چوٹالہ (مضافات
  مہار) |" (p. 334), "طبرستان |", "کوٹ بخشا |", "لوہگڈھ دروازہ |" (p. 335). **Whether the print is blank
  or the OCR dropped the numeral cannot be told; check the PDF.** Daud Bandagi Kirmani's own entry
  falls under د, outside the chunk.
- **darbar-malik-ahmad-ayaz: no "ملک ایاز" in the persons index.** The م section on p. 330 runs "ملا
  شاہ بدخشی | ۵۶ ، ۵۷ ، ۵۸" directly into "ملکو بی بی (والدہ میراں بھیکھ) | ۱۰۰", where "ملک ایاز" would
  fall. **Mahmud Ghaznavi is indexed** ("محمود غزنوی | ۷۵ ، ۱۷۰ ، ۱۸۰", p. 329, folio 317; "غزنی | ۱۸۰
  ، ۱۸۱", p. 335), so a Ghaznavid passage exists at folios 180–181 (PDF pp. 192–193). **As transcribed

- **golra-sharif: "مہر شاہ (شہاب الدین) | ۱۷۰" is not Pir Meher Ali Shah.** (p. 330, folio 318). The
  parenthetical gives the personal name Shihab al-Din, and the index places him on folio 170 among

- **ranmal-sharif: the page-list ends in a comma.** "نوشاہ گنج بخش (حاجی محمد) | ۵۸ ، ۵۹ ، ۶۰ ، ۶۴ ، ۶۵ ،
