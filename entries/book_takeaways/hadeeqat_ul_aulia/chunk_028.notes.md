# hadeeqat_ul_aulia — chunk 028 — pp. 291–306

**The end of the edition: the last eleven rows of the editor's concordance table of Lahore
shrines, the editor's complete source list ("مآخذ مقدمہ و حواشی", 265 numbered items in seven
sections), and the colophon dated 12 December 1974.** There is **no text by the author in this
chunk**; every line is the editor's apparatus, and the one column that reports the 1875 book does
so at second hand under the heading "پتہ بمطابق حدیقۃ الاولیاء ۱۸۷۵ء" (p. 291, folio 279). Three
archive rows gain **site facts** from the table — **bibi-pak-daman** (the six Bibis named
individually and a 1974 locality), **shrine-of-mian-mir** (a woman saint's grave "inside the
enclosure of the khanqah of Hazrat Mian Mir") and, tentatively, **shrine-of-mauj-darya-bukhari**
(a "Bibi Wadi" buried outside the mazar of Miran Muhammad Shah). Sixteen further rows gain
**citable sources** from the bibliography, the richest being **shrine-of-bahauddin-zakariya**
(five items, including a Samarqand manuscript of 854 AH) and **shrine-of-makhdoom-jahaniyan-
jahangasht** (three). The two most useful things for the archive as a whole: **the editor dates
the book to 1875 in a printed heading**, and **the colophon names the editor, his office and the
day the edition was finished** — while three bibliography entries carry imprints of 1975 and
1977, later than the colophon (see Doubts).

**Folio markers: all sixteen run folio = PDF index − 12**, p. 291 → folio 279 through p. 306 →
folio 294. No break observed.

## Shrines and figures in the archive

- **bibi-pak-daman** (حضرات بی بیاں پاکدامناں — row ۷۳ of the editor's table, p. 291,
  folio 279; and item ۲۳۴, p. 304, folio 292):
  - **The six Bibis are named individually.** Row ۷۳ reads: "**مزارات حضرات بی بیاں
    پاکدامناں، (بی بی حاج، بی بی تاج، بی بی نور، بی بی حور، بی بی گوہر، بی بی شہباز)**" — the
    mazars of the Bibiyan Pakdamanan: Bibi Haj, Bibi Taj, Bibi Nur, Bibi Hur, Bibi Gauhar, Bibi
    Shahbaz (p. 291, folio 279).
  - **The 1875 address is only the city; the 1974 address is a named locality.** Column "پتہ
    بمطابق حدیقۃ الاولیاء ۱۸۷۵ء": "**لاہور**"; column "موجودہ محل وقوع": "**آبادی بی بیاں
    پاکدامناں محمد نگر**" — the settlement of Bibiyan Pakdamanan, Muhammad Nagar (p. 291,
    folio 279).
  - **A dated monograph on the shrine is among the editor's Urdu sources**: item ۲۳۴ "**نامی
    غلام دستگیر پیر : بی بیاں پاکدامن۔ لاہور ۱۹۳۵ء**" — Pir Ghulam Dastgir Nami, *Bibiyan
    Pakdaman*, Lahore 1935 (p. 304, folio 292). The same author's *Bazurgan-e Lahore* (Lahore
    1966), *Tarikh-e Jalila* (Lahore 1960) and *Sawanih Hazrat Shah Muhammad Ghaus Lahori* are
    items ۲۳۳, ۲۳۱, ۲۳۲ on the same page.
  - **What the row gains**: the archive's principal figure reads "The six Bibis (Bibi Pak Daman)";
    this chunk supplies **six personal names** as the edition prints them, a **1974 locality
    (Muhammad Nagar)**, the fact that the **1875 book (as the editor read it) located the site
    no more precisely than "Lahore"**, and a **1935 printed monograph** usable as a bibliography
    item. The names are in **the editor's table, not the author's text** — the author's own
    entry on the Bibis is not in this chunk (chunk 022's Doubts already noted it was not in
    chunks 016–022 either). See Doubts.
- **shrine-of-mian-mir** (خانقاہ حضرت میاں میر — row ۷۵ of the editor's table, p. 291,
  folio 279):
  - **A woman saint's grave inside the khanqah enclosure, per the 1875 book.** Row ۷۵: "**بی بی
    جمال خاتون قدس سرہ** | **اندر احاطہ خانقاہ حضرت میاں میر** | **کراچی ریلوے لائن اور میانی
    صاحب گاؤں کے درمیان**" — Bibi Jamal Khatun; address per the 1875 book: inside the enclosure
    (احاطہ) of the khanqah of Hazrat Mian Mir; present location: between the Karachi railway
    line and Miani Sahib village (p. 291, folio 279).
  - **What the row gains**: evidence that the **khanqah of Mian Mir had a walled احاطہ that
    contained at least one other saint's grave** by 1875, and the **name of that saint**. No
    dates, no custodian, no fabric. **The two location columns do not obviously describe one
    place, and the archive puts Mian Mir at Alam Ganj / Dharampura, not Miani Sahib** — see
    Doubts before this enters the row.
- **shrine-of-mauj-darya-bukhari** (مزار میران محمد شاہ — row ۷۴ of the editor's table, p. 291,
  folio 279; **tentative identification**):
  - Row ۷۴: "**بی بی فاطمہ سیدہ المشہور بی بی وڈی** | **بیرون مزار میران محمد شاہ** | **لیک روڈ
    پرانی انارکلی**" — Bibi Fatima Sayyida, famous as Bibi Wadi; address per the 1875 book:
    outside the mazar of Miran Muhammad Shah; present location: Lake Road, Old Anarkali (p. 291,
    folio 279).
  - **Basis of the identification**: the archive's principal figure is "Hazrat Meeran Muhammad
    Shah Bukhari (Mauj Darya)" at Edward Road, Anarkali; the table's "میران محمد شاہ" matches the
    personal name and its 1974 location is Old Anarkali. **The table gives neither "موج دریا" nor
    "بخاری"**, so the match rests on name plus neighbourhood only. See Doubts.
  - **What the row gains, if confirmed**: a **second saint's grave, of a woman called "the elder
    Bibi" (بی بی وڈی), lying outside the mazar in 1875**, and a 1974 street address (Lake Road)
    for that grave.

  - **What the row gains**: five citable sources spanning a 854 AH Samarqand manuscript to a 1975
    Rawalpindi edition — **evidence that his awrad were being copied and glossed in Central Asia
    in the ninth Hijri century** and that a malfuzat existed in a 1033 AH copy. **Nothing about
    the shrine, Multan or the family.** The 1975 imprint postdates the colophon: see Doubts.

## Practices, institutions, economy

- **Shrine sites re-addressed across a century.** The table's two address columns put an 1875
  location beside a 1974 one for each saint: gate-based addresses become street addresses —
  "بیرون موچی دروازہ" → "گوالمنڈی چیمبرلین روڈ نزد پرانی سبزی منڈی" (row ۶۹); "بیرون دہلی
  دروازہ" → "انارکلی مکی مسجد[OCR?]" (row ۶۷); "لاہور" → "آبادی بی بیاں پاکدامناں محمد نگر"
  (row ۷۳) (p. 291, folio 279). **For the archive, this is a printed 1974 gazetteer of where
  Lahore's minor shrines then stood, keyed to the 1875 text.**
- **Takiyas in the Miani graveyard.** Two majzubs are located in 1974 at "**میانی، تکیہ نظام
  شاہ**" and "**میانی، تکیہ مستان شاہ**" (rows ۷۰–۷۱, p. 291, folio 279); row ۷۰'s 1875 address is
  "**گورستان میانی**". Named takiyas inside the graveyard that holds four archive rows
  (ghazi-ilm-din-shaheed, khawaja-feroz-ud-din-gharib-nawaz, tahir-bandagi-qadri,
  wasif-ali-wasif) — the chunk connects none of them.
- **A shrine recorded as erased.** Row ۷۶, Mai Bhagi Lahori: "**اب نشان مٹ چکا ہے (سلطان
  پورہ)**" — the trace has now been erased, at Sultanpura (p. 291, folio 279). Two other rows

## Arguments and interpretations

- **The edition's evidential base, by the editor's own arrangement.** "مآخذ مقدمہ و حواشی" —
  sources of the introduction and footnotes (p. 292, folio 280) — in seven sections: "مخطوطات"
  items ۱–۶۷ (pp. 292–296); "مطبوعات عربی" ۶۸–۷۲ (p. 296); "مطبوعات فارسی" ۷۳–۱۵۹ (pp. 296–300);
  "مطبوعات اُردو" ۱۶۰–۲۴۲ (pp. 300–304); "متفرق مطبوعات" ۲۴۳–۲۴۵ — Pashto, Punjabi and Punjabi
  verse (p. 304); "رسائل کے مضامین" ۲۴۶–۲۵۸ (p. 305); "English:" ۲۵۹–۲۶۵ (p. 306). **Manuscripts
  outnumber Arabic and English print together thirteen to one; Persian print is the largest
  printed class.** For an archive that cites this book, the footnotes' dates and corrections rest
  on this list, not on the 1875 author.
- **The editor is a participant in the literature he edits.** He owns eleven of the manuscripts
  (above); he lists his own works in progress — "**حیات حاجی محمد سعید لاہوری۔ زیر ترتیب ۱۹۷۴ء**"
  (item ۶۰), "**حیات شاہ عنایت قادری قصوری ثم لاہوری زیر تکمیل ۱۹۷۴ء**" (item ۶۱), "**حل المغلقات
  … تصحیح و حواشی محمد اقبال مجددی۔ زیر ترتیب**" (item ۵۰) (p. 295, folio 283); and his published
  work — item ۱۰۳ (1972), item ۲۱۸ (1972), item ۲۲۲ (encyclopaedia article), items ۲۵۱–۲۵۴
  (articles 1962–1974, pp. 297, 303, 305). **The colophon then gives his offices**: "مہتمم دار
  المورخین، لاہور۔ لیکچرر تاریخ شاہ حسین کالج لاہور" (p. 306, folio 294). Any archive use of
  this edition's footnotes should cite them as **Muhammad Iqbal Mujaddidi (1974)**, not as
  Ghulam Sarwar.
- **The author's other book, and a monograph on his family, are among the sources.** Item ۱۱۹
  "**غلام سرور مفتی لاہوری : خزینۃ الاصفیاء، مطبع ثمر ہند لکھنؤ ۱۸۷۳ء**" — the author's
  *Khazinat al-Asfiya*, printed at Lucknow in **1873**, two years before the date the table gives
  this book (p. 298, folio 286). Item ۲۲۰ "**محمود عالم مفتی : ذکر جمیل (اذکار اولاد شیخ الاسلام
  شہاب الدین الور خصوصاً مفتی غلام سرور لاہوری) لاہور ۱۹۶۸ء**" — a **1968 Lahore work on the
  descendants of Shaikh al-Islam Shihab al-Din Alwar, especially Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori**
  (p. 303, folio 291). Chunk 022 recorded the author's own pedigree passing through a "شیخ شہاب
  الدین" (its p. 232); this chunk does not say the two are the same man, but the 1968 title makes
  the family the subject of a printed genealogy. **Together with chunk 022's self-description,
  the archive can now cite the author, his second book and a secondary work on his family, all
  from inside the edition.**
- **The editor dates the book to 1875.** "حدیقۃ الاولیاء ۱۸۷۵ء" (p. 291, folio 279). Chunk 022
  derived an early-1290s AH composition from the book's internal figures; this is the first place
  in these chunks where the edition prints a CE year for the book itself.
- **Non-Muslim authors and editors are cited for Muslim saints without comment.** A 1054 AH work
  "**مؤلفہ صورت سنگھ (احوال شیخ حسو تیلی لاہوری)**" (item ۲۵۶, p. 305, folio 293); Ganesh Das's
  *Char Bagh-e Punjab* edited by Kirpal Singh (item ۱۵۵, p. 300, folio 288); Kanhaiya Lal's
  *Tarikh-e Lahore* (item ۲۰۴, p. 302, folio 290); Dara Shikoh's *Sakinat al-Awliya* edited by
  Tara Chand (item ۹۸, p. 297, folio 285); a *Karamat Nama* by "مشتاق رام گجراتی" on Shah Daula
  (item ۶۴, p. 296, folio 284). **The names are given as printed; the chunk states nothing about
  any author's religion.**
- **A homonym the archive must not conflate.** Item ۱۱۴ "**عبدالقادر ٹھٹھوی : حدیقۃ الاولیاء
  ۱۰۱۶ھ مرتبہ حسام الدین راشدی سندھ ۱۹۶۷ء**" (p. 298, folio 286) — a *Hadiqat al-Awliya* of
  1016 AH from Sindh, cited by the editor of this *Hadiqat al-Awliya* of 1875. Item ۷۸ "**امام
  بخش : حدیقۃ الاسرار فی اخبار الابرار**" (p. 296, folio 284) is a third near-title.
- **The geography of the edition's print sources.** Imprints named: Lucknow, Kanpur, Delhi, Agra,
  Meerut, Aligarh, Calcutta, Bombay, Hyderabad Deccan, Madras, Etawah, Azamgarh, Deoband,
  Amritsar, Ferozepur, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Rawalpindi, Multan, Pakpattan, Sialkot,
  Sharaqpur, Muridke, Lyallpur, Attock, Pindi Bhattian, Tehran, Kabul, Kazan, Cairo ("مصر"),
  Aurangabad, London (pp. 296–306). **A Lahore edition of 1974 drawing on a pre-Partition
  north-Indian print network as heavily as on Pakistani presses.**

## Doubts

- **Everything in this chunk is the editor's, and the table's middle column is his reading of the
  1875 text.** The heading "پتہ بمطابق حدیقۃ الاولیاء ۱۸۷۵ء" (p. 291) says the column follows the
  book, but the chunk contains none of the author's entries to check it against. **Facts entered
  from the table should be attributed to "the 1974 editor's table, reporting the 1875 text", not
  to the author directly.**
- **bibi-pak-daman: the six names are in the editor's table only.** Whether the author's own entry
  names "بی بی حاج، بی بی تاج، بی بی نور، بی بی حور، بی بی گوہر، بی بی شہباز" (p. 291, folio 279)
  is not determinable here; chunk 022 recorded that the author's entry on the Bibis was not in
  chunks 016–022. **Locate the author's entry before the names enter the row as the book's.**
- **shrine-of-mian-mir: the two location columns of row ۷۵ do not describe one place.** 1875:
  "اندر احاطہ خانقاہ حضرت میاں میر"; 1974: "کراچی ریلوے لائن اور میانی صاحب گاؤں کے درمیان"
  (p. 291, folio 279). The archive puts Mian Mir at Alam Ganj (Dharampura), not at Miani Sahib.
  Either the editor found Bibi Jamal Khatun's grave somewhere other than where the 1875 text put
  it, or one column is misread, or "میانی صاحب گاؤں" is being used for a place near Mian Mir's
  khanqah. **The chunk cannot settle it; do not let the 1974 column relocate the archive's
  shrine.** The enclosure fact itself ("اندر احاطہ") stands on the 1875 column alone.
- **shrine-of-mauj-darya-bukhari: the identification is by name and neighbourhood only.** "بیرون
  مزار میران محمد شاہ" with 1974 location "لیک روڈ پرانی انارکلی" (p. 291, folio 279) — no
  "موج دریا", no "بخاری". The archive's row is "Hazrat Meeran Muhammad Shah Bukhari (Mauj Darya)",
  Edward Road, Anarkali. **A human should check the book's own entry on Miran Muhammad Shah
