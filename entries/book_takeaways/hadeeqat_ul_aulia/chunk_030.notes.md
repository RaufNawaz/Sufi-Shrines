# hadeeqat_ul_aulia — chunk 030 — pp. 319–327

**No numbered tazkira entries and no running text of any kind in this chunk. It is nine pages of the
edition's index of persons ("اشاریہ — رجال"), continuing chunk 029 without a gap:** 029 broke off
mid-س at "سرخ بخاری اوچی" (p. 318) and this chunk opens at "سرمد دہلوی | ۱۹۷" (p. 319), then runs
**س → ش → ص → ض → ط → ظ → ع → غ → ف → ق → ک → گ → ل → م**, breaking off mid-م at "محمد بن محمود |
دیکھئے جلال الدین گازرونی" (p. 327). Every line has the form *name | printed pages*, with
parenthetical glosses of kinship and office and cross-references ("دیکھئے", "دیکھیے", "ر ۔ ک ۔").
The chunk's value is the same as 029's: **printed-page locators, plus the index's own glosses**,
which here supply fathers, mothers, sons, a wife, a daughter, a brother and two trades for archive
figures. It also carries the index's line for the author himself, **"غلام سرور مفتی لاہوری (مولف
ہذا)"** (p. 324).

**Folio markers: all nine pages run folio = PDF index − 12**, p. 319 → folio 307 through p. 327 →
folio 315, with no break. As in chunk 029, "est. p. N" below means folio + 12 — arithmetic on this
chunk's own offset, not something the chunk states, and to be verified against the PDF before use.

## Arguments and interpretations

- **The offset holds across another nine pages, and the index is continuous with chunk 029.**
  folio = PDF − 12 on all of pp. 319–327, and the alphabet resumes exactly where 029 stopped
  ("سرخ بخاری اوچی" → "سرمد دہلوی"). **Nothing in this chunk breaks either invariant**, so a
  consolidator can go on converting index numbers to PDF pages by adding 12 through folio 315.
- **The book's post-entry material reaches at least folio 275, and perhaps folio 297.** Chunk 029 put
  the highest index citation at folio 270; this chunk cites **271** ("غلام اکبر لاہوری", "لائق چراغ
  دین لاہوری", p. 326), **273** ("محمد باقر بن شرف الدین لاہوری", p. 327), **274** ("عبد الاحد بن
  میاں کالو سرہندی", p. 321), **275** ("قطب الدین بختیار کاکی اوشی", p. 326) — est. p. 287 — and one
  line reads **"فوق محمد الدین | ۲۹۷"** (p. 325, folio 313), unflagged. **Folio 297 is where chunk 029
  found the editor's "ضمیمۂ ثانی" (p. 309)**, so either the index covers the editor's appendix as well
  as the author's text, or the number is wrong; see Doubts. Between 275 and 297 this chunk cites
  nothing. **The floor for post-entry matter therefore rises from 47 to at least 52 printed pages
  (folios 224–275).**
- **The women's section chunk 029 inferred at folios 235–243 is corroborated, and it holds a shrine
  figure's wife.** Three further women's names fall inside it: "قرسم خاتون | ۷۵ ، ۲۳۸" (p. 326),
  "للّہ کشمیری بی بی | ۲۴۰" (p. 327), **"بی بی فاطمہ سیدہ زوجہ موج دریا بخاری | ۲۴۲"** (p. 325).
  **029 called the section an inference from locators; it now rests on eight names from two chunks,
  and one of them belongs to an archive row.** It is still an inference — no heading is transcribed.
- **The index marks modern authorities typographically, which extends chunk 029's author-vs-editor
  warning.** Six lines invert the name with a comma ("شیرانی ، حافظ محمود", "شیفتہ ، نواب مصطفی خاں",
  "قدوسی ، اعجاز الحق", "غلام کشمیری ، میر", "فرید بخش ، شیخ", "گھاسی ، شیخ", pp. 320–326) and others
  carry a modern title ("ڈاکٹر", "پروفیسر", pp. 320–321). **A folio reached through a comma-inverted or
  titled entry is more likely the editor's footnote than the author's text** — the check chunk 029
  asked for, now with a visible signal.
- **Where an index line's page numbers run out of order, the out-of-order number is usually the one
  the transcriber flagged.** "عبد القادر ثانی | ۲۹ ، ۳۹ [OCR?] ، ۳۱ ، ۳۳ ، ۳۶ ، ۵۶" (p. 322),
  "علیم اللہ جالندھری | ۱۰۲ ، ۹۸ [OCR?] ، ۱۰۶" (p. 324), "لطف اللہ انبالوی | ۹۷ ، ۹۸ ، ۹۸ [OCR?] ،
  ۱۰۳ ، ۱۰۴" (p. 327), "محمد رسول اللہ صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم | ۱۸ ، ۲۴ ، ۲۵ ، ۳۲ ، ۴۱ ، ۱۴۴ ، ۱۵۸ ، ۱۶۷
  [OCR?] ، ۱۶۶" (p. 327). **In four of five such lines the flag sits on the number that breaks the
  ascending order** — a cheap check for anyone using these numbers, and a reason to distrust the
  flagged digit rather than the line. The exception is "شرافت نوشاہی سید | … ۶۸ [OCR?] ، ۱۷۲"
  (p. 319), where the flagged number is in order.
- **The index is again shown not to be exhaustive.** Chunk 029's appendix discussed **"شیخ عبدالاحد
  وحدت"**, but the only Abd al-Ahads here are "عبد الاحد کابلی سرہندی | ۱۱۷" and "عبد الاحد بن میاں
  کالو سرہندی | ۲۷۴" (pp. 321, 322) — neither is "وحدت". **Use the index to find material, never to
  rule it out** (029's rule, independently confirmed).
- **The front matter is indexed, which supports chunk 029's reading of folios 12–20.** The author sits
  at folios 14–15, the **editor at folio 23**, and folios 14–20 gather Lahore muftis, Sir Syed and
  Barkat Ali Khan (029), Dr Lyons, Kanhaiya Lal [OCR?] Rai Bahadur and Storey (029) — **modern names,
  not saints**. "محمد ایوب لاہوری خواجہ | ۱۲ ، ۷۰" (p. 327) puts a name on folio 12 as well.
- **The index's glosses, not its numbers, are what the archive gains here.** Of the sixteen archive
  rows this chunk touches, **eight gain a kinsman's name** (Data Darbar's father and grandfather,
  Sakhi Sarwar's daughter and grandfather, Mauj Darya's father, two sons and wife, Mian Mir's mother,
  Naushah's father, Shah Kamal's brother, Shah Abu al-Maali's father). **None of those names is in the
  archive's rows, and all of them come from the index's parentheses rather than from the book's
  text** — so each must be confirmed at its folio before it is published.

## Doubts

- **sakhi-sarwar: the index names Sayyid Ahmad as the saint and, in chunk 029, as his father.** Here
  "سرور سلطان سخی [OCR?] (سید احمد) | ۱۸۵" (p. 319, folio 307); there "احمد والد سخی سرور | ۱۸۵".
  **Either the book's Sakhi Sarwar and his father are both Ahmad, or one of the two glosses is wrong.**
  The epithet on this line is flagged `[OCR?]`, the personal name is not. Folio 185 (est. p. 197) must
  be read before either name enters the row.
- **shrine-of-mauj-darya-bukhari: a father and a son both called Safi al-Din, on one folio.**
  "صفی الدین بن موج دریا بخاری | ۱۶۹" and "صفی الدین (والد موج دریا بخاری) | ۱۶۹" print as adjacent
  lines (p. 320, folio 308). Neither is flagged. **A duplicated line with one gloss mistyped is as
  likely as a real papponymy; do not put either name in the row unaided.** His wife is likewise
  indexed twice, at folios 20 and 242, under two different wordings (p. 325, folio 313).
- **darbar-abul-muali-qadri: the gloss "(والد شاہ ابوالمعالی)" is used for two different fathers.**
  Khair al-Din at folio 45 (chunk 029) and "محمد اشرف امبیٹھوی (والد شاہ ابو المعالی) | ۹۷" here
  (p. 327, folio 315), which by its folio and its nisba belongs to Abul Muali **Ambethwi**. **Chunk
  029's doubt about the archive row's name field stands unresolved and is now known to sit in an index
  that reuses the phrase.**
- **Two archive rows could take "عنایت قادری قصوری شاہ | ۶۳ ، ۶۴ ، ۶۹" (p. 324, folio 312), and the
  chunk cannot choose.** **shrine-of-shah-inayat-qadiri** (Mozang, Lahore; d. c. 1728; Qadri Shattari)
  and **dargah-roza-sufi-shah-inayat-shaheed** (Jhok Sharif, Sindh; d. 1718). The index repeats the
  editor's "Qadiri" + "Qasuri" (of Kasur) from chunk 029's book list and adds three folios — est.
  pp. 75, 76, 81, inside the Naushahi/Kasur run. **Left unmatched, exactly as in 029; the locators are
  now available to whoever resolves it.**
- **purana-bhalwal: two Sulaimans, one place-match and one order-match.** "سلیمان بھلوالی | ۵۸"
  against "سلیمان قادری لاہوری شاہ | ۵۴" (both p. 319, folio 307). The archive row is Hazrat Shah
  Sulaiman Nuri (Sakhi Badshah) of Purana Bhalwal, Qadiri. **Neither index line carries both; "نوری"
  and "سخی بادشاہ" appear on neither. Marked tentative above, and to be settled at folios 54 and 58.**
- **shrine-of-shah-yusaf-gardez: "گردیز شاہی ملتانی | ۲۶۴" carries the epithet and the city but no
  personal name**, and folio 264 falls in the post-entry Lahore run rather than the Multan block at
  146–172 (p. 326, folio 314). **Not assigned. Read folio 264 (est. p. 276) before using it.**
- **shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh / darbar-sakhi-shah-chan-charagh: the index holds at least four
  Chiragh-named men and cross-references none of them.** "شاہ چراغ لاہوری (عبدالرزاق) | ۵۵" and
  "چراغ شاہ لاہوری | ۲۶۲" (chunk 029), "شاہ چراغ دہلی" via his grandfather at folio 85 (p. 322,
  folio 310), "لائق چراغ دین لاہوری | ۲۷۱" (p. 326), plus 029's "چراغ دین مفتی | ۲۲۱" and "روشن مفتی
  چراغ دین | ۲۷۰". **The archive has a Shah Chiragh in Lahore and a Shah Chan Charagh in Rawalpindi;
  nothing here separates them, and the four Abd al-Razzaqs of the ع run are all on other folios.**
- **shrine-of-makhdoom-jahaniyan-jahangasht: a Kamal called "Pir Jahaniyan" is indexed twice.**
  "کمال بخاری شاہ (پیر جہانیاں) | ۳۹" (p. 326, folio 314) and 029's "جہانیاں پیر سید کمال | ۲۵۴".
  **Do not merge either with the archive's Sayyid Jalaluddin without reading folios 39 and 254.**
- **Shah Sikandar Kaithali: the appendix's key and the index's folios disagree.** Chunk 029 recorded
  the editor's correction keyed **"ص ۲۷"**; the index reads **"سکندر کیتھلی شاہ | ۴۷ ، ۱۱۷"**, and
  "کمال کیتھلی شاہ | ۴۷ ، ۱۱۷" repeats those two folios exactly (pp. 319, 326). **۲۷ and ۴۷ differ by
  one glyph and neither was flagged; the grandfather/grandson pair sharing both folios favours 47.**
  Flagged for a human, not decided here.
- **"فوق محمد الدین | ۲۹۷" (p. 325, folio 313) cites a folio inside the editor's own appendix.** Chunk
  029 places folio 297 at p. 309, among the appendix items on the Mujaddid's pedigree, Shah Sikandar
  Kaithali and Bahlol Gol — and records no such name there. A near-twin line, "فوقی محمود دین | ۱۲۵",
  stands two lines below it. **Either the index covers the editor's back matter (which would matter
  for the whole book) or the number, or the name, is corrupt. Neither is flagged.**
- **ranmal-sharif: "شرافت نوشاہی سید" is indexed at eight folios inside the author's text**
  (۲۹ ، ۳۲ ، ۳۹ ، ۵۵ ، ۵۹ ، ۶۴ ، ۶۸ [OCR?] ، ۱۷۲, p. 319, folio 307), yet chunk 029 met a Sayyid
  Sharafat Naushahi as the **modern** compiler of *Ganj Sharif* and the subject of the editor's 1971
  book. **Two men of one name, or the editor's notes indexed with the author's text — not
  determinable here.**
- **mazar-e-iqbal: "محمد اقبال مجددی | ۲۳" is the edition's editor, not Allama Iqbal** (p. 327,
  folio 315). Recorded because a name search would match the archive row.
- **Name-only look-alikes that must not be matched to archive rows from this chunk.** Three Abd
  al-Latifs, all glossed as someone's father or grandfather and none a Kazmi or a Bhittai, against
  **bari-imam** and **bhit-bhit-shah** (p. 322, folio 310); "عبد الکریم حافظ | ۲۰۹" beside "عبد الکریم
  لاہوری | ۸۷ ، ۹۱ ، ۹۲ ، ۱۷۶" and "عبد الکریم مجددی لاہوری | ۱۴۵", none with a place tying it to
  **eidgah-sharif** or **shrine-of-shah-abdul-karim-bulri** (p. 322); "عبد اللہ شاہ بلوچ لاہوری | ۷۰ ،
  ۷۱" against **shrine-of-abdullah-shah-ghazi** (p. 322); "غلام فرید لاہوری | ۲۰ ، ۲۱۰" against
  **mithankot-kot-mithan** (p. 324); "فتح شاہ بن موج دریا بخاری | ۱۷۰" — a Lahore Bukhari named Fath
  Shah — against **wadpagga-sharif**'s Fateh Muhammad Bukhari of Peshawar (p. 325); "علی اکبر
  اردستانی | ۷۸ ، ۱۷۰" against **shrine-of-hazrat-shah-ali-akbar-shamsi** (p. 323); "محمد ایوب لاہوری
  خواجہ | ۱۲ ، ۷۰" and "محمد ایوب قادری | ۹۶" against **shrine-of-hazrat-muhammad-ayub-shah-bukhari**
  (pp. 327, 324); "عبد الوہاب بخاری | ۱۶۴ ، ۲۲۶" and "سرمست سید | ۱۴۷" against
  **shrine-of-sachal-sarmast** (pp. 323, 319); four Tahirs, none "بندگی", against **tahir-bandagi-qadri**
  (p. 321); ten Shams al-Dins, none "سبزواری" or "سیالوی", against
  **shrine-of-shah-shams-ud-din-sabzwari** and **sial-sharif** (p. 320); three Qalandar Shahs and no
  Marwandi against **lal-shahbaz-qalandar** and **shamsabad** (p. 326); two Qutb al-Dins and no Aibak
  against **tomb-of-qutbuddin-aibak** (p. 326); "محمد باقر شرقپوری | ۱۰۹" — a Sharaqpur figure, but
  not the Mian Sher Muhammad of **shrine-of-pir-sher-muhammad** (p. 327); "لدھی شاہ لاہوری | ۲۰ ، ۲۱۷"
  against **shrine-of-lakhi-shah-saddar** (p. 326); "عبد الرحیم ضیاء حیدر آبادی | ۲۱۴" against
  **shrine-of-makhdoom-abdul-rahim-girhori** (p. 322). **None of these is a match; each is a trap a
  name search would fall into.**
- **Five index lines print a name and the rule but no page number and no cross-reference**, so a
