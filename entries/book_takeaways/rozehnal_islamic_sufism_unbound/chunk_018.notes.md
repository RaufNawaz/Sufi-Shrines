(p. 276), `I dex 271` (p. 277), `272 INDEX` (p. 278), `I dex 273` (p. 279), `274 INDEX` (p. 280),

headword, `ziyarat` (p. 281, folio 275).

**ALPHABETICAL SCOPE — read this before treating the chunk as a completeness check.** The chunk opens
mid-D on a continuation line, `dreams––continued` (p. 276, folio 270), and closes at `ziyarat`
(p. 281, folio 275). **Headwords A through D-up-to-"dreams" are NOT in this chunk** — they fall in

and `Deoband madrasa` would sit, all of which are named here only as `see also` targets. Any claim

**Every locator in this chunk is one of the book's own PRINTED FOLIOS.** The PDF→folio offset steps
(11→10→9→8→7→6), so no locator has been converted to a PDF page and none is written as `(p. N)`.
Locator runs are quoted as the index's own numbers and labelled "printed folios". Page references
below are always the index page the entry is *printed on*, in the long form.

**Column hazard (damage class 7) — checked entry by entry, and it does bite.** The text layer
interleaves the two columns by vertical position, so on a printed page the left column runs its full
height and then the right column runs its full height, but in the text layer a left-column headword
and a right-column headword share an output line. Two consequences were verified rather than
assumed: (1) the TOP of the right column continues the BOTTOM of the left column, e.g. `women
disciples` (p. 281, folio 275) breaks at `101–2,` in the left column and resumes `132, 133, 136, 137,
143, 147, 148–9, 162, 165, 166, 169, 170, 180, 181–2, 193, 200–1, 210, 211–12, 217, 223` at the top
of the right column; the same happens to `sunna` (p. 280, folio 274) and to the `Malaysian disciples`
sub-entries (p. 277, folio 271). (2) Two `see also` lines can collide on one output line and belong
to different entries — at p. 277, folio 271, `see also Sabir, 'Ala ad-Din 'Ali Ahmad` (Kalyar
Sharif's, left column) and `see also Pulau Besar; Sultan al-'Arifin` (Malaysian disciples', right
column) print on the same line. **Every attribution below was checked against indentation and against
the alphabetical sequence of its own column**; the two columns are each independently alphabetical
and both sequences are unbroken across all six pages, which is what makes the check possible.
An independent consistency check passed: the `murids … women` sub-entry (p. 278, folio 272) and the
`women disciples` headword (p. 281, folio 275) carry identical locator runs.

the book coins or owns** — these print as headwords in initial capitals with substantial spans, which
marks them as the author's own categories rather than Sufi vocabulary.
