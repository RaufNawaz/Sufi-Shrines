  description.** Under the 18 Sep 2026 ruling each takes its row's id, but a human should weigh each:
  1. `shrine-of-fariduddin-ganjshakar` is the strongest — the book gives him a death date `(d. 1265)`
     (p. 223, folio 212) and two entries about the Pakpattan shrine by name (p. 227, folio 216) — but

     of other people's articles.
