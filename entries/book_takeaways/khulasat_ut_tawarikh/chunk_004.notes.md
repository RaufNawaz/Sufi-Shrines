# khulasat_ut_tawarikh — chunk 004 — pp. 39–51

The chunk has three parts. Most of it (pp. 46 down to 39) is the 1918 editor's index, headed "فہرست اسمائے مردمان و بلاد و قلعجات و آبہا وغیرہ کہ در کتاب خلاصۃ [OCR?] التواریخ مذکور شدہ اند بترتیب حروف ہجا" (p. 46): an alphabetical index of persons, towns, forts and rivers. In reading order (high → low) it runs from alif (p. 46) through ba to the start of pe (p. 39). This is index matter: the entries are locators, not facts, and their numbers are the edition's printed pages. Next comes the editor's errata table, "فہرست اغلاطے چند کہ در حین [OCR?] انطباع شدہ" (p. 48, continued on p. 47), in the form page | line | wrong | right. The body text is pp. 51 down to 49. It opens mid-sentence (p. 51) with the end of a section on appointments. Then comes "در بیان بقیه حقیقت تعاقب افواج قاهره و آمدن دار اشکوه از تهتهه بجانب گجرات" (pp. 49–51): the imperial pursuit of Dara Shikoh by Bhakkar, Sukkur and Siwistan to Thatta, Dara's crossing toward Gujarat by the edge of the Jul, and the pursuers' return to Bhakkar. The section closes with "۞" (p. 49).

**Body text (pp. 49–51)**

- **gurdwara-dash-mesh-pita**, **gurdwara-pehli-patshahi-jind-pir-sukkur**, **kalka-cave-temple-asthan-of-kalka-devi**, **sadh-belo-sadh-belo-island-temple**, **sant-baba-asudaram-darbar-panno-aqil** (the book's "سکهر"):
  - News reached the joined pursuing armies that Dara "از بهکر گذشته بست و پنجم محرم در سکهر نزول نمود" (p. 51).
  - Distance: "ازین منزل تا سکهر شصت و سه کروه باین رود سے[OCR?] آب و قریب صد کروه ازانطرف دریا فاصله دارد". That is, from the pursuers' camp it was sixty-three kos to Sukkur on this side of the river and about a hundred kos on the other side (p. 51).
  - Shaikh Mir, Diler Khan and Qubad Khan crossed the river "بجانب سکهر". On 5 Safar Shaikh Mir reached "[illegible] ده کروسے سکهر". Because of "کثرت بیشه و تراکم اشجار خاردار و مراحل دشوارگذار", many beasts perished. The tents, baggage and provisions did not arrive at this stage, and on the 6th they reached Sukkur (p. 51).

- **lal-shahbaz-qalandar**, **shrine-of-lakhi-shah-saddar** (the book's "سیوستان"):
  - On the 12th (of Safar, by the run of the narrative) Saf Shikan Khan reached "سیزده کروسے سیوستان" (p. 50).
  - The fort had a commander: "محمد صالح ترخان[OCR?] فوجدار و قلعه دار سیوستان". He wrote that Dara had come within "پنج کروسے قلعه" and that the boats carrying Dara's treasure and goods were following behind (p. 50).
  - Saf Shikan Khan sent a thousand horse with "برق انداز و بان[OCR?] و شترنال" and a body of beldars and water-carriers. They were to build a battery on the bank "نزدیک قلعه جاےے[OCR?] که عرض دریا سے[OCR?] کمتر بوده باشد", near the fort where the river was narrowest, and to mount guns (p. 50).
  - His guns fired at the boats "لیکن ازجهت مسافت بعید کارگر نشد", and "کشتیها بے ممانعت از طرف قلعه عبور نمود" (p. 50).
  - The author blames the commander: "محمد صالح توفیق خدمت گذاری نیافت". Had he blocked the way, Dara's crossing would have been difficult, and Dara would probably have been taken (p. 50).
  - Dara passed "از [illegible] سیوستان که محل بیم و مکان هلاکت بود" and reached Thatta (p. 50).

**Body, pp. 49–51**
- The end of the previous section (p. 51). Someone "...داشت تعین شد" (the sentence begins on the previous page). "راے[OCR?] رای سنگه[OCR?] برادرزاده مهاراجه" was given the title of raja, "فیل با ماده و شمشیر مرصع و نقاره[OCR?]", "انعام یک لک روپیه" and the mansab "چهار هزاری و چهار هزار سوار". He was sent with Muhammad Amin Khan, and "رایات عالیات نیز ازانجا نهضت فرمودند".
- The pursuers. "در زمان بودن ملتان", first Saf Shikan Khan and then Shaikh Mir and Diler Khan had been sent after Dara. The two armies joined at "قصبه جنجی[OCR?] واهن[OCR?]" and halted there some days (p. 51).
- Bhakkar ("بهکر", also "بهکهر").
  - Saf Shikan Khan reached Bhakkar three days ahead of Shaikh Mir's arrival at Sukkur (p. 51).
  - Dara had put "احمال[OCR?] و اثقال و بردگان با برخی از خزانه و طلا و الات" into the fort. He set "خواجه سراے[OCR?]" together with "عبدالرزاق" to guard it, and left artillery, musketeers ("بندوقچی[OCR?]") and other garrison stores there (p. 51).
  - Dara left on "سلخ محرم", cutting a road through forest and jungle (p. 51).
  - Saf Shikan Khan made "اغر خان[OCR?] عرف امام قلی[OCR?]" faujdar of Bhakkar and left it on 5 Safar (p. 50). On the return, men, beasts and guns were left with "اغر خان[OCR?] فوجدار بهکر" (p. 49).
- Desertions. Daud Khan, "شیخ نظام[OCR?]" and others, about four thousand horse, left Dara near Bhakkar. Daud Khan went "براه جیسلمیر بسمت حصار فیروزه که وطن اوست", and the rest joined Saf Shikan Khan (p. 51).
- Qandahar. Dara wanted to go to Qandahar, but "نوکرانش همراهی نکردند و زوجه او نیز راضی نگشت", so he turned toward Thatta (p. 50).
- Thatta ("تهتهه"). Saf Shikan Khan on one side of the river and Shaikh Mir and Diler Khan on the other reached Thatta. Dara "در تهتهه نتوانست اقامت ورزید" and on "بست[OCR?] و نهم صفر" crossed the river "بسمت گجرات" (pp. 49–50).
  - Saf Shikan[OCR?] Khan bridged the river and camped on the far side on "هفتم ربیع الاول" (p. 49).
- The recall and the decision to turn back (p. 49).
  - A farman ordered Shaikh Mir and Diler Khan to give up the pursuit and come to court.
  - The amirs agreed to turn back. The troops were exhausted, most of the mounts and baggage animals were lost, there was "در خزانه زیاده از یک ماه زر موجود نیست", and Dara's road ran through "جول و بیابان بے آب و آبادانی".
- The Jul ("جول") (p. 49).
  - Spies reported that Dara, on "هشتم ربیع الاول", went "[illegible] تهتهه بسمت گجرات" and reached "بکنار جول".
  - The tanks were dry that year "بسبب کمی باران", and within two or three stages much of Dara's army and many beasts died for lack of water.
  - The book's description: "نوعے شورستان واقع شده که تا چهل کروه بر کنار دریا ست شور[OCR?] آب شیرین اصلا نیست و بواسطه قرب دریا نوعے خلاب[OCR?] است که دواب عبور نمیتوانند کرد". That is, a kind of salt waste stretching forty kos along the river, with no sweet water at all, and, near the river, a kind of mire[OCR?] that beasts cannot cross.
- The return. Shaikh Mir, Saf Shikan[OCR?] Khan and Diler[OCR?] Khan reached Bhakkar on "ششم[OCR?] ربیع الثانی". They put that region in order and left for court on "هشتم[OCR?]" of the month (p. 49).
- People: Dara Shikoh (unnamed here as anything but "دار اشکوه"), his wife (unnamed), Saf Shikan Khan and his "کوکیان[OCR?]", Shaikh Mir, Diler Khan, Qubad Khan, Muhammad Salih Tarkhan[OCR?], Daud Khan, and Muhammad Amin Khan (pp. 49–51).

## Practices, institutions, economy

- Officers named on the river route (pp. 49–50): a "فوجدار و قلعه دار" at Siwistan, faujdars at Bhakkar and at Sukkur, and the suba of Thatta.
- Zamindars as informants on routes: "از تقریر زمینداران ظاهر گشت" (p. 50).
- Treasure moved by river boat ("کشتیهاے[OCR?] خزانه"), and the imperial treasury in the field ran short: "در خزانه زیاده از یک ماه زر موجود نیست" (pp. 49–50).
- Rewards: "انعام یک لک روپیه" and a mansab of "چهار هزاری و چهار هزار سوار" (p. 51).

## Arguments and interpretations (academic books)

- **On fate and Muhammad Salih's failure.** "ازانجا که برامد هر کار در وقتے معین و حصول هر مطلب بازبسته[OCR?] بزمانے مقرر است از محمد صالح کوتاهی بوقوع امده" (p. 50). Since every task is fulfilled at an appointed time, Muhammad Salih fell short. The author also judges that blocking the river at Siwistan would probably have led to Dara's capture.

## Doubts

**Reading order and edges**
- The index and errata are editor's matter (pp. 39–48), not the author's text. No index entry has been turned into a fact.
- The index runs p. 46 (alif, with its heading) down to p. 39 (pe). It continues in a lower-numbered chunk, where p. 39 ends with "[illegible]".
  - Several column ends are "[illegible]" (pp. 39, 40, 41, 43, 44, 45). These include a headword whose locators "۴۵۲ - ..." begin p. 44, after "آ[illegible]" on p. 45. That headword is not filled.
- The errata begin on p. 48 with their heading (printed pages ۱۵–۲۰۸) and continue on p. 47 (printed pages ۲۲۵–۵۳۴).
- The body runs p. 51 → 50 → 49.
  - It opens mid-sentence ("...داشت تعین شد", p. 51), continuing from p. 52.
  - The joins p. 51 → 50 ("روانه / حضور پر نور گردانید") and p. 50 → 49 ("ازین طرف دریا صف شکن خان / و ازان طرف دریا شیخ میر و دلیر خان نیز در تهتهه رسیدند") read through.
  - p. 49 ends with "۞". Whether the work itself ends here is not stated in the chunk.
- A library stamp on p. 49 is not text.

**Place-level attachments (weigh accordingly)**

- **Siwistan → Sehwan Sharif (2 rows).** The attachment is place-level. "سیوستان" is taken as the brief's Sehwan form. The book speaks of its fort and the narrow river by it, and calls it "محل بیم و مکان هلاکت".
  - The book does not say whether "سیوستان" here is the town or a wider district.
  - Neither Lal Shahbaz nor Lakhi Shah Saddar is named.
  - "[illegible]" precedes "سیوستان" on p. 50 and is not filled.
- **Index toponyms.** These are attached place-level from bare index headwords with no text: Uch (5), Peshawar (10, the headword is flagged), Ichhra (2), Amarkot (2), Eminabad (3), Badin (1).

  - Badin: "بدین" is taken as Badin.

**Traps and unplaced names**
- **"گجرات" in the body (pp. 49, 51 heading).** It is read as suba Gujarat (India), not Punjab's Gujrat. The book's own route is Thatta → across the river → "بکنار جول", which places it there. Shah Daula is not mapped.
- **"عبدالرزاق"** (p. 51) is the man set with a eunuch to guard the Bhakkar fort. He is NOT mapped to shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh (Syed Abdul Razzaq): this is a bare common name for a garrison officer.
- **"اجودھن - ۳۳ - ۶۲"** (p. 46) is not mapped to Pakpattan. The index does not equate it with Pakpattan or name Farid's tomb.
- **"ایاز - ۶۴ - ۱۷۹ - ۱۸۱"** (p. 43), **"احمد ایاز - ۲۵۴"** and **"اعزالدین ایاز - ۱۹۱"** (pp. 44–45) are not mapped to darbar-malik-ahmad-ayaz. The index lists three separate Ayaz headwords and identifies none. A consolidator could check the body at those printed pages.

- **"برہان الدین غریب - ۳۴ [OCR?]"** (p. 42) is not mapped to langer-makhdoom (Makhdoom Burhan-ud-din, a held identity row). The index gives only the name, with "غریب", which the row does not carry.
