# khulasat_ut_tawarikh — chunk 003 — pp. 28–38

**This chunk is index matter. It contains no narrative facts.** It is part of the 1918 editor's index, printed in Persian script in two columns per page as `headword - page, page`. The book reads from the highest page down, so the alphabet runs from p. 38 down to p. 28. P. 38 (folio line 11) opens at the end of the letter پ ("پورن مل") and runs through ت into ج. Pp. 37–36 cover ج and چ, p. 35 covers چ, ح and the start of خ, p. 34 covers خ and the start of د, pp. 33–32 cover د, ذ and ر, and p. 31 covers ر, ز and the start of س. Pp. 30–29 continue س, and p. 28 (folio line 21) closes س and opens ش, ending inside the entry "شاہجہان". The numbers after each headword are the index's printed locators. They are quoted here as text and are NOT PDF pages. Every `(p. N)` below is the PDF page on which the index entry is printed. Two headwords of archive interest name a row's saint by name and carry ids. The archive places that appear are listed under Other, without ids, following this chunk's instruction (see Doubts).

## Doubts

**Locators**
- Every number quoted above is the editor's printed page in the 1918 edition. None has been converted to a PDF page. The consolidator can resolve them against the body chunks.

**The two mapped lines**
- Both rest on a bare headword.
  - "دولا شاہ" is the brief's "شاه دوله" in inverted order.
  - "سلطان سرور" is one of the brief's listed forms for Sakhi Sarwar. Its last locator, "۷۳", is flagged.
- The index gives no description, so the identification rests on the name alone. Weigh accordingly.
- Trap avoided: "سرور ملک" — "۲۷۴" (p. 30) is a different headword and is NOT mapped.

**Archive places left without ids, following this chunk's instruction (ids only where a headword names a row's saint or site)**
- City-level toponyms that would attach several rows under ruling (a), and so are left without ids here:
  - Sehwan: `lal-shahbaz-qalandar`, `shrine-of-lakhi-shah-saddar`.
  - Sialkot: 5 rows.
  - Sukkur: 5 rows.
  - Rawalpindi: 6 rows.
  - Jhelum: 2 rows.
- Single-row toponyms, also left without ids:
  - Khushab → `amb-temples-amb-sharif`.
  - Hasan Abdal → `gurdwara-panja-sahib`.
  - Chiniot → `langer-makhdoom`.
  - Jhang → `garh-maharaja-shorkot`.
- The place name alone does not name the site, so these attachments are left to the consolidator, place-level.
- "روہتاس" may be the Punjab Rohtas (toponym "rohtas fort" → `gurdwara-choa-sahib`) or another Rohtas. The index does not say which, and it may mix both.
- "تلونڈی": the brief equates Talwandi with Nankana Sahib only where the book itself does. The index does not, so it is not mapped.
- "سندھ[OCR?]" may be the river or the country. It is flagged, and it is NOT made a province bullet, because index entries are not statements.

**Held or ambiguous saints**
- "جلال بخاری" ("۶۲"): Uch Jalaluddin / Jahaniyan split, a held identity question. Candidate rows are `shrine-of-jalaluddin-surkh-posh-bukhari-jalaluddin-bukhari` and `shrine-of-makhdoom-jahaniyan-jahangasht`. The index gives no way to choose, so it is left unmapped.
- "رکن الدین" ("۶۱ - ۶۲") and "رکن الدین شیخ": the bare name is not "رکن عالم". There are several Rukn al-Din headwords, which the index itself distinguishes. `shrine-of-shah-rukn-e-alam` is not mapped.
- "شاہ برہان" ("۷۸"): `langer-makhdoom` (Makhdoom Burhan-ud-din, Chiniot; a held identity) is a candidate on the name only. "چنیوٹ" also carries the locator "۷۸", but that is a coincidence of locators, not an identification the index makes. Not mapped.
- "رامداس" may or may not be Guru Ram Das (`gurdwara-guru-ram-das-ji`), since one of its locators, "۳۷۷", may be another man. Not mapped.
- "چک گوز و ہزگوبند[OCR?]" is garbled. A Hargobind reading would point to 6 rows, but that is not supported. Not mapped.
- "تیغ بہادر" is not the principal figure of any row.
