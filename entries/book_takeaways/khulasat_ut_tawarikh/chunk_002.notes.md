## Doubts

**What this chunk is**
- This is index matter only. No headword here is evidence of anything beyond the editor's listing it. Every locator is a printed page number of the 1918 edition, quoted as text and not converted to a PDF page. The brief gives the body's folio as 589 − PDF, but that conversion is left to the consolidator.
- Reading order is p. 27 → p. 20. Two entries run across a page break: "غزمین[OCR?]" runs from p. 25 into p. 24, and "کابل" runs from p. 24 into p. 23. p. 27 opens with locators "۴۷۳ … ۴۹۱" belonging to a headword on p. 28, outside this chunk. "ملتان" closes p. 20 and may continue on p. 19.
- Absent from this chunk, because the alphabet here runs only from ش to م: Uch, Pakpattan/Ajodhan, Sehwan, Thatta, Peshawar (as "پشاور"), Hasan Abdal, Sialkot, Baba Nanak, Shah Daula, Sakhi Sarwar, Bahauddin Zakariya and Hinglaj. Their index entries, if any, fall in other chunks.

**Place-level and person-level attachments (weigh accordingly)**
- **Lahore (35 rows), Multan (8 rows) and Shorkot (1 row)** are place-level attachments by ruling (a), from a bare index headword.
  - On the Lahore line the held-identity rows are shrine-of-baba-shah-chiragh and shrine-of-shah-inayat-qadiri. On the Multan line the held row is shrine-of-shah-shams-ud-din-sabzwari ("Shams"). Nothing in the chunk bears on these questions.
  - "شورکوت" maps through toponym_map's "shorkot" to garh-maharaja-shorkot, whose location is "Garh Maharaja (Shorkot)". The index does not say that its Shorkot is that place.
- **Fariduddin Ganjshakar and Qutbuddin Aibak** are person-level attachments, on a bare index name. Each headword names the row's principal figure, but the index says nothing about a tomb.

**Not mapped, with reasons**
- "مخدوم جہانیان" — ۶۲[OCR?] (p. 20). This bears on the held Uch identity question (shrine-of-makhdoom-jahaniyan-jahangasht vs shrine-of-jalaluddin-surkh-posh-bukhari-jalaluddin-bukhari). The headword names no place and its locator is flagged, so it is not put on a bold line. A separate entry "شیخ جلال" carries the same printed locator ۶۲ (p. 26), and "ملتان" also lists ۶۲ (p. 20). That is only an index coincidence. The consolidator should check printed page ۶۲ in the body.
- "علی الحق[OCR?]" — ۷۳ (p. 25). This could be the principal figure of shrine-of-imam-ali-ul-haq ("Imam Ali-ul-Haq"), but the headword is flagged, has no "امام", and names no Sialkot. It is held for a body chunk to settle. The locator ۷۳ is also given for "ظفروال" (p. 26), "عبدالحکیم" (p. 26) and "صفدر خان" (p. 26).
- "شمس الدین تبریزی" — ۳۴, ۶۱ (p. 27). This is the held "Shams" (Multan) question, and the headword names no Multan. Printed ۶۱ also appears under "ملتان", "فریدالدین گنجشکر", "شہاب الدین سہروردی", "شیخ عراقی", "صدرالدین" and "کمال الدین علی شاہ". This looks like a cluster of saints on one printed page, but that is an inference about the index layout only.
- "شیخ موسیٰ گیلانی" — ۵۲۳ (p. 26). The calibration notes (chunk 006) leave the Musa Gilani / shrine-of-syed-musa-pak identity open. It is not mapped here.
- "صدرالدین" (p. 26) is a bare name and is not mapped to shrine-of-lakhi-shah-saddar. "کمال شیخ" (p. 23) is not mapped to shrine-of-baba-shah-kamal. "شیخ فرید" (p. 26) and "فرید بخاری" (p. 24) are not mapped to the Farid row.
- "گوبندراے گورو" (p. 22) does not match verbatim any row's principal figure. The only Gobind row is gurdwara-dash-mesh-pita (Guru Gobind Singh), and equating the two names would be general knowledge.
- "گجرات" (p. 22) is one headword with a long run of locators. The index does not separate Punjab Gujrat from suba Gujarat, so it takes no id, including shrine-of-hazrat-shah-daula-daryai.
- "شمس آباد" (p. 27) matches toponym_map's "shamsabad" (Okara), but nothing ties this index entry to that place, and the name is common.
- "فرشاور" (p. 24) is not "پشاور". Reading it as Peshawar would be general knowledge.
