Completes the notes stage for the first three books of the cloud queue and
consolidates each into entries/book_takeaways/<slug>.md (Pass 2 of
TAKEAWAYS_PROTOCOL.md), marked summarized in state.json:

  hadeeqat_ul_aulia   32/32 chunk notes, 52 archive rows informed
  eaton_essays…       20/20 chunk notes, Baba Farid plus the cross cutting argument
  sawaneh_shah_jamal   1/1

compile_findings.py was folding each bullet to its FIRST LINE only. These notes
are hard wrapped at about 100 characters and a bullet routinely runs to six
lines, so every continuation line was dropped: most of the text, and with it the
dates, folios and Urdu quotations that happened to wrap. Four consolidation
workers independently reported their input arriving truncated mid sentence
before the cause was found, and the first four part files had to be rewritten.
The parser now folds continuation lines into the bullet, and reports how much of
the notes text reached the CSV, failing under --min-coverage (default 85%).
Coverage is now 98% of non blank characters, against roughly a third before, and
findings attached to an archive row went from 1,036 across 47 shrines to 1,171
across 55. This is RULE 4: the check that could not see the notes it was checking
now says so out loud.

Three findings worth a human's attention, all recorded in the takeaways files:

  * The archive's figure_died for data-darbar, shrine-of-bahauddin-zakariya and
    darbar-malik-ahmad-ayaz each match the reading this edition's own editor
    rejects. Both readings are kept side by side (RULE 2); the choice is Rauf's.
  * madho-lal-hussain is one archive row, but the book treats Shah Husain and
    Madho as two saints, with separate entries, appendix rows and index lines,
    and the editor doubts Madho's historicity.
  * sawaneh_shah_jamal is NOT a life of Shah Jamal of Ichhra. Its title page
    gives Shah Jamal al-Bahr of Warangal, Deccan, compiled by Muhammad Ali Khan
    Mujaddidi, Hyderabad Deccan. The manifest's mapping to shah-jamal is
    unsupported and the manifest needs correcting.

Also folio arithmetic: the folio markers for pp. 176-197 read ten too high,
raised by nine separate workers, so folios cited from chunks 016-018 need ten
subtracted.
