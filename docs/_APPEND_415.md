**The registry.** `entries/verse_originals/registry.json` holds one entry per distinct rendering in the 94 public descriptions: 179, keyed by the md5 of the exact rendering (the full English stays only in the articles), with the rows that quote it, the translator, the page the team version cites, and for a found original the original line by line, its source and why it is the line rendered. `entries/verse_originals/README.md` explains the format, the display rules and the open work. **10 found, 169 open.**

**The 10 found:** Guru Nanak's lines in the fifteen gurdwara rows (Japji 20 on Ang 4; "Sahib mera eko hai", Ang 350; the Babur hymn of the pirs, Angs 417 to 418; the sacred thread, Ang 471; the aarti, Ang 663; "ve Lalo" and "aavan athatrai", Angs 722 to 723; the herons, Ang 729) and Baba Farid's "Ajj na suti kant siu" (Ang 1379) in his row. Text from BaniDB, read through the SikhiToTheMax site in the browser on the PC (the cloud sandbox cannot reach api.banidb.com; WebFetch would pass the Gurmukhi through a summarising model, so the browser's own fetch was used to get the exact text). Each original gives exactly what the English renders: where the English renders half a line, the half line and `…`; the verse numbers and the Rahau mark are left off. Where a row quotes two renderings of the aarti one after the other, the original stands once, above the first. **One Guru Nanak rendering stays open:** "God is One, / The Path is One" (Khalid, p. 144, his reconstruction near Pakpattan) is not in the Granth by a search of BaniDB's English; it may be a Janamsakhi saying, and nothing is inserted until it is identified.

**`pipeline/apply_verse_originals.py`** inserts each found original as its own paragraph above its rendering in the English and above the Urdu rendering in the Urdu (renderings are paired by order; the script refuses a row whose two counts differ), adds one bibliography item (`- *Sri Guru Granth Sahib*, Ang … (Gurmukhi text as given by BaniDB, banidb.com).`), then moves the V1/V2/V3 stamps only after proving that the new text, with the insertions taken out, is byte for byte the passed text. `--check` is the invariant (exit 1 on a missing original in either language or a missing source). 16 rows, 80 insertions per language, all stamps moved, check ok. The team versions are unchanged; each row's notes file records the originals.
