# Decision required — do oral histories happen, or come off the plan?

**Written 18 August 2026.** This is a one-page forcing document, not a plan. The plan already
exists and is good: `docs/internet_archive_setup.md` covers hosting, consent script, consent
log, and the reasoning for the Internet Archive over this repo. Nothing below re-documents it.

## The situation, stated plainly

`docs/HANDOVER.md` lists oral history as risk #4 and CLAUDE.md carries it as a standing
finding: **18 video files, zero audio recordings, despite oral history being the stated
purpose.** That finding has survived several sessions unchanged. It is not a backlog item that
keeps getting deprioritised — it is a stated purpose with nothing behind it, and every session
that writes it down again without deciding makes the gap a little more permanent.

Three facts constrain the decision:

1. **CID funding ended 29 May 2026.** No budget, one part-time engineer, hard horizon at 2028.
2. **The consent problem is real and one-way.** Internet Archive items can be darkened but
   never truly deleted. Consent has to be *on the tape*, in the speaker's own language, saying
   explicitly that the recording will be **published freely on the internet** — not "used for
   research." Those are different agreements and only the first is honest about what happens.
3. **The tooling is not the blocker and never was.** It is written down and ready.

## Why this can't stay open

Every quarter it stays open costs something specific: the *khādim*s who hold this material are
mostly elderly, the shrines are being renovated in ways that erase physical evidence, and
Saifullah's availability is unfunded goodwill that will not last indefinitely. "Not yet" and
"no" produce the same archive. Only "no" is honest about it.

## The three options

### A. Do it properly — a bounded pilot

**Three recordings at one shrine**, chosen because the entry is already strong enough that the
oral material has something to attach to. Bibi Pak Daman is the obvious candidate: it is the
gold-standard entry, the custodial family already has a documented account (the Sayyid Ahmad
Tokhta Tirmidhi tradition), and a recorded custodial voice would make the archive's central
editorial claim — two traditions, neither resolved — audible rather than just described.

- **Cost:** Saifullah's time, a phone, and the consent script already written.
- **Gate:** all three consents recorded on tape, or nothing uploads.
- **Success test:** would a listener learn something the text entry doesn't already say? If
  after three recordings the answer is no, stop — that is a real finding, not a failure.
- **Risk:** a betrayed consent costs the project its standing at every shrine in the district.
  This is why the pilot is three recordings and not thirty.

### B. Change the stated purpose

Amend CLAUDE.md and HANDOVER to say the archive documents shrines through **field survey,
photography and textual sources**, and that oral history was scoped out in August 2026 for lack
of funding. The standing finding disappears because it is no longer a gap.

- **Honest**, immediate, costs nothing.
- **Cost:** the thing that most distinguished this project from a well-made map is gone. The
  photography and the survey forms are excellent; they are not testimony.

### C. Keep it open

Not recommended, and named here only so it is chosen rather than defaulted into. It is what
has happened for three months.

## Recommendation

**A, scoped to three recordings at one shrine, with B as the pre-agreed fallback** if the pilot
does not happen by an agreed date. That converts an open-ended aspiration into a dated
experiment with a defined failure mode — which is the only version of this that survives an
unfunded project with one engineer.

Concretely: if three consented recordings do not exist by **31 December 2026**, adopt B and
amend the docs. Deciding the fallback *now*, while nobody is invested, is the part that makes
this work.
