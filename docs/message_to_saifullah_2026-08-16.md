**3. Mauj Darya Bukhari — the twelve files you uploaded are not in Drive any more.**

Your survey of 29 July recorded ten photographs and two videos, and the form kept all twelve

Drive, and they went from there.

Three things, in order:

**(a) Before anything else — could Adil check his Drive trash?** The form is his, so its
uploads land on his Drive. If they were deleted rather than purged, they will be at
[drive.google.com/drive/trash](https://drive.google.com/drive/trash) and restoring them takes

**(b) Do you still have the originals on your phone?** If the photos and the two videos are
still in your gallery, sending them again is far better than re-shooting — they are the
pictures from the day of the survey, and no second visit reproduces them.

**(c) If neither, the shrine needs a full re-shoot.** It is the only shrine in the archive
whose survey photographs were uploaded and then lost, and its page now shows a single borrowed
picture from Wikimedia. It is on Edward Road near the old Customs House in Anarkali. Whenever
you are next nearby, these are the things the archive already describes and cannot show:

- The **tomb itself from outside** — it is octagonal, with a low dome and a corner tower at
  each of the eight angles; a wide shot that shows the eight sides is the one picture the entry
  most needs.
- The **recessed blind arches and panelled facades** on the outer walls, close enough to read
  the surface.
- The **blessed tree** planted in the saint's lifetime, and the space around it where people
  sit.
- The **darasgah** where he is buried, and the **small graveyard** beside it — his family, his
  khulafa and gaddi nasheens, and the grave of his cook **Allahu Samad**.
- **Bibi Killah's shrine** (Syedna Bibi Fatima Sani, Mai Fatima) facing his own, near the Jain
  Mandir.
- **What the Orange Line left.** The 2018 station work took the mosque, the langar khana and
  the courtyard. A picture of how the site sits against the construction now would be a record

**Please send as files/documents or a Drive link, not as WhatsApp chat images**, and keep the
