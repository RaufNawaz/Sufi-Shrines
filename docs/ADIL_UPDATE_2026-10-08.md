Salam Adil, a short update on the archive.

The new version of https://spiritualsitespakistan.com/ is going up tonight, and it does three things.

Firstly, the Urdu edition now reads in Mehr Nastaliq, the face Rekhta uses, and the Urdu pages have shed most of their furniture: an entry reads as a clean article, with a simple fact list and a bibliography that runs left to right.

Secondly, the shared ground section and the nearby Auqaf mosques no longer appear on entry pages by default. Both are now switches under Settings, and the shared ground page and the map lens are untouched.

Thirdly, the sheet takes a round of corrections that had been waiting since September. Three Lahore darbars move into the Muslim Shrine category, the built form and status prose moves into proper note columns, Hinglaj Mata finally has its id, and two death years follow the books where two of them agree: Bulleh Shah to 1758 and Baba Farid to 1265. A third book gives 1754 for Bulleh Shah, so that date remains contested, and the note says as much.

Next is the descriptions. Research on forty of the thinnest entries is sitting ready, and turning it into fuller articles, in English and Urdu, is the work for the coming weeks.

Do tell me if anything looks off on your end.

Rauf
