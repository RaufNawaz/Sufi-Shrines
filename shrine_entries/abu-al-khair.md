- **ابوالخیر** — Baghdad scholar who settled at Lahore in Shah Jahan's era, taught by order of Alamgir and Zeb-un-Nisa[OCR?], died aged 105 in Muhammad Shah's reign and was **buried inside his own madrasa**, his grave still pakka and "لوگ اسکا بہت ادب کرتے ہیں"; now under trees with other dervish-students' graves nearby (pp. 362–363, folios 369–370; p. 365, folio 372). His khalifa **محمد نعیم** went on teaching in the madrasa until his own death (p. 363, folio 370).
