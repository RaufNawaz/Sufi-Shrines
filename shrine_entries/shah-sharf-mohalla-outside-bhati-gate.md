- **شاہ شرف (mohalla outside Bhati Gate)** — "ایک عالم و کامل شخص" with thousands of murids; founded the mohalla, built a magnificent mosque and his own pakka maqbara in his lifetime; both demolished for Ranjit Singh's glacis, coffin reburied under a pakka chabutra through فقیر عزیز الدین (pp. 80–81, folios 83–84); contents "شاہ شرف کا محلہ[OCR?]" ۸۴ and "مزار شاہ شرف" ۳۰۰ (pp. 2, 4).
