- **شاہ رستم غازی** — a famous and blessed mazar on a **high mound** west of Nawan Kot; "عالم فاضل یکتائے زمانہ"; the damaged phrase "علمی استہا[OCR?] اور زیب النسا بیگم کا" appears to make him **Zeb-un-Nisa Begum's teacher**; d. "سنہ ۱۰۶۰[OCR?] ہجری"; **Zeb-un-Nisa built his red-stone tomb and marble grave**, pulled down by Ranjit Singh; two underground chambers holding four graves, **the women's and men's separate** (p. 312, folio 315); contents "مزار شاہ رستم غازی[OCR?] ۳۱۵" (pp. 3–4, folios 2–3).
