## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3646: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Multan. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Sher Shah Qadri Multani / شیر شاہ قادری ملتانی** — entry no. ۲۱۶; date (not stated); "مشہور ترین بزرگان ملتان سے"; Qadiriya, "خاندان قادریہ عالیہ", whose "پیری شجرہ" joins the **Gilani sayyids of Uch** and who took faiz from **Sayyid Hamid Ganj Bakhsh Uchi**; "ہزاروں لوگ اس خاندان کے مرید ہیں"; "**کنارے دریا پر ملتان سے پانچ کوس پرے** ان کا مقبرہ معلیٰ زیارت گاہ خلق اللہ ہے" — on the riverbank five kos beyond Multan; the **sajjada nashins** "ظاہری و باطنی عزت رکھتے ہیں". Whether he is the **Sayyid Sher Shah Multani** named as a perfect khalifa of Hamid Ganj Bakhsh at p. 48 the book does not say. (pp. 48, 266, folio 254)
