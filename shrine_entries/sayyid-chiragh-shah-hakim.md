- **سید چراغ شاہ حکیم** — tenth enclosure; **alive and present in Lahore at the time of writing**; **Sayyid Sabzwari[OCR?]**, **physician**, also carrying on **piri-muridi**, murids in the **Chishtiya and Qadiriya**, "ہزار ایک مرید"; bought the land from the zamindars, built the enclosure, made his family's graves in it, planted a garden, mounted a Persian wheel and built a faqir's kotha (p. 296, folio 299). Possibly the same man as **سید چراغ علی شاہ حکیم** of Mubarak Haveli, a "طبیب و فقیر" with a damaged **Chishti** claim and many murids, sons **سید حاکم علی، بہادر علی، نادر علی** (p. 332, folio 339) — **not asserted**.
