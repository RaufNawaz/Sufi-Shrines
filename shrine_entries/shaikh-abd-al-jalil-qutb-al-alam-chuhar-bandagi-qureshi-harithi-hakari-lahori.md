- [PK] **Shaikh Abd al-Jalil, Qutb al-Alam Chuhar Bandagi Qureshi Harithi Hakari Lahori / چوہڑ بندگی** — entry no. 110; arrival dated by the editor to "۸۸۰ھ / ۱۴۷۵ء کے حدود میں", around 880 AH/1475 CE; buried **in his own khanqah**, with the emperor present at his ghusl; four intermediaries to Shaikh Hamid al-Din Abu al-Hakim, "بادشاہ کیچ مقران"; "پنجاب کے رہنے والوں نے خدمت حضرت کو اپنا فخر سمجھا"; his brother **Shaikh Jamal al-Din Abu Bakr b. Abu al-Fath** wrote a large life of him between 947–952 AH/1540–1545 CE, named by the editor *Tazkira-e Qutbiya*, "لاہور کی ثقافتی و مذہبی تاریخ کا ایک قابل قدر ماخذ", published Lahore 1952; appendix row 32 → McLeod Road opposite the Qila Gujjar Singh abadi. (pp. 172–173, folios 160–161; p. 288)
