- **مندر شوالہ لالہ چہوٹے لال** — an eight-sided red-stone shiwala with an underground jalhari, built by **Lala Durga[OCR?] Prashad** on the site of **بودہر داس[OCR?]**'s shiwala, with **eight shops' rent and a salaried Brahmin pujari** (pp. 447–449, folios 454–456). **بودہر داس[OCR?]**: a God-worshipping sadhu famous in the Sikhs' time, named for a بودہر tree he planted; "اصلی نام اُسکا شاید کوئی اور تہا" (p. 447, folio 454).
