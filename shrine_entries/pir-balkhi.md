## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Pir Balkhi / پیر بلخی** — entry no. ۲۰۸; "اصلی نام ان کا تحفۃ الواصلین میں تحریر نہیں"; mazar "کشمیری بازار کے سر راہ ایک مکان کے اندر پختہ چونہ گچ" — on the roadside of **Kashmiri Bazar**; of **Balkh**, came to Hind when Chingiz Khan took it, and was killed with his murids and students when the Chingizid army besieged Lahore hunting Prince Jalal al-Din Khwarazmi; **the editor reads a tablet in the Lahore Museum** that "پیر بلخی کا لوح مزار معلوم ہوتا ہے": *Abu al-Muhamid al-Husain b. Muhammad al-Husain Abu Bakr al-Dhikri al-Balkhi*, aged **98**, martyred **9 Zi al-Hijja 643 AH** (so born 545), and redates the martyrdom away from Chingiz Khan to the Mongol attacks under **Ala al-Din Masud Shah, 643 AH/1245 CE** — **the book's one shrine treated through its epigraphy, and among the strongest candidate rows here.** (pp. 262–263, folios 250–251; pp. 351–352, folios 339–340)

## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

- **پیر بائی[OCR?] / پیر بلخی[OCR?]** — roadside mazar in Kashmiri Bazaar; real name unknown even to the author's source; came from **Balkh** after Chingiz Khan's conquest, killed fighting Quli Khan at Lahore, buried in his own hujra; rebuilt when a bazaar was straightened (p. 165, folio 168); contents "پیر بلخی[OCR?] ۱۶۸" (pp. 3–4, folios 2–3) — the contents name is taken as this mazar on the printed page number; both spellings flagged.
