## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

- **حال مکان ٹالی صاحب (بابا اسری چند خلف بابا نانک)** — south of the lunatic asylum, abutting the railway; Sri Chand not counted among the ten Gurus but honoured as a sahibzada; the **Udwasi/Udasi** faqr defined, the **Nanakpanthi** sect named, and this gurdwara **"اس سب قوم کا مرجع"**; Nag Devta samadh; mandir of Sambat 1898 Bikrami; **a rupee a day stopped**; land lost to the railway and **the sacred tahli felled and regrown** (pp. 227–229, folios 230–232).
