- [PK] **Hazrat Ghulam Muhi al-Din Qasuri Mujaddidi / غلام محی الدین قصوری** — entry no. 97, the book's longest entry; **born 1202, died 1270, aged sixty-eight**, with an unusually elaborate chronogram; "**ان سب بزرگوں کے مزار قصور میں ہیں**" — **the mazars of all these elders are at Kasur**; madhhab "حنفی اعظمی"; held conviction toward **both** Ghaus al-Thaqalain and the Mujaddid; first murid of his paternal uncle **Hafiz Shaikh Muhammad**, with whom he read the Mujaddid's *Maktubat* lesson by lesson, then bai'at with Shah Ghulam Ali at Delhi; father **Hafiz Shaikh Murtaza** in the text, **Shaikh Mustafa** by the editor's correction; grandfather **Shaikh Ghulam Murtaza**; his son **Shaikh Abd al-Rasul** described as living; a standing miracle of the platform. (pp. 150–154, folios 138–142)
