- [PK] **Shaikh Ilm al-Din Chuniwal / شیخ علم الدین چونیوال** — entry no. 112; died **916 AH**; "روضہ حضرت کا اسی علاقہ میں ہے", in the Chunian area; khalifa of Abd al-Jalil Chuhar Lahori, to whom "خدمت کپڑے دھونے کی سپرد تھی" — the service of **washing clothes**; posted to qasba Chunian by his murshid's order; "**دھوبی لوگ حضرت کا عرس کرتے ہیں**" — the **dhobis perform his urs**; the editor reports a different affiliation for him.
