- [PK] **Sayyid Muhammad Nur b. Sayyid Bahawal Sher Gilani Jarwi / سید محمد نور** — entry no. 13; died "in the year nine hundred eighty-eight Hijri", **988 AH**; eldest son and khalifa of Bahawal Sher; his mother's father was **Shah Kamal Bukhari**, "whose mazar is in **qasba Chunian**" and who is famous as **Pir Jahanian**; **editor: the contemporary *Tazkira-e Muqimi* writes the name نور محمد, "جو درست سمجھنا چاہیے"** — which should be considered correct.
