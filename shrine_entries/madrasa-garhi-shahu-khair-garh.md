- **مدرسہ / گڑہی شاہو، formerly خیر گڑہ[OCR?]** — fort-like madrasa built by the subah of Lahore's order, with a pakka domed mosque, students' cells and a fort-style rampart, **charged on the Lahore treasury**; later seized by the plunderer **شاہو**, whence the name, then held by four Shaikhs whose progeny **still hold it** (pp. 362–365, folios 369–372). The contents' "گدھی شاہ ابو[OCR?] ۳۶۹" (pp. 3–4, folios 2–3) falls on this printed page and may be this foundation (or Abul Khair's grave in it) — both readings flagged, **not asserted**.
