## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3533: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Lahore / Qadiri and Gilani. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **The Takiya Ambli Wala enclosure / احاطہ تکیہ انبلی والا** — entry no. 237; dates (not stated); two chahar-diwaris and three lime-plastered chabutras holding the graves of the descendants of **Sayyid Muhammad Ghaus Uchi Halabi Gilani**: Sayyid Sufi Ali (father Sayyid Badr al-Din b. Sayyid Ismail) with Sayyid Umar, Sayyid Hashim and Sayyid Abdul Qadir "Shah Gada"; Sayyid Qasim b. Sayyid Sufi and his descendants; Sayyid Miran Shah, Mir Miran and Sayyid Abu al-Barakat Shah; Sayyid Ismail; and, on a separate structure, **Pir Muhammad Shah Gilani**, whose son Pir Shah Sardar was alive at writing. (pp. 274–275, folios 262–263)
