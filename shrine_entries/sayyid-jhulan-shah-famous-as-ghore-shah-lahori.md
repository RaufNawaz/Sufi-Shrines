## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

*Joined 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/tareekh_lahore.md` (reviewed: no), line 786: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive". Sayyid Jhulan Shah known as Ghore Shah, as in Hadiqat. The line below is copied verbatim.*

- **مزار گہوڑے شاہ بخاری** — "اصلی نام سید جہولن شاہ بخاری", also given as Baha-ud-Din; three-generation Bukhari line from **Uch**, grandfather **Sayyid Usman** the first to come to Lahore and buried **inside the Fort**, titled **Jhula Bukhari** from the palsy; "مادر زاد ولی", **died aged five**, "ماہ ربیع الاول سنہ ۱۰۰۳[OCR?] ہجری"; no dome; **mosque, well and rooms attached with a resident faqir sajjada nashin; thousands of clay horses offered at the annual mela** (pp. 236–237, folios 239–240); Shaikh Mahmud Shah Naqshbandi's tomb just north, "صرف رستہ اسکے درمیان حد فاصل ہے" (p. 260, folio 263). The contents' "مزار گوہر شاہ[OCR?] ۲۳۹" (pp. 3–4, folios 2–3) is on the same printed page and is probably this mazar — both spellings flagged, **not asserted**.
