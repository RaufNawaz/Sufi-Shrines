- **شیخ عبد الرزاق مکی and the نیلا گنبد** — octagonal tomb with a dome in **kashi-kari of green-turquoise**, Sadr Bazar Anarkali; came from **Mecca[OCR?]** in Humayun's era, became **Mauj Darya Bukhari's murid**; the nobles of Lahore believed in him and thousands attended; d. "سنہ ۱۰۸۵ [OCR?] ہجری عہد عالمگیری"; later a powder magazine, shot-store and musket smithy, then a British mess, then released and **now Najm-ud-Din's mosque** (pp. 286–287, folios 289–290); contents "مقبرہ عبدالرزاق مکی[OCR?] ۲۸۹" (pp. 3–4, folios 2–3).
