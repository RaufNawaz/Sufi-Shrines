## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3707: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Bahawalpur, Maharan and the south. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Khwaja Hafiz Abd al-Khaliq Uwaisi / خواجہ حافظ عبدالخالق اویسی** — entry no. 150; died **7 Zil-Hijja 1185 AH**; "مزار گوہر بار قصبہ **مبارک پور متصل بہاولپور** میں زیارت گاہ خلق ہے"; "خاندان اویسیہ کے یہ بزرگ **موجد** ہیں" — **the founder of the Uwaisiya family**, whose faiz came from the spirituality of **Uwais Qarni**; *Lata'if-e Nafisiya fi Faza'il-e Uwaisiya* has him living **on the bank of the Sutlej**, fainting at "Allahu Akbar" and not coming to himself "جب تک گانے والے نہ گاتے"; a journey with Bulleh Shah and three nights of Uwais Qarni's visits; three sons — **Khwaja Qutb al-Din**, who as a child flew away in ecstasy; **Khwaja Arif**, "جن کا مزار بریلی میں ہے"; and **Shaikh Muharram**, "جو قصبہ **لیّہ** میں مدفون ہیں"; khalifa **Khwaja Muhkam al-Din Sahib al-Sair**. (pp. 217, 219–220, folios 205, 207–208)
