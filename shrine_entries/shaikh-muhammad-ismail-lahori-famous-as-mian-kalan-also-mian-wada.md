## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3569: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Lahore / Suhrawardi, Qureshi and the Mufti family of Kotli Muftian. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Shaikh Muhammad Ismail Lahori, famous as Mian Kalan — also Mian Wada / میاں کلاں، میاں وڈا** — entry no. 126; born **995**, died **1085 AH**; "مزار گوہر بار لاہور میں زیارت گاہ خلق ہے"; father Fath Allah b. Abdullah b. Sarfaraz, "قوم کے زمیندار **کھوکھر**" — one of very few statements of a saint's clan; kept the teaching of the Quran running his whole life; thirteen-step chain to Baha al-Din Zakariya Multani; students Mian Taimur Lahori and Mian Hamid Qari Lahori, "پورے پنجاب کے علما نے ان سے … فیض حاصل کیا"; editor, per Sayyid Sharafat Naushahi: mother **Mai فربد [OCR?]** buried at qasba **جوگا کلیان [OCR?], district Gujrat**, father at **mauza جیبہ چیمہ [OCR?] adjoining Ahmad Nagar, district Gujrat**; a 1909 Lahore biography *Sawanih Mian Wada Lahori*; appendix row 41 → inside the **Khanqah Mian Wada**. **Not to be merged with the author's ancestor Mian Kalan (no. 1 above).** (pp. 187–188, folios 185–186 as printed; pp. 289, 303)

## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

*Joined 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/tareekh_lahore.md` (reviewed: no), line 770: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive". Shaikh Muhammad Ismail known as Mian Wadda, as in Hadiqat. The line below is copied verbatim.*

- **میان وڈا / شیخ محمد اسمعیل المشہور میان وڈا** — khanqah and teaching madrasa at Teel Pura, "مدرسہ مسجد [OCR?] تیل المشہور میان وڈا", still inhabited (p. 89, folio 92); born "سال نوسو نا نوین[OCR?] ہجری", pupil of Makhdum Abdul Karim at five, khirqa of khilafat in the **Suhrawardiyya**, madrasa a mile south of Shalamar, d. "سنہ ۱۰۴۵[OCR?] ہجری"; *kham* grave by his own will; three khalifas buried beside him — **Shaikh Jan Muhammad**, **Shaikh Nur Muhammad**, **Hafiz Muhammad Salih** (pp. 237–239, folios 240–242). His **sajjada nashin** keeps Shaikh Hamid Qari's khanqah (pp. 252–253, folios 255–256) and claimed Mian Jan Muhammad's grave (p. 259, folio 262); Raja Suchet Singh was killed at Khanqah Mian Wadda[OCR?] (pp. 194–196, folios 197–199). The richest institutional entry in pp. 236–348.
