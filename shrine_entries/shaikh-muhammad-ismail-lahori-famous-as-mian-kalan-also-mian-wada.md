- [PK] **Shaikh Muhammad Ismail Lahori, famous as Mian Kalan — also Mian Wada / میاں کلاں، میاں وڈا** — entry no. 126; born **995**, died **1085 AH**; "مزار گوہر بار لاہور میں زیارت گاہ خلق ہے"; father Fath Allah b. Abdullah b. Sarfaraz, "قوم کے زمیندار **کھوکھر**" — one of very few statements of a saint's clan; kept the teaching of the Quran running his whole life; thirteen-step chain to Baha al-Din Zakariya Multani; students Mian Taimur Lahori and Mian Hamid Qari Lahori, "پورے پنجاب کے علما نے ان سے … فیض حاصل کیا"; editor, per Sayyid Sharafat Naushahi: mother **Mai فربد [OCR?]** buried at qasba **جوگا کلیان [OCR?], district Gujrat**, father at **mauza جیبہ چیمہ [OCR?] adjoining Ahmad Nagar, district Gujrat**; a 1909 Lahore biography *Sawanih Mian Wada Lahori*; appendix row 41 → inside the **Khanqah Mian Wada**. **Not to be merged with the author's ancestor Mian Kalan (no. 1 above).** (pp. 187–188, folios 185–186 as printed; pp. 289, 303)
