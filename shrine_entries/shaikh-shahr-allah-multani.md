- [PK] **Shaikh Shahr Allah Multani / شیخ شہر اللہ ملتانی** — died **23 Zi al-Hijja 920 AH**; "آپ ملتان میں اپنے مشائخ کے پہلو میں مدفون ہیں" — **buried at Multan beside his own mashaikh**; b. Rahmat Allah b. ستاجی [OCR?] b. Kalu Langah Multani; a scholar and gnostic of his time; three sons Baha al-Din, Shaikh Muhammad Ismail, Muhammad Sadr al-Din; Shaikh Musa Ahangar's first pir; author of the *Tazkira-e Hamidiya*, written in Sultan Sikandar Lodhi's era (d. 923 AH/1517 CE) and translated into Urdu by Pir Ghulam Dastgir Nami, Lahore 1959.
