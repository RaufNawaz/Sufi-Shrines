## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3641: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Multan. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Shaikh Sadr al-Din Arif b. Baha al-Din Zakariya / شیخ صدر الدین عارف** — entry no. 102; died **23 Zi al-Hijja 684 AH** — **four death years are in play**; **mazar in Multan**; son and janishin, who sat on the masnad-e irshad after his father; the estate division is recorded; editor: he combined "درس و تدریس" with guidance, *Tasrif-e Jadwali* is his, and Shaikh Ziya al-Din collected his malfuzat as *Kunuz al-Fawaid* (extracts in *Akhbar al-Akhyar* 59–61, Meerut 1277 AH); a 1958 Faridi monograph exists. **He has no archive row though his mazar is at Multan.** (pp. 161–162, folios 149–150; p. 304)
