## Overview

On Paris Road, in the Mohallah Puran Nagar area of Sialkot's commercial quarter, stands a Hindu temple referred to locally as the Jagannath Temple — a considerably more recent structure than, and a separate building from, the ancient Shivala (Shawala) Teja Singh Temple in Sialkot's Mohallah Dharowal. The two are sometimes conflated in secondary sources — an English Wikipedia article titled "Jagannath Temple, Sialkot" redirects to the Teja Singh temple's page — but the facts recorded for each, location, founding circumstances, and history, do not overlap, and this entry treats them as distinct pending stronger evidence otherwise.

## Founding

Community and press accounts describe the Paris Road temple as built, or substantially re-established, in early 2007, with a grant of roughly Rs 200,000 from the Punjab provincial government under then-Chief Minister Chaudhry Pervaiz Elahi. The grant is reported to have been channelled through a minority-seat provincial legislator, Joseph Hakim Din, at the advocacy of Hakem Ratan Lal Bhagat of Sialkot's District Peace Committee, on behalf of the city's Hindu residents. A further renovation, funded by a roughly Rs 2,000,000 Punjab government grant, is reported for 2017.

## Community and Use

The temple serves Sialkot's small resident Hindu community as a place of worship in a city whose Hindu population, once substantial, was overwhelmingly displaced at Partition. Its modern, grant-funded origins make it emblematic of the more recent, state-assisted rebuilding of minority worship spaces in Punjab's cities, distinct from older survivors like Shivala Teja Singh that predate 1947.

## A Note on the Sources

Much of what is reported about this temple circulates through a small number of near-identical web accounts rather than independent documentation, and no primary institutional record — comparable to the Evacuee Trust Property Board's role at Shivala Teja Singh — has been located confirming its founding or administration. The dates and figures above should be treated as provisional until corroborated by a more authoritative source.
