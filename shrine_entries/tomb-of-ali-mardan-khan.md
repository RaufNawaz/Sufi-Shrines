- **علی مردان خان** — Shahjahani counsellor, son of Ganj Ali Khan, **subahdar of Qandahar under the Safavids**, who handed the fort to Shah Jahan and rose to administer **Lahore, Delhi and Kashmir**; "کروڑون روپیہ اس کے ہاتہ سے صرف ہوے"; the **Nau Lakha** garden, the **Delhi canal** and a canal watering Shalamar credited to him; d. "سنہ ۱۰۶۹ [OCR?] ہجری"; **the tallest tomb in Lahore** (pp. 253–255, folios 256–258).
