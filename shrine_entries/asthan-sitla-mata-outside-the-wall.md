- **استہان سیتلا ماتا** — outside the wall midway between Shah Alami and Lohari Gates; **smallpox vows, a worshipped tree and stone lion, rents and offerings, mortgaged land**, a hospital road bought across it; **resort of both communities** (pp. 186–188, folios 189–191). Named again only in cross-reference as the second Sitla Devi temple, "جسکا ذکر تحریر پر ہو چکا ہے" (p. 452, folio 459).
