## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3554: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Lahore / Naqshbandi and Mujaddidi. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Khwaja Khawand Mahmud Lahori, famous as Hazrat Ishan Naqshbandi / حضرت ایشاں** — entry no. 84; born at Bukhara [OCR?], editor's year **971 AH**; died 12 Shaban 1052 AH; **mazar in Lahore** (no quarter given); descended from Khwaja Ala al-Din Attar through his father Mir Sayyid Sharif; outward bai'at from Khwaja Abu Ishaq Safid [OCR?] — editor: Khwaja Muhammad Ishaq Dahbidi — but an **Uwaisi** nisbat with Baha al-Din Naqshband; Kabul, Kashmir, Delhi, Akbarabad, then Lahore, where he stayed "شاہجہان بادشاہ کی التجا سے", at Shah Jahan's request — **which the editor rewrites as an order of banishment**; his son Khwaja Muin al-Din wrote *Kitab-e Rizwani* on him (copy at the J&K Cultural Academy, Srinagar); a demolition order was issued against his rauza; appendix row 29 → Begampura near the Yatim Khana Dar al-Furqan. (pp. 132–134, folios 120–122; p. 288)
