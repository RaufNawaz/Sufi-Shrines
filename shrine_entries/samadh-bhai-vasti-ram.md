- **سمادہ بہائی وستی رام[OCR?]** — a renowned Granth-reciter **whom Ranjit Singh called his guru**; lakhs in nazr, **jagirs and allowances**; marble samadh on the pattern of the Darbar at Amritsar, against the fort's north wall, with fountains, inlay, a flag and a garden (pp. 201–203, folios 204–206). His haveli is the landmark for Thakurdwara Banke Bihari (pp. 117–118, folios 120–121).
