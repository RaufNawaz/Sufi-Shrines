## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

*Joined 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/tareekh_lahore.md` (reviewed: no), line 785: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive". Sayyid Shah Bilawal Qadiri, as in Hadiqat. The line below is copied verbatim.*

- **شاہ بلاول / مزار سید شاہ بلاول قادری** — the allama of Akbar's, Jahangir's and Shah Jahan's eras; father Sayyid Usman, grandfather Sayyid Isa, from **Herat**, granted the fort of Sheikhupura in jagir by Humayun; born at Sheikhupura, taught by Maulvi Abul Fath Lahori and Shaikh Shamsuddin Qadri; d. 1046[OCR?], era not printed; **a langar of thousands a day**; exhumed by Ranjit Singh's order through Faqir Nur-ud-Din, the body incorrupt after two hundred years (p. 235, folio 238); *pukhta* mazar at the centre of a khanqah **one kos east of Lahore** by Raja Dina Nath's garden on the old Shalamar road, the khanqah owning the land inside its wall; **mela 28 Shaban shared by Hindus and Muslims** with fireworks[OCR?]; walls fallen for want of repair and oversight (p. 236, folio 239); contents "مزار سید شاہ بلاول[OCR?] ۲۳۸" (pp. 3–4, folios 2–3). **Deliberately not mapped**; see Doubts.
