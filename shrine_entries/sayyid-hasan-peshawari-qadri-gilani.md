## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3696: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Peshawar and the Frontier. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Sayyid Hasan Peshawari Qadri Gilani / سید حسن پشاوری** — entry no. 31; died "in the year one thousand one hundred and fifteen", **1115**, and **is buried at Peshawar**; murid of his father Sayyid Abdullah Gilani; his ancestor Sayyid Mahmud migrated from Baghdad to "مٹھہ کے ملک"; "تمام عمر انہوں نے عبادت و ریاضت و تلقین طلبا میں صرف کی"; **the editor corrects him on three points** — it was **Sayyid Abdullah, known as Sahabi**, not Sayyid Mahmud, who came from Baghdad to Mittha; Sayyid Abdullah never went to Peshawar but "مٹھہ" and died there; and "یہ شجرہ نسب مفتی صاحب نے **غلط** لکھا ہے", the genealogy is wrongly written, the correct chain being the one Shah Muhammad Ghaus himself recorded (Amir Shah Qadri Gilani, *Tazkira-e Abdullah Sahabi*, Peshawar 1971; *Tazkira-e Mashaikh-e Qadiriya Hasaniya* 26); a *Khawariq al-Adat* (1189 AH) on him survives with Maulana Amir Shah Qadri, Peshawar. (pp. 72–73, folios 60–61; p. 294)
