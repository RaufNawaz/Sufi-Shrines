## Overview

In Multan stands the shrine of Hazrat Syed Musa Pak Shaheed — a revered Qadiri saint of the sixteenth century and the ancestor of the Gilani (Musa Pak) sayyids, one of Multan’s most eminent religious families. Honoured with the titles _Pak_ (“the pure”) and _Shaheed_ (“the martyr”), Musa Pak carried the Qadiri order of Abd al-Qadir Jilani in Multan and founded a spiritual line that remained influential in the city for centuries. His darbar is among the notable shrines of the “City of Saints.”

## The Life of the Saint

Syed Musa, called Musa Pak Shaheed, was a Sayyid of the Gilani line — descendants of the great Baghdad saint Shaikh Abd al-Qadir Jilani — who flourished in Multan in the sixteenth century. Learned and devout, he was a shaykh of the Qadiri order and a guide of many disciples, and he is remembered for a life of piety and for the sanctity that earned him the epithet _Pak_, “the pure.” Tradition honours him as a _shaheed_, a martyr. Through him and his descendants the Qadiri-Gilani presence was firmly established in Multan, and the Musa Pak Gilani family became one of the leading religious and landed houses of the region. He was buried in Multan, where his shrine arose.

## The Shrine and its Devotion

The shrine of Syed Musa Pak in Multan is kept by his Gilani descendants and is a continuous place of pilgrimage, where devotees come to recite the Fatiha, seek intercession, and honour the saint. It maintains the customary devotions of a Multan darbar — prayer, qawwali, and the free kitchen — and its annual urs gathers the faithful. It forms part of the great constellation of shrines that gives Multan its identity.

## Legacy

Syed Musa Pak Shaheed is remembered as a chief bearer of the Qadiri order in Multan and the founder of the distinguished Musa Pak Gilani line — a saint whose purity and martyrdom won him lasting veneration, and whose family carried his influence through the later history of the city. His shrine remains a revered darbar of the “City of Saints.”

The editor's footnote on the same page dates the first attack on Lahore only: "احمد شاہ ابدالی کا لاہور پر پہلا حملہ (۱۱۶۱ھ) ۱۷۴۸ء کو ہوا".
- (book's word: "پنجاب"; refers to: "۱۱۶۱ھ/۱۷۴۸ء" to "۱۱۸۷ھ/۱۷۷۳ء", as the footnote states; editor's footnote (modern)): rejecting the date the author gives for Ahmad Shah Durrani's farman to Mufti Muhammad Mukarram ("۱۱۲۰ھ [OCR?]"), the editor writes "یہ سنہ غلط ہے۔ احمد شاہ ابدالی نے ۱۱۶۱ھ/۱۷۴۸ء میں پنجاب پر مختلف حملے شروع کیے اور ۱۱۸۷ھ/۱۷۷۳ء میں فوت ہو گیا" (p. 277, folio 265).
- (book's word: "مہاراجہ رنجیت سنگھ والی پنجاب"; refers to: Ranjit Singh's rule, date not stated; Mastan Shah's death given as "سال ایک ہزار دو سو ستتر ہجری"; author's text (1875)): "مہاراجہ رنجیت سنگھ والی پنجاب کو کمال اعتقاد اس کی نسبت تھا". He offered thousands of rupees, which the majzub Mastan Shah refused (p. 246, folio 234).
- (book's word: "پنجاب کے مالک فرنگی"; refers to: the day "جس روز راجہ رنجیت سنگھ مرا تھا", with "نو برس" more of "یہ سلطنت" — the book's "سکھوں کی سلطنت"; author's text (1875), reporting the majzub Taj Shah's saying): "سکھوں کی سلطنت کی خرابی کا حال پہلے ہی اس نے بیان کر دیا تھا ... نو برس اور یہ سلطنت رہے گی پھر پنجاب کے مالک فرنگی ہو جائیں گے". The unit here is the book's "سکھوں کی سلطنت", passing to "فرنگی" (p. 244, folio 232).
- (book's word: "پنجاب", "ملک پنجاب"; refers to: the book's own scope, written in the author's present; the closing chronograms read "۱۲۹۲ھ"; author's text (1875)): chaman 5 will hold "ان بزرگوں کا ذکر ... جو پنجاب میں عالم و فاضل صاحب نام گذرے ہیں" (p. 191, folio 179). Chaman 7 is headed "عَورات صالحات کے ذکر میں جو پنجاب میں گذر چکی ہیں" (p. 247, folio 235). A later section is headed "حالات بزرگان پنجاب جن کی وفات کا سن و تاریخ بپایہ ثبوت نہیں پہنچا" (p. 261, folio 249). In the closing, the author says his Persian *خزینۃ الاصفیا* was too large, so "شائقین ملک پنجاب کا یہ مطلب حاصل نہیں ہوتا تھا کہ کتنے بزرگ پنجاب کے ملک میں صاحب طریقت گذرے ہیں", a difficulty this short Urdu book removes (p. 280, folio 268).
- (book's word: "پنجاب"; refers to: the book's scope, chronogram "۱۲۹۲ھ"; quoted source: chronogram "از مفتی غلام صفدر صاحب لاہوری خلف مؤلف"): "جو خدا کے دوست تھے پنجاب میں   حال ان کا اس سے ہوتا ہے عیان" (p. 282, folio 270).
- (book's word: "پنجاب"; refers to: the scholars of Punjab "شیخ حامد سے لے کر حضرت شیخ بہاؤالدین زکریا ملتانی تک"; editor's footnote (modern)): Maulana Muhammad Aqil Lahori's *تحفۃ المسلمین* records Shaikh Hamid Qari's sayings and also gives "پنجاب کے اکابر علما کے حالات" across that span. The editor says its rare manuscript is in his own library (p. 278, folio 266).
