## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3539: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Lahore / Chishti and Sabiri. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Shah Kaku Chishti Lahori / شاہ کاکو چشتی** — entry no. 51; died "eight hundred and eighty Hijri"; descendant of Baba Farid, khirqa from Shaikh Pir Muhammad Chishti Lahori; **mazar in Lahore outside Delhi Gate**; the editor gives him a different pir, a madrasa in present-day **Landa Bazar** next to the railway station, a famous scholar son, and **no certain death year at all**; appendix row 20 → "لنڈا بازار ، **اب یہ مزار نہیں ہے۔**" — this mazar no longer exists. (pp. 99–100, folios 87–88; pp. 288, 305)
