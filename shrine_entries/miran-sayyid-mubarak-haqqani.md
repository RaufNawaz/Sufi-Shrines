## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3653: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Uch. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Miran Sayyid Mubarak Haqqani b. Sayyid Muhammad Uchi Halabi Gilani / مبارک حقانی** — entry no. 4; died at Lahore in "نو سو چھبیس سال ہجری", **926 AH**, and "نعش مبارک حضرت کی اوچ میں لے جا کر دفن کی" — **body carried to Uch and buried**; left habitation for the jungle, twelve years intoxicated, "کوئی انسان اس کے روبرو نہ آ سکتا"; only one man, **Maruf Chishti**, received khilafat from him. (p. 44, folio 32)
