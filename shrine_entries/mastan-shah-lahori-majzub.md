- [PK] **Mastan Shah Lahori Majzub / مستان شاہ مجذوب** — entry no. 189; died "**سال ایک ہزار دو سو ستتر ہجری**", 1277 AH; "قبر لاہور میں ہے" with **no locality**; went naked in heat and cold, ignored offerings, ate tree leaves when hunger overcame him, and would fall to working the potters' and weavers' looms "نہایت خوبی کے ساتھ" when he passed their workshops; concordance row ۷۱ → **Miani, تکیہ مستان شاہ**. (p. 246, folio 234; p. 291)
