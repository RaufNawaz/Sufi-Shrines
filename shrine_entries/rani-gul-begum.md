- **رانی گل بیگم** — married by **Maharaja Ranjit Singh** "حسب رسوم اہل اسلام کے", the barat coming from **Amritsar**; her jagir the largest of all and, under the English, "ایک ہزار دو سو روپیہ ماہوار"; **garden and tomb built in her own lifetime**, chronogram numeral "۱۲۴۷[OCR?]"; grave on a **black stone platform** with marble over it; d. "سنہ ۱۲۸۲[OCR?] ہجری"; adopted son **Sardar Khan** buried with her, his sons **Bahawal Khan and Jahangir Khan** surviving; **the second enclosure in the Miani graveyard** (p. 292, folio 295; p. 293, folio 296).
