## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

*Joined 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/tareekh_lahore.md` (reviewed: no), line 781: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive". one enclosure, the two Huzuri Sayyid tombs Hadiqat gives as two rows. The line below is copied verbatim.*

- **سید محمود حضوری و سید شاہ نور الدین; سید جان محمد حضوری و سید سرور دین (the Huzuri Sayyids)** — two grand pukka **twelve-doored** tombs in one unheaded enclosure, with an ancient mosque and a **Persian-wheel well**, at mauza Garhi[OCR?]; **Musavi Sayyids** from **Koh-e Ghor** to **Uch** to Lahore, rising in Shah Jahan's era; "مقتدا و پیشوا ے طریقت"; the title *Huzuri* glossed; Jan Muhammad d. 764[OCR?] in one partial and "سنہ ۱۰۶۸ [OCR?] ہجری" in the other — **the two death years are irreconcilable as printed**; thirty-plus-generation shajra printed back to **Imam Musa Kazim**; descendants **Sayyid Ahmad Shah and Husain Shah** living; **annual mela "ایک روز چراغ دوسرے روز قوالی"** (pp. 166–167, folios 169–170; pp. 265–267, folios 268–270). A **سید جان محمد حضوری[OCR?]** and محلہ مسجد سرا[OCR?] are named again as the elders in whose service Abul Khair stayed (p. 362, folio 369).
