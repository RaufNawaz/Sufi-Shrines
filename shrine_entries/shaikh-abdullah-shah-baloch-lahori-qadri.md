- [PK] **Shaikh Abdullah Shah Baloch Lahori Qadri / شیخ عبد اللہ شاہ بلوچ** — entry no. 40; died 8 Jumada al-Awwal 1212 AH, chronogram "غریب"; "مزار بیرون شہر موضع مزنگ میں ہے"; murid and khalifa of Shaikh Sharaf al-Din Panipati; "ذات کے بلوچ", amassed wealth by camel-driving and founded **Kot Abdullah** at Mozang before renouncing; modern book: Muhammad Din Kalim, *Abdullah Shah Baloch*, Lahore 1971; **not the archive's Abdullah Shah Ghazi**; appendix row 19 → Kot Abdullah Shah, Mozang.
