## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3517: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Lahore / Qadiri and Gilani. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Shaikh Abdullah Shah Baloch Lahori Qadri / شیخ عبد اللہ شاہ بلوچ** — entry no. 40; died 8 Jumada al-Awwal 1212 AH, chronogram "غریب"; "مزار بیرون شہر موضع مزنگ میں ہے"; murid and khalifa of Shaikh Sharaf al-Din Panipati; "ذات کے بلوچ", amassed wealth by camel-driving and founded **Kot Abdullah** at Mozang before renouncing; modern book: Muhammad Din Kalim, *Abdullah Shah Baloch*, Lahore 1971; **not the archive's Abdullah Shah Ghazi**; appendix row 19 → Kot Abdullah Shah, Mozang. ## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

*Joined 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/tareekh_lahore.md` (reviewed: no), line 769: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive". Abdullah Shah Baloch of Mozang, Qadiri, as in Hadiqat. The line below is copied verbatim.*

- **عبد اللہ شاہ بلوچ / شیخ عبد اللہ شاہ بلوچ, Mozang** — "ایسا صاحب باطن فقیر ہوا جسکے لاہور کے علما و فضلا مرید تھے"; a settlement with pakka rampart named after him, the founder's name broken (pp. 77–78, folios 80–81); famous for zuhd, riyazat and karamat "شہر لاہور کے تین حاکمون کیوقت"; bai'at in the **Qadiriya**; founded **Kot Abdullah Shah**; his **Punjabi verses** still current; murid **Hafiz Ghulam Muhammad, imam of Wazir Khan's mosque**; d. "۱۲۱۸ [OCR?] ہجری"; present tomb and mosque built by **Sardar Khan Baloch, lambardar of Mozang** (pp. 282–283, folios 285–286). **Deliberately not mapped** — not shrine-of-abdullah-shah-ghazi; see Doubts.
