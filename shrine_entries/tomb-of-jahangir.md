- **نور الدین جہانگیر بادشاہ غازی** — northern bank of the **Ravi**, four miles north of Lahore; forty cells, four minarets, eleven surviving tanks, marble inlaid with agate, lapis, sapphire, coral and more, "اکثر نگہینے اتار بہی لئے گئے ہیں"; **jagirs and salaried Quran-readers**; an Afghan jagirdar quartered in it; marble sent to **Sri Darbar Sahib**; British embankment and repair (pp. 314–319, folios 321–326), with the adjoining **Shahjahani sarai** and its counted cells (pp. 319–320, folios 326–327).
