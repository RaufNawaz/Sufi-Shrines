## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

- **جنگلی چراغ شاہ, سید علی شاہ and چراغ شاہ** — so named because the murid **Chiragh Shah** planted many trees, "جنگلی پنجابی زبان میں … درختوں کو کہتے ہیں"; **Sayyid Ali Shah**, "قوم کا سید گیلانی", came from the **Deccan** in Ranjit Singh's early administration and d. "سنہ ۱۲۳۳[OCR?] ہجری"; Chiragh Shah his **جانشین**, d. "۱۲۵۵" (unflagged), built the present four-minaret pakka building; two more graves of their **servants**; pakka mosque, well and kotha attached (p. 314, folio 321). The contents' "خانقاہ سید علی ۳۲۵[OCR?]" (pp. 3–4, folios 2–3) may be this khanqah — number flagged and not equal to the folio, **not asserted**.
