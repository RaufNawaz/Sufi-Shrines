- [PK] **Shaikh Muhammad Ismail Muhaddith wa Mufassir Lahori / شیخ محمد اسماعیل محدث** — entry no. 129; died **448 AH**, chronogram "متاب"; "مزار شہر لاہور کے جنوب کی طرف ہے"; of the great **Bukhari** sayyids, settled in Lahore in the time of Sultan Masud Ghaznavi; *Tuhfat al-Wasilin* makes him **the first of the callers to Islam to come to Lahore**, through whom "دین اسلام … پنجاب میں شائع ہوا" — a claim of Islamic priority against Data Ganj Bakhsh, who arrives seventeen years later on the book's own figures; **the editor demolishes all of this**; appendix row 43 → Hall Road. ## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

*Joined 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/tareekh_lahore.md` (reviewed: no), line 812: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive". came to Lahore under the Ghaznavids and spread Islam: Hadiqat's Shaikh Muhammad Ismail Muhaddith. The line below is copied verbatim.*

- **شاہ اسماعیل** — a very ancient domeless tomb near the Deputy Commissioner's kothi and the **pani-wala well**, on a platform roughly one storey above road level, covered in creepers[OCR?] with a **neem tree** at the grave's head; came to Lahore in **Sultan Mahmud Ghaznavi's** time and spread Islam, preaching every Friday so that thousands "جامہ اسلام پہینتے تہے"; "سنہ ۴۱۸[OCR?] ہجری مین یہ لاہور مین آیا اور سنہ ۴۵۸[OCR?] ہجری مین مر گیا ۳۶[OCR?] برس لاہور مین قیام رکہا", chronogram "مہتاب"; **its garden sold to the English by the mujawir** (pp. 297–298, folios 300–301); contents "مزار شاہ اسمعیل ۳۰۰" (pp. 3–4, folios 2–3).
