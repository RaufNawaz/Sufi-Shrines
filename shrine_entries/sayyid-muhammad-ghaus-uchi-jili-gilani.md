## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3652: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Uch. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Sayyid Muhammad Ghaus Uchi Jili Gilani / سید محمد غوث اوچی حلبی گیلانی** — died **923 AH**, buried **at Uch**; "اول پنجاب میں آئے" — the first of Abd al-Qadir Jilani's line to come to the Punjab; his forebear Sayyid Abul Abbas Ahmad b. Sayyid Sufi left Baghdad with his brother Sayyid Abu Sulaiman at the sack under Hulagu (the footnote dates it "۶۵۶ھ / ۱۲۵۸ء") and settled at **Aleppo**, where he was born; travelled, performed Hajj several times, stayed some years at Lahore, returned to Aleppo, and came by Khurasan to Multan and Uch; **Sultan Husain Mirza of Sind and Sultan Sikandar Lodhi** became his murids; four sons — Sayyid Abdul Qadir Sani, Sayyid Abdullah Rabbani, Mubarak Haqqani, Sayyid Muhammad Nurani — "from each of whom a separate faiz flowed"; a scholar and poet with the takhallus "Qadri", diwan in the Siraj al-Din Azar collection, Punjab University, no. **۸۱۴ [OCR?]**, and *Miftah al-Ikhlas*. (pp. 40–41, folios 28–29; p. 294)
