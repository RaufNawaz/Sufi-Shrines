## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/tareekh_lahore.md` (reviewed: no), line 845: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Mosques. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- **مسجد پری محل** — built by **Nawab Wazir Khan** opposite Shah Alami Gate for his own prayers only; never desolate (p. 151, folio 154). The **عمارت پری محل** itself is listed among the range's havelis (pp. 326–347, folios 333–354) and as his memorial inside the city, bought from the Sarkar and rooted out to its foundations (p. 358, folio 365).
