## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3705: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Bahawalpur, Maharan and the south. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Shaikh Nur Muhammad Chishti, famous as Nur Muhammad مجھیل [OCR?] Maharvi / نور محمد مہاروی** — entry no. 72; died **1205 AH**; "مزار پر انوار قریہ **تاج سرور** متصل قصبہ **مہاراں** کے ہے" — at the village Taj Sarwar adjoining qasba Maharan; **greatest khalifa** of Fakhr-e Jahan, whom he went "پنجاب سے دہلی" to for bai'at, returning to settle "قصبہ مہاراں علاقہ **نواب بہاول خاں** میں"; four chief khalifas — Khwaja Thani titled **Khalifa Sahib**, Maulana Qazi Muhammad Aqil, Khwaja Muhammad Jamal, Khwaja Sulaiman — "اور خلیفے بھی آپ کے بہت ہیں"; editor: his ancestral name was **مجھیل [OCR?]** and Shah Fakhr al-Din changed it to Nur Muhammad; a ten-link paternal genealogy from Hindal b. Tatar; "آپ کا تعلق قوم **کھرل** سے تھا", of the **Kharral** tribe; **born 14 Ramadan 1142 AH at qasba Chotala**, east of Mahar towards Bahawalpur; bai'at **1165 AH**; three sons Nur al-Samad Shahid, Nur Ahmad, Nur Hasan; "جن میں سے **تیس** کے نام محفوظ ہیں" — thirty khalifa names preserved. (pp. 119–120, folios 107–108)
