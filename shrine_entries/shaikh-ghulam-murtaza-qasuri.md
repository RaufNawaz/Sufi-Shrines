- [PK] **Shaikh Ghulam Murtaza Qasuri / حافظ غلام مرتضیٰ قصوری** — the text gives his death as the year **1202** and the ijazat of all four orders, as a murid of the khalifa of Haji Fath Ali Muttaqi Sialkoti; editor: "مفصل حالات نہیں ملتے"; "آپ نے پنجاب میں **سکھ گردی** سے تنگ آکر **پشاور** ہجرت فرمائی", he migrated to Peshawar wearied by the Sikh *gardi*; a contemporary of **Ahmad Shah Abdali**, among the ulama who answered his question in the Punjab; "آپ پشاور میں فوت ہوئے۔ اور وہاں سے آپ کی نعش **قصور** لا کر دفن کی گئی" — died at Peshawar, corpse brought to **Kasur** and buried. (pp. 152, 208, folio 140)
