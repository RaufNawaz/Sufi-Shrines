## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/tareekh_lahore.md` (reviewed: no), line 815: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Muslim shrines, khanqahs and saints' tombs. - **امام بارہ شیعہ امامیہ and گامی شاہ** — outside Bhati Gate south of Data Ganj Bakhsh; a domed rauza "بنام نہاد امام حسین صاحب کے" over a **sard-khana** with **replica graves** of Imam Hasan and Imam Husain carrying the taziya, and the grave of **Gami Shah**, "بانی مبانی اس مکان امام بارے کا"; patrons **Nawab Nawazish Ali Khan Qizilbash and Nawab Nasir Ali Khan**; "یہ مکان مربع قوم شیعہ امامیہ کا ہے"; the **Zuljanah procession and the 1849 riot** (pp. 301–302, folios 304–305); contents "امام بارہ شیعہ امامیہ ۳۰۴" (pp. 3–4, folios 2–3).
