## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3655: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Uch. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Sayyid Hamid Ganj Bakhsh b. Sayyid Abdul Razzaq b. Sayyid Abdul Qadir Sani / سید حامد گنج بخش** — entry no. 8; died **978 AH**, sharpened by the footnote to **19 Zi Qada 978 AH**, chronogram "شیخ محبوبی"; **buried at Uch**, where he lived all his life; a great elder of the **Qadiriyya Azamiyya**; "بادشاہان وقت بھی ان کی آستان بوسی کو اپنا افتخار جانتے تھے"; perfect khalifas **Sayyid Sher Shah Multani** and **Shaikh Daud Kirmani**; after his death **Sayyid Jamal al-Din Musa** sat on the masnad-e mashikhat; a contemporary *Manaqib al-Asfiya* survives at Gujrat. (pp. 48, folio 36; p. 293)
