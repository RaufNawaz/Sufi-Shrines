- [PK] **Haji Jamiat / Haji Jamil and the Qadam Rasul / مزار حاجی جمعیت و مزار قدم رسول** — entry no. ۲۳۰; date (not stated); "یہ مکان ریل کے پڑاؤ کے شمال کی طرف غیر آباد پڑا ہے" — north of the railway halt, lying unfrequented; allegiance to Shah [rang OCR?] Bilawal, khalifa of Lal Husain Lahori; his forebears came **from Iran** bringing "قدم رسول کا ایک پتھر سرخ", a red footprint stone, over which he built a dome; the inscription was copied by the author. **The heading says حاجی جمعیت and the text حاجی جمیل.** (pp. 271–272, folios 259–260)
