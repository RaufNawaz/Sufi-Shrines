- [PK] **Shaikh Sa'di بلخاری [OCR?] Mujaddidi Lahori / شیخ سعدی** — entry no. 89; died 3 Rabi al-Thani, a Wednesday, "in the year 1108" (the editor elsewhere records d. 1100 AH [OCR?]); "مزار بیرون لاہور موضع مزنگ کے پاس ہے"; seized at eight, brought through Maulana Haji Sa'd Allah Wazirabadi to Shaikh Adam Banuri, went with him to the Haramain, returned to Lahore when Adam died at Medina; the editor supplies his real name and doubts the nisba; appendix row 30 → "**ترنڈی [OCR?]** سٹریٹ سعدی پارک لٹن روڈ". ## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

*Joined 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/tareekh_lahore.md` (reviewed: no), line 813: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive". Shaikh Sadi Balkhari, Naqshbandi Mujaddidi, as in Hadiqat. The line below is copied verbatim.*

- **شیخ سعدی بلخاری لاہوری** — old building south of Mozang; **Naqshbandiyya Mujaddidiyya**, murid of **Shaikh Adam Banuri**; exposition in **Khazinat al-Asfiya**; d. "سنہ ۱۰۸۵[OCR?] عہد عالمگیری"; chronogram "زندہ دل سعدی بلخاری"; **no dome, by his will**; garden and two wells lost, one closed for the road and one cultivated by **Hidayat Khan Baloch**; only the mazar and its three-gaz enclosure remain (p. 298, folio 301).
