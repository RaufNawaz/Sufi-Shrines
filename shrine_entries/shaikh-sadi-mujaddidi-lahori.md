- [PK] **Shaikh Sa'di بلخاری [OCR?] Mujaddidi Lahori / شیخ سعدی** — entry no. 89; died 3 Rabi al-Thani, a Wednesday, "in the year 1108" (the editor elsewhere records d. 1100 AH [OCR?]); "مزار بیرون لاہور موضع مزنگ کے پاس ہے"; seized at eight, brought through Maulana Haji Sa'd Allah Wazirabadi to Shaikh Adam Banuri, went with him to the Haramain, returned to Lahore when Adam died at Medina; the editor supplies his real name and doubts the nisba; appendix row 30 → "**ترنڈی [OCR?]** سٹریٹ سعدی پارک لٹن روڈ".
