## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

- **شاہ شمس الدین قادری لاہوری** — **Qadiri**, murid of **Shah Abu Ishaq Qadri** and **pir of Shah Bilawal Qadri**; "آزاد طبع و مجرد"; date "۱۰۲۴ [OCR?]" from two nastaliq couplets; square four-doored tomb, corner minarets now demolished, south-east of the Lieutenant Governor's kothi; **tomb and attached garden built by Shah Jahan**, the garden now traceless (pp. 255–256, folios 258–259). **Deliberately not mapped**; see Doubts.
