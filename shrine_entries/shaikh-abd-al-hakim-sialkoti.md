## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

- [PK] **Shaikh Abd al-Hakim Sialkoti / شیخ عبدالحکیم سیالکوٹی** — entry no. 142 (contents: printed p. 196); died "باقوال صحیح" **1068 AH** — and **no grave is given**; "اکابر علما و اعاظم فضلائے پنجاب سے"; held great honour at the **Chaghtai** court and "بادشاہ کی اجازت سے اس نے لاہور میں درس جاری کیا", his written fatwa accepted by all the ulama of Hind; **Shaikh Ahmad Mujaddid Alf-e Thani titled him "آفتاب پنجاب"**, Sun of the Punjab, and he wrote *Dala'il al-Tajdid* in the Mujaddid's support; he translated Abd al-Qadir Jilani's *Ghunyat al-Talibin* into Persian "حسب الایمائے حضرت **شاہ بلاول لاہوری**"; among the few honoured in the Mughal era with the title *Allami*; sons **Maulvi Abdullah Lahori, Maulvi Rahmat Allah, Maulvi Rahman Quli**; a 1969 Panjab University PhD thesis and Muhammad Din Fauq's *Malik al-Ulama Sialkoti*, Lahore 1924. **A Sialkot figure, not the archive's Imam Ali-ul-Haq.** (pp. 19, 196, 208–209, folio 196; pp. 296, 302, 344)
