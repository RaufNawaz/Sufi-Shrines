## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

*Joined 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/tareekh_lahore.md` (reviewed: no), line 794: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive". Shaikh Muhtaram Naqshbandi, as in Hadiqat. The line below is copied verbatim.*

- **شیخ محترم نقشبندی** — **Naqshbandi**; came in Akbar's era, lived to Alamgir's, d. **1102 AH**; square pukka tomb with four corner domelets north of the Shalamar road; three graves, his and **two of unknown name**; walls carrying Arabic and Persian verses, "کچہ پڑہے جاتے مین اور کچہ نہین"; **rauza auctioned to an Englishman, verandahed into a kothi and the graves demolished** (pp. 256–257, folios 259–260); contents "روضہ شیخ محترم[OCR?] ۲۵۹" (pp. 3–4, folios 2–3).
