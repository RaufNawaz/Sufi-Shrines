- [PK] **Pir Siraj al-Din, known as Pir Siraji / پیر سراجی** — entry no. ۲۰۹; date (not stated); mazar in **محلہ جوڑی [OCR?]**; came to Lahore **from Bukhara in the reign of Sultan Muhammad Tughluq**; refused the office of *qaza*, and when the king ordered him killed "لاہور کے عمائد و ارکان جمع ہو کر" got his life spared, after which he closed his teaching, dismissed his murids and lived in seclusion until he died.
