- [PK] **Sayyid Baha al-Din Gilani, known as Bahawal Sher Qalandar Hujrawi / بہاول شیر قلندر** — entry no. 7; died **18 Shawwal 973 AH**, "buried at **Hujra**", the year also derivable from the chronogram phrase "عبدالقادر ثانی"; descendant of Sayyid Abdul Razzaq b. Abdul Qadir Jilani through the full printed chain; born at **Baghdad**, came to Hindustan with his father and paternal aunt and settled at **Badayun**, where his father died and his aunt, "جو رابعہ عصر تھی", trained him; the founding of Hujra is given as a legend. The **fourth appendix** independently names him "**سید بہاء الدین (بہاول شیر قلندر)**", builder of the hujra at **Hujra Shah Muqim** whose "بنیانھا الی الان قائمۃ", the building still standing, and works the numerical value of "عبدالقادر ثانی" out to **۹۷۳ھ**. (pp. 46–47, folios 34–35; pp. 342–343, folios 330–331)
