## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3687: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Gujrat, Gujranwala, Wazirabad and Sialkot. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Maulana Haji Sa'd Allah / Asad Allah Wazirabadi / حاجی سعد اللہ وزیر آبادی** — **d. 1102 AH [OCR?]**; a great scholar with authority to guide and a khalifa of **Shaikh Adam Banuri**; "سلسلہ مجددیہ کے تذکروں میں آپ کا نام **اسد اللہ** بھی لکھا ہوا ہے" and in contemporary books it is Asad Allah that is written, with a letter of Khwaja Saif al-Din addressed to him; "ان کا مسکن و موطن **سرائے وزیر خان (وزیر آباد ضلع گجرات پاکستان)** تھا", and in **Peshawar, Lahore and Wazirabad** he propagated the **Mujaddidiya Adamiya** silsila; it was he who took Shaikh Sa'di Lahori to Shaikh Adam Banuri. (pp. 139, 213, folios 127, 201)
