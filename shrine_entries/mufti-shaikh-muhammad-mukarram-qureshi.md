- [PK] **Mufti Shaikh Muhammad Mukarram Qureshi / مفتی شیخ محمد مکرم قریشی** — entry no. 243; dates (not stated); held **both the offices of ifta and qaza** in Lahore at the end of Chaghtai rule, and again under Ahmad Shah Durrani, whose special farman "محررہ ماہ رمضان سنہ ۱۱۲۰ھ [OCR?]" was **in the author's own possession** — **editor: "یہ سنہ غلط ہے"**, Abdali's Punjab campaigns began 1161 AH/1748 CE and he died 1187 AH/1773 CE; "ہم جدی" with the author; a decline of his descendants recorded.
