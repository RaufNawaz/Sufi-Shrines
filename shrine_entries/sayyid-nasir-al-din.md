## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3657: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Uch. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Sayyid Nasir al Din b. Makhdoom Jahaniyan Jalal al-Din Bukhari Uchi / نر ناصر الدین** — entry no. 109; died **847 AH**; "مزار گوہر بار اوچ میں زیارت گاہ خلق اللہ ہے"; called *Nar* Nasir al-Din for his offspring — his sons "ایک سو تک پہنچ گئی تھی", a hundred, of whom the long-lived were **twenty-one sons and two daughters**; engaged in guidance by his father's instruction although the sajjada went to Rajan Qattal; "لاکھوں طالبان خدا ان کی دستگیری سے مقام قرب تک پہنچے". (p. 172, folio 160)
