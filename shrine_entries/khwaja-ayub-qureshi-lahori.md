- [PK] **Khwaja Ayub Qureshi Lahori / خواجہ ایوب قریشی** — entry no. 145; died **Thursday 21 Jumada al-Thani 1155 AH**; mazar in Lahore, no quarter; author of *Masnavi Makhzan-e Ishq* and the ***Sharh-e Ayubi*** on Rumi's *Masnavi*, permission for which he took from Rumi himself, finding the licensing verses at the head of the opened page; Rumi told a student in a dream "خواجہ ایوب کو **اویسی ہماری روحانیت سے** پہنچا ہے"; the editor has seen manuscripts at the Panjab University Library and with Maulana Sayyid Muhammad Tayyib Hamdani of Qasur, gives the completion year as **1120 AH** against a rival **1111 AH**, and shows Mulla Abd al-Khaliq's 1155 AH *Sharh-e Masnavi* drawing heavily on it; **Mufti Ghulam Sarwar's own relation by marriage** — described as Mufti Muhammad Taqi's pupil and son-in-law, not his son. (pp. 24, 26, 210–212, folios 198–200)
