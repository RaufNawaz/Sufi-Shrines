## From Mufti Ghulam Sarwar Lahori, Hadiqat al-Awliya

*New row proposed 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/hadeeqat_ul_aulia.md` (reviewed: no), line 3615: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive", under Lahore / Lahore's undated shrines, martyrs and unattributed sites. The line below is copied verbatim; nothing was added or reworded. Coordinates not yet known.*

- [PK] **Pir Zaki / پیر ذکی** — entry no. ۲۰۷; date (not stated); "مزار عین دروازہ ذکی کے درمیان ہے اور اسی بزرگ کے نام سے دروازہ شہر کا مشہور ہے" — in the middle of **Zaki Gate**, which is named after him; *Tuhfat al-Wasilin* has him **martyred in the Mughal fighting**, resisting them when the city fell; **two graves — the head's in the gate, the body's in a stable inside the city adjoining it**. (p. 262, folio 250)

## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore
