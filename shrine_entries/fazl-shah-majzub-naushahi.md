## From Kanhaiya Lal, Tarikh-e Lahore

*Joined 10 October 2026 from `entries/book_takeaways/tareekh_lahore.md` (reviewed: no), line 819: the book's "Figures and sites not in the archive". Fazl Shah majzub, Naushahi, as in Hadiqat. The line below is copied verbatim.*

- **فضل شاہ مجذوب** — a faqir who wandered Lahore in the last years of Sikh rule, from **mauza Masidpur[OCR?] Kuhna, Zafarwal, Sialkot**; a mosque post and then **spectacle-making** before faqiri; murid of **Rahman Shah, a Naushahi faqir**; died of **dropsy** "دہم ساون سمن ۱۹۱۰[OCR?] بکرمی"; khanqah between Masti and Kashmiri gates inside a government garden granted to Nawab Nawazish Ali Khan; son **Buland Shah** sold the properties and died destitute; granddaughter **Nawardah Begum** lives in a purdah house inside the mazar's enclosure; **no karamat is narrated at all** (pp. 325–326, folios 332–333); contents "خانقاہ فضل شاہ[OCR?] ۳۳۴[OCR?]" (pp. 3–4, folios 2–3), number flagged and not equal to the folio.
