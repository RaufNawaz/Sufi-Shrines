- [PK] **Shaikh Jan Muhammad Thani Lahori Suhrawardi — Jan Muhammad Parvezabadi / جان محمد ثانی** — entry no. 127; died **1100** and buried in **Mohalla Parvezabad**, then **reburied three years later**; khalifa of Shaikh Ismail Mudarris Lahori, first murid of Shaikh Abd al-Hamid; "تمام پنجاب کے علما اس کے قول پر صاد کرتے تھے"; had a special hujra built at Parvezabad for his residence and teaching; appendix row 42 → the same address as Mian Wada.
