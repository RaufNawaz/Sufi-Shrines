- **مندر دیوی دوارہ رجو و دہرمن** — an old and much-revered Devi Dwara in کوچہ رجو و دہرمن, **the lane itself named for it**; sanctum sunk like a cellar, eight steps down, a clay[OCR?] pindi marking where Rajo, Dharman and the Devi's manifestation sank into the earth, two sacred charpais, **two Ashtami melas and year-round jagas** (pp. 438–440, folios 445–447). **رجو و دہرمن**: two women of Akbar's era, of the اڑوٹری[OCR?] qaum, to whom the Devi gave darshan on call; they revived a dead child, passed Akbar's test of the seeds, and sank into the earth rather than marry Jahangir's Muslim subah of Lahore (pp. 438–439, folios 445–446).
