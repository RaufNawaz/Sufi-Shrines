## What changes

59 cells in 15 rows, nothing else (the builder compares every cell with the live sheet and refuses
otherwise): `Latitude`, `Longitude`, one sentence appended to the internal `qa_note` saying where the new
point comes from and what the old one was, and `COORD_FIXED` added to `flags` (one row had it already).

| Tier | Rows | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| A | 10 | Two or three independent records (DOAM, OpenStreetMap, Wikidata) agree on the point; satellite shows the shrine there |
| B | 5 | One precise record (OSM or DOAM), with the satellite image showing a shrine or the graveyard the row's own Location names |

Moves range from 385 m (Zanjani Sahib) to 2.1 km (Akhund Panju Baba). Lal Shahbaz Qalandar's pin was
in fields across the canal; Ranjit Singh's samadhi, Malik Ayaz (on Data Darbar's own point), and three
Miani Sahib darbars that shared one point each get their own.

**Not moved:** Chandragup (the pin is on the mud volcano itself, the sacred site; OSM's point is open
plain), and three rows with no independent point, flagged in the review for a person: Khawaja
Feroz-ud-Din (Miani Sahib), and the two Gandava rows, which share a town-centre point.

## Before importing

Open three or four images in `data/review/pin_check_2026-10-30/` (red: current pin; green: proposed;
right panel: close-up of the proposed point). Every image was read before this file was built, and the
reading is in the review CSV's `satellite_read` column, but a person's glance is the check this rests on.

## Import (RULE 3)

1. Import promptly; if the sheet has been edited since 31 October, rebuild: the builder refuses if any
   of these 15 pins has moved since the review.
2. File > Import > Upload `import_2026-10-31.csv` > **Replace current sheet**, separator **comma**,
   **"Convert text to numbers, dates and formulas" OFF**.

## After the import

`npm run data:build`, `npm run data:validate`, and the GIS rebuild for the archive layer
(`docs/GIS_FOUNDATION.md`), then commit the refreshed data. The PostgreSQL copy (`docs/DATABASE.md`)
takes it at its next import.
