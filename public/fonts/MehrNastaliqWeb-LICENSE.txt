Coverage note (measured 14 September 2026; revised 30 October 2026)
------------------------------------------
Mehr Nastaliq Web maps 108 codepoints; Noto Nastaliq Urdu maps 333. Two
codepoints used by this archive's Urdu content are absent from Mehr and are
served by Noto Nastaliq Urdu, which stays in the stack directly behind it:

  U+0768  ݨ  ARABIC LETTER NOON WITH SMALL TAH   (Punjabi/Saraiki)
  U+0680  ڀ  ARABIC LETTER BEHEH                 (Sindhi)

Both occur only inside quoted verse — Bulleh Shah's Punjabi kafi and a
Sindhi bait of Shah Abdul Latif. A third, U+066D (the hemistich separator of a
Persian couplet of Lal Shahbaz Qalandar), left the corpus with the Urdu
articles of 30 October 2026. src/styles/__tests__/nastaliqCoverage.test.ts holds this list against
the shipped Urdu corpus and fails if a fourth appears.
