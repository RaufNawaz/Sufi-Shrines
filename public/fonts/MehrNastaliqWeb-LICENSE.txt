Coverage note (measured 14 September 2026)
------------------------------------------
Mehr Nastaliq Web maps 108 codepoints; Noto Nastaliq Urdu maps 333. Three
codepoints used by this archive's Urdu content are absent from Mehr and are
served by Noto Nastaliq Urdu, which stays in the stack directly behind it:

  U+0768  ݨ  ARABIC LETTER NOON WITH SMALL TAH   (Punjabi/Saraiki)
  U+066D  ٭  ARABIC FIVE POINTED STAR            (hemistich separator)
  U+0680  ڀ  ARABIC LETTER BEHEH                 (Sindhi)

All three occur only inside quoted verse — Bulleh Shah's Punjabi kafi, a
Persian couplet of Lal Shahbaz Qalandar, and a Sindhi bait of Shah Abdul
Latif. src/styles/__tests__/nastaliqCoverage.test.ts holds this list against
the shipped Urdu corpus and fails if a fourth appears.
